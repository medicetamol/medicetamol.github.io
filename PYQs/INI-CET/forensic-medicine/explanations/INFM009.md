# Distant Range

**Distant-range** firearm wounds show only the mechanical effects of the bullet: an **abrasion collar** and a **grease (dirt) collar** at the entry point. Since the muzzle is beyond the reach of gunpowder gases and particles, there is **no tattooing (stippling), soot blackening, or singeing**, which are seen only in contact, close and intermediate range wounds.

## Classification

| Range | Findings |
|---|---|
| Contact | Muzzle imprint, soot, seared/blackened margins, stellate tearing |
| Close | Soot blackening and singeing, with or without tattooing |
| Intermediate (near) | Tattooing (stippling) present, no soot |
| **Distant** | **Only abrasion and grease collar; no soot, no tattooing, no singeing** |

## Trigger Point

> **No tattooing, no soot, no singeing**, only abrasion and grease collars: distant range by exclusion.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Contact Range**
Shows muzzle imprint, seared and blackened wound margins from hot gases, often with stellate splitting.
None of these are present here.

**C. Close Range**
Shows soot blackening and singeing of hair around the wound.
Both are absent in this case.

**D. Near (Intermediate) Range**
Characterised by gunpowder tattooing (stippling) around the wound.
Tattooing is explicitly absent here.

## Mind Capsule

> No soot, no tattooing, no singeing = distant range (abrasion + grease collar only).
