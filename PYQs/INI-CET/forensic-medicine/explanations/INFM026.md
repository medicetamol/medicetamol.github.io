# Bomb Blast Injuries

Flying fragments and the blast wind in an explosion produce a characteristic **triad of abrasions, bruises and punctate (punctured) lacerations** over the body surface facing the blast, with blast-related injuries on top of this.

## Trigger Point

> Triad of abrasions, bruises and punctate lacerations.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Fall from height**
Produces fractures, internal organ injuries and abrasions or contusions at the impact points, not a scattered triad.

**C. Road traffic accidents**
Produce patterned injuries such as bumper fractures, grazed abrasions and run-over injuries.

**D. Firearm injuries**
Have an entry wound with an abrasion collar, tattooing or blackening, and an exit wound.

## Mind Capsule

> Abrasions + bruises + punctate lacerations = explosive (blast) injury.
