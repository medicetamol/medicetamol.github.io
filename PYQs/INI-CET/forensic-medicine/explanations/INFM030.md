# 20 to 24 Weeks

Under the **MTP (Amendment) Act, 2021**, termination up to 20 weeks needs the opinion of one registered medical practitioner, and from **20 to 24 weeks** the opinion of **two** is needed (for specified categories of women). Beyond 24 weeks, a Medical Board decides for substantial fetal abnormalities.

## MTP Act Gestation Limits

| Gestation | Opinion required |
|---|---|
| Up to 20 weeks | One registered medical practitioner |
| 20 to 24 weeks | Two registered medical practitioners |
| Beyond 24 weeks | Medical Board (substantial fetal abnormalities) |

## Trigger Point

> "At what gestational age is the opinion of two doctors required?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 16 to 20 weeks**
This falls within the up-to-20-week limit, which needs one practitioner.

**B. Less than 16 weeks**
Also needs only one practitioner.

**D. 24 to 28 weeks**
Not covered by the two-doctor rule; a Medical Board is involved for fetal abnormalities.

## Mind Capsule

> MTP: ≤ 20 weeks = 1 doctor; 20–24 weeks = 2 doctors; > 24 weeks = Medical Board.
