# 18 Years

Under the MTP Act, a woman aged **18 years or above** can give valid consent herself. For a **minor (below 18)** or a mentally ill woman, the consent of her **guardian** is required.

## Trigger Point

> "Minimum age at which a girl can give valid consent for MTP."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 12 years**
Below the age at which the law recognises valid consent; a guardian's consent is required.

**B. 16 years**
Still a minor for the purpose of the Act, so guardian consent is needed.

**D. 21 years**
Not required; the age of majority of 18 is sufficient.

## Mind Capsule

> MTP consent: ≥ 18 years = self; < 18 years = guardian.
