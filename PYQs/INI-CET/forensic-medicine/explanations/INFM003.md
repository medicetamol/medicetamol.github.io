# Contusion

A **contusion (bruise)** is a blunt-force injury caused by rupture of small blood vessels in subcutaneous tissue, with **no break in the continuity of the skin**. It shows sequential colour change as haemoglobin breaks down, useful for rough age estimation.

## Trigger Point

> Blunt injury **without a break in the continuity of the skin**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Stab Wound of the Abdomen**
A penetrating, sharp-force injury that breaches the skin and often deeper structures.
The skin is clearly breached.

**B. Laceration**
A blunt injury, but one where the skin **is torn** once tissue tolerance is exceeded, leaving irregular, bridging-tissue-containing wound edges.
Continuity of the skin is broken.

**C. Bomb Blast Injury**
Usually combines burns, lacerations and penetrating fragment wounds.
The skin surface is typically breached.

## Mind Capsule

> Blunt injury with intact skin = contusion; blunt injury with torn skin = laceration.
