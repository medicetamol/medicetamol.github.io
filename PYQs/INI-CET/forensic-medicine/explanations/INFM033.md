# Severe Body Pain for 5 Days

Under **BNS Section 116**, grievous hurt includes emasculation, permanent loss of sight or hearing, loss of a member or joint, permanent disfigurement of head or face, fracture or dislocation of a bone or tooth, and any hurt that endangers life or leaves the person in **severe bodily pain or unable to follow ordinary pursuits for 20 days**. A 5-day duration is too short.

## Trigger Point

> "Which injury is **not** considered grievous hurt?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Emasculation**
The first kind of grievous hurt listed.

**B. Dislocation of elbow**
Fracture or dislocation of a bone or joint qualifies.

**D. Loss of a member**
Privation of any member or joint qualifies as grievous hurt.

## Mind Capsule

> Grievous hurt needs severe pain or inability to follow ordinary pursuits for 20 days, not 5.
