# MTP on the Opinion of a Single Doctor

Under the **MTP Act (as amended)**, termination **beyond 20 weeks (up to 24 weeks for specified categories, including survivors of rape/POCSO)** requires the opinion of **two registered medical practitioners**, not one. A minor's consent for both examination and MTP is given by a **guardian**, and in a case of rape/POCSO, the police must be informed and forensic evidence preserved.

## Trigger Point

> **16-year-old, raped, 16 weeks pregnant**: what is NOT part of her legally correct management.

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Inform the Police**
Rape and POCSO cases are medico-legal, and reporting to the police is mandatory.
This is a genuine part of management.

**B. Preserve Fetal Tissue for DNA Analysis**
Products of conception are vital forensic evidence linking the accused.
This is standard practice in such cases.

**C. Take Consent from the Parents/Guardian**
As a minor, she cannot give valid consent herself; a guardian must consent for examination and MTP.
This is correctly part of management.

## Mind Capsule

> MTP beyond 20 weeks = opinion of TWO registered doctors (not one); minors need guardian consent.
