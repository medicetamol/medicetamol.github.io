# Body Packing

**Body packing** is the deliberate, planned **swallowing of multiple, professionally sealed packets of narcotics** for smuggling, usually by couriers ("mules"). Packet rupture can cause fatal acute intoxication, making it a recognised cause of sudden death in traffickers.

## Trigger Point

> **Multiple, well-sealed, professionally prepared packets swallowed** for international trafficking.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Body Stuffing**
The hurried, unplanned swallowing of **poorly wrapped or unwrapped** drugs, typically to hide evidence when confronted by police.
The packaging is crude, unlike the professional packets here.

**C. Masking**
Not a standard forensic term for drug concealment in this context.
It does not describe internal concealment of packets.

**D. Body Pushing**
Not a recognised standard term; concealment inside body cavities other than swallowing is usually called "body pushing/stuffing" loosely, but the classic, examined term for swallowed professional packets is body packing.
It does not match the scenario as precisely as body packing.

## Mind Capsule

> Professional, sealed packets swallowed for smuggling = body packing; crude, hurried swallowing to hide evidence = body stuffing.
