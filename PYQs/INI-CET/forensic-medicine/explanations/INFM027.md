# Mees' Lines

**Mees' lines** (transverse white bands on the nails) are a feature of **arsenic** poisoning, and also seen with thallium. They are not a feature of mercury exposure.

## Trigger Point

> "Which finding is **not** seen in a worker exposed to mercury?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Tremors and Mad Hatter's disease**
Chronic mercury poisoning causes tremors ("hatter's shakes") and erethism (irritability, personality change), the basis of "mad as a hatter".

**B. Basophilic stippling**
Best known in lead poisoning, but listed as a feature of heavy metal exposure in this question's context.

**D. Peripheral neuropathy**
Chronic mercury poisoning can cause neuropathy along with tremor and erethism.

## Mind Capsule

> Mees' lines = arsenic (and thallium); mercury = tremors, erethism, stomatitis.
