# Elapidae – Myotoxic

**Elapidae** (cobra, krait, coral snakes) have mainly **neurotoxic** venom. **Viperidae** have haemotoxic (vasculotoxic) venom. **Myotoxic** venom is characteristic of **sea snakes**, so labelling Elapidae as myotoxic is the incorrect statement.

## Snake Venom Types

| Snake group | Main venom action |
|---|---|
| Elapidae (cobra, krait) | Neurotoxic |
| Viperidae (Russell's viper, saw-scaled viper) | Haemotoxic |
| Sea snakes | Myotoxic |

## Trigger Point

> "One of the following statements regarding venom is **incorrect**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Common cobra – Neurotoxic**
Cobra venom blocks neuromuscular transmission, causing ptosis and paralysis.

**B. Common krait – Neurotoxic**
Krait venom is neurotoxic, with delayed progressive paralysis.

**D. Viperidae – Hemotoxic**
Viper venom causes coagulopathy, bleeding and local tissue damage.

## Mind Capsule

> Elapids = neurotoxic; vipers = haemotoxic; sea snakes = myotoxic.
