# Cytochrome C Oxidase

**Cyanide** (and carbon monoxide) bind the iron of **cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV)**, blocking the final step of the electron transport chain. Cells cannot use oxygen, giving histotoxic hypoxia.

## ETC Inhibitors

| Complex | Inhibitors |
|---|---|
| I (NADH dehydrogenase) | Rotenone, amobarbital |
| II (Succinate dehydrogenase) | Malonate, carboxin |
| III (Cytochrome C oxidoreductase) | Antimycin A |
| IV (Cytochrome C oxidase) | Cyanide, CO, azide, H₂S |

## Trigger Point

> Cyanide and CO poisoning inhibit which enzyme as the primary mechanism.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. NADH dehydrogenase**
Complex I, inhibited by rotenone and amobarbital.

**B. Succinate dehydrogenase**
Complex II, inhibited by malonate.

**C. Cytochrome C oxidoreductase**
Complex III, inhibited by antimycin A.

## Mind Capsule

> Cyanide, CO, azide, H₂S = Complex IV (cytochrome c oxidase).
