# A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4

Poisons are classified by their action. **Lead** is a metallic irritant, **croton** an organic (vegetable) irritant, **opium** a CNS depressant (somniferous) and **cocaine**, in this classification, is listed as a deliriant.

## Correct Matches

| Substance | Type |
|---|---|
| Lead | Metallic irritant |
| Croton | Organic irritant |
| Opium | CNS depressant |
| Cocaine | Deliriant |

## Trigger Point

> Match lead, croton, opium and cocaine to types of poisoning.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4**
Swaps lead and croton: lead is a metal, croton is of plant origin.

**C. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2**
Pairs lead with CNS depressant and opium with metallic irritant, which is wrong.

**D. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1**
Reverses the classification of every substance.

## Mind Capsule

> Lead = metallic irritant; croton = organic irritant; opium = somniferous; cocaine = deliriant (as per this key).
