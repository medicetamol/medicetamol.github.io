# Strychnine

**Strychnine** poisoning causes **convulsant** effects by blocking inhibitory **glycine receptors** in the spinal cord, producing violent, painful tonic-clonic convulsions with the patient **fully conscious** between spasms. Death is from **exhaustion and asphyxia during a convulsion**, not from a direct central respiratory-centre depression.

## Trigger Point

> Poisoning where **central respiratory depression is NOT a feature**: a convulsant poison rather than a CNS depressant.

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Opium (Opioids)**
Opioids act on mu-receptors in the brainstem respiratory centre, causing direct, dose-dependent **respiratory depression**.
This is their classic lethal mechanism.

**C. Barbiturates**
Barbiturates potentiate GABA-A and depress the medullary respiratory centre.
Respiratory depression is a hallmark of overdose.

**D. Datura (Atropine-Type)**
Anticholinergic toxicity causes delirium and hyperthermia; severe cases can eventually compromise respiration, and datura is grouped here as a CNS-depressant-type poison in contrast to a convulsant.
Compared with strychnine, its mechanism is not direct medullary respiratory depression, so it fits the pattern of "not a pure convulsant."

## Mind Capsule

> Strychnine = convulsant (glycine antagonist), death by exhaustion/asphyxia during a fit, not direct respiratory centre depression.
