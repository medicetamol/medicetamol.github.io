# Quaternary Blast Injury

Blast injuries are classified by mechanism. **Quaternary** injuries are all other effects of the explosion **not covered by the first three categories**, including **burns from the fireball/heat**, toxic inhalation, and psychological effects, without needing the blast wave, fragments or displacement of the body.

## Classification

| Category | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Primary | Blast (over)pressure wave |
| Secondary | Flying fragments/debris striking the body |
| Tertiary | Body thrown against a structure/ground |
| **Quaternary** | **Burns, toxic gas, and other miscellaneous effects (not primary, secondary, or tertiary)** |

## Trigger Point

> Burns from **fireball and heat**, explicitly **not** from blast wave, fragments, or being thrown: quaternary by definition.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Primary Blast Injury**
Caused by the pressure wave itself, damaging air-filled organs (lungs, ears, bowel).
Not a burn mechanism.

**C. Secondary Blast Injury**
Caused by fragments and debris propelled by the explosion, causing penetrating and blunt trauma.
The question explicitly excludes flying fragments.

**D. Tertiary Blast Injury**
Caused by the body being physically displaced and striking a surface.
The question explicitly excludes this mechanism.

## Mind Capsule

> Primary = pressure wave; secondary = fragments; tertiary = body thrown; quaternary = burns and everything else.
