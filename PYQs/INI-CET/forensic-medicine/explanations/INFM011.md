# Atropine

The picture is **organophosphate poisoning**: **muscarinic** effects (**pinpoint pupils, bradycardia, salivation, sweating, lacrimation**) with respiratory depression. **Atropine** is the **treatment of choice** and is given in repeated doses until bronchial secretions dry up.

## Trigger Point

> **Pinpoint pupils + bradycardia + excessive salivation, sweating, lacrimation** after drinking a liquid.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Organophosphate poisoning | Opioid poisoning |
|---|---|---|
| Pupils | Pinpoint | Pinpoint |
| Secretions and sweating | Increased | Not increased |
| Antidote | Atropine (+ pralidoxime) | Naloxone |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Pralidoxime**
A cholinesterase reactivator used **in addition** to atropine, not as the first life-saving drug.

**C. Flumazenil**
Reverses benzodiazepines.

**D. Naloxone**
Reverses opioids, which cause pinpoint pupils and respiratory depression but **no salivation, sweating or lacrimation**.

## Mind Capsule

> Miosis + secretions + bradycardia = **organophosphate** → **atropine first**, then pralidoxime.

## Memory Hook

> **DUMBELS**: **D**iarrhoea, **U**rination, **M**iosis, **B**radycardia/bronchorrhoea/bronchospasm, **E**mesis, **L**acrimation, **S**alivation.
