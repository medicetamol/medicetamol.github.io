# Palmer's Notation

**Palmer (Zsigmondy-Palmer) notation** divides the mouth into four quadrants with a bracket symbol and marks the **permanent teeth as 1–8** and the **deciduous teeth as A–E** in each quadrant. The charts with numbers and letters in four quadrants marked R and L fit this system.

## Dental Notation Systems

| System | Format |
|---|---|
| Palmer | Quadrant brackets with 1–8 (permanent) and A–E (deciduous) |
| Universal | Permanent 1–32; deciduous A–T |
| FDI (ISO) | Two-digit code: quadrant number + tooth number |
| Haderup | Plus (+) and minus (−) signs with tooth numbers |

## Trigger Point

> Dental charts with numbered and lettered teeth in four quadrants, with R and L marked.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Universal system**
Numbers the teeth continuously from 1 to 32 (letters A–T for deciduous), without quadrant divisions.

**C. FDI formula**
Uses two-digit numbers (e.g. 11, 46) rather than letters and brackets.

**D. Haderup system**
Uses plus and minus signs with numbers, and no quadrant brackets.

## Mind Capsule

> Quadrant brackets + 1–8 / A–E = Palmer; two-digit codes = FDI; 1–32 = Universal.
