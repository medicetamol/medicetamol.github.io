# Aluminium Phosphide

**Aluminium phosphide** releases **phosphine** gas, which reduces silver nitrate to metallic silver. Filter paper soaked in silver nitrate turns **black** when held over gastric contents.

## Trigger Point

> Silver nitrate-impregnated filter paper turns black with gastric lavage contents.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Malathion**
An organophosphate that inhibits cholinesterase; it does not produce phosphine.

**C. Barbiturates**
CNS depressants, identified by chemical or toxicological tests, not by this paper test.

**D. Opium**
Identified by Marquis or ferric chloride tests, not by silver nitrate.

## Mind Capsule

> Silver nitrate paper turning black = phosphine gas (aluminium phosphide).
