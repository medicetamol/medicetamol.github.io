# Serology

**Palmar lesions** with a genital ulcer (chancre) three weeks earlier indicate **secondary syphilis**. Diagnosis is confirmed with **serology**: non-treponemal tests (VDRL or RPR) and treponemal tests (TPHA or FTA-ABS).

## Trigger Point

> Lesions on the palms with a genital lesion three weeks earlier.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. KOH mount**
For fungal infections.

**C. Gram stain**
Used for gonorrhoea and other bacteria; spirochaetes are not seen on Gram stain.

**D. Tzanck smear**
Shows multinucleated giant cells in herpes simplex or zoster.

## Mind Capsule

> Palmar rash + earlier genital ulcer = secondary syphilis; confirm with serology.
