# Scabies

**Scabies** is caused by the mite *Sarcoptes scabiei var. hominis*. It presents with **intense itching, worse at night**, with papules, vesicles and burrows. In **infants** the palms, soles, face and scalp are involved, and **family members are typically affected**. First-line treatment is topical permethrin applied to the whole family.

## Trigger Point

> **Intensely itchy papules and vesicles on palms and soles, with similar lesions in family members**: a contagious infestation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Psoriasis**
Well-defined erythematous plaques with silvery scale over extensors and scalp.
It is not intensely itchy, vesicular or contagious.

**C. Atopic Dermatitis**
Infantile eczema favours the face and extensor surfaces, with a personal or family atopic history.
Family members are not affected in the same acute pattern.

**D. Seborrhoeic Dermatitis**
Greasy yellowish scale on the scalp, face and napkin area.
It is usually non-itchy and not seen in contacts.

## Mind Capsule

> Nocturnal itch + palms/soles in an infant + similar lesions in contacts = scabies.
