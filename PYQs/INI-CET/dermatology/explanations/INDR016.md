# Permethrin, Benzyl Benzoate and Crotamiton (1, 3, 4)

In infants, the preferred scabicides are **5% permethrin, 25% benzyl benzoate (diluted for infants)** and **10% crotamiton**. **Oral ivermectin** is not recommended in small children (below 15 kg).

## Trigger Point

> Recommended treatment of scabies in an infant.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 2, 4**
Includes ivermectin and leaves out permethrin.

**B. 1, 2, 3**
Includes ivermectin.

**D. 1, 2, 4**
Includes ivermectin and leaves out benzyl benzoate.

## Mind Capsule

> Infant scabies = topical agents; ivermectin is avoided under 15 kg.
