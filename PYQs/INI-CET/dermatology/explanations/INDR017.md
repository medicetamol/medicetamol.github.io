# Blue-green Pigmentation

Topical steroids cause **atrophy, striae, telangiectasia, acneiform eruption, hypertrichosis, hypopigmentation** and perioral dermatitis. **Blue-green pigmentation** is not a recognised side effect.

## Trigger Point

> "Side effects of topical steroids include all **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Hypertrichosis**
A known local effect.

**B. Atrophy**
The most important local adverse effect.

**C. Acneiform eruption**
Steroid-induced acne.

## Mind Capsule

> Topical steroids: atrophy, striae, telangiectasia, hypertrichosis, acne, hypopigmentation.
