# Cerebriform Tongue - Pemphigus Vegetans

**Pemphigus vegetans** (Neumann and Hallopeau types) is a variant of pemphigus in which flexural erosions become hypertrophic, vegetating plaques. The tongue shows a furrowed, **cerebriform** pattern, the pairing the question keys as correct.

## Correct Matches

| Sign | Condition | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Positive Nikolsky | Pemphigus vulgaris | Standard association (suprabasal acantholysis) |
| Negative Nikolsky | Bullous pemphigoid | Standard association (subepidermal, tense bullae) |
| Pseudo-Nikolsky | SJS / TEN | SJS and TEN show extensive epidermal detachment; use of the term "pseudo-Nikolsky" varies between sources |
| **Cerebriform tongue** | **Pemphigus vegetans** | **Keyed answer** |

## Trigger Point

> **Cerebriform tongue**: a furrowed, brain-like tongue surface, classically described in pemphigus vegetans.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Positive Nikolsky - Pemphigus Vulgaris**
Nikolsky is indeed positive in pemphigus vulgaris.
It is conventionally a true pairing, so this option is weak as a distractor.

**B. Negative Nikolsky - Bullous Pemphigoid**
Subepidermal blisters have a strong roof and the sign is negative.
Also a conventional true pairing.

**C. Pseudo-Nikolsky - Stevens-Johnson Syndrome**
SJS/TEN involve full-thickness epidermal necrosis with skin sloughing on friction.
The "pseudo-Nikolsky" label is not used consistently, so this pairing is unreliable.

## Mind Capsule

> Cerebriform tongue = pemphigus vegetans; Nikolsky positive = pemphigus, negative = pemphigoid.
