# Ceftriaxone

Urethral discharge with confirmed **penicillin-resistant gonococcal infection** is treated with a **third-generation cephalosporin, ceftriaxone**, since penicillins, tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are unreliable.

## Trigger Point

> MSM patient with urethral discharge and confirmed penicillin-resistant infection.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Amoxiclav**
A penicillin combination, which is not suitable for penicillin-resistant strains.

**B. Tetracycline**
Widespread resistance, so unreliable for gonorrhoea.

**D. Vancomycin**
Acts on Gram-positive organisms, not gonococci.

## Mind Capsule

> Resistant gonococcal urethritis = ceftriaxone (plus coverage for chlamydia per protocol).
