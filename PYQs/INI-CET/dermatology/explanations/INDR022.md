# 1 and 3

The **epidermis regenerates** from the epithelium of hair follicles (bulge and bulb) and sebaceous glands. It is derived from **ectoderm**, whereas the dermis and hypodermis come from **mesoderm**.

## Statement Check

| Statement | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 1. Epidermis regenerates from hair bulbs and sebaceous glands | **True** |
| 2. Stratum corneum is the outermost cellular layer | **False as keyed** (it consists of dead, anucleate corneocytes) |
| 3. Epidermis originates from the ectoderm | **True** |
| 4. Dermis and hypodermis originate from the endoderm | **False** (they arise from mesoderm) |

## Trigger Point

> Four statements on the skin and its embryological origin.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1, 2**
Includes statement 2, which the key treats as false.

**B. 2, 3**
Includes statement 2 and leaves out statement 1.

**C. 3, 4**
Statement 4 is wrong, as the dermis is of mesodermal origin.

## Mind Capsule

> Epidermis = ectoderm; dermis and hypodermis = mesoderm.
