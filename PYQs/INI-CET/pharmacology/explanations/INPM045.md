# Valproate

**Sodium valproate** is the most teratogenic antiepileptic, associated with **neural tube defects**, craniofacial anomalies and impaired neurodevelopment. It should be avoided in women of child-bearing age where possible.

## Trigger Point

> "Which anti-epileptic is not given during pregnancy?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Lamotrigine**
Considered among the safer choices in pregnancy.

**C. Levetiracetam**
Preferred option with a low malformation risk.

**D. Carbamazepine**
Carries some risk (neural tube defects) but a lower risk than valproate; it can be used when needed, with folate.

## Mind Capsule

> Valproate = neural tube defects and cognitive impairment; avoid in pregnancy.
