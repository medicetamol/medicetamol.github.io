# Half-life

**Loading dose = (target plasma concentration × volume of distribution) / bioavailability**. It fills the body "compartment" to the desired level at once. **Half-life** governs the maintenance dose interval and the time to steady state, not the loading dose.

## Trigger Point

> "Loading dose of an oral drug depends on all of the following **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Volume of distribution**
Larger Vd needs a larger dose to reach the same plasma concentration.

**B. Plasma concentration**
The target concentration is a direct multiplier in the formula.

**D. Bioavailability**
For oral drugs, only a fraction (F) reaches circulation, so the dose is divided by F.

## Mind Capsule

> Loading dose = Cp × Vd / F; maintenance dose = Cp × clearance × dosing interval / F (depends on clearance).
