# Thalidomide as an Antimetabolite

**Thalidomide** is an **immunomodulatory** drug (TNF-α inhibition, anti-angiogenic, T-cell co-stimulation), not an antimetabolite immunosuppressant. Antimetabolites include azathioprine, mycophenolate and methotrexate.

> "Which statement regarding thalidomide is **incorrect**?"

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Multiple myeloma**
Used in combination regimens for multiple myeloma.

**C. Erythema nodosum leprosum**
Drug of choice for severe ENL (type 2 lepra reaction).

**D. Teratogenic**
Causes phocomelia; needs strict pregnancy-prevention programmes.

## Mind Capsule

> Thalidomide = immunomodulator (myeloma, ENL) and a classic teratogen (phocomelia).
