# Histamine H2 Receptor

**Ligand-gated ion channels (ionotropic receptors)** open directly on ligand binding (fast synaptic transmission): examples include **nicotinic ACh, GABA-A, and 5-HT3** receptors. The **histamine H2 receptor** is a **G-protein-coupled (metabotropic) receptor**, acting through Gs and cAMP, not a channel.

## Trigger Point

> **EXCEPT**: identify the receptor that is NOT ligand-gated, i.e., metabotropic instead.

## Why the Others Are True

**A. 5-HT3 Receptor**
A ligand-gated cation channel, one of the few ionotropic serotonin receptor subtypes.
Genuinely ligand-gated.

**B. Nicotinic Acetylcholine Receptor**
The prototypical ligand-gated cation channel, mediating fast synaptic and neuromuscular transmission.
Genuinely ligand-gated.

**C. GABA-A Receptor**
A ligand-gated chloride channel mediating fast inhibitory transmission.
Genuinely ligand-gated.

## Mind Capsule

> Ligand-gated receptors: nicotinic ACh, GABA-A, glycine, 5-HT3, NMDA/AMPA/kainate; H1 and H2 histamine receptors are GPCRs.
