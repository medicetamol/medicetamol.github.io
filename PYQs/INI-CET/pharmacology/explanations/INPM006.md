# Sunitinib-c-KIT/VEGFR, Nilotinib-BCR-ABL, Trastuzumab-HER2, Gefitinib-EGFR

Targeted anticancer drugs are matched to their molecular targets as follows: **sunitinib** is a multi-kinase inhibitor of **c-KIT and VEGFR**; **nilotinib** is a second-generation **BCR-ABL** tyrosine kinase inhibitor (like imatinib); **trastuzumab** is a monoclonal antibody against **HER2**; **gefitinib** inhibits **EGFR** tyrosine kinase.
