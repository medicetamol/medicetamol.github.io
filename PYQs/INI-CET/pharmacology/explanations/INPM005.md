# Beta-Adrenergic Receptor

**Beta-adrenergic receptors (beta1, beta2, beta3)** are coupled to the **stimulatory G protein (Gs)**, activating adenylate cyclase and raising intracellular **cAMP**, mediating effects like increased heart rate and contractility, and bronchodilation.

## Trigger Point

> **Coupled to Gs**: the classic stimulatory GPCR among the given receptors.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. M2 Muscarinic Receptor**
Coupled to the **inhibitory Gi** protein, decreasing cAMP and heart rate.
Opposite coupling to beta receptors.

**B. M1 Muscarinic Receptor**
Coupled to **Gq**, activating phospholipase C and the IP3/DAG pathway.
Different second-messenger system from Gs.

**D. Alpha-2-Adrenergic Receptor**
Coupled to the **inhibitory Gi** protein, decreasing cAMP.
Opposite in action to the Gs-coupled beta receptor.

## Mind Capsule

> Gs = beta-adrenergic (and H2, D1); Gi = alpha-2, M2, D2; Gq = alpha-1, M1, M3, H1.
