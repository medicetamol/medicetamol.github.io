# Stimulation of Thyroid Hormone Receptor-Beta

**Resmetirom** is a liver-directed, selective **thyroid hormone receptor-beta (THR-beta) agonist**, approved for **metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH/NASH)** with liver fibrosis. By selectively stimulating hepatic THR-beta, it increases fatty acid oxidation and reduces hepatic fat and fibrosis, while avoiding the systemic (cardiac, bone) effects of non-selective thyroid hormone action.

## Drug Facts

| Drug | Mechanism | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Resmetirom** | **Thyroid hormone receptor-beta agonist (liver-selective)** | MASH/NASH with fibrosis |

## Trigger Point

> **Mechanism of action of resmetirom**: selective thyroid hormone receptor-beta stimulation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Blockage at NPC1L1 Transporter**
This is the mechanism of **ezetimibe**, which reduces intestinal cholesterol absorption.
Not related to resmetirom's action on the liver.

**B. Activation of PPAR-gamma**
This is the mechanism of the **thiazolidinediones** (e.g., pioglitazone), used for insulin sensitisation in diabetes.
A different nuclear receptor target from resmetirom.

**C. Blockage of Acetyl-CoA Carboxylase**
This describes newer investigational agents targeting de novo lipogenesis (such as some ACC inhibitors), not resmetirom's mechanism.
Resmetirom acts through a nuclear hormone receptor, not this enzyme.
