# D1 Agonist

**Fenoldopam** is a selective **dopamine D1 receptor agonist**. It causes arterial vasodilation, especially in renal and mesenteric vessels, and is used by IV infusion in hypertensive emergencies. It does not cross the blood-brain barrier.
