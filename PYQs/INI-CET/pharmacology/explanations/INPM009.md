# Voriconazole

**Voriconazole has no activity against Mucorales (the agents of mucormycosis)**, and can paradoxically increase risk of breakthrough mucormycosis in patients on voriconazole prophylaxis. Effective agents include **amphotericin B (first-line), posaconazole and isavuconazole**.

## Drug Facts

| Antifungal | Activity against mucormycosis |
|---|---|
| Amphotericin B | Effective (first-line) |
| Posaconazole | Effective (salvage/step-down) |
| Isavuconazole | Effective (salvage/step-down) |
| **Voriconazole** | **NOT effective; can predispose to breakthrough mucormycosis** |

## Trigger Point

> **NOT effective for mucormycosis**: the triazole with a gap in Mucorales coverage.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Isavuconazole**
A newer triazole with proven efficacy against Mucorales, approved for mucormycosis treatment.
Effective, unlike voriconazole.

**B. Posaconazole**
Also active against Mucorales and used as salvage or step-down therapy.
Effective, unlike voriconazole.

**D. Amphotericin B**
The first-line drug for mucormycosis, active against most Mucorales species.
Effective, and in fact the mainstay of treatment.

## Mind Capsule

> Mucormycosis: amphotericin B, posaconazole, isavuconazole work; voriconazole does NOT (and can predispose to it).
