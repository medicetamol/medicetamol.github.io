# Vilazodone

**Vilazodone** is a newer antidepressant classified as a **serotonin partial agonist and reuptake inhibitor (SPARI)**: it inhibits the serotonin transporter (like an SSRI) while also acting as a **partial agonist at the 5-HT1A receptor**, aiming for a faster onset with less sexual dysfunction.

## Drug Facts

| Drug | Class | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Vilazodone** | **SPARI** | SERT inhibition + 5-HT1A partial agonism |

## Trigger Point

> **Novel antidepressant, SPARI**: serotonin partial agonist and reuptake inhibitor.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Trazodone**
A serotonin antagonist and reuptake inhibitor (**SARI**), sedating, often used for insomnia, not classified as a SPARI.
Different receptor mechanism (antagonist, not partial agonist).

**C. Imipramine**
A classical **tricyclic antidepressant (TCA)** inhibiting both serotonin and noradrenaline reuptake with anticholinergic effects.
An older drug class, not a SPARI.

**D. Amitriptyline**
Also a **TCA**, with prominent sedative and anticholinergic side effects.
Not a novel SPARI agent.

## Mind Capsule

> SPARI = vilazodone (and vortioxetine, a related multimodal agent); SARI = trazodone.
