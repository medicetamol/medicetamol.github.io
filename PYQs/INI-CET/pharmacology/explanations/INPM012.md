# Therapeutic Index = 4

The **therapeutic index (TI)** measures the safety margin of a drug: **TI = TD50 / ED50** (median toxic dose divided by median effective dose). A higher TI indicates a wider, safer margin between effective and toxic doses.

## Working

1. TD50 = 400 mg, ED50 = 100 mg
2. TI = TD50 / ED50 = 400 / 100 = **4**

## Trigger Point

> **Median toxic dose 400 mg, median effective dose 100 mg**: calculate TI = TD50/ED50.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 0.25**
This is the reciprocal (ED50/TD50 = 100/400), which is not how therapeutic index is conventionally defined.
Using this inverted ratio gives a misleadingly low number.

**C. 1**
This would only be true if TD50 and ED50 were equal, which is not the case here.
Does not reflect the given values.

**D. 500**
This appears to come from adding the two values rather than dividing them.
Not a valid pharmacological calculation.

## Mind Capsule

> Therapeutic Index = TD50 / ED50; higher TI = wider safety margin.
