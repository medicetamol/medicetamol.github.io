# Avoid Acidic Foods/Beverages Before Nicotine Gum/Lozenge

Nicotine from gum or lozenges is absorbed through the **buccal mucosa**, which requires an **alkaline pH**. **Acidic beverages (coffee, soda, juice)** lower salivary pH and reduce absorption, so patients are advised to **avoid them for about 15 minutes before and during use**.

## Trigger Point

> **Correct statement about NRT**: the acidic food/beverage precaution before buccal absorption products.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Nicotine Gum Delivers More Nicotine Than an Equivalent Lozenge**
In general, lozenges tend to deliver **similar or slightly more** nicotine than an equivalent-strength gum, since gum absorption depends on adequate chewing technique.
This statement misrepresents typical delivery comparisons.

**C. Varenicline Carries a Black Box Warning for Cardiovascular Risk**
The original black-box warning was for **neuropsychiatric** effects, and this warning was actually **removed** after further safety data; cardiovascular risk was assessed separately and found to be small.
This does not accurately describe the well-known original warning.

**D. Swallowed Nicotine is Well Absorbed from the GI Tract**
Swallowed nicotine undergoes **extensive first-pass hepatic metabolism**, giving poor bioavailability; hence gum/lozenges rely on buccal, not GI, absorption.
This is why patients are told not to swallow the saliva/nicotine.

## Mind Capsule

> Buccal nicotine absorption needs alkaline pH: no acidic drinks/food for about 15 minutes before nicotine gum or lozenge.
