# Flumazenil in Alcohol Withdrawal

**Flumazenil** is a benzodiazepine receptor antagonist used to reverse **benzodiazepine overdose**. In alcohol withdrawal, benzodiazepines are the treatment, and flumazenil could worsen withdrawal and precipitate seizures, so the statement is false.

## Trigger Point

> "Which statement about benzodiazepines and barbiturates is **not true**?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Both act on GABA-A**
Benzodiazepines increase the frequency of chloride channel opening; barbiturates increase the duration.

**B. With alcohol it causes CNS depression**
Additive or synergistic CNS and respiratory depression.

**D. Both can be sedative hypnotics**
Both classes have sedative-hypnotic action, although benzodiazepines are safer.

## Mind Capsule

> Flumazenil reverses benzodiazepines only; it does not treat alcohol withdrawal and may provoke seizures.
