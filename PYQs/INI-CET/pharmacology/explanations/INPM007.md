# Spermidine Increases (Not Decreases) Autophagy

**Spermidine** is a naturally occurring polyamine that **induces/increases autophagy** (partly via inhibition of histone acetyltransferases), which is thought to underlie its proposed anti-ageing, cardioprotective effects. Describing it as **decreasing** autophagy is the incorrect pairing.

## Trigger Point

> **Incorrectly matched pair**: the drug/target combination that has the action reversed.

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Rapamycin - mTOR Inhibitor**
Rapamycin is a well-established, direct inhibitor of the **mTOR** pathway, promoting autophagy and linked to lifespan extension in model organisms.
A correctly matched pair.

**B. Metformin - AMPK Activator**
Metformin activates **AMPK**, which in turn also promotes autophagy and has proposed anti-ageing/metabolic benefits.
A correctly matched pair.

**C. Resveratrol - Sirtuin (SIRT1) Activator**
Resveratrol is classically described as an activator of **SIRT1**, a longevity-associated deacetylase.
A correctly matched pair.

## Mind Capsule

> Autophagy inducers linked to longevity: rapamycin (mTOR inhibition), metformin (AMPK activation), resveratrol (SIRT1), and spermidine (increases, not decreases, autophagy).
