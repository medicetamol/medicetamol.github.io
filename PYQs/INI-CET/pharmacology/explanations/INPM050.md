# Toxicity of Carbamazepine

**Erythromycin** inhibits **CYP3A4**, the enzyme that metabolises carbamazepine. Plasma carbamazepine levels rise, producing toxicity with **ataxia, dizziness, diplopia** and nystagmus.

## Trigger Point

> Carbamazepine plus erythromycin, followed by ataxia and dizziness.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Toxicity of erythromycin**
Erythromycin does not typically cause cerebellar signs; its main adverse effects are GI upset, QT prolongation and cholestasis.

**C. Erythromycin speeds up carbamazepine metabolism**
Erythromycin is an enzyme inhibitor, not an inducer, so the levels would rise instead of fall.

**D. Causes recurrent seizure episodes**
This would follow a fall in carbamazepine levels (e.g. with enzyme inducers), the opposite of this interaction.

## Mind Capsule

> CYP3A4 inhibitor (macrolide, azole) + carbamazepine = carbamazepine toxicity.
