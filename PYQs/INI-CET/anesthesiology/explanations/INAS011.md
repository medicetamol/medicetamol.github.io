# Rapid Redistribution

**Thiopentone** acts for 5–10 minutes because it **redistributes** quickly from the brain to muscle and fat, not because it is cleared quickly. Its metabolism and elimination are slow, so repeated doses accumulate.

## Trigger Point

> Why sodium thiopentone is regarded as an ultrashort-acting drug.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Metabolism**
Hepatic metabolism is slow.

**C. Excretion**
Renal excretion of the unchanged drug is minimal.

**D. Short elimination half-life**
The elimination half-life is long (many hours), so this is incorrect.

## Mind Capsule

> Thiopentone: ultrashort action = redistribution; recovery after a single dose does not depend on metabolism.
