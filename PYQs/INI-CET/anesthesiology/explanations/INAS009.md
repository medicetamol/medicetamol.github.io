# Noradrenaline

In **septic shock** with persistent hypotension after adequate fluids, the first-line vasopressor is **noradrenaline**.

## Trigger Point

> Septic shock, still hypotensive after adequate fluid resuscitation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Adrenaline**
Added or used as second-line.

**C. Dopamine**
An alternative only in selected patients, because of arrhythmia risk.

**D. Hydrocortisone**
Used in refractory shock on vasopressors, not as the next drug.

## Mind Capsule

> Fluid-refractory septic shock = noradrenaline first.
