# Platelet Count Above 10,000

For a **lumbar puncture**, a platelet count of roughly **40,000–50,000/mm³ or more** is generally required, so allowing it at a count just above 10,000 is the incorrect statement.

## Trigger Point

> "Which statement about lumbar puncture is **incorrect**?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. SAH with inconclusive CT**
LP (looking for xanthochromia) is indicated when CT is negative but suspicion remains.

**C. Meningitis is an unusual complication**
Iatrogenic meningitis is rare with aseptic technique.

**D. Post-tap headache more common in females and old age**
Female sex is a recognised risk factor; the age part of the statement is debatable (younger adults are more commonly affected).

## Mind Capsule

> LP needs adequate platelets (about 40,000–50,000 or more) and a normal coagulation profile.
