# Supraspinous Ligament to Interspinous Ligament to Ligamentum Flavum to Dura Mater to Arachnoid Mater

A midline lumbar puncture needle passes through, in order: **skin, subcutaneous tissue, supraspinous ligament, interspinous ligament, ligamentum flavum, epidural space, dura mater, and finally arachnoid mater** before reaching the subarachnoid (CSF) space. Among the listed structures, the correct sequence is **supraspinous ligament -> interspinous ligament -> ligamentum flavum -> dura mater -> arachnoid mater**.

## Correct Order

1. **Supraspinous ligament**
2. **Interspinous ligament**
3. **Ligamentum flavum**
4. **Dura mater**
5. **Arachnoid mater**

## Trigger Point

> **Order of structures pierced during lumbar puncture**: superficial ligaments first, ligamentum flavum next, then the meninges (dura before arachnoid).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Interspinous Ligament -> Supraspinous Ligament -> Ligamentum Flavum -> Dura Mater -> Arachnoid Mater**
Reverses the order of the two superficial ligaments; the **supraspinous** ligament (running along the tips of the spinous processes) is pierced **before** the deeper interspinous ligament.
The needle encounters the supraspinous ligament first, not second.

**C. Supraspinous Ligament -> Ligamentum Flavum -> Interspinous Ligament -> Dura Mater -> Arachnoid Mater**
Places the ligamentum flavum before the interspinous ligament, but anatomically the **interspinous ligament lies superficial to the ligamentum flavum**.
The order of these two middle structures is reversed.

**D. Supraspinous Ligament -> Interspinous Ligament -> Dura Mater -> Ligamentum Flavum -> Arachnoid Mater**
Places the dura mater before the ligamentum flavum, but the ligamentum flavum (and the epidural space) is crossed **before** the needle reaches the dura.
The ligamentum flavum and dura mater are out of sequence.

## Mind Capsule

> LP needle path: skin -> supraspinous -> interspinous -> ligamentum flavum -> epidural space -> dura -> arachnoid -> CSF.
