# Neostigmine

**Neostigmine**, an anticholinesterase, reverses non-depolarising blockers such as rocuronium by increasing acetylcholine at the neuromuscular junction. It is given with **atropine or glycopyrrolate** to block muscarinic effects. The specific reversal agent for rocuronium is **sugammadex**, which is not listed here.

## Trigger Point

> Drug to reverse the effects of rocuronium.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Atropine**
Given alongside neostigmine to prevent bradycardia and secretions, and does not reverse the block.

**B. Baclofen**
A GABA-B agonist muscle relaxant.

**D. Dantrolene**
Treats malignant hyperthermia, not neuromuscular blockade.

## Mind Capsule

> Rocuronium reversal = neostigmine + atropine (or glycopyrrolate), or sugammadex.
