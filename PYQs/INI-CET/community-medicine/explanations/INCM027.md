# Geriatric Population

**RMNCH+A** covers **R**eproductive, **M**aternal, **N**ewborn, **C**hild and **A**dolescent health across the life cycle, with family planning included under reproductive health. Geriatric care is addressed in other programmes (such as the National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly).

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** included under the RMNCH+A strategy?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Family planning**
Included under the reproductive health component.

**B. Maternal and Child Health care**
The core of the strategy.

**D. Adolescent health**
The "+A" in RMNCH+A stands for adolescent health.

## Mind Capsule

> RMNCH+A = Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child, Adolescent; no geriatric component.
