# Truck Drivers

NACO's **high-risk groups (core groups)** are **female sex workers, men who have sex with men, transgender people and injecting drug users**. **Truck drivers** and migrants are classed as **bridge populations**, not as high-risk groups.

## NACO Population Groups

| Category | Groups |
|---|---|
| High-risk groups | FSW, MSM, transgender, IDU |
| Bridge populations | Truckers, migrant workers |
