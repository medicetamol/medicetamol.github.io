# To Detect Warning Signs of an Outbreak

**Syndromic surveillance** tracks symptom clusters (fever, diarrhoea) in real time, before a diagnosis is confirmed. It acts as an **early warning system** so that outbreaks at mass gatherings can be detected and responded to quickly.

## Trigger Point

> Syndromic surveillance using symptoms such as fever and diarrhoea during a mass gathering.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. To provide necessary immediate treatment**
Treatment is the job of the health units; surveillance is for information and detection.

**B. For immediate quarantine of healthy contacts**
Not a purpose of surveillance and not justified for healthy people.

**D. To reduce the need for manpower**
Surveillance teams actually require manpower; reducing it is not its objective.

## Mind Capsule

> Syndromic surveillance = early warning of outbreaks, before laboratory confirmation.
