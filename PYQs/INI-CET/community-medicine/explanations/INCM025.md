# 22%

**Incidence** counts only **new cases** in the population **at risk**. Those already ill (the 10 previous cases) are not at risk of a new episode in this question, so they are removed from the denominator.

## Working

- New cases = 20
- Population at risk = 100 − 10 = 90
- Incidence = 20 / 90 × 100 = 22.2%
- **Incidence ≈ 22%**

## Trigger Point

> 100 people, 10 previously had measles, 20 new cases during the year.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 10%**
Counts the pre-existing cases, which is not incidence.

**B. 20%**
Uses the whole population of 100 as the denominator instead of the population at risk.

**D. 30%**
Adds old and new cases, which gives prevalence.

## Mind Capsule

> Incidence = new cases / population at risk; prevalence = all cases / total population.
