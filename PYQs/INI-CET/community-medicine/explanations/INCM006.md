# HPV Vaccine Does Not Guarantee 100% Protection

HPV vaccines protect against the **specific oncogenic types they contain** (16 and 18 for the bivalent and quadrivalent products, with more types in the nonavalent one). They do not cover every oncogenic type and do not treat existing infection, so **cervical screening is still needed**. No vaccine can be claimed to guarantee complete protection.

## Trigger Point

> **INCORRECT statement**: the one that overstates the vaccine as giving complete, guaranteed protection.

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Primary Target Group 9-14 Years**
Vaccination is aimed at girls before sexual debut, when the immune response is strongest.
This is the standard target group.

**C. Single-Dose Schedule Endorsed**
WHO SAGE endorsed a single-dose schedule (2022) for the 9-14 age group, and this has been adopted in national guidance.
It is a valid statement.

**D. Highly Immunogenic**
Seroconversion approaches 100% in the target age group with very high antibody titres.
Immunogenicity is not the same as guaranteed clinical protection.

## Mind Capsule

> HPV vaccine = type-specific protection, not 100%; screening is still required.
