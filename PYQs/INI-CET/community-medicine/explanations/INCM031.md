# Measures of Location / Position

**Centiles (percentiles) and quartiles** divide ordered data into equal parts and show where a value lies in the distribution. They are called **measures of location (position)** or partition values.

## Trigger Point

> "Centiles and quartiles are considered as..."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Measures of central tendency**
These are mean, median and mode, which give a typical value.

**B. Measures of dispersion**
These are range, variance and SD, which measure spread (though the interquartile range is derived from quartiles).

**D. Measures of correlation**
Describe the relationship between two variables.

## Mind Capsule

> Quartiles, deciles and percentiles = partition values (measures of position).
