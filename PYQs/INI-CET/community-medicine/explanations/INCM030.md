# Mean, Median – Dispersion

The **mean and median** are **measures of central tendency**, not dispersion. Dispersion is described by range, variance, standard deviation and similar measures.

## Trigger Point

> "Which statistical parameter is paired **incorrectly**?"

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Standard error – Variation**
Describes the variation of sample means (sampling variation).

**C. Correlation coefficient – Relationship**
Measures the strength and direction of a relationship between two variables.

**D. Moments – Skewness**
The third moment describes skewness (and the fourth, kurtosis).

## Mind Capsule

> Central tendency = mean, median, mode; dispersion = range, variance, SD.
