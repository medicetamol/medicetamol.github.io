# Accidental Pregnancies per 100 Woman-Years

The **Pearl Index** expresses contraceptive failure as the **number of accidental pregnancies per 100 woman-years (HWY) of exposure**:

**(Total accidental pregnancies x 1200) / total months of exposure**, or x 100 divided by total woman-years.

## Trigger Point

> Index used to express **contraceptive failure rate**: pregnancies per 100 woman-years.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Accidental Pregnancies per Woman-Year**
The denominator is not standardised to 100.
The Pearl Index is expressed per 100 woman-years.

**C. Pregnancies Over Number of Losses per 100 Woman-Years**
Pregnancy losses (miscarriages, abortions) are not part of the definition.
Only accidental (method-failure or user-failure) pregnancies count.

**D. Accidental Pregnancies per 1000 Women per Year**
The standard unit is 100, not 1000.
The scaling makes it incorrect.

## Mind Capsule

> Pearl Index = accidental pregnancies per **100 woman-years** (HWY).
