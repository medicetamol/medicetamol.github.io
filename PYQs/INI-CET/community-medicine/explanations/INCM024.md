# 2, 1, 4, 3

A research study starts with **framing the research question**, from which the **objective** is derived. The **study setting** is then chosen, followed by **selection of the sample** from that setting.

## Correct Order

1. Frame a research question
2. Decide study objective
3. Select study setting
4. Select study sample

## Trigger Point

> Order of steps for conducting a research study.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1, 2, 3, 4**
Places the objective before the research question, but the objective is derived from the question.

**C. 1, 3, 2, 4**
Selects the sample before framing the question, which cannot be done without an objective.

**D. 4, 3, 2, 1**
Reverses the sequence completely.

## Mind Capsule

> Question → objective → setting → sample.
