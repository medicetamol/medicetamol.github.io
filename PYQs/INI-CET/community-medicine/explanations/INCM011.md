# Efficacy = 90%

**Efficacy** is the effect of an intervention under **ideal, controlled conditions** such as a well-conducted randomised controlled trial. **Effectiveness** is the effect in routine field practice, which is usually lower.

## Trigger Point

> **90% in a well-conducted randomised controlled trial**: performance under ideal conditions.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 85%**
Performance in routine community use is **effectiveness**.
It is not the trial value.

**C. 5%**
The difference between trial and field results.
It is not a vaccine performance measure.

**D. 87.5%**
An average of efficacy and effectiveness.
It has no epidemiological meaning.

## Mind Capsule

> Efficacy = ideal (RCT) conditions; effectiveness = everyday field conditions.
