# Paired t-test

The same patients are measured **before and after** an intervention, so the two sets of values are **paired (dependent)**. A **paired t-test** compares the mean difference of quantitative data in such a design.

## Choice of Statistical Test

| Situation | Test |
|---|---|
| Quantitative data, same subjects before and after | Paired t-test |
| Quantitative data, two independent groups | Unpaired t-test |
| Quantitative data, more than two groups | ANOVA |
| Qualitative (categorical) data | Chi-square test |

## Trigger Point

> Body weight measured before and 6 months after a dietary intervention in the same patients.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Unpaired t-test**
Used for two different (independent) groups.

**C. ANOVA**
Used to compare means across three or more groups.

**D. Chi-square test**
Used for categorical data and proportions, not for mean weight.

## Mind Capsule

> Before-after in the same people = paired t-test.
