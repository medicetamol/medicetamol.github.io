# Stratified Random Sampling

In **stratified random sampling**, the population is first divided into homogeneous **strata** (here low, medium and high disease prevalence), and a **random sample is then drawn from each stratum**.

## Trigger Point

> Patients divided into three groups, then random selection within each group.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Simple random sampling**
Every unit has an equal chance, with no grouping first.

**B. Systematic random sampling**
Every *k*th unit is selected after a random start.

**D. Cluster random sampling**
Whole groups (clusters) are randomly chosen, and all or many members of the chosen clusters are studied.

## Mind Capsule

> Divide into strata, then sample within each = stratified; randomly pick whole groups = cluster.
