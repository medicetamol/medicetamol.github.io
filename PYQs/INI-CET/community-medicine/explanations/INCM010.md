# Effectiveness = 85%

**Effectiveness** is the benefit of an intervention under **real-world, routine field conditions**, while **efficacy** is the benefit under ideal, controlled trial conditions. Effectiveness is usually lower than efficacy because of imperfect compliance, cold chain and population variation.

## Trigger Point

> **85% when used in the routine community programme**: performance in the field.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 90%**
Performance in the ideal, randomised trial setting is **efficacy**.
It is not the field value.

**C. 5%**
The gap between the two figures.
It is not a measure of vaccine performance.

**D. 87.5%**
An arithmetic average of the two values.
It has no epidemiological meaning.

## Mind Capsule

> Effectiveness = real world (field); efficacy = ideal trial conditions.
