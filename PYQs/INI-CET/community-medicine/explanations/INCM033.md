# 66%

**Dependency ratio** = (population < 15 years + population ≥ 65 years) / population aged 15–64 years × 100.

## Working

- Dependents = 6000 + 2000 = 8000
- Working-age population = 12000
- Ratio = 8000 / 12000 × 100 = 66.7%
- **Dependency ratio ≈ 66%**

## Trigger Point

> Population distribution by age: < 15 years, 15–64 years and ≥ 65 years.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 33%**
Does not result from the formula using both dependent groups.

**B. 50%**
Gives the young-age dependency ratio only (6000 / 12000).

**D. 75%**
Not derived from these numbers.

## Mind Capsule

> Dependency ratio = (0–14 + 65 and above) / (15–64) × 100.
