# Thumb Spica Cast and Review at 10–14 Days

**Snuffbox tenderness** after a fall on an outstretched hand suggests a **scaphoid fracture** even when the initial X-ray is normal. Treat as a fracture with a **thumb spica cast** and re-image at **10–14 days**.

## Trigger Point

> Laborer with a fall on an outstretched hand and pain in the anatomical snuffbox.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. NSAIDs and discharge**
Risks missing a fracture, leading to avascular necrosis or non-union.

**B. MRI of the wrist**
Useful for early confirmation but not the first step.

**D. CT scan of the wrist**
Used for fracture detail or union assessment, not the first-line step.

## Mind Capsule

> Snuffbox tenderness = presumed scaphoid fracture: spica cast, repeat X-ray at 10–14 days.
