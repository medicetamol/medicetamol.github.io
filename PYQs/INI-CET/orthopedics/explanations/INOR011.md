# CBNAAT

WHO recommends a **rapid molecular test (CBNAAT/Xpert MTB/RIF)** on tissue or aspirate as an initial test for extrapulmonary TB, including spinal TB. It confirms the diagnosis and detects **rifampicin resistance** quickly.

## Trigger Point

> Investigation included in the WHO diagnostic work-up for spinal tuberculosis.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. X-ray**
A supportive imaging study that cannot confirm TB.

**B. CT scan**
Shows bone destruction and abscesses but is not microbiological confirmation.

**D. Culture and sensitivity**
Remains important but is slow, so the rapid molecular test is preferred first.

## Mind Capsule

> Spinal TB: MRI for imaging; CBNAAT on tissue for rapid microbiological confirmation and rifampicin resistance.
