# Atlantoaxial Dislocation

In **rheumatoid arthritis**, synovitis erodes the transverse ligament and odontoid, causing **atlantoaxial subluxation** that can compress the cord, producing neck pain and **myelopathy**.

## Trigger Point

> Rheumatoid arthritis with neck pain and features of myelopathy.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Osteoarthritis of vertebrae**
Causes degenerative spondylosis, not a typical RA complication.

**B. C2–C3 intervertebral stenosis**
Not the characteristic RA lesion.

**D. Disc herniation**
Not specific to RA.

## Mind Capsule

> RA + neck pain + myelopathy = atlantoaxial subluxation (check a flexion lateral X-ray).
