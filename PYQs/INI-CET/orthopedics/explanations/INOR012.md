# Intramedullary Nailing

For **intertrochanteric fractures**, internal fixation is the treatment. **Intramedullary nails** (PFN, gamma nail) are favoured, especially for unstable patterns, while a **dynamic hip screw** can be used for stable ones.

## Trigger Point

> Inter-trochanteric fracture in a 72-year-old woman.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Hemiarthroplasty / DHS**
Hemiarthroplasty is for displaced femoral neck fractures, and DHS is mostly for stable patterns.

**C. ORIF with plating**
A plate is poorly suited to this fracture, with higher failure rates.

**D. Boot and bar**
Used for clubfoot (Denis Browne) and has no role here.

## Mind Capsule

> Intertrochanteric fracture = fixation (nail or DHS); femoral neck fracture in the elderly = arthroplasty.
