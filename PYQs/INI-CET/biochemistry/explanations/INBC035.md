# Skeletal Muscle

Skeletal muscle (and adipose tissue) uses **GLUT4**, which moves to the cell surface only in response to **insulin**. In insulin deficiency or resistance, muscle glucose uptake falls. RBCs and brain use insulin-independent transporters.

## Glucose Transporters

| Transporter | Site | Insulin-dependent? |
|---|---|---|
| GLUT1 | RBC, brain (BBB) | No |
| GLUT2 | Liver, pancreatic β cell, kidney, intestine | No |
| GLUT3 | Neurons | No |
| GLUT4 | Skeletal muscle, adipose tissue, heart | **Yes** |

## Trigger Point

> Tissue that cannot take up glucose in insulin resistance, absence of insulin or diabetes.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Red blood cells**
Use GLUT1, which is insulin-independent.

**C. Brain**
Uses GLUT1 and GLUT3, which are insulin-independent, so glucose supply is maintained.

**D. Kidney**
Uses GLUT2 and SGLT, which are not insulin-dependent.

## Mind Capsule

> Insulin-dependent glucose uptake = GLUT4 (skeletal muscle, adipose tissue).
