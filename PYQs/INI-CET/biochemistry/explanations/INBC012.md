# Peroxisomal Biogenesis Defect

A neonate with **craniofacial abnormalities, severe hypotonia and seizures**, together with **elevated very long-chain fatty acids (VLCFA)**, points to a **peroxisomal biogenesis disorder** such as **Zellweger syndrome**, caused by mutations in PEX genes that impair peroxisome assembly, so VLCFAs and other peroxisomal substrates cannot be metabolised.

## Trigger Point

> **Craniofacial dysmorphism + hypotonia + seizures + elevated VLCFA** in a neonate: the classic Zellweger/peroxisomal biogenesis picture.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Mitochondrial Inheritance**
Mitochondrial disorders typically affect high-energy-demand tissues (muscle, brain, heart) with lactic acidosis, not specifically raised VLCFA.
VLCFA accumulation is a peroxisomal, not mitochondrial, signature.

**B. Lysosomal Storage Disorder**
These typically present with organomegaly, coarse facial features and accumulation of specific storage substrates (glycosaminoglycans, sphingolipids), not primarily elevated VLCFA.
The biochemical marker described is peroxisomal, not lysosomal.

**D. Simultaneous Activation of Multiple Tumour Suppressor Genes**
Not a mechanism of neonatal disease; tumour suppressor activation, if anything, restrains abnormal cell growth rather than causing a metabolic/developmental syndrome.
Does not fit this clinical and biochemical picture at all.

## Mind Capsule

> Neonatal hypotonia + seizures + dysmorphism + raised VLCFA = peroxisomal biogenesis defect (Zellweger spectrum).
