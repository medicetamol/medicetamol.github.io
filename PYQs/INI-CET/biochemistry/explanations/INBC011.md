# Uncharged Polar Amino Acid-Cysteine, Basic Amino Acid-Lysine

**Cysteine** is classified as an **uncharged polar** amino acid (its thiol group is polar but not ionised at physiological pH), while **lysine** is a **basic** amino acid, carrying a positively charged side-chain amino group at physiological pH. **Histidine** is itself a basic amino acid (not nonpolar), and **proline** is a nonpolar, cyclic (imino) amino acid (not basic).

## Trigger Point

> Identify which amino acid-classification pairings are correctly matched: cysteine (uncharged polar) and lysine (basic).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Statements 1 and 2**
Statement 2 wrongly classifies histidine as nonpolar; histidine is in fact a **basic** amino acid.
Only statement 1 in this pair is correct.

**B. Statements 3 and 4**
Statement 3 wrongly classifies proline as basic; proline is a **nonpolar**, cyclic amino acid.
Only statement 4 in this pair is correct.

**C. Statements 2 and 3**
Both statements here are incorrect: histidine is basic (not nonpolar), and proline is nonpolar (not basic).
Neither pairing in this option is accurate.

## Mind Capsule

> Cysteine = uncharged polar; histidine and lysine = basic; proline = nonpolar (cyclic/imino).
