# Pyrimidine Dimers

**UV-B/UV-C** light makes adjacent **pyrimidines on the same strand (mainly thymine-thymine) form covalent cyclobutane dimers**. The dimers distort the helix and block replication and transcription. They are repaired by **nucleotide excision repair**, which is defective in **xeroderma pigmentosum**.

## Trigger Point

> **UV radiation** causing DNA damage: the classic mechanism is thymine (pyrimidine) dimer formation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Inhibition of p53**
p53 is a tumour suppressor activated by DNA damage; UV can produce p53 mutations later, but it does not directly damage DNA by inhibiting p53.
This is a consequence, not the primary mechanism.

**C. Deamination of Cytosine**
Cytosine converts to uracil by spontaneous or nitrous-acid-induced deamination and is repaired by base excision repair.
It is not the characteristic UV lesion.

**D. Increase in Cyclins**
Cyclin overexpression drives cell-cycle progression and oncogenesis.
It is a downstream cell-cycle change, not a mechanism of direct DNA damage.

## Mind Capsule

> UV = thymine dimers = nucleotide excision repair = xeroderma pigmentosum.
