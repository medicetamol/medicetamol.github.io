# A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Blotting techniques are named by direction: **Southern** detects **DNA**, **Northern** detects **RNA**, **Western** detects **protein**. **Eastern** blotting is used for lipids, carbohydrates and post-translational modifications.

## Correct Matches

| Blot | Detects |
|---|---|
| Southern | DNA |
| Northern | RNA |
| Western | Protein |
| Eastern | Lipids |

## Trigger Point

> Match Southern, Northern, Western and Eastern blots with DNA, RNA, protein and lipids.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4**
Swaps Southern and Northern, and mismatches Western and Eastern.

**C. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3**
Southern is paired with protein, which is the Western blot target.

**D. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1**
Southern is paired with lipids and Northern with protein; every pairing is wrong.

## Mind Capsule

> Southern = DNA; Northern = RNA; Western = Protein; Eastern = lipids / post-translational modifications.

## Memory Hook

> **SNoW DRoP**: **S**outhern-**D**NA, **N**orthern-**R**NA, **W**estern-**P**rotein.
