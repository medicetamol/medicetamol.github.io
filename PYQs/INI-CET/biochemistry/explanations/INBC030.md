# RFLP

**RFLP (restriction fragment length polymorphism)** compares the pattern of DNA fragments produced by restriction enzymes. For *M. tuberculosis*, **IS6110 RFLP** is the classic DNA-fingerprinting method used to show whether two isolates are the same or different strains.

## Trigger Point

> Confirm that specimens from two patients come from different *M. tuberculosis* strains.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Mutation analysis**
Looks for specific known mutations (e.g. drug resistance genes) and is not used for strain identity.

**C. Pyrosequencing**
Sequences short DNA stretches in real time; used mainly for detecting resistance mutations.

**D. RAPD**
Random amplified polymorphic DNA can type strains but has poor reproducibility, so RFLP is preferred as the standard fingerprinting tool.

## Mind Capsule

> Strain typing / DNA fingerprinting of TB = RFLP (IS6110); newer methods are MIRU-VNTR and whole-genome sequencing.
