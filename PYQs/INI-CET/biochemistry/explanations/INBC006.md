# CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing System

**CRISPR-Cas9** is a bacterial-adaptive-immunity-derived gene-editing tool: a **guide RNA** directs the **Cas9 endonuclease** to a complementary DNA sequence (adjacent to a PAM site), where Cas9 creates a **double-strand break**, enabling targeted gene knockout, correction, or insertion via cellular repair pathways.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Microarray Analysis**
Uses a chip of immobilised probes to detect **expression levels** of thousands of genes simultaneously by hybridisation.
It measures expression; it does not cut or edit DNA.

**B. In Situ Hybridisation**
Uses a labelled probe to localise a specific nucleic acid sequence **within tissue or cells**, without cutting DNA.
A detection technique, not an editing one.

**D. DNA Sequencing**
Determines the **nucleotide order** of a DNA sample (as in Sanger or next-generation sequencing).
It reads sequence; it does not introduce a targeted cut.

## Mind Capsule

> Guide RNA + Cas9 nuclease + targeted double-strand break = CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing.
