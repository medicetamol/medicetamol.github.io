# Dependence on a Single Oncogenic Pathway

**Oncogene addiction** means a tumour, despite many mutations, **depends on one activated oncogene or pathway for survival and growth**. Blocking that single driver causes the cancer cells to die, which is the basis of **targeted therapy**.

## Oncogene Addiction Examples

| Oncogene | Cancer | Targeted drug |
|---|---|---|
| BCR-ABL | CML | Imatinib |
| HER2/neu | Breast cancer | Trastuzumab |
| BRAF V600E | Melanoma | Vemurafenib |
| EGFR | Lung adenocarcinoma | Erlotinib, gefitinib |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Resistance to all chemotherapy**
Describes multidrug resistance, not dependence on one pathway.

**C. Addiction to carcinogenic substances**
A literal misreading; the term is about cell biology, not substance use.

**D. Activation of multiple tumour suppressor genes**
Tumour suppressors are **inactivated** in cancer, not activated.

## Mind Capsule

> Oncogene addiction = **dependence on one driver oncogene**, so a single targeted drug can work.
