# Metabolic Acidosis-Glycol Poisoning, Metabolic Alkalosis-Pyloric Stenosis, Respiratory Acidosis-Bronchopneumonia, Respiratory Alkalosis-Hyperventilation

Each acid-base disorder has a classic clinical trigger: **glycol (ethylene glycol) poisoning** produces a high-anion-gap **metabolic acidosis**; **pyloric stenosis** causes loss of gastric HCl through vomiting, producing **metabolic (hypochloraemic) alkalosis**; **bronchopneumonia** impairs alveolar ventilation, causing CO2 retention and **respiratory acidosis**; and **hyperventilation** blows off CO2, causing **respiratory alkalosis**.

## Correct Matches

| Acid-base disorder | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Metabolic acidosis** | **Glycol poisoning** |
| **Metabolic alkalosis** | **Pyloric stenosis** |
| **Respiratory acidosis** | **Bronchopneumonia** |
| **Respiratory alkalosis** | **Hyperventilation** |

## Trigger Point

> Match each acid-base disorder to its classic clinical cause.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Respiratory Acidosis-Hyperventilation, Respiratory Alkalosis-Bronchopneumonia**
Swaps the two respiratory disorders; hyperventilation **removes** CO2 (alkalosis), while bronchopneumonia **retains** CO2 (acidosis).
The respiratory pairings are reversed.

**B. Respiratory Acidosis-Hyperventilation**
Also swaps hyperventilation into the acidosis slot, when it in fact produces alkalosis by CO2 loss.
Inconsistent with the direction of CO2 change in hyperventilation.

**C. Metabolic Acidosis-Hyperventilation, Metabolic Alkalosis-Glycol Poisoning**
Misassigns both metabolic causes; hyperventilation is a respiratory (not metabolic) cause, and glycol poisoning causes acidosis, not alkalosis.
Multiple pairings are incorrect here.

## Mind Capsule

> Vomiting/pyloric stenosis = metabolic alkalosis; toxic alcohols/glycols = metabolic acidosis; CO2 retention (pneumonia) = respiratory acidosis; CO2 loss (hyperventilation) = respiratory alkalosis.
