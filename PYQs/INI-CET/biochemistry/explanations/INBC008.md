# Linoleic Acid-Most Essential Fatty Acid, Protein Scoring System-Amino Acid Score, Safflower Oil-Rich in EFA, NPU-Protein Quality and Digestibility

**Linoleic acid** is considered the most essential fatty acid (the body cannot synthesise it, and it is the precursor for arachidonic acid). The **amino acid (AA/chemical) score** is a **protein scoring system** comparing a protein's essential amino acid content to a reference pattern. **Safflower oil** is among the richest dietary sources of essential fatty acids (linoleic acid). **Net Protein Utilisation (NPU)** reflects both a protein's **amino acid quality and its digestibility**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. AA Score-Protein Quality and Digestibility, NPU-Protein Scoring System**
Swaps the definitions of AA score and NPU; the AA score is a scoring system based on essential amino acid pattern, while NPU specifically incorporates digestibility.
The two definitions are exchanged.

**B. Most Essential Fatty Acid-Protein Scoring System, Oil Rich in EFA-Linoleic Acid**
Confuses a fatty acid concept with a protein scoring concept, and mismatches the oil with the wrong term.
The category types themselves don't align.

**D. Most Essential Fatty Acid-Safflower Oil, NPU-Linoleic Acid**
Confuses an oil (a source) with the fatty acid itself, and misapplies linoleic acid to the NPU definition.
Category types are mismatched here as well.
