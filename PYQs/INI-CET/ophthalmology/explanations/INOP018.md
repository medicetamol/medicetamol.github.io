# Trigeminal Nerve

The **ophthalmic division (V1) of the trigeminal nerve** supplies sensation to the orbit and its contents through the **lacrimal, frontal and nasociliary nerves**.

## Trigger Point

> "Which nerve gives sensory supply to the orbit?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Vagus nerve**
Supplies the pharynx, larynx and viscera, not the orbit.

**C. Hypoglossal nerve**
A pure motor nerve to the tongue.

**D. Oculomotor nerve**
Mainly motor to the extraocular muscles, with parasympathetic fibres to the pupil.

## Mind Capsule

> Sensation to the orbit = V1 (lacrimal, frontal, nasociliary).
