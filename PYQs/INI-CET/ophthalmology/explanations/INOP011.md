# Tractional Retinal Detachment

In **proliferative diabetic retinopathy**, new vessels (neovascularisation) grow on the retina with **fibrous proliferation**. Contraction of this fibrovascular tissue pulls on the retina, causing **tractional retinal detachment**.

## Trigger Point

> Characteristic finding of proliferative diabetic retinopathy.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Rhegmatogenous retinal detachment**
Caused by a retinal break, as in myopia, lattice degeneration and trauma.

**C. Exudative retinal detachment**
Caused by fluid leakage under the retina (Coats disease, VKH, tumours).

**D. No retinal detachment**
Proliferative disease characteristically leads to traction detachment, so this does not fit.

## Mind Capsule

> PDR = neovascularisation + fibrosis = tractional detachment and vitreous haemorrhage.
