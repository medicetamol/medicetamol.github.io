# McCarey-Kaufman, Nylon

**McCarey-Kaufman (M-K) medium** is an intermediate-term corneal storage medium kept at 4°C. A corneal graft is sutured with **10-0 monofilament nylon**, which is non-absorbable and inert.

## Corneal Preservation Methods

| Method | Storage duration |
|---|---|
| Moist chamber | 24–48 hours |
| McCarey-Kaufman medium | Up to 4 days |
| Optisol-GS | Up to 14 days |
| Organ culture | Up to about 4 weeks |

## Trigger Point

> Donor corneal graft preservative and suture material for corneal transplant.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Polyethylene glycol, Nylon**
Polyethylene glycol is not a corneal storage medium.

**C. Ethanol, Silk**
Ethanol would kill the endothelial cells, and silk is not used for corneal grafts.

**D. Moist chamber, Vicryl**
Moist chamber storage is only short term, and absorbable Vicryl is not used.

## Mind Capsule

> Cornea storage: M-K medium (4 days), Optisol (14 days); suture = 10-0 nylon.
