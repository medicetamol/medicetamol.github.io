# Strabismus Surgery

**Amblyopia** is treated by correcting the cause and forcing use of the weaker eye: **refractive correction, occlusion (patching)** and **penalisation or binocular/video game therapy**. Strabismus surgery realigns the eyes (cosmetic and binocular function) but does not treat amblyopia itself.

## Trigger Point

> "All of the following are done in the treatment of amblyopia, **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Video game therapy**
A newer binocular therapy used to improve vision in the amblyopic eye.

**C. Patching**
Standard therapy that occludes the good eye to force use of the amblyopic eye.

**D. Refractive error correction**
The first step in management, since uncorrected refractive error is a common cause.

## Mind Capsule

> Amblyopia treatment = glasses + patching (or atropine penalisation); surgery only aligns the eyes.
