# OCT

**Optical coherence tomography (OCT)** gives high-resolution cross-sectional images of the retina using light interferometry, showing each **retinal layer** and the vitreoretinal interface.

## Trigger Point

> "Which investigation permits visualization of all the retinal layers?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. B-scan ultrasonography**
Used when the media are opaque to show retinal detachment or masses, but its resolution is too low to show individual layers.

**C. Perimetry**
Tests the visual field and not retinal structure.

**D. Fundus fluorescein angiography**
Shows retinal and choroidal vessels and leakage, not the layers.

## Mind Capsule

> Retinal layers = OCT; retinal vessels = FFA; opaque media = B-scan.
