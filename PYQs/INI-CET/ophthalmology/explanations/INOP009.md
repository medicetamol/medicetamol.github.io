# Coxsackie A and Enterovirus 70

**Acute haemorrhagic conjunctivitis** is caused mainly by **enterovirus 70** and **Coxsackie virus A24**. It presents with sudden painful conjunctivitis, subconjunctival haemorrhages and rapid spread in epidemics.

## Trigger Point

> Organisms causing acute haemorrhagic conjunctivitis.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. c and d**
Coxsackie B is not a cause of this conjunctivitis.

**C. b, c and d**
Includes Coxsackie B, which is wrong.

**D. b and c**
Leaves out enterovirus 70, the most important cause, and includes Coxsackie B.

## Mind Capsule

> Acute haemorrhagic conjunctivitis = enterovirus 70 + Coxsackie A24.
