# Excimer Laser

The **excimer (ArF, 193 nm) laser** performs **photoablation** of corneal tissue, which reshapes the cornea in **LASIK and PRK** to correct refractive errors.

## Ophthalmic Lasers

| Laser | Main use |
|---|---|
| Nd:YAG | Posterior capsulotomy, iridotomy |
| Argon | Retinal photocoagulation, trabeculoplasty |
| Diode | Retinal photocoagulation, cyclophotocoagulation |
| Excimer | Refractive surgery (LASIK, PRK) |

## Trigger Point

> Laser used in refractive eye surgery.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Nd:YAG**
A photodisruptor used for posterior capsule opacification and peripheral iridotomy.

**B. Argon**
A photocoagulator used on the retina and trabecular meshwork.

**D. Diode**
Used for retinal photocoagulation and cyclophotocoagulation in glaucoma.

## Mind Capsule

> Refractive surgery = excimer laser (photoablation).
