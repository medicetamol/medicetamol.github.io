# Sacral Insufficiency Fracture

The **Honda (H) sign** is the H-shaped pattern formed by **bilateral vertical sacral ala fractures** joined by a horizontal fracture line through S1–S2, seen on STIR MRI or bone scan in **sacral insufficiency fractures** (e.g. osteoporosis).

## Trigger Point

> Honda or H sign on STIR MRI.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Multiple myeloma**
Gives lytic lesions and diffuse marrow signal change.

**C. Acute osteomyelitis**
Shows marrow oedema with abscess or periosteal change, not an H pattern.

**D. Bone marrow edema**
A non-specific finding and not the named sign.

## Mind Capsule

> H or Honda sign = sacral insufficiency fracture.
