# Neurocysticercosis

**Neurocysticercosis** is the commonest cause of seizures in children in endemic regions. CT or MRI shows a **cystic lesion with an eccentric dot (scolex)**, the **"dot-in-a-hole"** sign.

## Trigger Point

> Child with seizures and a cystic lesion with a dot sign on contrast CT.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Tuberculoma**
Shows a "target sign" (central calcification with ring enhancement).

**C. Brain abscess**
Ring-enhancing lesion with restricted diffusion and surrounding oedema, with no scolex.

**D. Cerebral metastasis**
Seen in older adults with a known primary, without a scolex.

## Mind Capsule

> Cyst with an eccentric scolex dot = neurocysticercosis.
