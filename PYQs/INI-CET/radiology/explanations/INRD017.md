# Sestamibi Scan

**Tc-99m sestamibi** is taken up by hyperfunctioning parathyroid tissue and retained longer than in the thyroid, which localises **parathyroid adenomas**, including ectopic ones.

## Trigger Point

> Most appropriate investigation for locating a parathyroid adenoma.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. USG**
Useful and often combined, but operator-dependent and poor for ectopic glands.

**C. FDG PET**
Not routine for parathyroid localisation.

**D. SPECT**
Adds 3D localisation to sestamibi (SPECT/CT), rather than being a stand-alone choice.

## Mind Capsule

> Parathyroid adenoma localisation = sestamibi scan (with USG).
