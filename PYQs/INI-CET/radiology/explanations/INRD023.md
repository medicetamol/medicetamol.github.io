# MRI

**MRI** is the investigation of choice for **spinal TB**: it shows early marrow oedema, disc involvement, paravertebral or epidural abscess and cord compression.

## Trigger Point

> Investigation of choice for spinal TB.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Ultrasound**
Limited role; may detect a paravertebral or psoas abscess.

**C. CT scan**
Shows bone destruction in detail, but soft tissue and cord are shown less well.

**D. CBNAAT**
A microbiological test on tissue, not an imaging investigation.

## Mind Capsule

> Spinal TB imaging = MRI; microbiological confirmation = CBNAAT or culture on tissue.
