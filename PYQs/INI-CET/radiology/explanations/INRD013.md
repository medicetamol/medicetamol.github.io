# Osteosarcoma

**Osteosarcoma** in adolescents commonly affects the metaphysis of long bones around the knee, and X-ray shows the **sunburst periosteal reaction** and **Codman triangle**.

## Trigger Point

> 17-year-old with leg pain, swelling, sunburst pattern and Codman triangle.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ewing sarcoma**
Diaphyseal lesion with onion-skin periosteal reaction.

**C. Giant cell tumour**
Eccentric lytic epiphyseal lesion in skeletally mature patients.

**D. Chondroblastoma**
Epiphyseal lesion in adolescents, with calcification.

## Mind Capsule

> Sunburst + Codman triangle in a teenager's metaphysis = osteosarcoma.
