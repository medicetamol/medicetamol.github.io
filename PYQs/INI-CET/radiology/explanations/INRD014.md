# CT Scan

**CT** involves **ionising radiation** to the fetus, so it is avoided in pregnancy unless essential. **MRI, ultrasound and Doppler** do not use ionising radiation and are preferred.

## Trigger Point

> Investigation contraindicated (to be avoided) in pregnancy.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. MRI**
No ionising radiation; used when necessary.

**C. USG**
The preferred, safe modality in pregnancy.

**D. Doppler**
Ultrasound-based and safe.

## Mind Capsule

> In pregnancy: USG and MRI are safe; avoid ionising radiation (CT) unless essential.
