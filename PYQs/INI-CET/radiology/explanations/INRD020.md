# Pantothenate Kinase-Associated Degeneration

**PKAN** (NBIA type 1) shows **T2 hypointensity of the globus pallidus with a central hyperintense area**, the **"eye of the tiger"** sign.

## MRI Signs in Neurology

| Sign | Disease |
|---|---|
| Eye of the tiger | PKAN |
| Face of the giant panda | Wilson disease |
| Hot cross bun | Multiple system atrophy |
| Hummingbird | Progressive supranuclear palsy |

## Trigger Point

> "Eye of the tiger" appearance on MRI.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Wilson disease**
Shows the "face of the giant panda" sign in the midbrain.

**C. Krabbe disease**
A leukodystrophy with white matter changes.

**D. Huntington chorea**
Caudate atrophy with "boxcar" ventricles.

## Mind Capsule

> Eye of the tiger = PKAN; giant panda = Wilson.
