# X-ray Defecogram

**Defecography** (evacuation proctography) uses barium paste in the rectum with lateral X-rays during evacuation. It dynamically evaluates **rectocele, intussusception, enterocele** and anorectal angle changes.

## Trigger Point

> X-ray image from a rectal evacuation study.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Barium swallow**
Studies the oesophagus and swallowing.

**C. MR defecogram**
An MRI-based technique, not a plain X-ray image.

**D. X-ray barium enema**
Outlines the colon by retrograde contrast, not evacuation.

## Mind Capsule

> Barium paste in the rectum and films during evacuation = X-ray defecogram.
