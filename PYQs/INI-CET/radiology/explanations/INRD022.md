# Follicular Carcinoma

**Follicular carcinoma** spreads by the **blood stream**, with metastases to **bone (often lytic, punched-out)** and lung. Multiple punched-out lytic skull lesions in a thyroid cancer patient fit it best.

## Trigger Point

> Skull X-ray with multiple punched-out lytic lesions in a thyroid cancer patient.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Papillary carcinoma**
Spreads mainly through lymphatics to cervical nodes.

**B. Hurthle cell carcinoma**
An oncocytic variant of follicular carcinoma, considered separately here.

**D. Thyroid lymphoma**
Presents with a rapidly enlarging goitre, not lytic skull metastases.

## Mind Capsule

> Follicular = haematogenous spread (bone, lung); papillary = lymphatic spread.
