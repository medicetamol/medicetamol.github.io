# Non-contrast CT KUB

**Non-contrast CT KUB** is the **most sensitive and specific** investigation for ureteric stones, showing size, position and hydronephrosis.

## Trigger Point

> Colicky pain with a ureteric stone; the best investigation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ultrasonography KUB**
Less sensitive for ureteric stones, though useful in pregnancy and children.

**C. Contrast-enhanced CT KUB**
Contrast can obscure stones and is not needed.

**D. Ureteroscopy**
An interventional procedure, not the diagnostic investigation of choice.

## Mind Capsule

> Suspected ureteric colic = non-contrast CT KUB.
