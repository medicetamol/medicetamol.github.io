# Lithium, Valproate and Haloperidol

**Acute mania** is treated with mood stabilisers (**lithium, valproate**) and antipsychotics (**haloperidol**, olanzapine). **Amitriptyline**, an antidepressant, can precipitate mania.

## Trigger Point

> Medications used to treat acute mania.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Only 1**
Valproate and haloperidol are also used.

**C. 1, 2 & 4**
Includes amitriptyline, which is not used for mania.

**D. 2 & 4**
Includes amitriptyline and omits lithium and haloperidol.

## Mind Capsule

> Mania = mood stabiliser + antipsychotic; antidepressants risk a switch into mania.
