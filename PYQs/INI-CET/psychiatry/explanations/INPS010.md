# Flumazenil in Alcohol Dependence

**Flumazenil** is a benzodiazepine antagonist and is not used for long-term alcohol dependence. Treatment uses benzodiazepines for withdrawal, and naltrexone, acamprosate or disulfiram for relapse prevention.

## Trigger Point

> "All of the following statements are correct **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Opioid withdrawal is rarely fatal**
It is very distressing but seldom fatal, unlike alcohol or benzodiazepine withdrawal.

**B. Buprenorphine for opioid withdrawal**
A partial agonist used for detoxification and maintenance.

**D. Cannabis withdrawal has minimal physical symptoms**
Mainly irritability, sleep disturbance and anxiety.

## Mind Capsule

> Flumazenil reverses benzodiazepines; it does not treat alcohol dependence.
