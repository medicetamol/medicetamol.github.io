# Autistic Thinking

In **autistic (paralogical) thinking**, the patient reasons by private, illogical rules. Here the patient concludes identity from a shared attribute (celibacy), the **Von Domarus principle**.

## Trigger Point

> "Lord Hanuman was celibate, I am celibate, therefore I am Lord Hanuman."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Verbigeration**
Stereotyped repetition of words or phrases without meaning.

**C. Neologism**
Coining of a new word that has meaning only for the patient.

**D. Loosening of association**
Shifts between unrelated ideas without connecting links, rather than a false chain of reasoning.

## Mind Capsule

> Identity concluded from a shared attribute (Von Domarus principle) = illogical (autistic) thinking.
