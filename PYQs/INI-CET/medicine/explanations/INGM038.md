# Arthralgia and Myalgia

**Musculoskeletal symptoms**, joint pain and myalgia, are the **most common clinical feature of SLE**, occurring in the large majority of patients. Skin, renal and haematological features are common but less frequent.

## Trigger Point

> "Which clinical feature is most commonly seen in SLE?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Photosensitivity**
Common, but seen in fewer patients than joint symptoms.

**B. Nephrotic syndrome**
A manifestation of lupus nephritis, seen in only a subset of patients.

**D. Anemia and thrombocytopenia**
Haematological abnormalities are common but less so than arthralgia.

## Mind Capsule

> SLE commonest feature = arthralgia/arthritis (non-erosive).
