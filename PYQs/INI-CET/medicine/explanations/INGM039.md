# Pulsus Alternans

In **pulsus alternans**, strong and weak beats alternate while the rhythm remains regular, seen in **severe LV failure**. Alternating end-diastolic volumes lead to alternating stroke volumes through the **Frank-Starling mechanism**.

## Trigger Point

> "Which pulse type is explained by the Frank-Starling law?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Pulsus bisferiens**
Double-peaked pulse seen in aortic regurgitation with stenosis or HOCM.

**B. Pulsus paradoxus**
Exaggerated fall in systolic BP during inspiration (cardiac tamponade, severe asthma), related to intrathoracic pressure and ventricular interdependence.

**D. Pulsus parvus**
Small-volume, weak pulse from low stroke volume (e.g. aortic stenosis).

## Mind Capsule

> Alternating strong and weak beats with regular rhythm = pulsus alternans (LV failure).
