# P Wave

The murmur of **mitral stenosis** has a **presystolic accentuation** caused by atrial contraction, which follows the **P wave**. In atrial fibrillation, the P wave is lost and so is the presystolic accentuation.

## Trigger Point

> "The murmur of mitral stenosis corresponds to which ECG wave?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. QRS complex**
Ventricular depolarisation, corresponding to S1 and the onset of systole, not the diastolic murmur.

**B. T wave**
Ventricular repolarisation at the end of systole, unrelated to the mitral stenosis murmur.

**D. ST segment**
Occurs during systole, whereas the mitral stenosis murmur is diastolic.

## Mind Capsule

> Presystolic accentuation of MS = atrial systole (P wave); lost in atrial fibrillation.
