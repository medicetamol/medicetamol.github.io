# Sterile Pyuria

**Sterile pyuria** is pus cells in the urine (here > 50/HPF) with a **negative routine culture**. Causes include genitourinary **tuberculosis**, chlamydial or other atypical infection, and recent antibiotic use. A normal prostate examination makes prostatitis unlikely.

## Trigger Point

> Burning urination, normal prostate examination, > 50 pus cells/HPF, sterile culture.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Chronic bacterial prostatitis**
Gives recurrent UTIs with the same organism on culture and an abnormal (boggy or tender) prostate.

**B. Acute bacterial prostatitis**
Fever, perineal pain and a very tender, boggy prostate, with a positive culture.

**C. Granulomatous prostatitis**
Typically gives a hard, nodular prostate that may mimic cancer.

## Mind Capsule

> Pus cells + negative routine culture = sterile pyuria (think TB, chlamydia, prior antibiotics).
