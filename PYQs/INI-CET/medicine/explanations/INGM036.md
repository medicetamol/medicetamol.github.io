# Air Trapping with Hyperinflation

An **FEV₁/FVC ratio below 70%** indicates **obstructive lung disease** (COPD in a chronic smoker). A markedly raised **RV/TLC** shows **air trapping and hyperinflation**.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic smoker with FEV₁/FVC of 65% and a raised RV/TLC ratio.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Decreased lung compliance**
A feature of restrictive disease such as fibrosis, with a normal or high FEV₁/FVC ratio.

**C. Restrictive lung disease**
Gives reduced volumes (TLC) and a preserved or raised FEV₁/FVC, the opposite of this picture.

**D. Increased lung compliance**
Seen in emphysema from loss of elastic recoil, but it does not directly explain the raised RV/TLC; air trapping does.

## Mind Capsule

> Low FEV₁/FVC + raised RV/TLC = obstruction with air trapping and hyperinflation.
