# Sensory Disturbances

**Sensory symptoms** (paraesthesia, numbness) are the most common early presentation of **multiple sclerosis**, along with optic neuritis and motor weakness. Ataxia, vertigo and facial palsy occur less often at onset.

## Trigger Point

> "Early presenting symptom in multiple sclerosis."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ataxia**
Usually appears later, with cerebellar involvement.

**C. Vertigo**
Can occur from brainstem lesions but is less common at onset.

**D. Facial palsy**
Uncommon as a presenting feature.

## Mind Capsule

> MS onset: sensory symptoms and optic neuritis are the most common.
