# High-Frequency Chest Wall Oscillation Therapy

In Parkinson's disease with chronic aspiration, secretions pool and cause recurrent infections and **bronchiectasis**. Long-term care focuses on **airway clearance**, for which **high-frequency chest wall oscillation** is the keyed option.

## Trigger Point

> Parkinson's disease with recurrent aspiration, excessive secretions and bilateral bronchiectasis on imaging.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Nebulized N-acetylcysteine**
Mucolytic with no clear proven benefit in this setting.

**C. PEG tube placement**
Does not prevent aspiration of oral secretions and does not clearly reduce pneumonia, so it is not the best option here.

**D. Macrolide therapy**
Long-term macrolides are for frequent exacerbations in bronchiectasis, not the key management step here.

## Mind Capsule

> Aspiration-related bronchiectasis = airway clearance techniques first.
