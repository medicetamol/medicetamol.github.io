# Bacterial Meningitis

Fever, severe headache, vomiting, photophobia and **neck stiffness with positive Brudzinski's sign** indicate **meningeal irritation**, most likely from acute **bacterial meningitis**. **Hydrocephalus** is a recognised complication from impaired CSF absorption.

## Trigger Point

> Fever, severe headache, photophobia, neck stiffness and Brudzinski's sign, with hydrocephalus on CT.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Normal pressure hydrocephalus**
Chronic triad of gait disturbance, dementia and urinary incontinence, without fever or meningism.

**C. Viral encephalitis**
Mainly causes altered sensorium, behavioural change and seizures; meningism is not the dominant feature.

**D. Subarachnoid haemorrhage**
Sudden thunderclap headache with blood on CT, not fever with a hydrocephalus picture.

## Mind Capsule

> Fever + headache + neck stiffness + Brudzinski sign = meningitis; hydrocephalus may complicate bacterial meningitis.
