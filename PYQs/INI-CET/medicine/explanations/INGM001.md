# Wilson Disease

**Wilson disease** is an autosomal recessive disorder of **copper metabolism** (ATP7B mutation) causing copper accumulation in the liver, brain (basal ganglia) and cornea (Kayser-Fleischer rings). Among neurodegenerative disorders, it stands out as **treatable/potentially curable** with **copper chelation** (D-penicillamine, trientine) and zinc.

## Trigger Point

> Neurodegenerative disorder with a **distinct, potentially curable treatment**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. PKAN**
An iron-accumulation neurodegenerative disorder (NBIA group) with progressive dystonia.
No curative treatment exists; management is supportive.

**C. PML**
Caused by JC virus reactivation in immunosuppression; treatment is improving immune status (for example, stopping the causative drug).
It is not classically grouped as a "curable neurodegenerative disorder" in this context.

**D. PLA2G6-Associated Neurodegeneration**
Another NBIA-group disorder with iron accumulation and progressive neurodegeneration.
No specific curative therapy exists.

## Mind Capsule

> Neurodegeneration + treatable/curable = think Wilson disease (copper chelation).
