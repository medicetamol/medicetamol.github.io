# Loop Diuretics and SGLT2 Inhibitors

This is **heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF)**: EF 60%, concentric LVH, left atrial enlargement and diastolic dysfunction in a hypertensive patient. **Loop diuretics** relieve congestion (breathlessness, oedema), and **SGLT2 inhibitors** reduce HF hospitalisation in HFpEF.

## Trigger Point

> Dyspnoea and pedal oedema with EF 60%, concentric LVH, LA enlargement and grade 2 diastolic dysfunction.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Coronary angiography**
Not the next step for a picture explained by hypertensive diastolic dysfunction, with no ischaemic features given.

**C. Digoxin**
Increases contractility, which is not the problem in HFpEF, and gives no benefit here.

**D. Ivabradine**
Slows the heart rate and has no proven benefit in HFpEF; shorter diastole is also not helpful.

## Mind Capsule

> HFpEF = diuretics for congestion + SGLT2 inhibitor + control of hypertension.
