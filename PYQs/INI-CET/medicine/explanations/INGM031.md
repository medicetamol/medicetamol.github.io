# Diarrhea

**Diarrhoea** causes bicarbonate loss, giving a **normal anion gap (hyperchloraemic) metabolic acidosis**. The other options add unmeasured anions (ketones, uraemic anions, toxin metabolites) and give a **high anion gap** acidosis.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** a cause of high anion gap metabolic acidosis?"

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Acute kidney injury**
Retention of phosphates, sulphates and organic anions raises the anion gap.

**C. Ketoacidosis**
Ketoacids (β-hydroxybutyrate, acetoacetate) are unmeasured anions.

**D. Toxins**
Methanol, ethylene glycol and salicylates produce acid metabolites.

## Mind Capsule

> High anion gap = MUDPILES; diarrhoea and renal tubular acidosis = normal anion gap.

## Memory Hook

> **MUDPILES**: **M**ethanol, **U**raemia, **D**KA, **P**araldehyde/propylene glycol, **I**soniazid/iron, **L**actic acidosis, **E**thylene glycol, **S**alicylates.
