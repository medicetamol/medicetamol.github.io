# Non-pulsatile Elevation of JVP

In cardiac failure, the JVP is raised **with visible pulsations**. A raised JVP with **no pulsation** points to **SVC obstruction**.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** a symptom of cardiac failure?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Right hypochondriac pain**
Caused by hepatic congestion with capsular stretching in right-sided failure.

**C. Orthopnea**
Breathlessness on lying flat, due to pulmonary congestion.

**D. Paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea**
Sudden night-time breathlessness from fluid redistribution in left heart failure.

## Mind Capsule

> Heart failure = raised pulsatile JVP; raised non-pulsatile JVP = SVC obstruction.
