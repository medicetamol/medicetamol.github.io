# Duration of Antibiotic Therapy

**Procalcitonin** is a biomarker of bacterial infection. Serial values help decide **when to stop antibiotics** (de-escalation and shorter courses). Levels may be raised by kidney injury because of reduced clearance, so interpretation needs care.

## Trigger Point

> Ventilator-associated pneumonia with AKI; a statement about procalcitonin is asked.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Identifying which empirical antibiotics to start**
Procalcitonin does not identify the organism or drug of choice.

**C. Confirming source of infection**
It is non-specific and does not locate the source.

**D. Differentiating bacterial from fungal cause**
Not reliable; it cannot dependably separate fungal from bacterial infection.

## Mind Capsule

> Procalcitonin: best for deciding when to stop antibiotics; it can be raised in AKI.
