# Pseudohyphae

A white patch in an immunocompromised patient suggests **oral candidiasis**. Microscopy shows **budding yeast cells with pseudohyphae**, best seen in KOH mounts or Gram stain.

## Trigger Point

> White oral patch in an immunocompromised patient.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Branching septate hyphae**
Seen in *Aspergillus*.

**C. Budding yeast with capsule**
Describes *Cryptococcus*.

**D. Sulfur granules**
Seen in actinomycosis.

## Mind Capsule

> Oral thrush = *Candida*: budding yeast + pseudohyphae.
