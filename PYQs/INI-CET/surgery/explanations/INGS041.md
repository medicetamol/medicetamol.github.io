# GERD

In a young infant, **non-bilious, non-projectile vomiting** with a normal abdominal examination is most likely **gastro-oesophageal reflux**, which is physiological or mildly pathological and usually improves with age.

## Trigger Point

> 6-week-old with non-bilious, non-projectile vomiting and a normal abdominal examination.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Milk allergy**
Usually has additional features such as rash, loose stools or blood in stool.

**C. Small bowel obstruction**
Vomiting is bilious, with distension.

**D. Hypertrophic pyloric stenosis**
Presents at 2–8 weeks with **projectile** non-bilious vomiting and a palpable olive-shaped mass.

## Mind Capsule

> Non-projectile non-bilious vomiting = GERD; projectile = pyloric stenosis; bilious = obstruction.
