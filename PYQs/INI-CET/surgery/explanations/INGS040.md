# SADI-S

**SADI-S (single anastomosis duodeno-ileal bypass with sleeve gastrectomy)** combines a **sleeve gastrectomy** with a **single duodeno-ileal anastomosis**, so food passes from the sleeve through the duodenum directly to a long ileal limb.

## Key Finding

> Sleeve-shaped stomach connected to a long limb of small bowel.

## Trigger Point

> Diagram of a sleeve-shaped stomach joined to a long small bowel limb.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Duodenal switch**
Also uses a sleeve, but the biliopancreatic limb is reconnected by a second (ileo-ileal) anastomosis, giving two anastomoses.

**C. Roux-en-Y gastric bypass**
Uses a small gastric pouch, not a sleeve, with a Roux limb and a biliopancreatic limb.

**D. Sleeve gastrectomy**
Only reduces gastric volume, with no intestinal bypass.

## Mind Capsule

> Sleeve + single duodeno-ileal anastomosis = SADI-S; the duodenal switch has two anastomoses.
