# Multiple Cancers in One Quadrant

**Breast-conserving surgery** can be done for a localised tumour or multiple foci in the **same quadrant** (multifocal). **Multicentric** disease in different quadrants is a contraindication.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** a contraindication to breast-conserving surgery?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Scleroderma**
Connective tissue diseases such as scleroderma cause severe radiotherapy toxicity, so BCS is contraindicated.

**B. History of radiation**
Prior chest or breast irradiation prevents a further radiation course.

**D. Persistent positive margin**
Residual disease after repeated re-excision needs mastectomy.

## Mind Capsule

> BCS is contraindicated with multicentric disease, prior radiation, scleroderma, persistent positive margins or pregnancy (first two trimesters).
