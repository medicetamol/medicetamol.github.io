# Grade Group 4

The **ISUP/WHO Grade Group** system simplifies the Gleason score: **Gleason 4+4=8 corresponds to Grade Group 4**, reflecting an aggressive, high-grade prostate cancer.

## Classification

| Gleason score | ISUP Grade Group |
|---|---|
| 3+3=6 | 1 |
| 3+4=7 | 2 |
| 4+3=7 | 3 |
| **4+4=8** | **4** |
| 9 or 10 (any combination) | 5 |

## Trigger Point

> **Gleason 4+4 = 8**: mapped to ISUP Grade Group 4.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Grade Group 1**
Corresponds to Gleason 3+3=6, the lowest-risk category.
Far less aggressive than the score given.

**B. Grade Group 2**
Corresponds to Gleason 3+4=7 (primary pattern 3 is favourable).
Does not match a primary pattern of 4.

**C. Grade Group 3**
Corresponds to Gleason 4+3=7 (primary pattern 4, secondary pattern 3), a different combination summing to 7, not 8.
The total Gleason score here is 8, not 7.

## Mind Capsule

> Gleason 6 = GG1; 3+4=7 = GG2; 4+3=7 = GG3; 4+4=8 (or any score of 8) = GG4; 9-10 = GG5.
