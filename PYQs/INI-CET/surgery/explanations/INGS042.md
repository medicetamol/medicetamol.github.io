# Dog Ear Excision

A **dog ear** (standing cone) is a redundant fold of skin at the end of a closed wound. It is removed by excising the fold so that the scar lies flat.

## Trigger Point

> Step-by-step illustration of removing a fold of skin at a wound end.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Keloid excision**
Removes a raised scar and is not a skin fold excision.

**C. Z-plasty**
Two triangular flaps are transposed to lengthen or reorient a scar.

**D. Transposition flap**
A flap rotated into an adjacent defect, not a fold excision.

## Mind Capsule

> Excess skin fold at the end of a closure = dog ear (standing cone), excised to flatten.
