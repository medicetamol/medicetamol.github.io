# Alvarado Score 6

The **Alvarado (MANTRELS) score** gives points for migratory pain, anorexia, nausea or vomiting, right iliac fossa tenderness, rebound, fever, leucocytosis and shift to the left.

## Alvarado Score

| Feature | Points |
|---|---|
| Migration of pain to RIF | 1 |
| Anorexia | 1 |
| Nausea / vomiting | 1 |
| Tenderness in RIF | 2 |
| Rebound tenderness | 1 |
| Elevated temperature (fever) | 1 |
| Leucocytosis (> 10,000) | 2 |
| Shift to the left | 1 |

## Working

- Migration of pain = 1
- Nausea = 1
- Fever = 1
- Rebound tenderness = 1
- WBC 14,000 = 2
- **Total = 6**

## Trigger Point

> Pain migrating from the umbilicus to the RIF, with fever, nausea, rebound tenderness and WBC 14,000/cmm.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 4, B. 5**
Undercount by missing features such as the 2 points for leucocytosis.

**D. 7**
Would need an additional feature (such as anorexia or a left shift) not mentioned in the stem.

## Mind Capsule

> Alvarado total is 10; a score of 5–6 is compatible with appendicitis, 7 or more is probable.

## Memory Hook

> **MANTRELS**: **M**igration, **A**norexia, **N**ausea/vomiting, **T**enderness RIF, **R**ebound, **E**levated temperature, **L**eucocytosis, **S**hift to left.
