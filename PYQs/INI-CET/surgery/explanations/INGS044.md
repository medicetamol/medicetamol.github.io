# To Increase Length

**Z-plasty** transposes two triangular flaps, which **lengthens a contracted scar** and changes its direction. The gain in length depends on the angle of the flaps.

## Z-plasty Angle and Length Gain

| Angle | Gain in length |
|---|---|
| 30° | About 25% |
| 45° | About 50% |
| 60° | About 75% |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Flap turning**
Describes flap transposition in general, not what Z-plasty achieves.

**C. Zigzag suturing**
It is a flap technique, not simply a suture pattern.

**D. Split-thickness skin graft**
A separate technique for resurfacing defects.

## Mind Capsule

> Z-plasty = lengthens a contracted scar and reorients it; larger angle = more gain.
