# Inflammatory Breast Cancer (T4d)

**Inflammatory carcinoma** is classified as **T4d**. Ulceration, ipsilateral satellite nodules and skin oedema (peau d'orange) belong to **T4b**.

## Breast Cancer T4 Subcategories

| Category | Feature |
|---|---|
| T4a | Chest wall invasion (not pectoralis alone) |
| T4b | Ulceration, ipsilateral satellite nodules, peau d'orange |
| T4c | T4a + T4b |
| T4d | Inflammatory carcinoma |

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** included in stage 4B of breast cancer?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Ulceration**
A criterion of T4b.

**B. Peau d'orange**
Skin oedema is a T4b feature.

**C. Satellite nodule**
Ipsilateral satellite skin nodules are T4b.

## Mind Capsule

> T4b = ulceration + satellite nodules + peau d'orange; T4d = inflammatory carcinoma.
