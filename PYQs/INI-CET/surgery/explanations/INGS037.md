# ABPI 0.3

An **ABPI below 0.3** indicates severe ischaemia, with **imminent gangrene** and risk of tissue loss.

## Ankle-Brachial Pressure Index

| ABPI | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| > 1.3 | Non-compressible (calcified) vessels |
| 0.9–1.3 | Normal |
| 0.5–0.9 | Intermittent claudication |
| 0.3–0.5 | Rest pain |
| < 0.3 | Imminent gangrene |

## Trigger Point

> ABPI value indicating imminent gangrene.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 0.5**
Corresponds to claudication or the threshold of rest pain.

**C. 0.7**
Moderate disease with claudication.

**D. 0.9**
Lower limit of normal or very mild disease.

## Mind Capsule

> ABPI: claudication 0.5–0.9, rest pain 0.3–0.5, gangrene < 0.3.
