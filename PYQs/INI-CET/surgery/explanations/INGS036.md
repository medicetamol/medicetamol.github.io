# LA Classification – Achalasia

The **LA (Los Angeles) classification** grades **reflux oesophagitis** (grades A–D). Achalasia is classified by the **Chicago classification** (high-resolution manometry) or assessed by the Eckardt score.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **wrongly** matched with its classification?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Boston classification – colon preparation**
The Boston Bowel Preparation Scale rates bowel cleanliness at colonoscopy.

**C. Mannheim classification – chronic pancreatitis**
A multifactorial classification for chronic pancreatitis.

**D. Miami classification – parathyroid**
Linked to intraoperative PTH criteria in parathyroid surgery, as keyed.

## Mind Capsule

> LA = reflux oesophagitis; Chicago = achalasia motility patterns.
