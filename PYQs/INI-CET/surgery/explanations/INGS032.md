# Secure Airway

In trauma, management follows **ATLS ABCDE**. A patient with a head injury and **GCS 8** cannot protect the airway, so **securing the airway (with cervical spine protection)** comes first. Decompressing the chest (ICD) follows at once in the breathing step.

## Trigger Point

> Head injury, respiratory distress, absent breath sounds on the right, GCS 8/15.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Intercostal chest drain**
Needed for the probable pneumothorax or haemothorax, but it comes under "B" after the airway is secured.

**B. Contrast-enhanced CT**
Imaging is done only after the patient is stabilised, not in an unstable patient.

**D. Oxygen by nasal prongs**
Inadequate for a patient with a compromised airway and respiratory distress.

## Mind Capsule

> GCS ≤ 8 = secure the airway first; ATLS order is A, B, C, D, E.

## Memory Hook

> **ABCDE**: **A**irway with C-spine protection, **B**reathing, **C**irculation, **D**isability, **E**xposure.
