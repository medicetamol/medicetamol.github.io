# Barotrauma

A ventilated **ARDS** patient has stiff lungs and high airway pressures, which predisposes to **barotrauma**. A sudden **tension pneumothorax** gives reduced breath sounds on the affected side with tracheal deviation to the opposite side.

## Trigger Point

> ARDS on a ventilator with reduced breath sounds on the right and tracheal deviation to the left.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Increase tidal volume**
A ventilator setting that can contribute to barotrauma, but it is not a diagnosis.

**C. Oxygen toxicity**
Causes diffuse lung injury, not unilateral signs with tracheal shift.

**D. Endotracheal malposition**
Right main bronchus intubation reduces breath sounds on the left and does not push the trachea.

## Mind Capsule

> Ventilated patient + sudden unilateral absent breath sounds + tracheal shift = tension pneumothorax from barotrauma.
