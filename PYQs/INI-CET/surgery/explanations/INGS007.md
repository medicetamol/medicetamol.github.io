# Fistulectomy

**Fistulectomy (complete excision of the fistula tract)** cuts through the sphincter complex, risking incontinence, so it is **not** considered sphincter-preserving. Techniques like **LIFT, FiLaC and VAAFT** are specifically designed to treat the fistula while **preserving sphincter integrity**.

## Trigger Point

> **EXCEPT**: the technique that is NOT sphincter-preserving.

## Why the Others Are True

**A. LIFT**
Ligates the fistula tract in the intersphincteric plane, leaving the external sphincter undisturbed.
A genuinely sphincter-preserving technique.

**B. FiLaC**
Uses laser energy to close the fistula tract from within, without cutting through sphincter muscle.
A genuinely sphincter-preserving technique.

**C. VAAFT**
A video-assisted, minimally invasive technique that identifies and treats the tract internally without dividing the sphincter.
A genuinely sphincter-preserving technique.

## Mind Capsule

> Sphincter-sparing anal fistula techniques: LIFT, FiLaC, VAAFT, fistula plug; fistulotomy/fistulectomy cut the sphincter.
