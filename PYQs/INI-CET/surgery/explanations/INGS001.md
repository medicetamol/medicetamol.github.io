# 2 cm

In **laparoscopic (TAPP/TEP) inguinal hernia repair**, adequate exposure of the entire **myopectineal orifice of Fruchaud** requires dissection extending at least **2 cm below the inguinal ligament**, ensuring the mesh covers the direct, indirect and femoral spaces without leaving a gap for recurrence.

## Trigger Point

> **Minimum distance of dissection below the inguinal ligament** to expose the whole myopectineal orifice.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 0.5 cm**
Far too little dissection to expose the lower margin of the myopectineal orifice, risking inadequate mesh coverage and recurrence.
Below the accepted minimum.

**B. 1 cm**
Still insufficient to reliably expose the full extent of the orifice, particularly the femoral space.
Below the standard recommended distance.

**D. 10 cm**
Excessive dissection risks unnecessary injury to structures such as nerves and vessels in the "triangle of pain" and "triangle of doom."
Far more than the necessary minimum.

## Mind Capsule

> TAPP/TEP mesh placement needs at least 2 cm of dissection below the inguinal ligament to cover the whole myopectineal orifice of Fruchaud.
