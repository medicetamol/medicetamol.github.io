# Hypercitraturia

**Citrate** inhibits stone formation by binding calcium, so **hypocitraturia**, not hypercitraturia, is the risk factor for recurrent stones.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** a cause of recurrent renal stones?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Hypercalciuria**
The commonest metabolic abnormality in calcium stone formers.

**B. Hyperoxaluria**
Increases calcium oxalate supersaturation.

**D. Hyperuricosuria**
Promotes uric acid stones and calcium oxalate nucleation.

## Mind Capsule

> Stone risk = hypercalciuria, hyperoxaluria, hyperuricosuria and hypocitraturia.
