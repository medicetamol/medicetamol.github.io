# GCS = 9

**Glasgow Coma Scale** totals eye, verbal and motor responses. **Eye opening to pain = 2**, **incomprehensible sounds = 2**, **localises to pain = 5**. Total = 2 + 2 + 5 = **9**.

## Working

| Component | Finding | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Eye opening | To pain | 2 |
| Verbal | Incomprehensible sounds | 2 |
| Motor | Localises to pain | 5 |
| **Total** | | **9** |

## Trigger Point

> **Opens eyes only to pain, incomprehensible sounds, localises to pain**: sum the three GCS components.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 8**
Would result from, for example, scoring the verbal response one point lower (such as "no response") rather than incomprehensible sounds (2).
Does not match the stated verbal finding.

**C. 10**
Would require a higher score in one component, such as a motor response of "withdraws to pain" (4) added differently, or "confused speech" (3) instead of incomprehensible sounds.
Does not match the findings exactly as given.

**D. 11**
Would require, for example, a verbal score of "confused" (3) rather than incomprehensible sounds (2), added to the same eye and motor scores.
The verbal finding described does not support this total.

## Mind Capsule

> GCS = Eye (max 4) + Verbal (max 5) + Motor (max 6); here 2 + 2 + 5 = 9.

## Memory Hook

> **GCS mnemonic**: **E**yes (4), **V**erbal (5), **M**otor (6) — "4, 5, 6" from lowest to highest max score.
