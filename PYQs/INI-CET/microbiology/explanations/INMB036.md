# 1, 3 and 4 Are Correct

## Statement Check

| Statement | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 1. Weil-Felix test for typhus fever | **True** (Proteus OX-19 and OX-2 cross-react with rickettsiae) |
| 2. Widal test in the first week | **False** (antibodies appear from the second week; blood culture is used in week 1) |
| 3. Standard agglutination test for brucellosis | **True** |
| 4. Microscopic agglutination test for leptospirosis | **True** (reference serological test) |

## Trigger Point

> Four statements on serological tests, with one being timing of the Widal test.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1, 2, 3 are correct**
Includes the Widal test in week 1, and leaves out statement 4.

**C. 1, 2, 3, 4 are correct**
Statement 2 is false, so not all four are correct.

**D. 1, 4 are correct**
Leaves out statement 3, which is correct.

## Mind Capsule

> Widal: blood culture in week 1, serology from week 2; MAT = leptospirosis; SAT = brucellosis; Weil-Felix = rickettsiae.
