# Entamoeba histolytica

In an HIV patient with a low CD4 count, **chronic diarrhoea with weight loss**, and colonic biopsy showing **trophozoites admixed with red blood cells**, the classic diagnosis is **amoebic colitis from Entamoeba histolytica** — the organism's ability to ingest erythrocytes (erythrophagocytosis) is a distinguishing microscopic feature.

## Trigger Point

> **Colonic biopsy trophozoites containing/associated with RBCs**: the hallmark of Entamoeba histolytica, not other HIV-related diarrhoeal pathogens.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Giardia lamblia**
Causes malabsorptive diarrhoea with trophozoites/cysts seen on duodenal aspirate or stool, but it does **not** ingest red blood cells.
The RBC-containing trophozoite is specific to E. histolytica.

**B. Cryptococcus neoformans**
Causes meningitis (and occasionally pulmonary disease) in HIV, not a colonic trophozoite-based diarrhoeal illness.
Wrong organism and wrong disease site.

**C. Cryptosporidium parvum**
A common cause of chronic watery diarrhoea in AIDS patients, identified by **oocysts** on modified acid-fast stool stain, not tissue trophozoites with ingested RBCs.
Different organism and different diagnostic finding.

## Mind Capsule

> Colonic trophozoites with ingested RBCs on biopsy = Entamoeba histolytica (amoebic colitis).
