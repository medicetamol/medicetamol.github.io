# Haemophilus influenzae

*H. influenzae* needs **X factor (hemin)** and **V factor (NAD)**. A *Staphylococcus aureus* streak on blood agar releases V factor and lyses red cells to release X factor, so *H. influenzae* grows as tiny colonies close to the streak, the **satellite phenomenon**.

## Trigger Point

> Colonies growing close to a single streak of another organism on blood agar.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Streptococcus pneumoniae**
Alpha-haemolytic and optochin-sensitive; it grows without help from other organisms.

**C. Corynebacterium diphtheriae**
Grown on Loeffler's serum slope or tellurite agar; no satellitism.

**D. Klebsiella pneumoniae**
Gives large mucoid colonies on MacConkey agar without any need for growth factors.

## Mind Capsule

> Satellite colonies around a *Staph aureus* streak = *H. influenzae* (needs X and V factors).
