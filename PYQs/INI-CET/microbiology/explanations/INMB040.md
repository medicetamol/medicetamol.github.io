# Enterobius

The egg of **Enterobius vermicularis** is **plano-convex (D-shaped)**, colourless and thin-shelled, and often contains a larva. The gravid female lays eggs on the perianal skin, causing **nocturnal anal itching**. Eggs are best collected with the **cellophane tape (NIH) swab** method.

## Trigger Point

> Child with anal pruritus; egg that is oval with one flattened side (plano-convex).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Trichuris**
Barrel- or lemon-shaped egg with bipolar mucoid plugs.

**C. Ascaris**
Round or oval egg with a thick, mammillated outer coat (fertilised egg).

**D. Ancylostoma**
Oval, thin-shelled egg with a clear space and a segmented morula inside.

## Mind Capsule

> Child + perianal itching + plano-convex egg = *Enterobius* (cellophane tape test).
