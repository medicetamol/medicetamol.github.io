# 26 Cycles

PCR doubles the DNA each cycle, so the number of copies after *n* cycles is **2ⁿ**. Half the product after 27 cycles is 2²⁷ / 2 = 2²⁶, which is reached at cycle 26.

## Working

- Copies after 27 cycles = 2²⁷
- Half of this = 2²⁷ / 2 = 2²⁶
- **Cycles required = 26**

## Trigger Point

> Number of cycles needed to give half the copies produced after 27 cycles.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 25**
Gives 2²⁵, one quarter of the 27-cycle yield.

**C. 13**
Wrongly halves the number of cycles instead of the number of copies.

**D. 12**
Not related to the doubling calculation.

## Mind Capsule

> PCR copies = 2ⁿ; halving the yield takes away exactly one cycle.
