# Trichomonas vaginalis

**Trichomonas vaginalis** is a sexually transmitted protozoan with **only a trophozoite stage** (no cyst). On wet mount it is a **motile, pear-shaped flagellated trophozoite** with **four anterior flagella and an undulating membrane**, showing jerky, twitching motility. It causes a **greenish, frothy discharge** with vulvovaginal irritation, and the whiff test can be positive.

## Trigger Point

> **Greenish vaginal discharge** + irritation, **Amsel criteria negative** (so not bacterial vaginosis).

## Quick Comparison

| Infection | Discharge | Wet mount |
|---|---|---|
| Trichomoniasis | Greenish, frothy | Motile pear-shaped trophozoites |
| Bacterial vaginosis | Thin, grey-white, fishy | Clue cells |
| Candidiasis | Thick, curdy white | Budding yeast with pseudohyphae |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Budding yeast with pseudohyphae**
*Candida*; gives curdy white discharge with itching and a normal vaginal pH.

**C. Intracellular Gram-negative diplococci within neutrophils**
*Neisseria gonorrhoeae*, which causes cervicitis or urethritis and is seen on Gram stain, not wet mount.

**D. Clue cells with coccobacilli**
Bacterial vaginosis (*Gardnerella*); this would meet the Amsel criteria, which were negative here.

## Mind Capsule

> Green frothy discharge + **motile pear-shaped flagellate with undulating membrane** = *Trichomonas vaginalis*.
