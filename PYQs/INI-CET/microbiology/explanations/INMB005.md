# Diphtheria Toxin

**Diphtheria toxin** (from *Corynebacterium diphtheriae* lysogenised by a beta-phage carrying the *tox* gene) **ADP-ribosylates elongation factor 2 (EF-2)**, halting host protein synthesis and causing tissue necrosis, notably in the myocardium and nerves. *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* exotoxin A shares this exact mechanism.

## Trigger Point

> Toxin that **inhibits EF-2**: the classic ADP-ribosylating toxin.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Cholera Toxin**
ADP-ribosylates the Gs protein, permanently activating adenylate cyclase and raising cAMP, causing massive secretory diarrhoea.
It does not act on EF-2.

**C. Tetanospasmin**
A zinc metalloprotease that cleaves **synaptobrevin**, blocking release of inhibitory neurotransmitters (GABA, glycine) at spinal synapses.
Acts on neurotransmitter release, not EF-2.

**D. Heat-Labile Toxin of E. coli**
Also ADP-ribosylates Gs protein, raising cAMP, similar to cholera toxin's mechanism.
Not related to EF-2 inhibition.

## Mind Capsule

> EF-2 ADP-ribosylation = diphtheria toxin and Pseudomonas exotoxin A.
