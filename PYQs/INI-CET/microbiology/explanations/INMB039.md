# 1, 2 and 3

*Acanthamoeba* (trophozoite and cyst), *Naegleria* (trophozoite and cyst in humans; a transient flagellate form appears in water or CSF culture) and *Giardia* (trophozoite and cyst) all exist in two main forms. *Trichomonas* exists only as a **trophozoite**.

## Statement Check

| Organism | Forms |
|---|---|
| 1. *Acanthamoeba* | Trophozoite + cyst: **True** |
| 2. *Naegleria* | Trophozoite + cyst (as counted here): **True** |
| 3. *Giardia* | Trophozoite + cyst: **True** |
| 4. *Trichomonas* | Trophozoite only: **False** |

## Trigger Point

> Organisms that have two morphological forms.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1, 2 are correct**
Leaves out *Giardia*, which also has trophozoite and cyst forms.

**B. 1, 2, 3, 4 are correct**
*Trichomonas* has no cyst form, so it is not included.

**D. 1, 4 are correct**
Includes *Trichomonas* and leaves out *Naegleria* and *Giardia*.

## Mind Capsule

> *Trichomonas* = trophozoite only (no cyst); *Giardia*, *Entamoeba*, free-living amoebae = trophozoite + cyst.
