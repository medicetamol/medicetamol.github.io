# pXO2

*Bacillus anthracis* virulence depends on **two plasmids**: **pXO1** encodes the **tripartite toxin** (protective antigen, lethal factor, oedema factor), and **pXO2** encodes the **poly-D-glutamic acid capsule**, which resists phagocytosis. Loss of either plasmid attenuates virulence.

## Trigger Point

> **Poly-D-glutamic acid capsule**: encoded by the capsule plasmid, pXO2.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. pXO1**
Encodes the toxin components (PA, LF, EF), not the capsule.
A different plasmid governs toxin production.

**C. Chromosomal Genes Only**
The capsule genes are plasmid-borne, not chromosomal.
Loss of pXO2 abolishes capsule formation, confirming its plasmid location.

**D. pXO1 and pXO2 Jointly**
Toxin and capsule are separable, plasmid-specific virulence factors; each plasmid encodes its own trait independently.
The capsule specifically maps to pXO2 alone.

## Mind Capsule

> pXO1 = toxin (PA + LF + EF); pXO2 = capsule (poly-D-glutamate).
