# Cryptococcus

**Cryptococcus neoformans** is an **encapsulated yeast** in both tissue and culture, so it is not dimorphic. **Dimorphic fungi** grow as moulds at 25°C and as yeasts at 37°C (*Coccidioides* forms spherules in tissue).

## Trigger Point

> "All of the following are dimorphic fungi **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Coccidioides**
A dimorphic fungus; mould in the environment and spherules with endospores in tissue.

**B. Talaromyces**
*T. marneffei* is the only dimorphic *Talaromyces* species, a yeast in tissue.

**C. Histoplasma**
Mould at 25°C and small intracellular yeast at 37°C.

## Mind Capsule

> Cryptococcus = yeast only (capsule, India ink); dimorphic fungi are Histoplasma, Blastomyces, Coccidioides, Paracoccidioides, Sporothrix and Talaromyces.

## Memory Hook

> **Mould in the Cold, Yeast in the Heat**: dimorphic fungi grow as moulds at 25°C and yeasts at 37°C.
