# Skin Penetration by Filariform Larvae

**Necator americanus** (and *Ancylostoma duodenale*) infective **filariform (L3) larvae penetrate intact skin**, usually of the bare foot, then migrate via the bloodstream to the lungs, are coughed up and swallowed, and mature in the small intestine.

## Trigger Point

> **Necator americanus, mode of infection**: skin penetration.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ingestion of Embryonated Eggs**
This is the route of infection for *Ascaris lumbricoides* and *Trichuris trichiura*.
Necator does not infect through ingested eggs.

**C. Bite of an Infected Female Sandfly**
This describes transmission of *Leishmania* species.
Not relevant to a soil-transmitted helminth like Necator.

**D. Ingestion of Undercooked Pork with Cysts**
This describes *Taenia solium* (pork tapeworm) or *Trichinella* infection.
Necator is acquired through skin, not through meat.

## Mind Capsule

> Hookworm (Necator/Ancylostoma) = filariform larvae penetrate skin; Ascaris/Trichuris = ingested eggs.
