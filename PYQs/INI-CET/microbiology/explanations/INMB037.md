# A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III

Each rickettsial disease has its own vector. Lice transmit **epidemic typhus**, mites transmit **scrub typhus**, fleas transmit **endemic (murine) typhus** and ticks transmit **Rocky Mountain spotted fever**.

## Correct Matches

| Vector | Disease (organism) |
|---|---|
| Louse | Epidemic typhus (*R. prowazekii*) |
| Mite | Scrub typhus (*O. tsutsugamushi*) |
| Flea | Endemic typhus (*R. typhi*) |
| Tick | Rocky Mountain spotted fever (*R. rickettsii*) |

## Trigger Point

> Match louse, mite, flea and tick with their diseases.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II**
Pairs louse with scrub typhus and mite with spotted fever, so the vectors are mismatched.

**B. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I**
Pairs louse with endemic typhus and tick with scrub typhus, which is wrong.

**D. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II**
Gets the louse right but mismatches mite, flea and tick.

## Mind Capsule

> Louse = epidemic typhus; flea = endemic typhus; mite = scrub typhus; tick = spotted fever.
