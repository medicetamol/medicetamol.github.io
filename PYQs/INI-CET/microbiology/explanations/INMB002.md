# Vibrio Cholerae

**Vibrio cholerae** is unusual among common bacterial pathogens in having **two circular chromosomes**: a large one carrying most housekeeping genes and a smaller one carrying additional genes, including some linked to virulence and drug resistance.

## Trigger Point

> **Two circular chromosomes**: the defining genomic feature among these organisms.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Escherichia coli**
Has a single circular chromosome, the standard bacterial arrangement.
Only one chromosome, unlike Vibrio.

**C. Mycobacterium tuberculosis**
Also has a single circular chromosome.
No second chromosome present.

**D. Mycoplasma genitalium**
Has one of the smallest bacterial genomes, on a single circular chromosome, and lacks a cell wall.
Only one chromosome, and notably minimal genome size.

## Mind Capsule

> Two chromosomes = Vibrio cholerae (an exception to the single-chromosome rule of most bacteria).
