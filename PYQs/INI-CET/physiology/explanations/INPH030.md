# Hypopnea, Bradycardia and Hypotension

The **Bezold-Jarisch reflex** is a cardioprotective vagal reflex triggered by chemoreceptors and mechanoreceptors in the left ventricle (unmyelinated C-fibres). It produces the triad of **bradycardia, hypotension and apnoea (hypopnea)**. It can be triggered by noxious ventricular stimuli such as inferior wall MI or certain drugs.

## Statement Check

| Statement | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 1. Hypopnea | **True** (apnoea or shallow breathing) |
| 2. Tachycardia | **False** (it causes bradycardia) |
| 3. Bradycardia | **True** |
| 4. Hypotension | **True** (vasodilation) |

## Trigger Point

> "Features of Bezold Jarisch reflex."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1, 2, 3**
Includes tachycardia, which is wrong, and leaves out hypotension.

**C. 1, 3**
Omits hypotension, which is part of the classic triad.

**D. All of the above**
Wrong because tachycardia is not a feature.

## Mind Capsule

> Bezold-Jarisch = bradycardia + hypotension + apnoea (vagal reflex from the LV).
