# Alpha Waves

**Alpha waves** (8–13 Hz) are seen over the occipito-parietal regions in a relaxed, awake person with **eyes closed**. When the eyes are opened or the person concentrates, alpha activity is replaced by faster, low-amplitude beta activity, called **alpha block** (desynchronisation).

## EEG Waves

| Wave | Frequency | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Delta | < 4 Hz | Deep sleep, infants |
| Theta | 4–7 Hz | Drowsiness, light sleep, children |
| Alpha | 8–13 Hz | Awake, relaxed, eyes closed |
| Beta | > 13 Hz | Alert, eyes open, mental activity |

## Trigger Point

> Normal awake person resting with eyes closed; the eyes are then opened.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Beta waves**
Increase, not diminish, when the eyes open and the person is alert.

**B. Theta waves**
Seen in drowsiness and light sleep; they are not the main waves of the relaxed awake state.

**D. Delta waves**
Seen in deep slow-wave sleep, not in wakefulness.

## Mind Capsule

> Eyes closed + awake = alpha; eyes open = alpha block (replaced by beta).
