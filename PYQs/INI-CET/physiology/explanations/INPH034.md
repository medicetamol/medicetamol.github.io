# Pre-optic Nucleus

**Pyrogens** (IL-1, IL-6, TNF-α) act via **PGE₂** on the **pre-optic area** of the anterior hypothalamus (near the OVLT) and raise the thermoregulatory set point, producing fever. This region is the main heat-regulating (heat-loss) centre and set-point controller.

## Trigger Point

> Patient presents with fever, and the area most likely affected is asked.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Insular cortex**
Involved in conscious thermal perception and interoception, not in resetting the set point.

**C. Periventricular hypothalamus**
Contains neurosecretory neurons for neuroendocrine control, not the primary site of pyrogen action.

**D. Dorsomedial hypothalamus**
Acts as a relay to effector pathways for thermogenesis and autonomic responses, not the primary site of the set-point change.

## Mind Capsule

> Fever = pyrogen → PGE₂ → pre-optic area set point raised.
