# Prominent Occiput

**Prominent occiput** is a feature of **trisomy 18 (Edwards syndrome)**, not Turner syndrome (45,X).

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** true in Turner syndrome?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Webbed neck**
Due to resolved cystic hygroma.

**B. Short 4th metacarpals**
A recognised skeletal feature.

**C. Widely spaced hypoplastic nipples**
Associated with a shield-shaped chest.

## Mind Capsule

> Turner = webbed neck + shield chest + short 4th metacarpal + streak gonads; prominent occiput = Edwards.
