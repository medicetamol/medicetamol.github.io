# Sphingomyelinase

**Niemann-Pick disease (types A and B)** is due to **sphingomyelinase deficiency** and causes **hepatosplenomegaly, cherry-red spot** and neuro-regression. The **startle response is normal**, which differs from Tay-Sachs.

## Quick Comparison

| Disease | Enzyme | Hepatosplenomegaly | Startle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tay-Sachs | Hexosaminidase A | Absent | Exaggerated |
| Niemann-Pick | Sphingomyelinase | Present | Normal |
| Metachromatic leukodystrophy | Arylsulfatase A | Absent | Not a feature |
| Gaucher | Glucocerebrosidase | Present | Not a feature |

## Trigger Point

> Hepatosplenomegaly, cherry-red spots, developmental regression with normal startle response.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Hexosaminidase A**
Tay-Sachs disease: cherry-red spot with exaggerated startle but no organomegaly.

**B. Arylsulfatase A**
Metachromatic leukodystrophy, a white matter disease.

**D. Glucocerebrosidase**
Gaucher disease: organomegaly and bone disease, with no cherry-red spot in the typical form.

## Mind Capsule

> Cherry-red spot + hepatosplenomegaly = Niemann-Pick; cherry-red spot + hyperacusis, no organomegaly = Tay-Sachs.
