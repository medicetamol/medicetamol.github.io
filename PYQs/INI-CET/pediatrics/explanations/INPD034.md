# Tuberous Sclerosis

**Ash-leaf spots** (hypopigmented macules best seen under **Wood's lamp**) are an early cutaneous marker of **tuberous sclerosis**, which later shows facial angiofibromas, shagreen patches, seizures and cardiac rhabdomyomas.

## Trigger Point

> Newborn with several hypopigmented macules showing ash-leaf spots under Wood's lamp.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Sturge-Weber syndrome**
Facial port-wine stain with leptomeningeal angioma and glaucoma.

**B. Neurofibromatosis type 1**
Café-au-lait macules, neurofibromas and axillary freckling.

**D. Incontinentia pigmenti**
Vesicular, verrucous and then hyperpigmented swirling lesions, mostly in girls.

## Mind Capsule

> Ash-leaf macules = tuberous sclerosis (TSC1 or TSC2).
