# IVIG

A child with petechiae, **platelets 10,000**, and a marrow with **normal cellularity and increased megakaryocytes** has **ITP** (peripheral destruction). **IVIG** gives a rapid rise in platelets, and corticosteroids are an alternative first-line option.

## Trigger Point

> Petechiae, platelets 10,000/cu mm, marrow with normal cellularity and more megakaryocytes.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Aspirin**
Antiplatelet drug that would worsen bleeding.

**B. Bone marrow transplant**
For marrow failure, not for ITP.

**D. Methotrexate**
Not used in ITP.

## Mind Capsule

> ITP: isolated low platelets, marrow megakaryocytes increased; treat with IVIG or steroids.
