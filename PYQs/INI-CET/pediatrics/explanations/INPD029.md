# Protein Loss Causing Edema in Marasmus

**Marasmus** is mainly caloric deficiency, with severe wasting and loss of subcutaneous fat but **no oedema**. Oedema from hypoalbuminaemia is the feature of **kwashiorkor**.

## Trigger Point

> "Which statement about marasmus is **not** true?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Caloric deficiency**
Marasmus is due to deficiency of total energy.

**C. Emaciated, loss of subcutaneous fat**
The characteristic appearance.

**D. Weight-for-age < 60%**
Corresponds to severe malnutrition (Gomez grade III).

## Mind Capsule

> Marasmus = calorie deficit, wasting, no oedema; kwashiorkor = protein deficit with oedema.
