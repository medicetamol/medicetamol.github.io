# Infundibular Pulmonary Stenosis

**Tetralogy of Fallot** has four components: **VSD, overriding aorta, right ventricular outflow (infundibular) obstruction** and **right ventricular hypertrophy**. Cyanosis arises from the right-to-left shunt.

## Trigger Point

> 3-year-old with cyanosis since infancy; which component indicates TOF.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Left ventricular hypertrophy**
TOF causes right, not left, ventricular hypertrophy.

**C. Transposition of arteries**
A separate cyanotic lesion.

**D. Inter-atrial septal defect**
Not part of the tetralogy (with ASD it is called pentalogy of Fallot).

## Mind Capsule

> TOF = VSD + overriding aorta + infundibular PS + RVH.

## Memory Hook

> **PROVe**: **P**ulmonary stenosis, **R**VH, **O**verriding aorta, **V**SD.
