# Bloom Syndrome

**Aneuploidy** is an abnormal number of chromosomes (trisomies, monosomies). **Bloom syndrome** is an autosomal recessive disorder of DNA repair (BLM helicase) with chromosomal breakage and instability, not aneuploidy.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is **not** an aneuploidy?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Trisomy 21**
Extra chromosome 21.

**B. Trisomy 13**
Extra chromosome 13 (Patau syndrome).

**C. Klinefelter syndrome**
47,XXY, a sex chromosome aneuploidy.

## Mind Capsule

> Aneuploidy = abnormal chromosome number; Bloom syndrome = chromosomal breakage disorder.
