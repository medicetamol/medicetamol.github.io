# Statements 2 and 3

In a good latch, the **chin touches the breast**, the **lower lip is everted** (turned outward), **more areola is visible above** the mouth than below, and the **cheeks are full and rounded** during sucking.

## Statement Check

| Statement | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 1. Chin touching the breast | **Correct** |
| 2. Lower lip inverted | **Incorrect** (lower lip should be everted) |
| 3. More areola covered above than below | **Incorrect** (more areola is visible above, so more is covered below) |
| 4. Cheeks full during sucking | **Correct** |

## Trigger Point

> "Which statements about proper breastfeeding technique are **incorrect**?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1 and 4**
Both are correct statements.

**C. 2, 3 and 4**
Statement 4 is correct.

**D. 1, 2, 3 and 4**
Statements 1 and 4 are correct.

## Mind Capsule

> Good latch = chin touching, lip everted, more areola visible above, cheeks round.
