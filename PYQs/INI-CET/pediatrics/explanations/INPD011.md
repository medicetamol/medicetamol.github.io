# Trial of Feeds

An **asymptomatic** neonate with a **mildly low blood sugar (38 mg/dL)** should first be **fed (breastfeeding)** and the glucose **rechecked after 30-60 minutes**. IV dextrose is reserved for symptomatic babies, very low values or failure of feeds. Exact thresholds vary slightly by guideline.

## Trigger Point

> **Blood sugar 38 mg/dL** and the neonate is **otherwise asymptomatic**.

## Neonatal Hypoglycaemia: Approach

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Asymptomatic, mildly low glucose | Feed and recheck |
| Symptomatic, very low glucose, or no response to feeds | IV 10% dextrose bolus (2 mL/kg), then infusion |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Immediate IV bolus of 10% dextrose**
For symptomatic babies or severely low values, not for an asymptomatic neonate at this level.

**C. Continuous glucose infusion**
Used when glucose remains low despite feeds or after a bolus.

**D. Reassurance and discharge**
A low glucose needs treatment and a repeat check before the baby is considered safe.

## Mind Capsule

> **Asymptomatic** neonatal hypoglycaemia = **feed and recheck**; symptomatic = IV dextrose.
