# MMR

**SCID** is a profound T-cell defect, so **live vaccines** (MMR, BCG, OPV, varicella, rotavirus) can cause disseminated vaccine infection and are **contraindicated**. The other options are inactivated or subunit vaccines.

## Trigger Point

> Vaccine contraindicated in severe combined immunodeficiency.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. DPT**
Inactivated or toxoid vaccine; safe, although response may be poor.

**B. Hepatitis B**
Recombinant subunit vaccine, which is safe.

**D. IPV**
Inactivated polio vaccine; the live oral vaccine is the contraindicated one.

## Mind Capsule

> SCID or other severe T-cell defects = no live vaccines.
