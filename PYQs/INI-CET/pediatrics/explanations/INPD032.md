# Inhibition of Conjugation

**Breast milk jaundice** is a prolonged unconjugated hyperbilirubinaemia in a healthy breastfed baby, attributed to substances in the milk (free fatty acids, **β-glucuronidase**) that **inhibit bilirubin conjugation** and increase enterohepatic recirculation.

## Breastfeeding vs Breast Milk Jaundice

| Feature | Breastfeeding jaundice | Breast milk jaundice |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | First week | After day 5–7 |
| Cause | Inadequate milk intake | Factors in the milk |
| Management | Improve feeding | Usually continue breastfeeding |

## Trigger Point

> Mechanism behind breast milk jaundice.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Hepatocellular injury by breast milk**
There is no liver injury in this condition.

**B. Increased RBC breakdown**
Describes haemolytic jaundice, not breast milk jaundice.

**D. Reduced intestinal absorption of bilirubin**
Enterohepatic reabsorption is increased, not reduced.

## Mind Capsule

> Breast milk jaundice = factors in milk inhibit conjugation; breastfeeding jaundice = low intake.
