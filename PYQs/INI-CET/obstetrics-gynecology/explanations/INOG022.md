# Dinoprostone

**Dinoprostone is PGE₂** and is used for cervical ripening and labour induction; it is not a treatment for PPH. **Carboprost (15-methyl PGF₂α)**, **misoprostol** and **oxytocin** are uterotonics used in PPH.

## Trigger Point

> "Which drug is **not** used in the management of PPH?"

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Methyl PGF2 alpha**
Carboprost is a second-line uterotonic.

**C. Misoprostol**
Used when oxytocin is unavailable or as an adjunct.

**D. Oxytocin**
First-line uterotonic.

## Mind Capsule

> PPH uterotonics: oxytocin, ergometrine, carboprost, misoprostol; dinoprostone is for induction.
