# Goodell Sign

Signs of pregnancy are graded as **presumptive, probable, or positive**. **Goodell sign (cervical softening)** is a **probable sign**: it strongly suggests pregnancy but can occasionally have other causes, unlike positive signs (fetal heart sounds, fetal movements felt by the examiner, or ultrasound visualization of the fetus).

## Classification

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Presumptive | Amenorrhoea, nausea, breast changes |
| **Probable** | **Goodell sign, Hegar sign, Chadwick sign, Braxton Hicks contractions, Osiander sign** |
| Positive | Fetal heart sounds, fetal movement felt by examiner, ultrasound visualisation |

## Trigger Point

> Identify the **probable** sign among the given options.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Hegar Sign**
Softening of the lower uterine segment, also a probable sign, so this option is also technically probable, but Goodell sign is the option keyed here.
Both are probable signs, making this a close distractor.

**B. Braxton Hicks Contractions**
Also classified as a probable sign in most texts, similar to Goodell and Hegar signs.
A plausible alternative probable sign, but not the one selected as the answer.

**C. Osiander Sign**
Also a probable sign (increased pulsation felt in the lateral vaginal fornix).
Another probable sign, making the options collectively debated; Goodell is the keyed choice here.

## Mind Capsule

> Probable signs of pregnancy: Goodell, Hegar, Chadwick, Braxton Hicks, Osiander — none of these alone confirms pregnancy.
