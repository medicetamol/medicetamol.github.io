# PCOS

**PCOS** gives delayed or irregular cycles and anovulatory infertility, with **bilaterally enlarged polycystic ovaries** on ultrasound. Normal uterus and patent tubes (bilateral spillage on chromopertubation) point to an ovulatory cause.

## Trigger Point

> Delayed cycles, infertility, bilateral tubal spillage, normal uterus and bilaterally enlarged ovaries.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Ovarian cyst**
Usually unilateral and does not explain delayed cycles with enlarged ovaries.

**B. Endometriosis**
Causes dysmenorrhoea, adhesions and tubal distortion, and the tubes are often not freely patent.

**D. Tubo-ovarian mass**
An inflammatory mass with pain and fever, and the tubes would not show normal spillage.

## Mind Capsule

> Irregular cycles + infertility + patent tubes + bilateral enlarged ovaries = PCOS.
