# a, c, d

A **Pap smear** is taken in the **lithotomy position** using a **speculum**, and the sample is collected from the transformation zone with an **Ayre's spatula**. A digital vaginal examination is avoided before sampling because it can distort the sample.

## Statement Check

| Statement | Verdict |
|---|---|
| a. Lithotomy position | **True** |
| b. Vaginal examination | **False** (not before taking the smear) |
| c. Speculum examination | **True** |
| d. Ayre's spatula sample | **True** |

## Trigger Point

> Features of the Pap smear procedure.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. a, b, d**
Includes vaginal examination and leaves out the speculum.

**C. a, c**
Leaves out sampling with Ayre's spatula.

**D. b, a, c**
Includes vaginal examination and leaves out the spatula.

## Mind Capsule

> Pap smear = lithotomy + speculum + Ayre's spatula from the squamocolumnar junction, with no prior digital examination.
