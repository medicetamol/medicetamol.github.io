# McRoberts Manoeuvre

In **shoulder dystocia**, after calling for help, the sequence begins with simple, non-invasive measures: **McRoberts manoeuvre (hyperflexing the maternal hips)** combined with **suprapubic pressure**, which resolves most cases by increasing the pelvic outlet and dislodging the anterior shoulder. More invasive manoeuvres are reserved for failure of first-line measures.

## Correct Order

1. Call for help
2. **McRoberts manoeuvre with suprapubic pressure**
3. Internal manoeuvres (Wood's corkscrew, delivery of the posterior arm)
4. Rarely, Zavanelli manoeuvre or symphysiotomy as last resort

## Trigger Point

> **First/next manoeuvre after calling for help** in shoulder dystocia.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Zavanelli Manoeuvre**
Reserved as a last resort (cephalic replacement followed by caesarean) when all other manoeuvres fail.
Far too aggressive for a first step.

**C. Wood's Corkscrew Manoeuvre**
An internal rotational manoeuvre used after simple external measures fail.
It comes later in the sequence.

**D. Symphysiotomy**
A rare, invasive last-resort procedure.
Not appropriate as an initial step.

## Mind Capsule

> Shoulder dystocia: help, then McRoberts + suprapubic pressure, before any internal manoeuvres.

## Memory Hook

> **HELPERR**: Help, Evaluate for episiotomy, Legs (McRoberts), Pressure (suprapubic), Enter (internal rotation), Remove posterior arm, Roll over.
