# Within 10 Days of Next Menstrual Cycle

An **LNG-IUS** can be inserted **within 7 days of the start of menstruation**, **within 48 hours** of delivery, or **immediately postpartum**. An insertion window of 10 days into the cycle is not part of the programme guidance.

## Trigger Point

> "LNG-containing IUCD may be inserted in all situations **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Within 48 hours of delivery**
A recognised postpartum insertion window.

**B. Within 7 days of menstruation**
A standard interval insertion window.

**D. Immediate post delivery**
Permitted as immediate postpartum insertion.

## Mind Capsule

> LNG-IUS: ≤ 7 days of menses; postpartum ≤ 48 hours (or after 4 weeks).
