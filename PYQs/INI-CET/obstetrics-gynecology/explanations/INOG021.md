# a, b (Hirsutism and Reduced Cycles)

The **Rotterdam criteria** require any **two of three**: oligo- or anovulation (reduced cycles), clinical or biochemical hyperandrogenism (e.g. hirsutism), and polycystic ovaries on ultrasound. Insulin resistance and obesity are associated features but are not criteria.

## Rotterdam Criteria (2 of 3)

| Criterion | Example |
|---|---|
| Oligo- or anovulation | Reduced cycles |
| Hyperandrogenism | Hirsutism, raised androgens |
| Polycystic ovaries | On ultrasound |

## Trigger Point

> Features included in the Rotterdam criteria for PCOS.
