# Serous Adenocarcinoma

**High-grade serous carcinoma** is the commonest malignant ovarian tumour, presenting with abdominal fullness, early satiety, weight loss, **ascites, omental caking, bilateral adnexal masses** with solid or septated areas, and a raised **CA-125**.

## Trigger Point

> Fullness, early satiety, weight loss, raised CA-125, ascites, omental caking and bilateral septated adnexal masses.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Mucinous cystadenoma**
A benign, usually unilateral large cyst, with no ascites or omental deposits.

**C. Germ cell tumour**
Occurs in young women, with markers such as AFP, hCG or LDH.

**D. Endometriosis**
Causes endometriomas with pelvic pain, without omental caking.

## Mind Capsule

> Bilateral adnexal masses + ascites + omental caking + raised CA-125 = serous ovarian carcinoma.
