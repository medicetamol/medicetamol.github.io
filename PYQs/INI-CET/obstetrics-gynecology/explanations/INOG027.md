# Prevent Preterm Labour

**Magnesium sulphate** in pre-eclampsia reduces **eclamptic seizures**, provides **fetal neuroprotection** in preterm births, and has been reported to reduce placental abruption (Magpie trial). It is not an effective agent for preventing preterm labour.

## Trigger Point

> "Magnesium sulfate in pre-eclampsia has all of the following effects **except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Neuroprotection**
Reduces the risk of cerebral palsy in babies born before 32 weeks.

**B. Prevent abruption of placenta**
Reduced abruption was reported in the Magpie trial.

**C. Decrease seizure incidence**
Its main indication in pre-eclampsia.

## Mind Capsule

> MgSO₄ = seizure prophylaxis + fetal neuroprotection; it is not a reliable tocolytic.
