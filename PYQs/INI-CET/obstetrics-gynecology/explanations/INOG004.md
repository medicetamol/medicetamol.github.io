# Vulvovaginal Hematoma

Instrumental (forceps) delivery can lacerate vaginal or paravaginal vessels, causing a **rapidly enlarging, painful, tense, fluctuant vulvovaginal hematoma**. Pressure on the urethra from the swelling causes **difficulty voiding (urinary retention)**. Management ranges from observation to surgical evacuation and ligation, depending on size and stability.

## Trigger Point

> **Painful, tense, fluctuant vulvovaginal swelling with urinary difficulty after forceps delivery**: classic hematoma presentation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Urethral Diverticulum**
A chronic condition presenting with post-void dribbling and recurrent UTIs, unrelated to acute instrumental trauma.
Does not present acutely after forceps delivery.

**C. Cervical Tear**
Presents with **bright red, continuous vaginal bleeding** with a well-contracted uterus, not a fluctuant vulvar swelling.
The clinical picture described does not match a cervical tear.

**D. Local Infection/Abscess**
Develops over days with signs of infection (fever, erythema), not immediately after an instrumental delivery.
Timeline and presentation don't fit an acute post-delivery event.

## Mind Capsule

> Painful, tense vulvar swelling + urinary retention after instrumental delivery = vulvovaginal hematoma.
