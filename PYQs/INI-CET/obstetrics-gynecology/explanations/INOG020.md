# HPV 16 and 18

**HPV types 16 and 18** are high-risk oncogenic types responsible for about 70% of cervical cancers. Their E6 and E7 proteins inactivate p53 and Rb.

## Trigger Point

> HPV strains linked to cervical cancer.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 31, 33**
Also oncogenic but much less frequent.

**C. 6, 8**
Types 6 and 11 are the low-risk types causing genital warts; type 8 is not a typical cervical type.

**D. 1, 2**
Cause common skin warts.

## Mind Capsule

> Cancer = HPV 16 and 18; genital warts = HPV 6 and 11.
