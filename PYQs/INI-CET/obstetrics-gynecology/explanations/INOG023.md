# Maternal Fever

**Maternal fever** (as in chorioamnionitis) raises the fetal metabolic rate and causes **fetal tachycardia**, not bradycardia.

## Trigger Point

> "Which does **not** cause fetal bradycardia?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Abruptio placenta**
Causes fetal hypoxia that can lead to bradycardia.

**C. Meconium aspiration**
Causes hypoxia and can be associated with bradycardia.

**D. Fetal head compression**
Increases vagal tone, producing early decelerations or bradycardia.

## Mind Capsule

> Maternal fever = fetal tachycardia; hypoxia and vagal stimulation = bradycardia.
