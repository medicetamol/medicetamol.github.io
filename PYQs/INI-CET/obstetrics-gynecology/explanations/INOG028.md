# Preterm Labour

**Urinary tract infection** (including asymptomatic bacteriuria) in pregnancy is associated with **preterm labour** and low birth weight, which is the most common complication among the options. Screening and treating bacteriuria reduces pyelonephritis and preterm delivery.

## Trigger Point

> Most common complication of UTI in pregnancy.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Miscarriage**
Not the characteristic complication.

**B. Chorioamnionitis**
Occurs less often.

**D. Neonatal mortality**
A less frequent outcome of UTI.

## Mind Capsule

> UTI in pregnancy = risk of pyelonephritis and preterm labour; screen for bacteriuria.
