# Depression

The indicated attachment is taken to belong to a muscle of the head and neck region whose action is **depression**. The mandible is depressed by the lateral pterygoid, digastric, mylohyoid and geniohyoid acting together, while masseter, temporalis and medial pterygoid elevate it.

## Trigger Point

> A marked area on a skeleton indicates a muscle attachment, and the function of that muscle is asked.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Protraction**
Produced mainly by the lateral pterygoids (pulling the mandible forward), not by a depressor muscle.

**B. Retraction**
Produced by the posterior fibres of temporalis and the deep fibres of masseter.

**D. Elevation**
Produced by masseter, temporalis and medial pterygoid, which attach to different areas of the skull and mandible.

## Mind Capsule

> Mandible: depressors = lateral pterygoid, digastric, mylohyoid, geniohyoid; elevators = masseter, temporalis, medial pterygoid.
