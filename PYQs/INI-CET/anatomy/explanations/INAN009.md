# Thyroid-Endoderm, Adrenal Medulla-Neural Crest, Kidney-Intermediate Mesoderm

The **thyroid gland** parenchyma develops from the **endodermal** thyroid diverticulum in the floor of the primitive pharynx. The **adrenal medulla** is derived from **neural crest cells** that migrate in and differentiate into chromaffin cells (unlike the adrenal cortex, which is mesodermal). The **kidney** (metanephros) arises from **intermediate mesoderm**.

## Correct Matches

| Organ | Embryological origin |
|---|---|
| **Thyroid gland** | **Endoderm** |
| **Adrenal medulla** | **Neural crest cells** |
| **Kidney** | **Intermediate mesoderm** |

## Trigger Point

> Match each organ to its embryological tissue of origin — the standard endoderm/neural crest/intermediate mesoderm triad.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Thyroid-Neural Crest, Adrenal Medulla-Endoderm, Kidney-Intermediate Mesoderm**
Swaps the thyroid and adrenal medulla origins.
The adrenal medulla, not the thyroid, comes from neural crest.

**C. Thyroid-Intermediate Mesoderm, Adrenal Medulla-Neural Crest, Kidney-Endoderm**
Swaps the thyroid and kidney origins.
The kidney, not the thyroid, is of intermediate mesodermal origin.

**D. Thyroid-Neural Crest, Adrenal Medulla-Intermediate Mesoderm, Kidney-Endoderm**
Rotates all three origins incorrectly.
None of the three pairings matches the accepted embryology.

## Mind Capsule

> Endoderm = thyroid parenchyma; neural crest = adrenal medulla; intermediate mesoderm = kidney and gonads.
