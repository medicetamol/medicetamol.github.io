# Paracrine

The marked pale rounded cluster in the tissue is taken to be an **islet of Langerhans** in the pancreas. Islet cells release hormones such as somatostatin and insulin that diffuse through the interstitial fluid to act on neighbouring islet cells, which is **paracrine** signalling.

## Trigger Point

> "By what mechanism does the marked structure exert its effects on nearby cells?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Nerve mediated**
Neurocrine or neural signalling needs a synapse and a neurotransmitter, not diffusion between adjacent endocrine cells.

**B. Vein mediated**
Hormone delivery through the bloodstream is endocrine signalling and acts on distant cells, not nearby cells.

**D. Epithelium to mesothelium transformation**
Not a signalling mechanism. The recognised process is epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition.

## Mind Capsule

> Endocrine = blood to distant cells; paracrine = diffusion to neighbouring cells; autocrine = same cell.
