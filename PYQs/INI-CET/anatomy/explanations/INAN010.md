# Ileum-Peyer's Patches, Transverse Colon-Taenia Coli, Oesophagus-Squamous Epithelium, Duodenum-Villi

Each GI segment has a signature histological feature: the **ileum** is marked by prominent lymphoid **Peyer's patches**; the **transverse colon** (like the rest of the large bowel) shows the three longitudinal **taenia coli**; the **oesophagus** is lined by **non-keratinised stratified squamous epithelium**; and the **duodenum** shows tall intestinal **villi** (alongside submucosal Brunner's glands).

## Correct Matches

| Organ | Distinctive histology |
|---|---|
| **Ileum** | **Peyer's patches** |
| **Transverse colon** | **Taenia coli** |
| **Oesophagus** | **Non-keratinised stratified squamous epithelium** |
| **Duodenum** | **Villi** |

## Trigger Point

> Match each GI organ to the histological feature that best identifies it.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ileum-Taenia Coli, Transverse Colon-Peyer's Patches**
Swaps ileum and colon; taenia coli is a colonic feature, and Peyer's patches are concentrated in the ileum, not the colon.
The oesophagus and duodenum pairings are correct here, but the first two are reversed.

**C. Transverse Colon-Villi, Duodenum-Peyer's Patches**
Swaps the colon and duodenum findings; the colon has no villi, and Peyer's patches are an ileal feature, not duodenal.
Two of the four pairings are incorrect.

**D. Ileum-Non-Keratinised Squamous Epithelium, Oesophagus-Taenia Coli**
Swaps the ileum and oesophagus features; squamous epithelium lines the oesophagus, and taenia coli belong to the colon.
Two of the four pairings are incorrect.

## Mind Capsule

> Ileum = Peyer's patches; colon = taenia coli; oesophagus = squamous epithelium; duodenum = villi + Brunner's glands.
