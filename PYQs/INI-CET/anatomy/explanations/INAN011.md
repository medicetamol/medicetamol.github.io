# Tailgut Cyst

A **tailgut cyst (retrorectal cystic hamartoma)** is a rare congenital lesion arising from **incomplete regression of the embryonic postanal gut (tailgut)**, presenting as a multilocular cystic mass in the **retrorectal/presacral space**, typically lined by variable epithelium (squamous, transitional or columnar).

## Trigger Point

> **Retrorectal (presacral)** cystic remnant of the embryological **hindgut**: the classic description of a tailgut cyst.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Chondroma**
A benign cartilaginous tumour, not a hindgut developmental remnant.
Unrelated to the embryonic tailgut.

**C. Epidermoid Cyst**
Arises from ectodermal inclusion (epidermis only), not from hindgut endoderm.
A different germ layer and mechanism.

**D. Dermoid Cyst**
A mature cystic teratoma-like lesion containing skin appendages, from ectodermal inclusion, not hindgut remnant tissue.
Also arises from a different embryological process.

## Mind Capsule

> Retrorectal cystic mass from a hindgut remnant = tailgut cyst.
