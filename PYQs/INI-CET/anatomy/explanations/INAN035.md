# Orbicularis Oris

In cleft lip, the **orbicularis oris** muscle is discontinuous and its fibres insert abnormally into the margins of the cleft. Primary repair rebuilds the **muscle ring** so that the lip is functional (competent), the philtral columns are restored and nasal base distortion is reduced.

## Trigger Point

> Primary muscle repair in cleft lip surgery.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Orbicularis oculi**
Sphincter of the eyelids, not involved in the cleft lip defect.

**C. Levator palpebrae superioris**
Elevates the upper eyelid, unrelated to the lip.

**D. Masseter**
A muscle of mastication, not part of the lip musculature.

## Mind Capsule

> Cleft lip repair = restore the continuity of the orbicularis oris.
