# Zonula Occludens

The **zonula occludens (tight junction)** is the most apical junction of epithelial cells. Strands of **claudin and occludin** fuse the outer leaflets of adjacent membranes, sealing the paracellular space and keeping the apical and basolateral membrane domains separate.

## Trigger Point

> A junction that **seals the paracellular space** = tight (occluding) junction.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Fascia Adherens**
An adherens-type junction that anchors actin filaments via cadherins; prominent in the intercalated discs of cardiac muscle.
It gives mechanical attachment, not a seal.

**B. Desmosome**
A spot-weld anchoring junction linking keratin filaments through desmogleins and desmocollins (the target in pemphigus).
It gives strength but leaves the intercellular space open.

**C. Gap Junction**
Connexon channels that allow passage of ions and small molecules between cells (electrical and metabolic coupling).
It communicates, it does not seal.

## Mind Capsule

> Seal = zonula occludens; glue = adherens junction and desmosome; talk = gap junction.
