# Cough Reflex

The **piriform recess** is lined by mucosa supplied by the **internal laryngeal nerve** (branch of the superior laryngeal nerve of the vagus), which lies just under the mucosa. It is the afferent limb of the **cough reflex** for the supraglottic larynx, so a lodged foreign body can impair it and raise the risk of aspiration.

## Trigger Point

> Bone lodged in the left piriform recess.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Gag reflex**
Afferent is the glossopharyngeal nerve from the posterior pharyngeal wall and tonsillar region, not the piriform recess.

**C. Mastication**
Controlled by the motor root of the mandibular nerve (CN V3), unrelated to the hypopharynx.

**D. Salivation**
Controlled by parasympathetic fibres of CN VII and CN IX to salivary glands.

## Mind Capsule

> Piriform recess + foreign body = internal laryngeal nerve injury risk (loss of cough reflex).
