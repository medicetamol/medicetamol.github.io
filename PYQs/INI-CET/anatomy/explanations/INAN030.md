# Back of Neck

The dura of the **posterior cranial fossa** is supplied by branches of the upper cervical nerves (**C2, C3**), plus meningeal branches of the vagus and hypoglossal nerves. Irritation here is referred along C2–C3 territory to the **back of the neck** and occiput.

## Trigger Point

> Referred pain from the dura mater of the posterior cranial fossa.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ear**
Referred from the middle cranial fossa and external ear region through the trigeminal (V3, auriculotemporal) and the facial, glossopharyngeal and vagus sensory branches, not from the posterior fossa.

**C. Vertex**
Supratentorial dura is mostly supplied by the trigeminal nerve; vertex pain is not the classic referral from the posterior fossa.

**D. Forehead**
Referred pain from the anterior cranial fossa and supratentorial dura via the ophthalmic division of the trigeminal nerve.

## Mind Capsule

> Supratentorial dura = trigeminal (pain to forehead, face); infratentorial dura = C2–C3 and vagus (pain to back of neck).
