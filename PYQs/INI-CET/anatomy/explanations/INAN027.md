# Hypoglossal Nerve

**Olfaction** is mediated by the olfactory nerve (CN I). The trigeminal, glossopharyngeal and vagus nerves carry general chemical or irritant sensation from the nasal and oral mucosa and contribute to the overall perception of smell and flavour. The hypoglossal nerve (CN XII) is purely **motor** to the tongue muscles and has no sensory role.

## Trigger Point

> "Which nerve is not related to olfaction?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Trigeminal**
Carries pungent and irritant sensation (e.g. ammonia, menthol) from the nasal mucosa, so it is related to smell perception.

**C. Glossopharyngeal**
Sensory supply of the oropharynx and posterior tongue; contributes to retronasal flavour perception.

**D. Vagus**
Sensory supply of the laryngopharynx and part of the airway; contributes to chemosensory perception.

## Mind Capsule

> CN XII = pure motor to the tongue, so it has no role in smell or taste.
