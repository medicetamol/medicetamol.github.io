# Peroneus Longus and Brevis

The **superficial peroneal nerve** supplies the **lateral compartment** of the leg: peroneus longus and peroneus brevis, which evert the foot. The anterior compartment muscles (extensor hallucis longus, peroneus tertius) are supplied by the **deep peroneal nerve**.

## Trigger Point

> "Which muscles are supplied by the superficial peroneal nerve?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Extensor hallucis longus**
An anterior compartment muscle, supplied by the deep peroneal nerve.

**C. Peroneus tertius**
Although named "peroneus", it is part of the extensor (anterior) compartment and is supplied by the deep peroneal nerve.

**D. Both A & C**
Incorrect, because option C is wrong.

## Mind Capsule

> Superficial peroneal = lateral compartment (peroneus longus + brevis); deep peroneal = anterior compartment (including peroneus tertius).
