# 1-D, 2-C, 3-A, 4-B

In the male pelvis, the **vas deferens** passes over the ureter near the bladder ("water under the bridge"), and its ampulla lies medial to the **seminal vesicle** on the posterior bladder wall. Both ducts then enter the **prostate**, which lies inferiorly.

## Correct Matches

| Structure | Label |
|---|---|
| Seminal vesicles | D |
| Ureter | C |
| Prostate | A |
| Vas deferens | B |

## Trigger Point

> Match seminal vesicles, ureter, prostate and vas deferens to labels A, B, C and D.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1-A, 2-B, 3-C, 4-D**
Places the seminal vesicles at the prostate position and misplaces the remaining structures.

**B. 1-B, 2-C, 3-D, 4-A**
Gets only the ureter correct and mislabels the other three.

**C. 1-D, 2-B, 3-A, 4-C**
Gets the seminal vesicles and prostate correct but swaps the ureter and vas deferens.

## Mind Capsule

> Vas deferens crosses anterior to the ureter ("water under the bridge"); the prostate is the lowest organ of the group.
