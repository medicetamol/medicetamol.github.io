# All of the Above

The **ulnar nerve** supplies the hypothenar muscles, the **medial two lumbricals (3rd and 4th)**, all the **interossei (palmar and dorsal)**, adductor pollicis and the deep head of flexor pollicis brevis. All three muscles listed belong to this group.

## Trigger Point

> Three separate muscles are listed, followed by "All of the above".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Dorsal interossei**
Supplied by the deep branch of the ulnar nerve, but this is only one of the three correct muscles.

**B. Abductor digiti minimi**
A hypothenar muscle supplied by the ulnar nerve, but again only one of the correct muscles.

**C. 4th lumbrical**
Medial lumbricals are ulnar; the lateral two are median. Correct, but incomplete by itself.

## Mind Capsule

> Ulnar nerve = hypothenar + all interossei + medial 2 lumbricals + adductor pollicis.

## Memory Hook

> **Lumbricals 1 and 2 are Median, 3 and 4 are Ulnar**: "LOAF" muscles (lateral 2 lumbricals, opponens pollicis, abductor pollicis brevis, flexor pollicis brevis) are median-supplied.
