# Lateral Ventricle (Septum Pellucidum)

The marked structure is the **septum pellucidum**, a thin, double-layered translucent partition stretching between the **corpus callosum** above and the **fornix** below.   
It separates the **anterior (frontal) horns of the right and left lateral ventricles**. Its absence is seen in conditions like septo-optic dysplasia.

## Trigger Point

> Sagittal brain section: a thin membrane **below the corpus callosum** and above the fornix, in front of the thalamus.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Third ventricle**
A midline slit between the two thalami and hypothalamus. It is not separated by the septum pellucidum, and the lateral ventricles drain into it through the interventricular foramen.

**C. Fourth ventricle**
Lies in the posterior fossa between the pons/medulla (floor) and the cerebellum (roof). It is far from the septum pellucidum and is a single midline cavity.

**D. Cisterna magna**
A subarachnoid CSF space below the cerebellum (cerebellomedullary cistern). It is not a ventricle and has no relation to the septum pellucidum.

## Mind Capsule

> Septum pellucidum = between corpus callosum and fornix, separating the anterior horns of the lateral ventricles.
> Separates : 
> Falx cerebri => cerebral hemispheres
> Septum pellucidum => lateral ventricles.
