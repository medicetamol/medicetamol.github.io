# 1-D, 2-C, 3-A, 4-B

The **left atrium** receives oxygenated blood through four pulmonary veins. The **ascending aorta** arises from the left ventricle, and the **pulmonary trunk** lies anterior to it. In pulmonary stenosis, the **right ventricle** hypertrophies against the increased afterload.

## Correct Matches

| Feature | Structure |
|---|---|
| Receives oxygenated blood from pulmonary veins | D. Left atrium |
| Lies anterior to ascending aorta | C. Pulmonary trunk |
| Arises from LV | A. Ascending aorta |
| Hypertrophy in pulmonary stenosis | B. Right ventricle |

## Trigger Point

> Match four descriptions with A. Ascending aorta, B. Right ventricle, C. Pulmonary trunk, D. Left atrium.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 1-A, 2-B, 3-C, 4-D**
Every pairing is wrong; for example, the aorta does not receive pulmonary venous blood.

**C. 1-B, 2-D, 3-C, 4-A**
The right ventricle does not receive pulmonary venous blood, and the pulmonary trunk does not arise from the LV.

**D. 1-C, 2-A, 3-D, 4-B**
Pulmonary trunk and aorta are mismatched with the left atrium, and the left atrium does not arise from the LV.

## Mind Capsule

> Pulmonary veins go to the LA; aorta comes from the LV; the pulmonary trunk lies anterior to the aorta; pulmonary stenosis causes RV hypertrophy.
