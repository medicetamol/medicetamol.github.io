# Corticosteroids

**Sudden sensorineural hearing loss** is treated as an otologic emergency, and **corticosteroids** (systemic, started as early as possible) are the **mainstay**. If there is inadequate recovery, **intratympanic steroids** are used as salvage. Most cases are idiopathic, so the cause is not treated directly.

## Trigger Point

> **SSNHL** (at least 30 dB loss over three contiguous frequencies within 3 days): steroids are the recognised treatment.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Oral Antiviral Therapy Alone**
A viral aetiology is postulated, but trials show no proven benefit of antivirals.
Antivirals alone are not recommended.

**C. Immediate Cochlear Implantation**
Reserved for profound, permanent hearing loss that does not recover.
It is not an initial treatment.

**D. Broad-Spectrum Intravenous Antibiotics**
Useful for bacterial causes of hearing loss such as otitis-related labyrinthitis.
There is no role in idiopathic SSNHL.

## Mind Capsule

> SSNHL = otologic emergency = steroids (systemic first, intratympanic as salvage).
