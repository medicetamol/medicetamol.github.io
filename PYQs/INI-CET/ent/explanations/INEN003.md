# Proptosis

**Melkersson-Rosenthal syndrome** is a rare disorder classically presenting as a **triad**: recurrent **lower motor neuron facial nerve palsy**, **orofacial (labial) granulomatous oedema**, and a **fissured ("scrotal") tongue**. **Proptosis** is not a recognised part of this triad.

## Trigger Point

> **NOT part of the Melkersson-Rosenthal triad**: proptosis is the odd one out among the four options.

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Lower Motor Neuron Facial Palsy**
A recurrent, often relapsing facial nerve palsy is one of the three defining features of the syndrome.
A genuine component of the triad.

**C. Orofacial Granulomatous Oedema**
Persistent, non-pitting swelling of the lips/face from granulomatous inflammation is a defining feature.
A genuine component of the triad.

**D. Fissured (Scrotal) Tongue**
A deeply grooved tongue surface is the third classic component of the triad.
A genuine component of the triad.

## Mind Capsule

> Melkersson-Rosenthal triad: recurrent facial palsy + orofacial oedema + fissured tongue — proptosis is NOT part of it.
