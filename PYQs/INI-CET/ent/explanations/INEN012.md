# 1 and 2

**Cochlear implants** are for severe to profound **sensorineural hearing loss** where hearing aids do not help. **Ototoxicity** and **congenital hearing loss** (cochlear damage) are valid candidates. **Ossicular fixation** and **otosclerosis** are conductive problems, treated with surgery (stapedotomy) or hearing aids.

## Trigger Point

> "Most appropriate candidates for cochlear implants."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 2 and 3**
Ossicular fixation is a conductive loss, so it does not qualify.

**C. 3 and 4**
Both are conductive pathologies, which are corrected with ear surgery.

**D. 1 and 4**
Ototoxicity qualifies, but otosclerosis is primarily conductive.

## Mind Capsule

> Cochlear implant = severe to profound sensorineural loss; conductive loss = surgery or hearing aid.
