# CA-125 Positivity

**Granulosa cell tumours** are sex cord-stromal tumours that show **Call-Exner bodies** and **coffee-bean (grooved) nuclei**, and secrete **inhibin A/B** and oestrogen. **CA-125** is a marker of epithelial ovarian tumours and is not a feature.

## Trigger Point

> "Histopathological features of granulosa cell tumor include **all except**."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Call-Exner bodies**
Small follicle-like spaces with eosinophilic material between granulosa cells, classic for this tumour.

**B. Inhibin A**
Immunohistochemical marker positive in granulosa cell tumours (along with calretinin and FOXL2).

**D. Coffee bean nuclei**
Longitudinally grooved nuclei are characteristic.

## Mind Capsule

> Granulosa cell tumour = Call-Exner + coffee-bean nuclei + inhibin + FOXL2; CA-125 = epithelial ovarian cancer.
