# Surface Plasmon Resonance for Affinity

**Surface plasmon resonance (SPR)** is a label-free method that measures binding in real time. The sensorgram shows the **association** and **dissociation** phases, from which rate constants and the **affinity (KD)** of the antigen-antibody interaction are derived.

## Trigger Point

> Graph of antigen-antibody interaction kinetics.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Competitive ELISA for antibody affinity**
It is an end-point assay and does not give a real-time kinetic curve.

**B. Competitive ELISA for antibody concentration**
Used to measure the amount of antigen or antibody at one time point, not binding kinetics.

**D. Surface plasmon resonance for concentration**
SPR can estimate concentration, but a kinetic curve of association and dissociation is used to determine affinity.

## Mind Capsule

> Real-time binding curve with association and dissociation phases = SPR (kinetics and affinity).
