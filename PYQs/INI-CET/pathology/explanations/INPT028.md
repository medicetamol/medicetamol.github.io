# Population

The **gene pool** is the total collection of alleles present in a breeding **population** at a given time. Allele frequencies in this pool form the basis of population genetics (Hardy-Weinberg principle).

## Trigger Point

> "Alleles of gene pool belong to..."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Individual**
A person carries only two alleles per locus, which is a tiny sample of the pool.

**B. Family**
A small related group, not the whole breeding population.

**D. Cell**
A cell carries one individual's genome and is not a unit of gene pool.

## Mind Capsule

> Gene pool = all alleles of a breeding population.
