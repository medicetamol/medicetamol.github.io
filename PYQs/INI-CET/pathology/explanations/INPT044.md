# Warburg Effect

Cancer cells rely heavily on **aerobic glycolysis**: they take up large amounts of glucose and convert it to lactate even when oxygen is available (**Warburg effect**). This gives rapid ATP and provides carbon building blocks for rapid growth.

## Trigger Point

> Question on the metabolic explanation for cancer cell energy behaviour.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cancer cells need increased lactate production**
Lactate is a by-product of the glycolytic shift, not the reason for it.

**B. Cancer cells have increased mitochondrial function**
Mitochondria are not generally more active; respiration is relatively reduced.

**D. Cancer cells undergo increased oxidative phosphorylation**
The hallmark of Warburg metabolism is the opposite: glycolysis over oxidative phosphorylation.

## Mind Capsule

> Warburg effect = glycolysis + lactate despite oxygen (the basis of FDG-PET uptake).
