# Armanni-Ebstein Lesion

The **Armanni-Ebstein lesion** is glycogen vacuolation of renal tubular epithelial cells seen in poorly controlled diabetes. It is a rare finding today, unlike the common changes of diabetic nephropathy such as **GBM thickening, mesangial expansion** and **podocyte loss**.

## Trigger Point

> "Least common finding in diabetic kidney."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Podocyte loss**
An early and well-recognised change in diabetic nephropathy, associated with proteinuria.

**C. Mesangial widening**
Common; mesangial matrix expansion leads to Kimmelstiel-Wilson nodules.

**D. GBM thickening**
One of the earliest and most consistent lesions of diabetic nephropathy.

## Mind Capsule

> Common in diabetic kidney = GBM thickening + mesangial expansion + hyalinosis; rare = Armanni-Ebstein lesion.
