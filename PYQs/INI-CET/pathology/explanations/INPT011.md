# CMV and Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma

**Nasopharyngeal carcinoma** is associated with **EBV**, not CMV. CMV is an opportunistic pathogen (retinitis, pneumonitis, colitis) and is not an established oncogenic virus.

## Trigger Point

> "Which of the following is **wrongly matched**?"

## Oncogenic Viruses and Tumours

| Virus | Associated tumours |
|---|---|
| EBV | Burkitt lymphoma, nasopharyngeal carcinoma, Hodgkin lymphoma |
| HHV-8 | Kaposi sarcoma, primary effusion lymphoma |
| HTLV-1 | Adult T-cell leukaemia/lymphoma |
| HPV | Cervical and anogenital cancers, oropharyngeal cancer |
| HBV, HCV | Hepatocellular carcinoma |

## Why the Others Are True

**A. EBV – Burkitt's lymphoma**
Correct association, especially the endemic form.

**B. HHV-8 – Kaposi sarcoma**
Correct; HHV-8 is also called KSHV.

**C. HTLV – Adult T-cell lymphoma**
Correct; HTLV-1 causes adult T-cell leukaemia/lymphoma.

## Mind Capsule

> **NPC = EBV**; CMV is not a tumour virus.
