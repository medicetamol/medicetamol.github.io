# Oral Corticosteroids

This picture is **immune thrombocytopenia (ITP)**: isolated thrombocytopenia with a normal smear and normal other cell lines, often after a viral illness. In adults with symptomatic or severe thrombocytopenia (< 30,000/mm³), **oral corticosteroids** are first-line.

## Trigger Point

> Easy bruising and petechiae, platelets 18,000/mm³, normal Hb and WBC, normal smear after a recent URI.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Platelet transfusion**
Reserved for life-threatening bleeding, because transfused platelets are rapidly destroyed in ITP.

**C. Bone marrow biopsy**
Not needed in a young patient with typical features; considered in older patients, atypical features or non-response.

**D. Splenectomy**
A second-line option for relapsed or refractory disease after steroids and other agents.

## Mind Capsule

> Isolated low platelets + normal smear + otherwise well = ITP; first-line = steroids.
