# Pyroptosis

**Pyroptosis** is inflammatory programmed cell death driven by **caspase-1** (and caspases 4, 5 and 11) activated through the **inflammasome**. Cleaved **gasdermin D** forms membrane pores, and cells release **IL-1β and IL-18**, producing inflammation.

## Trigger Point

> "Caspase-1 mediated cell death with inflammation."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Necroptosis**
Regulated necrosis dependent on RIPK1, RIPK3 and MLKL, not caspase-1.

**C. Ferroptosis**
Iron-dependent cell death from lipid peroxidation, and it is caspase-independent.

**D. Necrosis**
Accidental, unregulated cell death with membrane rupture that is not caspase-1 driven.

## Mind Capsule

> Caspase-1 + inflammasome + gasdermin D + IL-1β = pyroptosis.
