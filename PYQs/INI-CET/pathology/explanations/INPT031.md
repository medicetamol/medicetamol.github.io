# TP53 – Mucosal Neuroma

**TP53** mutations cause **Li-Fraumeni syndrome** (sarcomas, breast cancer, brain tumours, adrenocortical carcinoma, leukaemia). Mucosal neuromas are a feature of **MEN 2B** (RET mutation), so this pairing is incorrect.

## Hereditary Cancer Genes

| Gene | Syndrome | Typical tumours |
|---|---|---|
| TP53 | Li-Fraumeni | Sarcoma, breast, brain, adrenal cortex |
| PTEN | Cowden | Breast, thyroid, endometrium |
| STK11 | Peutz-Jeghers | GI, breast, pancreas |
| RET | MEN 2B | MTC, pheochromocytoma, mucosal neuromas |

## Trigger Point

> "Identify the **incorrect** association."

## Why the Others Are True

**A. BRCA2 – Prostate**
BRCA2 carriers have a raised risk of prostate cancer, along with breast, ovarian and pancreatic cancer.

**B. PTEN – Thyroid**
Cowden syndrome carries a risk of thyroid cancer (especially follicular), as well as breast and endometrial cancers.

**C. STK11 – Breast**
Peutz-Jeghers syndrome raises risk of GI, breast and pancreatic cancers.

## Mind Capsule

> Mucosal neuromas = MEN 2B (RET), not TP53 (Li-Fraumeni).
