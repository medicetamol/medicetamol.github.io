# Congo Red

**Congo red** is used to detect **amyloid** (apple-green birefringence under polarised light), not microorganisms. The other stains highlight fungi or pathogens in tissue sections.

## Trigger Point

> "Which stain is **not** used to identify organisms in a renal transplant biopsy?"

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Gomori methenamine silver**
Stains fungal walls and *Pneumocystis* cysts black.

**B. PAS stain**
Highlights fungal hyphae and yeast walls in tissue.

**C. Masson Fontana**
Stains melanin-containing (dematiaceous) fungi and can help with *Cryptococcus*.

## Mind Capsule

> Organisms = GMS, PAS, Fontana; Congo red = amyloid.
