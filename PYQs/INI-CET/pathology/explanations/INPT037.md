# Corynebacterium kroppenstedtii

**Cystic neutrophilic granulomatous mastitis** is associated with the lipophilic Gram-positive bacillus *Corynebacterium kroppenstedtii*. Histology shows lipogranulomas: clear vacuoles surrounded by a ring of neutrophils, within which the bacilli may be seen.

## Trigger Point

> Neutrophilic granulomatous mastitis, and the causative organism is asked.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Corynebacterium minutissimum**
Causes erythrasma, a superficial skin infection of intertriginous areas.

**C. Mycobacterium tuberculosis**
Causes granulomatous mastitis with caseating granulomas, unlike the neutrophil-rimmed lipid vacuoles here.

**D. Staphylococcus aureus**
Typical cause of lactational mastitis and breast abscess, not neutrophilic granulomatous mastitis.

## Mind Capsule

> Neutrophil-rimmed lipid vacuoles in a granulomatous mastitis = *C. kroppenstedtii*.
