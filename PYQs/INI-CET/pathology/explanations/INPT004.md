# Masson Trichrome

**Masson trichrome** stains **collagen (fibrosis) blue/green**, muscle red, and nuclei black, making it the standard special stain to **demonstrate and quantify fibrosis** in a liver biopsy (as in cirrhosis staging).

## Trigger Point

> **Special stain to demonstrate fibrosis (collagen)** in liver biopsy: Masson trichrome.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Periodic Acid-Schiff (PAS)**
Stains glycogen, mucin and basement membranes; used for glycogen storage disease and fungal walls.
Not the standard stain for collagen/fibrosis.

**C. Gomori Methenamine Silver (GMS)**
Highlights **fungal organisms** (and some basement membranes) with a silver stain.
Used for mycology, not fibrosis assessment.

**D. Gram Stain**
Differentiates Gram-positive from Gram-negative bacteria.
Irrelevant to fibrosis or liver architecture.

## Mind Capsule

> Fibrosis/collagen in liver biopsy = Masson trichrome (blue-green collagen).
