# Terminal Bronchiole

The **terminal bronchiole** is the last purely conducting airway. It has a simple cuboidal epithelium with club (Clara) cells, no cartilage and no glands, and **no alveoli in its wall**.

## Quick Comparison

| Airway | Cartilage / glands | Alveoli in wall |
|---|---|---|
| Bronchus | Present | None |
| Terminal bronchiole | Absent | None |
| Respiratory bronchiole | Absent | Few, scattered |
| Alveolar duct | Absent | Wall made entirely of alveoli |

## Trigger Point

> Lung histology with a marked airway structure to identify.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Respiratory bronchiole**
Has scattered alveoli opening into its wall, so it takes part in gas exchange.

**C. Alveolar duct**
Its wall is made up almost entirely of alveolar openings.

**D. Bronchus**
Has cartilage plates and submucosal glands, with a pseudostratified ciliated epithelium.

## Mind Capsule

> Conducting zone ends at the terminal bronchiole: no cartilage, no glands and no alveoli.
