# Early Antibiotic Discontinuation

**Procalcitonin (PCT)** rises in bacterial infections and falls as infection resolves. In pneumonia, its main proven use is to **guide stopping antibiotics earlier**, shortening duration without harm. Guidelines (such as IDSA/ATS) advise against using it to decide whether to start antibiotics.

## Trigger Point

> "What is the role of Pro calcitonin in pneumonia?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Guiding initiation of antibiotic**
Not recommended by guidelines; the decision to start is based on clinical and radiological findings.

**C. Requirement of further investigation**
PCT is an adjunct and does not by itself call for further work-up.

**D. No role**
It does have a role in guiding the duration of therapy.

## Mind Capsule

> Procalcitonin: falling levels support stopping antibiotics early; it is not a reliable tool for starting them.
