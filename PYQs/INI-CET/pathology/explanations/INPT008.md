# AA Amyloid

**Secondary (reactive) amyloidosis**, seen with chronic inflammatory diseases such as **rheumatoid arthritis**, deposits **AA amyloid**, derived from the acute-phase reactant **serum amyloid A**. It classically affects the kidney, causing nephrotic-range proteinuria, and shows **apple-green birefringence** with Congo red stain under polarised light, as in all amyloid types.

## Trigger Point

> **Long-standing rheumatoid arthritis + nephrotic-range proteinuria + Congo red apple-green birefringence**: secondary (AA) amyloidosis.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. AL Amyloid**
Derived from immunoglobulin light chains, seen in **plasma cell dyscrasias** (multiple myeloma) or primary amyloidosis, not chronic inflammatory arthritis.
The clinical context here points to a reactive, not a plasma cell, process.

**C. Abeta2-Microglobulin Amyloid**
Seen in patients on **long-term haemodialysis**, depositing around joints (dialysis-related amyloidosis).
Not linked to rheumatoid arthritis as the primary driver here.

**D. ATTR Amyloid**
Either hereditary (mutant transthyretin) or senile systemic (wild-type transthyretin), affecting the heart and nerves.
Not related to chronic inflammatory disease.

## Mind Capsule

> Chronic inflammatory disease (like RA) = secondary amyloidosis = AA protein (from serum amyloid A).
