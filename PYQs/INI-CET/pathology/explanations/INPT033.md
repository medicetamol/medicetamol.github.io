# Von Willebrand Disease

**Von Willebrand disease** is the most common inherited bleeding disorder. Deficient or defective vWF impairs platelet adhesion and also reduces factor VIII stability, giving mucocutaneous bleeding with a prolonged bleeding time and variable aPTT.

## Trigger Point

> "Most common inherited bleeding disorder."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Factor V deficiency**
Rare autosomal recessive disorder (parahaemophilia), with prolonged PT and aPTT.

**C. Hemophilia A**
The most common inherited deficiency of a clotting factor (and the commonest severe one), but less common than vWD overall.

**D. Hemophilia B**
Factor IX deficiency (Christmas disease), roughly one-fifth as common as hemophilia A.

## Mind Capsule

> Commonest inherited bleeding disorder = vWD; commonest inherited coagulation factor deficiency = hemophilia A.
