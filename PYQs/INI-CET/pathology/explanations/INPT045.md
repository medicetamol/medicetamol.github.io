# IgA

**Secretory IgA** is a dimer joined by a **J chain** and carries a **secretory component** that protects it from proteolysis in secretions. It is the main antibody at mucosal surfaces, found in saliva, tears, milk and gut and respiratory secretions.

## Trigger Point

> Antibody with a joining chain and secretory component, located primarily on mucosal surfaces.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. IgG**
A monomer with no J chain; the main serum antibody and the only one that crosses the placenta.

**B. IgM**
A pentamer with a J chain, but it stays mostly intravascular and has no secretory component in the serum form.

**C. IgE**
A monomer with no J chain; involved in type I hypersensitivity and anti-parasite immunity.

## Mind Capsule

> J chain + secretory component + mucosal surfaces = secretory IgA (dimer).
