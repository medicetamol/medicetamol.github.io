# Whole Exome Sequencing

**BRCA1 and BRCA2** are large genes in which pathogenic variants are scattered across the whole gene, so testing relies on **sequencing** (Sanger, NGS panels or exome sequencing), usually supplemented by deletion/duplication analysis such as MLPA. Of the listed options, sequencing-based testing is the only suitable one.

## Trigger Point

> "BRCA testing is done by..."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. RT-PCR**
Starts from RNA and is used for expression studies or detecting transcripts, not for screening a large gene for variants.

**C. qPCR**
Quantifies the amount of a specific DNA or cDNA target; it does not scan an entire gene for mutations.

**D. Southern blot**
An old, low-throughput method that detects only large rearrangements and is not used routinely.

## Mind Capsule

> BRCA1/2 testing = sequencing (NGS or exome) + MLPA for large deletions.
