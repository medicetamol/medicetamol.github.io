# Skipped Generation - Obligate Unaffected Carrier

**Incomplete penetrance** means some individuals who carry a disease-causing genotype **do not express the phenotype**. On a pedigree, this can make a generation appear "skipped": an **obligate carrier (inferred from affected relatives on either side) is clinically unaffected**, even though they carry and can pass on the gene.

## Trigger Point

> Pattern that best shows **incomplete penetrance**: an apparently skipped generation due to a silent carrier.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Only One Gender is Ever Affected**
This describes **X-linked inheritance**, not penetrance.
It concerns which sex is affected, not whether a genotype is expressed.

**C. None of the Family Members are Affected**
This does not represent a Mendelian pedigree pattern relevant to penetrance; it would simply suggest no inherited disease is segregating.
Incomplete penetrance still requires some affected individuals to establish the pattern.

**D. Every Individual in All Generations is Affected**
This describes **complete (100%) penetrance** with a dominant trait, the opposite of what is being asked.
It shows full expression, not incomplete penetrance.

## Mind Capsule

> Incomplete penetrance = genotype present but phenotype absent in some carriers = apparent skipped generation.
