# Wuchereria bancrofti

The microfilaria of ***W. bancrofti*** is **sheathed**, with **nuclei that do not reach the tail tip** (a clear tail). By contrast, ***Brugia malayi*** has **two terminal nuclei** at the tail tip.

## Trigger Point

> Sheathed microfilariae with **no nuclei at the tail tip**.

## Microfilariae Compared

| Feature | *W. bancrofti* | *B. malayi* |
|---|---|---|
| Sheath | Present | Present |
| Tail nuclei | Absent in tip | **Two terminal nuclei** |
| Periodicity | Nocturnal | Nocturnal (subperiodic in some) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Brugia malayi**
Has two discrete terminal nuclei at the tail tip.
This distinguishes it from *W. bancrofti*.

**C. Loa loa**
Sheathed, but nuclei **continue to the tip of the tail** in a continuous row.
It is a diurnal filaria.

**D. None**
*W. bancrofti* fits this description exactly.

## Mind Capsule

> *W. bancrofti* = clear tail tip; *B. malayi* = two terminal nuclei; *Loa loa* = nuclei to the tip.
