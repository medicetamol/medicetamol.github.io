# Selenite F Broth

**Selenite F broth** is an **enrichment medium** for stool specimens that suppresses normal flora (coliforms) and allows ***Salmonella* and *Shigella*** to multiply. A child with **bloody stools and abdominal pain** is likely to have bacillary dysentery or enteric pathogens.

## Trigger Point

> **Bloody stool** sample needing **enrichment medium**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Alkaline peptone water**
An enrichment medium for ***Vibrio cholerae***.
Cholera has rice-water stools, not bloody stools.

**C. Blood agar**
A general-purpose enriched, differential medium, not an enrichment broth for faecal pathogens.
It cannot suppress normal flora.

**D. Mueller-Hinton broth**
Used for antibiotic susceptibility testing.
It is not an enrichment medium.

## Mind Capsule

> Selenite F = *Salmonella*/*Shigella*; APW = *Vibrio*; Cary-Blair = transport medium.
