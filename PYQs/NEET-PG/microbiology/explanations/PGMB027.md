# Weil's Disease

**Weil's disease** is the severe form of **leptospirosis** with **fever, jaundice, acute kidney injury (reduced urine output) and conjunctival suffusion**, seen after exposure to water contaminated with rat urine (e.g. **sewage workers**).

## Trigger Point

> Sewage worker + fever, **raised bilirubin**, reduced urine output, **conjunctival redness**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Brucellosis**
Undulant fever, sweats and arthritis from raw milk or livestock contact.
Jaundice and renal failure are not typical.

**C. Enteric fever**
Step-ladder fever with abdominal symptoms, not conjunctival suffusion.
There is no sewage-linked renal failure.

**D. Dengue fever**
Fever, rash and thrombocytopenia, usually without jaundice and oliguria.
Conjunctival suffusion is not typical.

## Mind Capsule

> Fever + jaundice + AKI + conjunctival suffusion + sewage/rat urine = **Weil's disease**.
