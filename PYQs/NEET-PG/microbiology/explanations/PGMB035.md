# Bacillus anthracis

***Bacillus anthracis*** is a **large, capsulated, gram-positive bacillus** with square ends ("bamboo-stick" arrangement). Smears stained with **polychrome methylene blue** show a **pink-purple amorphous capsule** around blue bacilli: the **McFadyean reaction**. Cutaneous anthrax gives a **malignant pustule** with a black necrotic eschar.

## Trigger Point

> Farmer with necrotic ulcer + **McFadyean reaction positive** capsulated bacilli.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Clostridium perfringens**
A capsulated, non-motile gram-positive rod causing **gas gangrene**.
It does not show a McFadyean reaction.

**C. Yersinia pestis**
A gram-negative coccobacillus with **bipolar (safety-pin) staining**, causing plague.
It does not produce the McFadyean reaction.

**D. Francisella tularensis**
A small gram-negative coccobacillus causing tularaemia.
It is not related to the McFadyean reaction.

## Mind Capsule

> **McFadyean reaction** + bamboo-stick bacilli = **anthrax**; capsule is poly-D-glutamic acid.
