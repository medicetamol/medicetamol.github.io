# Herpes Simplex Virus

**HSV** causes painful **vesicular lesions of the mouth** (gingivostomatitis, herpes labialis). A **Tzanck smear** from the base of a vesicle shows **multinucleated giant cells with intranuclear (Cowdry type A) inclusions**. The test cannot differentiate HSV from VZV.

## Trigger Point

> Painful oral vesicles + Tzanck smear with **multinucleated giant cells**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Adenovirus**
Causes pharyngoconjunctival fever and respiratory illness, with smudge cells.
Oral vesicles are not typical.

**B. Cytomegalovirus**
Gives **owl's-eye** inclusions, not painful vesicles with multinucleated giant cells.
It is mainly an opportunistic infection.

**D. Epstein-Barr virus**
Causes infectious mononucleosis and **oral hairy leukoplakia**.
It does not cause vesicles.

## Mind Capsule

> Tzanck smear: multinucleated giant cells = **HSV/VZV**; confirm with PCR or DFA.
