# CSF PCR for Viral DNA

**HSV encephalitis** (usually HSV-1, temporal lobe involvement) is confirmed by **HSV DNA PCR in CSF**, which is highly sensitive and specific. IV **acyclovir** should be started empirically without waiting for results.

## Trigger Point

> Fever, altered sensorium, seizures, neck rigidity with **HSV encephalitis** to be confirmed.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. IgM in CSF**
Antibodies appear late, so the test cannot support early treatment.
It is less sensitive than PCR.

**B. Tzanck smear of CSF**
Used for skin or mucosal vesicles, not CSF.
It is nonspecific and does not distinguish HSV from VZV.

**D. CSF culture on chick embryo lines**
Viral culture from CSF has poor sensitivity and is slow.
It is not used for diagnosis.

## Mind Capsule

> HSV encephalitis = **CSF PCR** (gold standard) + MRI temporal lobe changes; treat with acyclovir.
