# Single-Stranded Enveloped RNA Virus

The picture of **fever, rash beginning behind the ears, conjunctivitis, coryza, cough and Koplik spots** is **measles**. The measles virus is a **paramyxovirus**: **single-stranded, negative-sense, enveloped RNA virus** with helical symmetry.

## Trigger Point

> Rash starting **behind the ears** + Koplik spots = measles.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. DS enveloped RNA virus**
No human pathogen of this group is relevant here.
Double-stranded RNA viruses (reoviruses, rotavirus) are generally non-enveloped.

**C. SS naked RNA virus**
Describes picornaviruses (polio, hepatitis A) and caliciviruses.
Measles is enveloped.

**D. DS naked RNA virus**
Describes the reoviruses, e.g. **rotavirus**.
It is not measles.

## Mind Capsule

> Measles = paramyxovirus = **SS (–) enveloped RNA**; Koplik spots precede the rash; rotavirus = DS naked RNA.
