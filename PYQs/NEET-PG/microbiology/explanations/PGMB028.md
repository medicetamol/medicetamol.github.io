# Campylobacter jejuni

***Campylobacter jejuni*** is a **curved (comma/seagull-shaped) gram-negative rod** transmitted through **raw milk, undercooked poultry** and contaminated water. It causes **inflammatory (bloody) diarrhoea** with neutrophils in stool and is associated with **Guillain-Barré syndrome**.

## Trigger Point

> **Raw milk**, dysentery, **gram-negative curved rods** with polymorphs.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Clostridium perfringens**
A gram-positive spore-forming rod causing meat-related food poisoning.
It does not cause dysentery with curved rods.

**B. Staphylococcus aureus**
A gram-positive coccus causing preformed-toxin food poisoning.
It is not curved gram-negative.

**C. Vibrio parahaemolyticus**
A curved gram-negative rod from **raw seafood**, causing watery or mildly bloody diarrhoea.
The raw milk clue favours *Campylobacter*.

## Mind Capsule

> Curved gram-negative rod + raw milk/poultry + bloody diarrhoea = ***Campylobacter***; grows at 42 °C on Skirrow's medium.
