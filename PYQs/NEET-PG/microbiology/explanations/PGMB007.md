# Microscopic Agglutination Test (MAT)

The picture of **fever, jaundice, conjunctival suffusion and haematuria/renal involvement after sewage exposure** indicates **leptospirosis (Weil's disease)**. The **MAT**, using live leptospires, is the **gold standard serological test**. ELISA IgM is commonly used for screening.

## Trigger Point

> Sewage worker + jaundice + conjunctival injection + haematuria = leptospirosis.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Widal test**
Detects antibodies to *Salmonella* O and H antigens in typhoid.
It does not diagnose leptospirosis.

**C. Weil-Felix reaction**
Uses *Proteus* OX-19, OX-2 and OX-K antigens for rickettsial infections.
It has no role in leptospirosis despite the name Weil.

**D. Paul-Bunnell test**
Detects heterophile antibodies in infectious mononucleosis (EBV).
It is unrelated.

## Mind Capsule

> Leptospirosis = **MAT** (gold standard); dark-field microscopy in early disease; treat with penicillin or doxycycline.
