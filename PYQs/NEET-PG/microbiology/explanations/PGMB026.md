# Lipoteichoic Acid

**Group A streptococcus (*S. pyogenes*)** adheres to pharyngeal epithelium through **lipoteichoic acid** bound to **fibronectin** (with M protein and protein F assisting). The **capsule** (hyaluronic acid) acts mainly in antiphagocytosis.

## Trigger Point

> Component responsible for **attachment to fibronectin** on pharyngeal epithelium.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Lipoprotein**
A general bacterial membrane component.
It is not the primary adhesin of GAS.

**C. Capsule**
Hyaluronic acid capsule is **antiphagocytic** and resembles host tissue.
It does not mediate attachment to fibronectin.

**D. Flagella**
GAS is **non-motile** and has no flagella.
Flagella cannot be involved.

## Mind Capsule

> GAS adhesion = **lipoteichoic acid + protein F** (to fibronectin); anti-phagocytosis = M protein and capsule.
