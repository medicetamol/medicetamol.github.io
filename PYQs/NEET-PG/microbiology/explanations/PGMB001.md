# Dengue

**Dengue** (flavivirus, *Aedes aegypti*) presents with fever, arthralgia/myalgia, rash and **thrombocytopenia**. The platelet fall and capillary fragility give **petechiae and a prolonged bleeding time**, while clotting time (coagulation factors) stays normal. Delhi is an endemic area.

## Trigger Point

> Fever + arthralgia + petechiae + **platelets 20,000**, prolonged bleeding time, **normal clotting time** = platelet-type bleeding.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Malaria**
Causes fever with chills, anaemia and thrombocytopenia, but petechial rash with arthralgia is not typical.
Diagnosis needs a peripheral smear or RDT.

**C. Scrub typhus**
Rickettsial illness with an **eschar**, lymphadenopathy and a maculopapular rash.
Marked thrombocytopenia with bleeding is less typical.

**D. Typhoid**
Presents with step-ladder fever, relative bradycardia and **rose spots**.
Severe thrombocytopenia with bleeding is not characteristic.

## Mind Capsule

> Fever + arthralgia + petechiae + low platelets with normal clotting time = **dengue**; NS1 antigen (days 1-5), IgM ELISA later.
