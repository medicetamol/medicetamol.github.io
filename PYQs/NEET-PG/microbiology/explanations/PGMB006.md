# Pneumocystis jirovecii

***Pneumocystis jirovecii*** (a fungus) causes pneumonia in advanced HIV (CD4 < 200). It has no routine fungal culture growth. **GMS stain** shows **crescent-shaped or cup-shaped cysts**, and bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL) or induced sputum is the usual specimen.

## Trigger Point

> HIV + cough + **negative fungal culture** + GMS-stained **crescent-shaped cysts**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Paracoccidioides brasiliensis**
Shows **"pilot wheel"** multiple budding yeasts and grows in culture.
It is mainly found in South America.

**B. Histoplasma capsulatum**
Small intracellular yeasts within macrophages with narrow-based budding, and it grows in culture.
It does not form crescent-shaped cysts.

**C. Coccidioides immitis**
Forms large **spherules with endospores** and grows in culture.
It is not a crescent-shaped cyst.

## Mind Capsule

> HIV + not cultured + GMS crescent cysts = **PCP**; treat with cotrimoxazole, steroids if hypoxic.
