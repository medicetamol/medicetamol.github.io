# Leishmania tropica

**Cutaneous leishmaniasis** (oriental sore) in **West Rajasthan** is caused by ***Leishmania tropica***, transmitted by the **sandfly (Phlebotomus sergenti)**. Biopsy shows intracellular **amastigotes (LD bodies)** with a **nucleus and a rod-shaped kinetoplast**.

## Trigger Point

> Ulcer from West Rajasthan + organisms with **nucleus and kinetoplast**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Babesia**
Intra-erythrocytic parasite with a **Maltese cross** form, transmitted by ticks.
It has no kinetoplast and causes no skin ulcer.

**C. Trypanosoma**
Has a kinetoplast but causes sleeping sickness or Chagas disease, not a skin ulcer in Rajasthan.
Seen extracellularly in blood.

**D. Histoplasma**
A dimorphic fungus, with small intracellular yeasts in macrophages.
It lacks a kinetoplast.

## Mind Capsule

> Kinetoplast + intracellular in macrophages + skin ulcer = **Leishmania**; *L. donovani* = kala-azar, *L. tropica* = oriental sore.
