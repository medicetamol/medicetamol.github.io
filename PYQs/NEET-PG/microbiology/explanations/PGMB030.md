# Histoplasmosis

***Histoplasma capsulatum*** is a **dimorphic fungus**. In culture at 25 °C it shows hyphae with **tuberculate macroconidia** (round with finger-like projections). In tissue (37 °C), it is a **small, narrow-based budding yeast inside macrophages**. It presents with chronic cough, fever and weight loss, mimicking TB.

## Key Finding

> Branching hyphae with round spiky (tuberculate) macroconidia.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic cough + weight loss with **tuberculate macroconidia** and narrow-based budding.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Blastomycosis**
Shows **broad-based budding** thick-walled yeasts.
Its mould form lacks tuberculate macroconidia.

**C. Cryptococcosis**
A **capsulated** yeast (India ink positive) with narrow-based budding but no mould-phase conidia.
It is not dimorphic.

**D. Coccidioidomycosis**
Shows **spherules with endospores** in tissue, and arthroconidia in culture.
It has no tuberculate macroconidia.

## Mind Capsule

> **Tuberculate macroconidia** = *Histoplasma*; **spherules** = *Coccidioides*; **broad-based budding** = *Blastomyces*.
