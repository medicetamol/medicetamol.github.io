# Staphylococcus aureus

**Cream-filled pastries and custards** contaminated by food handlers carrying *S. aureus* allow the bacterium to produce **pre-formed heat-stable enterotoxin**. This causes **vomiting within 1-6 hours** (intoxication) and is self-limited.

## Trigger Point

> **Pastry** consumption + food poisoning.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. *B. cereus***
Linked to **fried rice**, with an emetic form (2-5 h) and diarrhoeal form (8-16 h).
The classic food clue differs.

**C. EIEC**
Enteroinvasive *E. coli* causes dysentery-like illness from contaminated food and water.
It does not fit a pastry history.

**D. Verotoxin *E. coli***
EHEC (O157:H7) is linked to **undercooked beef** and causes bloody diarrhoea and HUS.
It is not a preformed-toxin illness.

## Mind Capsule

> Pastry, custard, ice cream = ***S. aureus***; fried rice = *B. cereus*; undercooked beef = EHEC.
