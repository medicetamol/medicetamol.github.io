# Haemophilus influenzae (Satellitism)

***Haemophilus influenzae*** is a **pleomorphic gram-negative coccobacillus** requiring **X (haemin) and V (NAD)** factors. On blood agar it grows as **satellite colonies around *S. aureus*** (which releases V factor and lyses red cells). Type b is the main cause of meningitis in unvaccinated children.

## Trigger Point

> Child with meningitis + CSF **pleomorphic gram-negative bacilli**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Bacitracin sensitive**
Characteristic of **group A streptococci** (*S. pyogenes*).
It is not a feature of *Haemophilus*.

**B. Non-lactose fermenting colonies on MacConkey agar**
Seen in *Salmonella*, *Shigella* and *Pseudomonas*.
*H. influenzae* does not grow on MacConkey agar.

**D. Alpha haemolysis on sheep blood agar**
Seen with *S. pneumoniae* and viridans streptococci.
*Haemophilus* is non-haemolytic.

## Mind Capsule

> *Haemophilus* = **X + V factors**, **satellitism** around *S. aureus*, grows on chocolate agar.
