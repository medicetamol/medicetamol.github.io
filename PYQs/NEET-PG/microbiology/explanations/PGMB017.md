# Pseudomonas aeruginosa

***Pseudomonas aeruginosa*** is a slender **gram-negative bacillus** (pink rods on Gram stain). It is **oxidase positive** and makes **pyocyanin** (blue-green pigment) and a grape-like odour.

## Key Finding

> Many slender **pink rod-shaped** organisms = gram-negative bacilli.

## Trigger Point

> Gram stain showing pink slender rods.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Meningococcus**
A gram-negative **diplococcus** (kidney-bean shaped).
It is not rod-shaped.

**C. Streptococcus pyogenes**
A gram-positive coccus in chains.
It stains purple, not pink.

**D. Pneumococcus**
A gram-positive **lanceolate diplococcus**.
It is not a gram-negative rod.

## Mind Capsule

> Pink rods + oxidase-positive + blue-green pigment = **Pseudomonas**.
