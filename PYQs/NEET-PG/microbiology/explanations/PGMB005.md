# Cholangiocarcinoma

***Clonorchis sinensis*** (Chinese liver fluke) lives in the **bile ducts** and chronic infection causes biliary inflammation, hyperplasia and fibrosis, a recognised risk factor for **cholangiocarcinoma**. Infection is acquired by eating raw or undercooked freshwater fish.

## Trigger Point

> *Clonorchis sinensis* infection = chronic bile duct infestation.

## Parasites and Cancer

| Parasite | Associated cancer |
|---|---|
| *Clonorchis sinensis*, *Opisthorchis viverrini* | Cholangiocarcinoma |
| *Schistosoma haematobium* | Squamous cell carcinoma of bladder |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cervical cancer**
Caused by high-risk HPV types 16 and 18.
It has no link to liver flukes.

**C. Gastric carcinoma**
Linked to *H. pylori* and EBV.
It is not caused by flukes.

**D. Bladder carcinoma**
Associated with *Schistosoma haematobium*, not *Clonorchis*.
It is a different trematode.

## Mind Capsule

> Liver flukes (Clonorchis, Opisthorchis) = **cholangiocarcinoma**; *S. haematobium* = bladder SCC.
