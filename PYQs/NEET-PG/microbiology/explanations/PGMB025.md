# Sand Fly

The **kinetoplast** is a mitochondrial DNA structure found in trypanosomatids: *Leishmania* and *Trypanosoma*. Among these, the **sand fly (*Phlebotomus*)** transmits *Leishmania*, which has a kinetoplast in its amastigote and promastigote forms.

## Trigger Point

> Parasite with **kinetoplast** in one morphological form + the vector.

## Vectors of Haemoflagellates

| Parasite | Vector |
|---|---|
| *Leishmania* | Sand fly (*Phlebotomus*) |
| *T. brucei* | Tsetse fly |
| *T. cruzi* | Triatomine (reduviid) bug |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Tsetse fly**
Transmits *T. brucei* (African sleeping sickness), which also has a kinetoplast.
Since the question expects a single vector, the common one from India is sand fly.

**C. Female Anopheles mosquito**
Transmits *Plasmodium*, which has no kinetoplast.
It is the malaria vector.

**D. Triatomine bug**
Transmits *T. cruzi* (Chagas disease), which also has a kinetoplast.
It is not endemic in India.

## Mind Capsule

> Kinetoplast organisms = **Leishmania + Trypanosoma**; sand fly = *Leishmania* (India).
