# Nocardia asteroides

***Nocardia*** are **gram-positive, branching filamentous, weakly (modified) acid-fast bacteria**. They are aerobic, grow on **paraffin bait**, and cause **pulmonary disease, brain abscess and chronic meningitis**, especially in immunocompromised hosts. They stain positive with ZN using **1% H₂SO₄** (not 20%).

## Trigger Point

> Chronic meningitis + **gram-positive branching filaments**, **ZN positive**, **paraffin bait** growth.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Actinomyces israelii**
Gram-positive branching anaerobe with **sulphur granules**, but **not acid-fast**.
It causes cervicofacial and abdominal lesions.

**B. Mycobacterium tuberculosis**
Strongly acid-fast, non-branching, and grows on LJ medium.
It does not grow on paraffin bait.

**D. Cryptococcus neoformans**
A capsulated yeast seen on India ink, causing meningitis in AIDS.
It is not a filamentous bacterium.

## Mind Capsule

> Branching gram-positive + **weakly acid-fast** + paraffin bait = **Nocardia**; *Actinomyces* is not acid-fast.
