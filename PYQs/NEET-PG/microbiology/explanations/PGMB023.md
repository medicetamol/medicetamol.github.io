# Sodium Hypochlorite

**Blood spills** are managed by covering with absorbent material and applying **1% sodium hypochlorite (bleach)** (about 10,000 ppm), which is a high-level, rapid disinfectant for blood-borne viruses. Clean up after about 10-30 minutes, wearing gloves.

## Trigger Point

> Disinfectant for a **blood spill**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Ethyl alcohol**
Used for skin antisepsis and small surfaces; it evaporates quickly.
It is not sufficient for large blood spills.

**B. Chlorhexidine**
A skin and hand antiseptic.
It is inactivated by organic matter such as blood.

**C. Formaldehyde**
Used for fumigation and tissue fixation, and is toxic.
It is not used for spills.

## Mind Capsule

> Blood spill = **1% sodium hypochlorite**; HIV/HBV-safe cleaning with gloves.
