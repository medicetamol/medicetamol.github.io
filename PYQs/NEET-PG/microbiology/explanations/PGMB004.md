# Cytomegalovirus

**CMV** infected cells are enlarged (**cytomegaly**) with a large **intranuclear inclusion surrounded by a clear halo**, the **owl's-eye** appearance. These cells can be found in **urine, saliva and tissues** and support diagnosis of congenital or acquired CMV.

## Trigger Point

> **Owl's-eye** cells in urine in a child with fever and rash.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Epstein-Barr virus**
Causes infectious mononucleosis with **atypical lymphocytes** and a positive Paul-Bunnell test.
It has no owl's-eye inclusions.

**C. Herpes simplex virus**
Shows multinucleated giant cells with Cowdry type A inclusions on Tzanck smear.
It is not seen in urine.

**D. Toxoplasma gondii**
A protozoan with crescent-shaped tachyzoites and tissue cysts.
It does not form viral inclusions.

## Mind Capsule

> Owl's-eye intranuclear inclusion = **CMV**; Cowdry A inclusions = HSV/VZV.
