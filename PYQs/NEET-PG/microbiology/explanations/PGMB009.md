# Plasmodium falciparum

**HRP-2 (histidine-rich protein 2)** is produced only by ***Plasmodium falciparum***, so HRP-2 based **rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs)** detect *P. falciparum*. Combination RDTs also use **pLDH or aldolase** to detect other species.

## Trigger Point

> Rapid test positive for **HRP-2 antigen**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Plasmodium malariae**
Does not make HRP-2; detected by pan-pLDH or aldolase.
Causes quartan fever.

**C. Plasmodium vivax**
Detected by **pLDH (vivax-specific)** in RDTs.
It does not produce HRP-2.

**D. Plasmodium ovale**
Detected by pan-pLDH or aldolase.
It does not produce HRP-2.

## Mind Capsule

> **HRP-2 = falciparum**; pLDH and aldolase = other species (pan-malarial).
