# Strongyloides stercoralis

***Strongyloides stercoralis*** can **autoinfect** and, in patients on **steroids or other immunosuppression**, cause **hyperinfection** with filariform larvae spreading through the lungs (cough, wheeze, larvae in BAL). **Larva currens** produces migratory serpiginous urticarial rash.

## Trigger Point

> **Steroid use** + nocturnal cough + **chronic urticaria** + larvae in BAL.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Enterobius**
Causes perianal itching and does not migrate through lungs.
Diagnosed by cellophane tape test.

**C. Ancylostoma**
Hookworm with a lung migration phase, causing anaemia.
It does not autoinfect or cause hyperinfection.

**D. Capillaria philippinensis**
Causes chronic diarrhoea and malabsorption, and autoinfection in the gut.
It does not involve the lungs.

## Mind Capsule

> Steroids + Strongyloides = **hyperinfection syndrome**; larva currens = urticarial rash; treat with ivermectin.
