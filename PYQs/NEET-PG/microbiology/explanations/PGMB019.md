# Yellow Bag

Under **Biomedical Waste Management Rules**, **soiled items such as swabs, dressings and cotton contaminated with blood or body fluids** are placed in the **yellow bag** for incineration or deep burial.

## Trigger Point

> Colour bin for a **swab**.

## Biomedical Waste Colour Codes

| Colour | Contents |
|---|---|
| Yellow | Anatomical waste, soiled waste (swabs, dressings), expired medicines |
| Red | Contaminated recyclable plastic (tubing, catheters, syringes without needles) |
| White (puncture-proof container) | Sharps (needles, blades) |
| Blue (cardboard box) | Glassware, metallic implants |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Red bag**
For contaminated recyclable plastics such as IV tubes and catheters.
Swabs are not recyclable plastic.

**C. White bag**
Used for sharps in a translucent, puncture-proof container.
A swab is not a sharp.

**D. Blue bag**
For glassware and metallic body implants.
It does not take swabs.

## Mind Capsule

> Soiled swabs and dressings = **yellow**; sharps = white; plastics = red; glass = blue.
