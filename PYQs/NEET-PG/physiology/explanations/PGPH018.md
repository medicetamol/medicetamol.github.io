# Kearns-Sayre Syndrome

**Mitochondrial disorders** are inherited through the **maternal line**, since the zygote's mitochondria come from the ovum. **Kearns-Sayre** results from mitochondrial DNA deletions (sporadic or maternal) and presents with **progressive external ophthalmoplegia, retinitis pigmentosa and cardiac conduction block**.

## Trigger Point

> Mitochondrial inheritance disorder.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Williams syndrome**
A microdeletion at **7q11.23** involving the elastin gene, with supravalvular aortic stenosis and "elfin" facies.
It is a contiguous gene deletion, not mitochondrial.

**C. Achondroplasia**
An **autosomal dominant** disorder from an FGFR3 gain-of-function mutation.
It is nuclear, not mitochondrial.

**D. Cystic fibrosis**
An **autosomal recessive** disorder of the CFTR gene on chromosome 7.
It is nuclear in origin.

## Mind Capsule

> Mitochondrial = maternal inheritance: LHON, MELAS, MERRF, **Kearns-Sayre**, Leigh disease.
