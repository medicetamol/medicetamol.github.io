# Intracellular Fluid (ICF)

**ICF** has **K⁺ as the major cation (~140 mEq/L)** and very low Na⁺ and Cl⁻, with proteins and phosphates as major anions. This matches Na 10, K 140 and Cl 15. **ECF** has Na⁺ ~142, Cl⁻ ~103, K⁺ ~4.

## Trigger Point

> Na 10, **K 140**, Cl 15.

## Composition of Body Fluids

| Ion (mEq/L) | ECF | ICF |
|---|---|---|
| Na⁺ | 142 | 10 |
| K⁺ | 4 | 140 |
| Cl⁻ | 103 | 4 |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Interstitial fluid**
Part of the ECF, with high Na⁺ and Cl⁻ and low K⁺.
It resembles plasma but has less protein.

**B. ECF**
Na⁺ is the main cation, at about 140 mEq/L.
The given high K⁺ rules it out.

**D. Total body fluid**
It is a mixture of ECF and ICF and not a distinct compartment with its own composition.
The values given are specific to the ICF.

## Mind Capsule

> ICF = **K⁺ high inside**; ECF = Na⁺ and Cl⁻ high outside.
