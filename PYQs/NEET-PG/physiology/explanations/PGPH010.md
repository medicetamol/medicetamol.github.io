# Adenylyl Cyclase

**β2 receptors** are **Gs-coupled**, activating **adenylyl cyclase**, raising **cAMP** and PKA activity. This relaxes bronchial and uterine smooth muscle and causes vasodilatation in skeletal muscle.

## Trigger Point

> Secondary messenger of β2 receptors.

## Receptor Signalling

| Receptor | G protein | Second messenger |
|---|---|---|
| β1, β2, β3, D1, H2 | Gs | ↑ cAMP |
| α2, M2, D2 | Gi | ↓ cAMP |
| α1, M1, M3, H1 | Gq | IP3 / DAG, ↑ Ca²⁺ |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Tyrosine kinase receptors**
Used by insulin and growth factor receptors.
They are enzyme-linked, not G-protein-coupled as in β2.

**C. TGF-beta receptors**
They are serine/threonine kinase receptors that signal through SMAD proteins.
They are unrelated to adrenergic receptors.

**D. Cytokine receptors**
They signal through the JAK-STAT pathway.
Adrenergic receptors do not use it.

## Mind Capsule

> β2 = Gs = ↑ cAMP = bronchodilatation; α1 = Gq; α2 = Gi.
