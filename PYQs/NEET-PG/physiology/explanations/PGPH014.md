# Stress Incontinence

**Stress urinary incontinence** is leakage of urine when **intra-abdominal pressure rises** (cough, sneeze, straining) without an urge to void. It is due to a **weak urethral sphincter or pelvic floor support**, common in multiparous and elderly women.

## Trigger Point

> Dribbling of urine **only on coughing and straining**.

## Types of Incontinence

| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| Stress | Leak on cough or strain |
| Urge | Sudden urge, detrusor overactivity |
| Overflow | Constant dribbling, full bladder |
| Neurogenic | Underlying neurological lesion |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Urge incontinence**
Leakage is preceded by a sudden, strong urge, due to detrusor overactivity.
There is no urge in this patient.

**C. Overflow incontinence**
Continuous dribbling from a chronically distended bladder, with outlet obstruction or atonic bladder.
It is not triggered by coughing.

**D. Neurogenic bladder**
Due to a spinal or peripheral nerve lesion, with retention or reflex voiding.
There is no neurological history in this patient.

## Mind Capsule

> Leak on cough = **stress**; leak with urge = **urge**; constant dribble with full bladder = **overflow**.
