# Anopheles

**Anopheles larvae have no siphon** and lie **parallel to the water surface**, using spiracles on the abdomen. This is the classic way to identify them.

## Trigger Point

> Larva with **no siphon**, resting **parallel to the water surface**.

## Mosquito Larvae

| Genus | Larva |
|---|---|
| Anopheles | No siphon, parallel to surface |
| Culex | Siphon present, hangs at an angle |
| Aedes | Short siphon, hangs at an angle |
| Mansonia | Siphon adapted to pierce aquatic plants |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Culex**
Has a siphon and hangs head-down at an angle.

**C. Aedes**
Has a short siphon and hangs at an angle.

**D. Mansonia**
Its siphon pierces roots of aquatic plants to get air.

## Mind Capsule

> **No siphon + parallel = Anopheles.**
