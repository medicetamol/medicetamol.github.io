# PHC

**School health services** are delivered through the **Primary Health Centre (PHC)**, where the medical officer and health team examine school children and arrange referral.

## Trigger Point

> "School health service is at which level?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. District**
Acts as the referral level for school health problems, not the level of delivery.

**B. Subcentre**
The first contact point for village health work, but it is not staffed by a medical officer for school examinations.

**C. Subdistrict**
An administrative and first-referral level; school health services are not organised here.

## Mind Capsule

> School health service = **PHC level** (medical officer + team).
