# Lead Time Bias

**Screening detects disease earlier**, so survival from the time of diagnosis looks longer even though the **time of death is unchanged**. This is **lead time bias**, shown here by higher 5-year survival with unchanged mortality.

## Trigger Point

> **Increased 5-year survival** but **no change in mortality**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Survival bias**
Arises when only survivors are studied or counted.

**C. Berksonian bias**
A selection bias from hospital-based admission rates.

**D. Detection bias**
Occurs when exposure leads to more diagnostic scrutiny of the outcome.

## Mind Capsule

> Screening + survival up + mortality unchanged = **lead time bias**.
