# Cluster Randomised Controlled Trial

When **whole units (PHCs)** rather than individuals are allocated to **intervention or standard care**, the design is a **cluster randomised controlled trial**. It suits community interventions where individual allocation is not practical.

## Trigger Point

> **20 PHCs vs 20 PHCs** allocated to different care.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cross-sectional study**
A one-time survey of exposure and outcome, with no intervention.

**B. Case-control study**
Starts from diseased and non-diseased people, then looks back at exposure.

**C. Quasi-experimental study**
An intervention study without randomisation; the key assumes the PHCs were randomly allocated.

## Mind Capsule

> Intervention allocated to **groups/clusters** = **cluster RCT**.
