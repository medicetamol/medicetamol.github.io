# Xenopsylla (Rat Flea)

**Buboes** point to **bubonic plague** caused by *Yersinia pestis*. The plague bacillus is carried from rats to humans by the **rat flea** (*Xenopsylla cheopis*, the main vector in India), which regurgitates bacilli after feeding on an infected rodent.

## Trigger Point

> Outbreak of **buboes** in a community.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Tse tse fly**
Vector of African trypanosomiasis (sleeping sickness).
It has no role in plague.

**C. Human flea**
*Pulex irritans* is a minor or occasional vector and not the main plague vector.
Rat fleas drive epidemics because plague is primarily an epizootic of rodents.

**D. Sand fly**
Transmits kala-azar, sandfly fever and Carrion's disease.
Not associated with buboes in plague.

## Mind Capsule

> Bubo + rodent die-off = plague = **Xenopsylla cheopis** (rat flea).
