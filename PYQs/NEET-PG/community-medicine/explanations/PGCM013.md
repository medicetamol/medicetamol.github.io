# Severe

An **AQI of 401-500** is classed as **Severe**. The 470 reading in this question falls in that band.

## Trigger Point

> **AQI value of 470** after Diwali.

## Air Quality Index Categories (India)

| AQI | Category |
|---|---|
| 0-50 | Good |
| 51-100 | Satisfactory |
| 101-200 | Moderate |
| 201-300 | Poor |
| 301-400 | Very poor |
| 401-500 | Severe |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Poor**
Corresponds to 201-300.

**B. Very poor**
Corresponds to 301-400.

**D. Moderate**
Corresponds to 101-200.

## Mind Capsule

> AQI above 400 = **Severe**.
