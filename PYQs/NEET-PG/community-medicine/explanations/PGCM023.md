# Retrospective Cohort Study

Groups are formed by **exposure** (dye handlers versus clerks) and followed for the **outcome** (bladder cancer). Because both exposure and outcome have already occurred and data come from **past records**, this is a **retrospective (historical) cohort study**.

## Trigger Point

> Exposed vs non-exposed workers, **years of occupation noted from records**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Prospective cohort study**
Follows people forward in real time from exposure to outcome.

**C. Case-control study**
Starts with people who have the disease (cases) and compares with controls.

**D. Intervention and response**
The investigator assigns the exposure, which is not the case here.

## Mind Capsule

> Exposure groups + outcome already happened + records = **retrospective cohort**.
