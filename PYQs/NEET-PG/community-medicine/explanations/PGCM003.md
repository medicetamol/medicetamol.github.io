# Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)

The **Global Hunger Index (GHI)** combines four indicators: **undernourishment** in the population, **child stunting**, **child wasting** and **under-5 mortality**. IMR is not one of them.

## Trigger Point

> "Global Hunger index does **not** include..."

## Components of the Global Hunger Index

| Indicator | Weight in GHI |
|---|---|
| Undernourishment (population) | 1/3 |
| Under-5 mortality | 1/3 |
| Child stunting | 1/6 |
| Child wasting | 1/6 |

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Under 5 mortality rate**
Included as the child mortality component.

**C. Child mortality rate**
This is the under-5 mortality component of the index.

**D. Child undernutrition**
Included through child **stunting and wasting**.

## Mind Capsule

> GHI = undernourishment + stunting + wasting + **under-5 mortality** (IMR is not used).
