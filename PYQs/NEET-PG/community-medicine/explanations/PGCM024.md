# Tracking of Blood Pressure

**Tracking** is the tendency of a person to **keep their rank (percentile) of BP over time**, so children with low BP stay low and those with high BP stay high into adulthood.

## Trigger Point

> BP **followed from childhood to adulthood**; low stays low, high stays high.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Rule of halves**
Hypertension epidemiology: about half are undetected, half of those detected are untreated, and so on.

**C. STEPwise approach**
WHO method for surveillance of NCD risk factors.

**D. Primordial approach**
Prevention of emergence of risk factors in the population.

## Mind Capsule

> Same BP rank from childhood to adulthood = **tracking**.
