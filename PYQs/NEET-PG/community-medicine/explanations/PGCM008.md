# Measles/MMR (Live Vaccine)

Recurrent **fungal (yeast) and viral infections from 3 months of age** suggest a **primary T-cell or combined immunodeficiency (such as SCID)**. **Live vaccines are contraindicated** because the attenuated organism can cause disseminated disease. Of the options, only **measles/MMR** is live.

## Trigger Point

> Recurrent **yeast + viral infections since 3 months** and immune work-up pending.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. TT/Td**
Toxoid (inactivated), safe in immunodeficiency.

**C. DPT**
Contains toxoids and killed pertussis components, so it is safe.

**D. Killed IPV**
Inactivated polio vaccine is safe; it is the **live OPV** that is avoided.

## Mind Capsule

> Immunodeficiency = **no live vaccines** (BCG, MMR, OPV, varicella, rotavirus); killed and toxoid vaccines are safe.
