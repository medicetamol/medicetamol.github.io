# 100 mg per 1000 kcal

For the **population strategy** against coronary heart disease, dietary **cholesterol** is recommended to stay **below 100 mg per 1000 kcal per day**.

## Trigger Point

> Recommended **dietary cholesterol** limit under the population strategy for CAD.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 200 mg/1000 kcal**
Higher than the recommended limit.

**C. 400 mg/1000 kcal**
Several times above the target.

**D. 500 mg/1000 kcal**
Far above the recommendation.

## Mind Capsule

> Dietary cholesterol **under 100 mg per 1000 kcal**.
