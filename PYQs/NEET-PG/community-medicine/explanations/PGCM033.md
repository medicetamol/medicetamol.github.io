# Isolate for 6 Days

A **fever with headache and pleomorphic (crops of) rash sparing palms and soles** is **varicella (chickenpox)**. The next step is to **isolate** cases until all vesicles have crusted, about **6 days** from rash onset. **VZIG** is for exposed high-risk contacts, not for cases.

## Trigger Point

> Hostel outbreak, **pleomorphic rash sparing palms and soles** = varicella.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Isolate 6 days after acyclovir, then VZIG**
VZIG is for susceptible exposed contacts at risk, not for cases, and acyclovir is not routine for all cases.

**B. Isolate 12 days, acyclovir, VZIG within 48 hours**
The isolation period is too long and the treatment plan is incorrect.

**D. Only VZIG**
Does not control spread; cases require isolation.

## Mind Capsule

> Varicella case = **isolate until lesions crust (about 6 days)**; VZIG = exposed high-risk contacts.
