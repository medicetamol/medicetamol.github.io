# Td Vaccine

Under the **school health programme** in India, the **Td (tetanus-diphtheria) vaccine** is given to children at **10 years and 16 years**, replacing the older TT.

## Trigger Point

> 10-year-old school child, vaccine under the **school health programme**.

## Td/TT in the National Immunization Schedule

| Group | Td schedule |
|---|---|
| Pregnant women | Td-1 early in pregnancy, Td-2 after 4 weeks (or Td booster if immunised in a recent pregnancy) |
| Children | Td at **10 years** and **16 years** |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. BCG**
Given at birth (up to 1 year); not a school-age vaccine.

**B. MMR**
Not part of India's universal schedule, which uses **MR** at 9-12 months and 16-24 months.

**D. DPT**
Boosters are at 16-24 months and 5-6 years; at 10 years Td replaces it.

## Mind Capsule

> School age: **Td at 10 and 16 years**.
