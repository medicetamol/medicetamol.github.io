# Alum and Lime

The **Nalgonda technique** removes **fluoride** from water by **flocculation and sedimentation** using **alum and lime** (with bleaching powder for disinfection), followed by filtration. It is used for **defluoridation** in endemic fluorosis areas.

## Trigger Point

> "**Nalgonda technique** for water purification".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Alum and gypsum**
Gypsum is not part of the technique.

**B. Alum and charcoal**
Charcoal is not used; Nalgonda relies on coagulation.

**D. Charcoal and lime**
Lacks alum, the coagulant, so it cannot be the technique.

## Mind Capsule

> Nalgonda = **alum + lime** for **defluoridation**.
