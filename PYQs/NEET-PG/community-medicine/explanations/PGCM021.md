# Susceptible to Chickenpox

A **negative varicella IgG** means **no immunity**. The woman is therefore **susceptible to primary varicella (chickenpox)**. Because she is pregnant and exposed, **VZIG** is advised, ideally as early as possible and within about 10 days.

## Trigger Point

> Pregnant, exposed to varicella, **serum antibodies negative**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Susceptible to zoster**
Zoster comes from reactivation of **latent** virus in someone who has had varicella; she has never been infected.

**C. Immune to chickenpox**
Immunity would need positive IgG.

**D. Immune to zoster**
Zoster immunity is not conferred by a negative serology.

## Mind Capsule

> VZV IgG negative = **non-immune**; exposed pregnant woman = **VZIG**.
