# Seal the Cracks and Close the Windows

After release of toxic fumes, residents should **shelter indoors**: **close doors and windows** and **seal gaps** to keep the fumes out, and follow instructions from authorities.

## Trigger Point

> **Toxic fumes** released after an industrial explosion.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Use the telephone and call friends**
Keeps emergency lines busy and does not protect against fumes.

**C. Open the window for ventilation**
Lets toxic gas into the house.

**D. Use a mask**
An ordinary mask does not filter toxic gases; only proper respirators do.

## Mind Capsule

> Toxic fumes outside = **shelter in place** (seal cracks, close windows).
