# CRP Raises the Risk

The relative risks rise **stepwise (1.5, 1.7, 1.8)** as CRP levels rise, a **dose-response (biological gradient)** pattern. This supports a **positive association**: higher CRP is linked to higher risk of MI or cancer.

## Trigger Point

> **RR values increase** with increasing CRP readings.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. CRP has no relationship**
Would need an RR of about 1 across all levels.

**C. CRP decreases and disease decreases**
An inverse pattern would show RR falling below 1 as CRP rises.

**D. No association in the first interval**
The first value is 0.5, not 1, so it is not a null value; and the trend afterwards is clear.

## Mind Capsule

> RR above 1 and rising with exposure = **dose-response**, supporting causation.
