# Cobalt Therapy Units

The **Colombo Plan** is a regional cooperative economic and technical-assistance scheme. In cancer control, its contribution recognised in this question is help in **setting up cobalt (teletherapy) units**.

## Trigger Point

> "Cancer treatment according to the **Colombo plan**".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. PET scan units**
A newer diagnostic technology that is not the plan's recognised contribution.

**B. Human resource strengthening**
General capacity building; not the specific item tied to cancer treatment in this plan.

**C. Chemotherapy units**
Drug treatment facilities were not the focus.

## Mind Capsule

> Colombo Plan + cancer = **cobalt units**.
