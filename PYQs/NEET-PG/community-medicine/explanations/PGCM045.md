# ICDS

**ICDS (Integrated Child Development Services)** targets **children under 6 years**, pregnant and lactating women, with supplementary nutrition, immunisation, health check-ups, referral and pre-school education through the **anganwadi**.

## Trigger Point

> 3-year-old with rickets-like features; "scheme addressing nutritional deficiencies in children **under 6**".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Mid-Day Meal Scheme**
Meant for school-going children, not those under 6.

**B. Anemia Mukt Bharat**
Focuses on anaemia and iron-folic acid supplementation, not general nutrition for under-6s.

**D. National Nutritional Deficiency Control Programme**
A generic title; the umbrella scheme for under-6 nutrition is ICDS.

## Mind Capsule

> Under 6 years = **ICDS**; school children = mid-day meal.
