# Live Attenuated, Subcutaneous (JE Vaccine)

An **encephalitis outbreak** in India points to **Japanese encephalitis**. The vaccine in the universal immunisation programme is the **live attenuated SA-14-14-2** vaccine, given **subcutaneously** (9 months and 16-24 months in endemic districts).

## Trigger Point

> **Outbreak of encephalitis** + "according to the universal immunization schedule".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Killed and subcutaneous**
Inactivated JE vaccines exist but are not in the universal schedule.

**C. Live and intramuscular**
The live JE vaccine is not given intramuscularly.

**D. Killed and intramuscular**
Vero cell-derived killed JE vaccines are given intramuscularly, but they are not the programme vaccine.

## Mind Capsule

> UIP JE vaccine = **live (SA-14-14-2) + subcutaneous**.
