# Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA)

The **RDA** is the average daily intake that meets the needs of **97-98% of healthy people** in a life-stage and gender group. It is calculated as **EAR + 2 SD**.

## Trigger Point

> Intake sufficient for "nearly all (**97-98%**)" healthy individuals.

## Nutrient Reference Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EAR | Meets the needs of 50% of the group |
| RDA | EAR + 2 SD, covers 97-98% |
| Adequate Intake | Used when an EAR cannot be set |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Adequate intake**
Estimated intake of a healthy group, used when the EAR cannot be determined.

**B. Dietary goal**
A population-level target to lower chronic disease risk, not an individual requirement.

**C. Estimated average requirement**
Meets the needs of only 50% of the group.

## Mind Capsule

> **RDA = EAR + 2 SD** (97-98%); EAR covers 50%.
