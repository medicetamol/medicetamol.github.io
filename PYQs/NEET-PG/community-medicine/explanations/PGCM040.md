# Randomisation, Manipulation, Follow-up, Assessment

In an **RCT**, subjects are first **randomised** to groups, then the **intervention is applied (manipulation)**, the groups are **followed up**, and finally the **outcome is assessed**.

## Trigger Point

> "Correct **sequence** of steps in a randomized controlled trial".

## Correct Order

1. Randomisation
2. Manipulation (intervention)
3. Follow-up
4. Assessment of outcome

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Follow-up, manipulation, assessment, randomisation**
Follow-up cannot precede the intervention.

**C. Assessment, randomisation, follow-up, manipulation**
Assessment of outcome comes last, and manipulation comes before follow-up.

**D. Manipulation, assessment, follow-up, randomisation**
Allocation (randomisation) cannot come after the intervention.

## Mind Capsule

> **R**andomise, **M**anipulate, **F**ollow up, **A**ssess.
