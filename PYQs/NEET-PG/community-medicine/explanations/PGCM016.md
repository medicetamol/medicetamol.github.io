# Outcome

In **Donabedian's framework** of evaluation, **outcome** (recovery, case fatality, complications) shows the **effect of clinical management** on patients. It is therefore the best determinant for assessing how well clinical care works.

## Trigger Point

> "Most important determinant for assessing **clinical management**" in evaluation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Input**
The resources put in (staff, drugs, money); shows capacity, not results.

**B. Process**
The activities carried out in delivering care; shows how care is given, not what it achieved.

**C. Structure**
Facilities, equipment and organisation of the centre; the setting rather than the result.

## Mind Capsule

> Structure = resources; process = activities; **outcome = effectiveness of care**.
