# SGLT-1

In the small intestine, **SGLT-1** carries **glucose with sodium** across the brush border. This coupled uptake drags water along, which is the physiological basis of **oral rehydration solution (ORS)**.

## Trigger Point

> Glucose absorption in the intestine when **ORS** is given.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. SGLT-2**
Reabsorbs glucose in the renal proximal tubule.

**C. GLP-1**
An incretin hormone and its receptor, not an absorptive transporter.

**D. GLUT-1**
Facilitated glucose carrier in red cells and the blood-brain barrier; the intestinal exit step is by GLUT-2.

## Mind Capsule

> ORS works because of **Na⁺-glucose co-transport (SGLT-1)** in the gut.
