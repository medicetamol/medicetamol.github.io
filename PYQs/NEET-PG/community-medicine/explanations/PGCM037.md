# Passive Surveillance

Routine **reports of cases or deaths sent by hospitals** to the health system, without the health team searching for them, constitute **passive surveillance**.

## Trigger Point

> A **hospital death report** of a COVID-19 patient.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Active surveillance**
The health team actively seeks cases (house visits, record checks).

**C. Sentinel surveillance**
Data from a few designated reporting sites.

**D. Syndromic surveillance**
Uses symptom clusters before a confirmed diagnosis.

## Mind Capsule

> Routine facility reporting = **passive**; team searches for cases = active.
