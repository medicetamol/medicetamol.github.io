# Years Lost Due to Premature Death

**Years of potential life lost (YPLL)** counts the years a person would have lived had they not died **before a reference age** (expected life span). It measures the burden of **premature mortality** only.

## Trigger Point

> "The years of potential life lost..."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Years lost to morbidity**
Illness burden is captured by YLD (years lived with disability) in DALY, not by YPLL.

**C. Years lost to disability**
Also the YLD component of DALY.

**D. Years lost to poor quality of life**
Quality-of-life adjustment belongs to indices such as QALY.

## Mind Capsule

> YPLL = **premature death** only; DALY = YLL + YLD.
