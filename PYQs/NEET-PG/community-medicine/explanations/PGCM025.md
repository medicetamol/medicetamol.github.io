# ASHA

The **ASHA** is the **village-level community health worker** of the NRHM/NHM who counsels women on **nutrition, antenatal care and institutional delivery** and dispels myths. Her avoidance of fruit rich in vitamin A (papaya, mango), with night blindness, needs this community-level counselling.

## Trigger Point

> Pregnant woman avoiding fruit due to a **myth** (abortifacient) + night blindness; "primary duty to **counsel**".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. ANM**
Provides clinical care at the sub-centre (ANC check-ups, immunisation).

**B. AWW**
Runs supplementary nutrition and pre-school education under ICDS.

**C. Trained birth attendant**
Conducts deliveries; not responsible for community counselling.

## Mind Capsule

> Community counselling and myths = **ASHA**.
