# Infant Mortality Rate

**IMR** is the most **sensitive indicator** of health care availability, utilisation and effectiveness, because infant deaths respond quickly to nutrition, sanitation, immunisation and medical care.

## Trigger Point

> "Sensitive indicator" of **availability, utilization and effectiveness** of health care.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Maternal mortality rate**
Reflects obstetric care specifically and is more difficult to measure.

**C. Immunization coverage**
A programme coverage indicator, not an overall outcome indicator.

**D. DALY**
A composite measure of disease burden.

## Mind Capsule

> Most sensitive index of overall health care = **IMR**.
