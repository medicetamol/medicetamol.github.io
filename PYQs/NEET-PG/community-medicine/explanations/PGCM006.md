# Niacin Deficiency (Pellagra)

**Pellagra** results from **niacin (or tryptophan) deficiency**. In maize eaters, niacin is present in a **bound, unavailable form** and maize protein (zein) is poor in tryptophan. It presents with **photosensitive dermatitis**, **glossitis**, diarrhoea and dementia.

## Trigger Point

> **Sun-exposed skin rash + red tongue** in a farmer whose staple is **maize**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Folic acid deficiency**
Gives megaloblastic anaemia and glossitis, but not a photosensitive rash.
It is not linked to a maize diet.

**C. Vitamin C deficiency**
Scurvy causes perifollicular haemorrhages, bleeding gums and poor wound healing.
No photosensitive dermatitis.

**D. Vitamin K deficiency**
Causes bleeding due to defective clotting factors.
No skin photosensitivity or glossitis.

## Mind Capsule

> Maize diet + photosensitive dermatitis + glossitis = **pellagra (niacin)**.

## Memory Hook

> **4 Ds of pellagra**: Dermatitis, Diarrhoea, Dementia, (Death).
