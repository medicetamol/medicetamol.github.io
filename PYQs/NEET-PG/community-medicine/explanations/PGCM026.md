# Grey Kit

**Cervicitis** (minimal discharge with cervical erosion) is treated by **Kit 1 (Grey)**, which contains **cefixime + azithromycin** and covers gonococcal and chlamydial infection.

## Trigger Point

> Minimal discharge + **cervical erosions** = cervicitis.

## NACO Colour-Coded STI/RTI Kits (selected)

| Kit | Syndrome |
|---|---|
| Grey (Kit 1) | Urethral discharge, cervicitis |
| Green (Kit 2) | Vaginitis (vaginal discharge) |
| White (Kit 3) | Genital ulcer (syphilis) |
| Red | Genital herpes |
| Yellow (Kit 6) | Lower abdominal pain (PID) |
| Black | Inguinal bubo |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Green**
For vaginitis (trichomonas, bacterial vaginosis, candida), not cervicitis.

**B. Red**
For herpetic genital ulcers (acyclovir).

**D. Yellow**
For lower abdominal pain (PID).

## Mind Capsule

> Discharge from the urethra or **cervix** = **Grey**; vaginal discharge = Green.
