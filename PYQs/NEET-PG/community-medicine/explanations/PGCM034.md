# CBNAAT

In a presumptive TB patient with **negative sputum smears** and a **suggestive chest X-ray**, current **NTEP** guidance is a **nucleic acid amplification test (CBNAAT/TrueNat)**, which gives a rapid diagnosis and **rifampicin resistance** at the same time.

## Trigger Point

> **Smear-negative** + chest X-ray suggestive of TB; "recent NTEP guidelines".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Repeat sputum smears**
Smear is insensitive and gives no resistance information.

**C. Line probe assay**
Used for confirmation of drug resistance (first and second-line), not as the first test here.

**D. Wait for culture**
Takes weeks; treatment should not be delayed in a sick patient.

## Mind Capsule

> Presumptive TB, smear negative = **CBNAAT** (diagnosis + rifampicin resistance).
