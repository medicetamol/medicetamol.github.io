# Single-Dose Tetanus Toxoid

For a **clean, minor wound** in a person with a **full primary course** whose last dose was **10 years ago**, a **single booster of tetanus toxoid (Td)** is enough. No immunoglobulin is needed.

## Trigger Point

> **Clean-cut, non-bleeding wound** + **full course 10 years ago**.

## Tetanus Prophylaxis in Wound Management

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Clean minor wound, full course, last dose under 10 years | None |
| Clean minor wound, full course, last dose 10 years or more | Td booster |
| Other (dirty) wound, full course, last dose 5 years or more | Td booster |
| Unknown or incomplete immunisation | Td course, plus TIG for dirty wounds |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. TIG and full course**
For unimmunised persons with a contaminated wound.

**B. TIG only**
Passive cover alone is not the approach; vaccination is also needed in those who qualify.

**D. Full course**
For unimmunised or incompletely immunised persons.

## Mind Capsule

> Clean minor wound + completed course + 10 years = **booster only**.
