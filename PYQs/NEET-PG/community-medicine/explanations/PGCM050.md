# Generation Time

**Generation time** is the interval between **acquiring an infection and reaching maximum infectivity**.

## Trigger Point

> Interval from infection to **maximum infectivity**.

## Time Intervals in Infectious Disease

| Term | Interval |
|---|---|
| Incubation period | Infection to onset of symptoms |
| Generation time | Infection to maximum infectivity |
| Serial interval | Onset of primary case to onset of secondary case |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Communicable period**
The whole time during which an infectious agent can be transmitted.

**C. Incubation period**
Infection to onset of symptoms.

**D. Serial interval**
Onset of the primary case to onset of the secondary case.

## Mind Capsule

> Infection → maximum infectivity = **generation time**.
