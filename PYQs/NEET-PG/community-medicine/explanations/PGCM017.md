# Psychomotor Domain

Learning the **steps of hand hygiene** is acquiring a **skill** performed with the hands, which is the **psychomotor domain** of learning (Bloom's taxonomy).

## Trigger Point

> Child learning the **steps** of hand hygiene.

## Domains of Learning

| Domain | Concerns |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | Knowledge and understanding |
| Affective | Attitudes and values |
| Psychomotor | Skills and actions |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cognitive**
Concerns knowing facts, not performing a technique.

**C. Affective**
Concerns attitude or feelings towards hygiene.

**D. Affective and cognitive**
Neither domain covers the practical skill being learnt.

## Mind Capsule

> Knowledge = cognitive; attitude = affective; **skill = psychomotor**.
