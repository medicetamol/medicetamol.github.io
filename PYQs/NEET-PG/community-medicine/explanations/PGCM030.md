# Beta-Oxalyl-Amino-Alanine (BOAA)

**BOAA (β-ODAP)** is the excitotoxic amino acid in **khesari dal** (*Lathyrus sativus*). Chronic consumption, especially in famine or crop failure, causes **neurolathyrism**: **spastic paraparesis** with upper motor neuron signs.

## Trigger Point

> Poor farmer after crop failures + **spastic paraparesis**, UMN signs.

## Food Toxins and Diseases

| Toxin | Disease or effect |
|---|---|
| BOAA (ODAP) | Neurolathyrism |
| Aflatoxin | Liver damage, hepatocellular carcinoma |
| Ergot alkaloids | Ergotism |
| Sanguinarine | Epidemic dropsy |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Aflatoxin**
From *Aspergillus* on stored grains and groundnuts; hepatotoxic and carcinogenic.

**C. Ergot alkaloids**
From *Claviceps* on millets; cause gangrenous or convulsive ergotism.

**D. Fusarium toxin**
Mainly gastrointestinal and haematological toxicity, not spastic paraparesis.

## Mind Capsule

> Crop failure + spastic paraparesis = **BOAA** (khesari dal).
