# 60 mg Iron + 500 µg Folic Acid

For adolescents aged **10-19 years**, the weekly **blue IFA tablet** under the current NHM/**Anemia Mukt Bharat** programme contains **60 mg elemental iron and 500 µg folic acid**. The older WIFS tablet had 100 mg iron, so the figure is guideline-dependent.

## Trigger Point

> WIFS tablet composition for **10-19 years**.

## IFA Supplementation (Anemia Mukt Bharat)

| Group | Preparation |
|---|---|
| 6-59 months | IFA syrup (20 mg iron + 100 µg folic acid), twice weekly |
| 5-9 years | Pink tablet (45 mg iron + 400 µg folic acid), weekly |
| 10-19 years | Blue tablet (60 mg iron + 500 µg folic acid), weekly |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 60 mg + 100 µg**
Folic acid is too low for this age group.

**B. 100 mg + 500 µg**
This was the older WIFS iron dose, now revised.

**C. 100 mg + 100 µg**
Neither value matches the programme.

## Mind Capsule

> Adolescent weekly IFA (blue) = **60 mg iron + 500 µg folic acid**.
