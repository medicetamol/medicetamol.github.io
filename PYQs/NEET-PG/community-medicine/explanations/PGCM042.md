# 42 UPHCs

Under **NUHM**, one **Urban Primary Health Centre** is planned for about **50,000 slum population**.

## Trigger Point

> 70 lakh population, 30% in slums; "UPHCs required for the slum population".

## Working

1. Slum population = 70,00,000 × 30% = 21,00,000
2. UPHCs = 21,00,000 ÷ 50,000 = **42**

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 22**
Would serve only 11 lakh people.

**B. 32**
Would serve only 16 lakh people.

**D. 52**
Would serve 26 lakh, more than the slum population.

## Mind Capsule

> NUHM: **1 UPHC per 50,000 slum population**.
