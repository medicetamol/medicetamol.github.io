# BCG

A child with HIV and **CD4 count 50** has **severe immunosuppression**. **Live vaccines such as BCG** can cause disseminated disease and are avoided. The other options are inactivated or toxoid vaccines.

## Trigger Point

> **HIV** child with **CD count 50**; "which vaccine is avoided".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Rabies**
An inactivated vaccine, safe in HIV (immune response may be weaker).

**B. TT**
A toxoid, safe.

**C. DPT**
Toxoid and killed components, safe.

## Mind Capsule

> Severe immunosuppression (HIV, low CD4) = avoid **live vaccines** (BCG, MMR, OPV, varicella).
