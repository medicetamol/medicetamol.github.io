# Evaluation

**Evaluation** measures how far **objectives and targets** of a health programme were **achieved** and the **quality of results**. **Monitoring**, in contrast, is the ongoing tracking of activities while the programme runs.

## Trigger Point

> Degree of **objective and target achievement**, quality of results.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Planning**
Deciding objectives, strategies and resources before implementation.

**B. Surveillance**
Continuous watch over disease occurrence and trends.

**C. Monitoring**
Continuous checking of whether activities proceed as planned.

## Mind Capsule

> Monitoring = **during**, tracking activities; evaluation = **achievement of objectives**.
