# Fluoride Supplementation

The picture (**genu valgum, osteoporosis, Andhra Pradesh, jowar diet**) is **skeletal fluorosis**. Management lowers fluoride intake, so **adding fluoride is the one thing that must not be done**.

## Trigger Point

> Bowed legs + osteoporosis in **endemic Andhra Pradesh**; "what should **not** be done".

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Provision of running surface water**
Surface water usually has less fluoride than deep groundwater, so it is a suitable alternative source.

**B. Change the water source**
A standard step in managing endemic fluorosis.

**D. Add lime and alum to drinking water**
The Nalgonda technique removes fluoride.

## Mind Capsule

> Fluorosis = **remove** fluoride (new source, Nalgonda); **never supplement**.
