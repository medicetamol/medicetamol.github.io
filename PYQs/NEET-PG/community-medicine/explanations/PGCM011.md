# Kata Thermometer

The **Kata thermometer** measures the **cooling power of air** and is used to estimate **low air velocity** (below about 1 m/s), such as indoor air movement.

## Trigger Point

> "**Low air velocity** will be measured by..."

## Instruments in Environmental Health

| Instrument | Measures |
|---|---|
| Kata thermometer | Low air velocity, cooling power |
| Globe thermometer | Radiant heat |
| Wet bulb (psychrometer) | Humidity |
| Anemometer | High air velocity |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Globe thermometer**
Measures radiant heat, not air movement.

**C. Wet thermometer**
Used with a dry bulb to find humidity.

**D. Beckmann's thermometer**
A differential thermometer for very small temperature changes (calorimetry); it does not measure air velocity.

## Mind Capsule

> Low air velocity = **Kata**; radiant heat = globe; humidity = wet bulb.
