# No Malnutrition (Reassure)

On the **WHO growth standards**, a weight-for-age value between the **median and -2 SD** is **normal**. Malnutrition begins only below **-2 SD**, and severe acute malnutrition below **-3 SD**. The child needs reassurance and routine growth monitoring.

## Trigger Point

> Child falls **between the standard (median) and -2 SD**.

## WHO Classification by Z-score

| Z-score | Category |
|---|---|
| Median to -2 SD | Normal |
| Below -2 to -3 SD | Moderate malnutrition |
| Below -3 SD | Severe malnutrition |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Mild malnutrition, home treatment**
The WHO chart has no "mild" band for this purpose; a child above -2 SD is not malnourished.

**C. Moderate malnutrition**
Needs a value between -2 and -3 SD, which this child does not have.

**D. Severe malnutrition, refer to NRC**
Needs a value below -3 SD (or other SAM criteria), not seen here.

## Mind Capsule

> Above -2 SD = normal; -2 to -3 SD = moderate; below -3 SD = severe (NRC).
