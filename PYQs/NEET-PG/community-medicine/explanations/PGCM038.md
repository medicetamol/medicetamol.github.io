# Location at the Time of Enumeration

The **de facto** method counts people **where they are physically present on the census night**, regardless of where they usually live. India's census follows this method.

## Trigger Point

> "**De facto**" census data collection.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Place of birth**
Collected as a census variable but does not define either counting method.

**C. Usual place of residence**
This is the **de jure** method.

**D. Place of employment**
Not the basis for either method.

## Mind Capsule

> **De facto = where found**; de jure = where usually resident.
