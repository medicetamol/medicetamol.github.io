# Dietary History (Lathyrism)

Progressive **stiffness and spastic paralysis of both lower limbs** in a person from central India suggests **neurolathyrism**, caused by **BOAA (beta-oxalyl-amino-alanine, also called ODAP)** in **khesari dal** (*Lathyrus sativus*). The history that clinches it is the **dietary history**.

## Trigger Point

> Spastic paraparesis in a 55-year-old from **Chhattisgarh** (an area where khesari dal is eaten).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Medical history**
Too general; it will not identify the toxin exposure.

**C. Present illness**
Already described in the stem; the cause lies in the diet.

**D. Socioeconomic**
Poverty predisposes, but the causal exposure is the food.

## Mind Capsule

> Spastic paraparesis in an endemic area = **lathyrism**; ask about **khesari dal**.
