# Lux

**Illuminance** (light falling on a surface) is measured in **lux**, which is **one lumen per square metre**.

## Trigger Point

> "SI unit of brightness/**illuminance**".

## Photometric Units

| Quantity | Unit |
|---|---|
| Luminous intensity | Candela |
| Luminous flux | Lumen |
| Illuminance | **Lux** (lumen/m²) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Candela**
Unit of luminous intensity.

**B. Luminance**
A quantity (brightness of a surface, in cd/m²), not the SI unit of illuminance.

**C. Lumen**
Unit of luminous flux, the total light emitted.

## Mind Capsule

> Illuminance = **lux** = lumen per m².
