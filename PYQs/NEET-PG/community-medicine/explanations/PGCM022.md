# Vitamin C

Farmers with a **crossed (scissoring) gait** and stick support, living on **rice and pulses**, fit **neurolathyrism** from a monotonous diet of grass pea. Experimental and epidemiological work shows that diets lacking **vitamin C** predispose to it, and that vitamin C is protective, which is why it is the answer here.

## Trigger Point

> **Scissoring gait** in villagers on a **rice-and-pulse** diet; "which vitamin could have prevented this".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Vitamin A**
Deficiency causes night blindness and xerophthalmia.

**B. Vitamin D**
Deficiency causes rickets and osteomalacia (bone pain, bowing), not spastic gait.

**D. Vitamin B**
A group; deficiencies cause beriberi, pellagra, and neuropathies, but the question is built around the lathyrism link.

## Mind Capsule

> Neurolathyrism = spastic gait from **BOAA**; **vitamin C** is the protective vitamin in this question.
