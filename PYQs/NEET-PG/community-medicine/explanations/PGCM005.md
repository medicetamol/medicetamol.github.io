# Chronic Malnutrition (Stunting)

**Height-for-age below -2 SD** defines **stunting**, which reflects **long-standing (chronic) undernutrition**. Low weight-for-height (wasting) reflects acute malnutrition, and low weight-for-age (underweight) is a mixed measure.

## Trigger Point

> **Height for age < -2 SD** in a 4-year-old.

## Indices of Malnutrition

| Index | Low value means |
|---|---|
| Height-for-age | Stunting (chronic) |
| Weight-for-height | Wasting (acute) |
| Weight-for-age | Underweight (acute + chronic) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Acute malnutrition**
Shown by low weight-for-height (wasting) or low MUAC, not by low height-for-age.

**C. Recent malnutrition**
Not a recognised category; recent loss would affect weight rather than height.

**D. No malnutrition**
Incorrect, since a value below -2 SD is abnormal.

## Mind Capsule

> Low height-for-age = **stunting = chronic**; low weight-for-height = **wasting = acute**.
