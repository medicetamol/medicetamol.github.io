# Dietary Pattern, Dementia and Diarrhoea

The image shows **Casal's necklace**, a symmetric, pigmented, scaly photosensitive dermatitis around the neck, which is the hallmark of **pellagra**. The most informative history covers the **diet (maize or millet staple)** and the other two Ds, **diarrhoea and dementia**.

## Key Finding

> Symmetric thickened, hyperpigmented scaly skin around the neck and upper chest = Casal's necklace.

## Trigger Point

> Symmetric pigmented scaly rash on the **neck and upper chest** (sun-exposed).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Dietary history**
Important, but asking only about diet misses the systemic features that confirm pellagra.

**C. Dementia**
Only one part of the triad; it does not cover the diet or the gut symptoms.

**D. Depression**
Neuropsychiatric symptoms in pellagra can include depression, but it is non-specific and not the key history.

## Mind Capsule

> Casal's necklace = pellagra; ask about **diet + diarrhoea + dementia**.

## Memory Hook

> **4 Ds of pellagra**: Dermatitis, Diarrhoea, Dementia, (Death).
