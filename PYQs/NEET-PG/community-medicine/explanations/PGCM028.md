# Td Vaccine

In India's **school immunisation programme**, **Td** (replacing TT) is given at **10 and 16 years**.

## Trigger Point

> 10-year-old school child, vaccine in the **school immunization programme**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Measles vaccine**
Given in infancy (MR at 9-12 months and 16-24 months).

**B. Rotavirus vaccine**
Oral vaccine given in infancy (6, 10 and 14 weeks).

**D. Hepatitis B vaccine**
Given at birth and as part of pentavalent in infancy.

## Mind Capsule

> School age 10 and 16 years = **Td**.
