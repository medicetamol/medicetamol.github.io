# Gentle Stream, Avoiding Splashing

For **bacteriological** samples, the tap is cleaned and flamed, run for a short time, and the sample is collected in a **sterile bottle from a gentle stream**, avoiding splashing and contamination.

## Trigger Point

> Collecting water for **bacteriological examination** during an outbreak.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Collect from already leaking taps**
Leaking taps are avoided because water can contaminate the tap from outside.

**B. Let water flow for at least 1 minute**
Flushing the line is part of routine practice, but this option does not describe the collection technique the question tests.

**C. Container kept close to the tap**
The bottle must not touch the tap, to avoid contaminating the sample.

## Mind Capsule

> Sterile bottle + flamed tap + **gentle stream, no splashing, no touching the tap**.
