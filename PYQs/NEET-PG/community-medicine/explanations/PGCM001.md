# Folic Acid Deficiency

**Folate** is needed for DNA synthesis, so its lack gives **megaloblastic (macrocytic) anaemia**. Dietary folate comes from **green leafy vegetables, fruits and pulses**, and body stores last only about **3-4 months**, so a poor diet causes deficiency quickly.

## Trigger Point

> **No vegetables or fruits** in the diet + **MCV 120 fL** with macrocytes on the smear.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Vitamin B12 deficiency**
Also causes macrocytic anaemia, but its sources are animal foods and body stores last for years.
A short period of faulty diet rarely explains it, and it usually brings neurological features.

**C. Vitamin D deficiency**
Causes rickets or osteomalacia through poor calcium handling.
It does not produce macrocytic anaemia.

**D. None**
Incorrect, because the diet and the blood picture clearly fit folate deficiency.

## Mind Capsule

> Macrocytic anaemia + no fruits/greens = **folate**; macrocytic anaemia + neurological signs or strict vegetarian = B12.
