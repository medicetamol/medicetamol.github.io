# Unpaired t-Test

To compare the **means of a quantitative variable (Hb)** between **two independent groups**, use the **unpaired (independent) t-test**.

## Trigger Point

> Compare **mean Hb** between **two independent groups**.

## Choosing a Statistical Test

| Situation | Test |
|---|---|
| Means, 2 independent groups | Unpaired t-test |
| Means, same group before and after | Paired t-test |
| Means, more than 2 groups | ANOVA |
| Proportions or categories | Chi-square test |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Paired t-test**
For the same subjects measured twice, or matched pairs.

**C. Chi-square test**
For qualitative data (proportions).

**D. ANOVA**
For more than two groups.

## Mind Capsule

> Mean + 2 independent groups = **unpaired t-test**.
