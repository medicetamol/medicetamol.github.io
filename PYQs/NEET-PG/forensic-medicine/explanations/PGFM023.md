# Psychiatric Evaluation

When an accused **pleads insanity**, the medico-legal step is **psychiatric evaluation** to assess his mental state and whether he is fit for trial. The defence rests on legal insanity (**McNaghten rules, IPC 84**), which the court decides after expert evidence.

## Trigger Point

> Accused claims **delusions of persecution** at the time of killing and pleads **insanity**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Anger management program**
Not suitable for a claim of psychosis.

**C. Sent directly to jail**
Ignores the plea and the need for assessment.

**D. Declare him not guilty and release**
Only the court can decide, after proper evaluation.

## Mind Capsule

> Plea of insanity = **psychiatric evaluation** first, then the court decides.
