# Section 314 IPC

**Section 314 IPC** deals with **death of the woman caused by an act done with intent to cause miscarriage**. (IPC has been replaced by the BNS, but the question uses the IPC numbering.)

## Trigger Point

> "**Section 314 IPC** deals with..."

## IPC Sections on Miscarriage

| Section | Offence |
|---|---|
| 312 | Causing miscarriage |
| 313 | Causing miscarriage without the woman's consent |
| 314 | Death caused by act done with intent to cause miscarriage |
| 315 | Act to prevent child being born alive |
| 316 | Causing death of a quick unborn child |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. With the consent of the mother**
Section 312.

**B. Without the consent of the mother**
Section 313.

**D. Death of the quick unborn child by culpable homicide**
Section 316.

## Mind Capsule

> 312 miscarriage, 313 without consent, **314 mother dies**, 316 quick unborn child dies.
