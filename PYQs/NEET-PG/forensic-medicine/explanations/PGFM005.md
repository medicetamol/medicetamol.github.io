# Cross-Examination

A **leading question** suggests the answer to the witness. It is **allowed in cross-examination**, but not in examination-in-chief or re-examination unless the court permits it (Indian Evidence Act, Sections 142-143).

## Trigger Point

> "Were you present when A killed B?" with the answer "yes", a question that **suggests its own answer**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Examination in chief**
The party's own witness is examined; leading questions are not allowed here.

**B. Direct examination**
Another name for examination in chief, so the same rule applies.

**C. Re-examination**
Confined to explaining matters raised in cross-examination; leading questions are not allowed.

## Mind Capsule

> Leading question = **cross-examination only**.
