# Bhilawanol (Marking Nut)

The **marking nut** (*Semecarpus anacardium*, bhela) has a vesicant juice containing **bhilawanol** and semecarpol. It causes severe contact dermatitis and, when ingested, gastrointestinal irritation.

## Key Finding

> Dark, rough, irregular nuts = marking nut.

## Trigger Point

> **Dark, rough, irregular nuts**; "active principle".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Abrin**
Toxalbumin of *Abrus precatorius* (jequirity or gunchi), whose seeds are bright red with a black spot.

**B. Ricin**
Toxalbumin of castor (*Ricinus communis*) seeds, which are glossy and mottled.

**C. Cannabinol**
A constituent of cannabis, not a poisonous seed of this appearance.

## Mind Capsule

> Marking nut = **bhilawanol**; jequirity = abrin; castor = ricin.
