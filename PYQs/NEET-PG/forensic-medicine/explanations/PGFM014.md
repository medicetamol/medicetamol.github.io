# Barberio Test

The **Barberio test** detects **spermine** in seminal stains using **saturated picric acid**, which forms **yellow, needle-shaped rhombic crystals** of spermine picrate.

## Trigger Point

> Specimen with **picric acid** gives **yellow needle-shaped rhombic crystals**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Florence test**
Uses iodine to detect **choline**, giving dark brown crystals.

**C. Teichmann test**
Detects **haemin crystals** in blood stains.

**D. Acid phosphatase test**
An enzymatic chemical test for seminal fluid, not a crystal test.

## Mind Capsule

> **Barberio = picric acid = spermine** (yellow); Florence = iodine = choline (brown).
