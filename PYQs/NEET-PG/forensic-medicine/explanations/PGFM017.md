# Septicaemia

Body temperature may **rise after death** in conditions with intense bacterial activity or heat-regulation failure, such as **septicaemia**, heat stroke, tetanus, pontine haemorrhage and cholera. A reading of 39 °C in a corpse fits septicaemia.

## Trigger Point

> **Temperature 39 °C** in a dead body.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cyanide poisoning**
Not associated with a postmortem rise in temperature.

**C. Corrosive poisoning**
Not a typical cause of postmortem heat.

**D. Intra-abdominal haemorrhage**
Blood loss leads to cooling, not a rise.

## Mind Capsule

> Postmortem **rise** in temperature = septicaemia, heat stroke, tetanus, pontine haemorrhage.
