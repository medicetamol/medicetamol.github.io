# Bradycardia

The picture (**sweating, headache, coronary spasm**) fits a **sympathomimetic** such as cocaine. These cause **tachycardia, hypertension, hyperthermia** and sweating. **Bradycardia** is therefore the least likely feature.

## Trigger Point

> **Diaphoresis, headache, coronary spasm**, "least likely" feature.

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Tachycardia**
Expected, due to excess catecholamine effect.

**C. Hyperthermia**
Seen with increased metabolic and muscular activity and impaired heat loss.

**D. Hypertension**
Expected from alpha-adrenergic vasoconstriction.

## Mind Capsule

> Sympathomimetic toxidrome = **fast heart rate, high BP, high temperature, sweating**; bradycardia does not fit.
