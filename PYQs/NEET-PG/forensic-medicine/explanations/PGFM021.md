# Throttling

**Throttling** is **manual strangulation**: fingers and thumb compress the neck, leaving **fingertip bruises, curved nail scratches** and often a fractured **hyoid or superior horn of the thyroid cartilage**. The other bruises show struggle.

## Trigger Point

> **Curved scratches and grouped bruises** on the neck, **fracture of superior horn** of thyroid.

## Types of Strangulation

| Type | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Throttling | Compression by hands |
| Ligature strangulation | Ligature tightened by force other than body weight |
| Garrotting | Ligature tightened by twisting with a stick |
| Mugging | Forearm around the neck from behind |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Garroting**
A ligature twisted with a stick, leaving a ligature mark.

**C. Mugging**
Arm-lock from behind, with few marks on the neck.

**D. Ligature strangulation**
Gives a horizontal ligature furrow, not fingertip bruises.

## Mind Capsule

> Fingertip bruises + nail scratches + fractured thyroid horn = **throttling**.
