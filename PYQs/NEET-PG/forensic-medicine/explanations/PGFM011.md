# Datura and Physostigmine

**Datura** contains **anticholinergic alkaloids** (atropine, hyoscyamine, hyoscine). Poisoning presents with **hyperthermia, confusion or delirium, mydriasis with photophobia and urinary retention**. **Physostigmine** is the antidote.

## Trigger Point

> Fruit in the garden + **high fever, confusion, photophobia, unable to urinate**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Datura, pralidoxime**
Pralidoxime is for organophosphate poisoning.

**C. Yellow oleander, pralidoxime**
A cardiac glycoside poison treated with digoxin-specific antibody fragments, not pralidoxime.

**D. Yellow oleander, physostigmine**
Yellow oleander gives vomiting and arrhythmias, not an anticholinergic picture.

## Mind Capsule

> Hot, dry, delirious, retention of urine = **Datura**; antidote **physostigmine**.
