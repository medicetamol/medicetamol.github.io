# Superfecundation

**Superfecundation** is the fertilisation of **two ova released in the same cycle** by sperm from **separate acts of intercourse**, which may involve different fathers. This explains twins of whom only one is the husband's child.

## Trigger Point

> **Twins**, DNA shows **one baby is not the husband's**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Superfetation**
Fertilisation of a second ovum from a **later cycle** after pregnancy has begun.

**C. Atavism**
Reappearance of an ancestral trait in an offspring.

**D. Posthumous child**
A child born after the father's death.

## Mind Capsule

> Same cycle, two ova, two acts = **superfecundation**; different cycles = superfetation.
