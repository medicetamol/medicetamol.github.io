# All Children Below 18 Years

The **POCSO Act, 2012** protects **all children below 18 years of age**, boys and girls alike.

## Trigger Point

> "POCSO Act covers which groups?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Girls below 18**
The Act is gender neutral.

**C. Girls below 16**
Wrong age and gender restriction.

**D. All children below 16**
The age limit is 18, not 16.

## Mind Capsule

> POCSO = **both sexes, below 18 years**.
