# Postmortem Tendon Reflex

The **Zsako phenomenon** is a **postmortem tendon (muscle) reflex**, a muscle contraction elicited by tapping in the early period after death.

## Trigger Point

> "What is the **Zasko phenomenon**?"

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Seeping of blood through wounds around the time of death**
This describes bleeding in wounds close to death, not a reflex.

**C. Clotting of blood after death**
Postmortem clotting is a separate change.

**D. Gaping of wound along skin tension lines**
Relates to Langer's lines.

> **Zsako = postmortem tendon reflex**.
