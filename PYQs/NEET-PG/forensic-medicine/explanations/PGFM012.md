# Letulle Technique

The **Letulle technique** removes **all the organs "en masse"** (thoracic, cervical, abdominal and pelvic organs as one block, from the tongue to the prostate).

## Trigger Point

> Removal of organs **en masse from tongue to prostate**.

## Autopsy Techniques

| Technique | Method |
|---|---|
| Virchow | Organs removed one by one |
| Rokitansky | In situ dissection with minimal removal |
| Ghon | Organs removed in blocs (thoracic, cervical, abdominal, urogenital) |
| Letulle | All organs removed en masse |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Virchow**
Organs removed and examined one at a time.

**B. Rokitansky**
Dissected in place without complete removal.

**C. Ghon**
Removed in separate blocs, not as a single mass.

## Mind Capsule

> **En masse = Letulle**; en bloc = Ghon; one by one = Virchow; in situ = Rokitansky.
