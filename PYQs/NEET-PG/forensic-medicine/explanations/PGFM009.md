# Sweating

The patient has **heat stroke** (collapse in the heat with 106 °F). Classic heat stroke features **hot, dry skin**, so **sweating** is the least likely finding. Note that in exertional heat stroke some sweating may persist.

## Trigger Point

> Labourer collapses on a **hot day**, temperature **106 °F**; "least likely" symptom.

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Hot skin**
Core temperature is very high and skin is hot to touch.

**C. Hypotension**
Seen from vasodilatation and fluid loss.

**D. Disorientation**
CNS dysfunction is a defining feature of heat stroke.

## Mind Capsule

> Heat stroke = **high temperature + CNS signs + hot, dry skin**.
