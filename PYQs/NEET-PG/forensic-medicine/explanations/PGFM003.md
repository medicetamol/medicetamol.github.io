# Marbling (Sulphaemoglobin)

**Marbling** is a **putrefactive change** in which **bacteria** (mainly *Clostridium welchii*) **produce hydrogen sulphide**. It combines with haemoglobin to form **sulphaemoglobin**, which stains the walls of superficial veins **greenish-black** and gives a branching pattern.

## Trigger Point

> **Branching dark discolouration along superficial veins** of a limb.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Seen at 24 hours**
Marbling appears later in putrefaction, after the first day.

**C. Non-bacterial**
Incorrect; it is caused by bacteria.

**D. Associated with lightning**
Lightning leaves fern-like (Lichtenberg) skin marks, which do not follow veins.

## Mind Capsule

> Branching dark veins after death = **marbling** (bacterial H₂S + haemoglobin = sulphaemoglobin).
