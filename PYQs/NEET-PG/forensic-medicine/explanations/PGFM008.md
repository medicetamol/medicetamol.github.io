# Exhibitionism

**Exhibitionism** is a paraphilia in which a person obtains sexual gratification by **exposing the genitals or body to unsuspecting persons**, often strangers.

## Trigger Point

> Sitting **naked in a balcony overlooking a park** for sexual gratification.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Voyeurism**
Sexual gratification from secretly watching others undress or engage in sex.

**B. Masochism**
Sexual pleasure from suffering pain or humiliation.

**D. Fetishism**
Sexual arousal from objects or body parts that are not usually sexual.

## Mind Capsule

> **Showing** oneself = exhibitionism; **watching** others = voyeurism.
