# Consent Invalid Below 18 Years

In law, a girl **below 18 years cannot give valid consent** to a sexual act. The act with a 16-year-old is treated as rape regardless of her statement or absence of injury.

## Trigger Point

> **16-year-old** says the act was consensual; **no injuries**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. No punishment since consensual**
Consent is irrelevant below the age of consent.

**C. No punishment since there are no injuries**
Absence of injuries does not negate the offence.

**D. Parents must prove non-consent**
Non-consent need not be proved when the girl is under 18.

## Mind Capsule

> Age of consent = **18 years**; below it, consent is not valid.
