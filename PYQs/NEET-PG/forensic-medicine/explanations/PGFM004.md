# Incised-Looking Laceration

An **incised-looking laceration** is a **tear caused by blunt force** (for example over a bony prominence) that resembles a clean-cut wound at first look. Careful examination shows **tissue bridging** and bruised or abraded margins, so it is a laceration despite its appearance.

## Key Finding

> Irregular wound with elongated lacerated appearance.

## Trigger Point

> **Irregular margins** with an **elongated lacerated** look.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Laceration**
Describes a typical blunt tear; the question asks for the specific type in the image.

**C. Laceration-looking incision**
An incised wound whose margins appear ragged; it would have sharp-cut edges without bridging.

**D. Incision**
A clean cut by a sharp edge with regular margins and no bridging.

## Mind Capsule

> Bridging and bruised margins = **laceration**, even if it looks cut.
