# Family Meeting

The doctor should **convene a family meeting**, explain the prognosis, and consider the **patient's own needs and wishes** in a shared decision. Treatment that will not help need not be offered, and comfort-focused (palliative) care is explained as an active plan.

## Trigger Point

> Doctors feel aggressive treatment will **not help**, but the daughter insists on "**trying everything**".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Shift to the ICU and start aggressive treatment**
Would impose non-beneficial treatment and cause harm.

**C. Discharge and shift to palliative care**
An abrupt step without the family's understanding.

**D. Give placebo care**
Deceptive and unethical.

## Mind Capsule

> Family insists on futile treatment = **communicate**, involve the patient, avoid futile care.
