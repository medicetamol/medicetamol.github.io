# Strychnine

**Strychnine** blocks **glycine receptors** in the spinal cord, causing exaggerated reflexes. It produces **violent tonic spasms with opisthotonos (arching of the back)**, **risus sardonicus**, and the patient remains **conscious** between spasms.

## Trigger Point

> Repeated seizures, **conscious in between**, **back arching** during episodes, herbal medicine.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ricinus**
Castor seed toxin; gastroenteritis, haemolysis, kidney injury.

**C. Nerium odorum**
White oleander, a cardiac glycoside; vomiting, arrhythmias.

**D. Datura**
Anticholinergic delirium and hyperthermia, not conscious spasms.

## Mind Capsule

> Spasms with arching + **clear consciousness** = **strychnine**.
