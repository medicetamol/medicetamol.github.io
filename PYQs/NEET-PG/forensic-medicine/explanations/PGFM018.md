# Viper

**Viper bites** cause **local pain, swelling, bullae or blisters and bleeding** because the venom is **haemotoxic/vasculotoxic**. Bleeding from the bite site is a key clue.

## Trigger Point

> Sting while sleeping + **profuse bleeding** from the wound, **blisters**, pain.

## Snake Venom Types

| Snake | Main venom effect |
|---|---|
| Viper | Haemotoxic: local swelling, bleeding, blisters |
| Cobra, krait | Neurotoxic: ptosis, paralysis |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Cobra**
Primarily neurotoxic, with paralysis and ptosis.

**C. Wasp bite**
Painful local reaction or allergy, not continuing bleeding.

**D. Scorpion**
Severe pain and autonomic effects; not profuse bleeding.

## Mind Capsule

> Bleeding and blisters = **viper**; ptosis and paralysis = elapid.
