# Datura and Physostigmine

**Datura** seeds and fruit contain **atropine-like anticholinergic alkaloids**. They cause **dry skin, hyperthermia, mydriasis (photophobia), urinary retention and restlessness or delirium**. The antidote is **physostigmine**, a cholinesterase inhibitor that crosses the blood-brain barrier.

## Trigger Point

> Fruit ingestion + **dry skin, photophobia, urinary retention, fever, restlessness**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Yellow oleander and physostigmine**
Yellow oleander (*Thevetia*) is a cardiac glycoside poison giving vomiting, arrhythmias and hyperkalaemia, not an anticholinergic picture.

**C. Datura and pralidoxime**
Pralidoxime reactivates cholinesterase in organophosphate poisoning; it does not treat anticholinergic toxicity.

**D. Yellow oleander and pralidoxime**
Both parts are wrong: the toxin is a cardiac glycoside, and pralidoxime is for organophosphates.

## Mind Capsule

> "Dry as a bone, red as a beet, mad as a hatter" = **Datura**; antidote = **physostigmine**.
