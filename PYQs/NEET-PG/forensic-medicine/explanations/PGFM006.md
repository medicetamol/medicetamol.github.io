# Prolonged Immersion in Water

**Prolonged immersion** produces **pale, wrinkled, macerated skin** of the palms and soles, the so-called **washerwoman's hands and feet**, as the epidermis absorbs water. It is a sign of the body having been in water for hours.

## Key Finding

> Pale, wrinkled, waterlogged skin with deep creases over the soles.

## Trigger Point

> **Waterlogged, wrinkled skin** with creases on the soles.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Torture in hot water**
Would cause scalds with erythema and blisters, not just wrinkling.

**B. Postmortem hanging**
Would show a ligature mark with changes in the neck, not skin maceration.

**D. Colliquative liquefaction**
Late putrefaction with soft tissue breakdown, not wrinkled skin.

## Mind Capsule

> Wrinkled, bleached skin of hands and feet = **immersion in water (washerwoman's change)**.
