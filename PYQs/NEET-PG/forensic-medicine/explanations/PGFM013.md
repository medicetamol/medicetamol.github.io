# Rokitansky Technique

In an **infectious (HIV) case**, the aim is to **limit handling of organs and splashing**. The **Rokitansky (in situ) technique** dissects organs in place with minimal manipulation, reducing exposure.

## Trigger Point

> **HIV-positive** deceased; "method of autopsy to be done".

## Autopsy Techniques

| Technique | Method |
|---|---|
| Virchow | Organs removed one by one |
| Rokitansky | In situ dissection with minimal removal |
| Ghon | Organs removed in blocs |
| Letulle | All organs removed en masse |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Ghon technique**
Removes organs in blocs, which means more handling.

**B. Letulle technique**
Removes all organs in one mass, requiring heavy handling.

**C. Virchow technique**
Organs removed and cut one by one, with the most handling.

## Mind Capsule

> Infectious body = **Rokitansky (in situ, minimal handling)**.
