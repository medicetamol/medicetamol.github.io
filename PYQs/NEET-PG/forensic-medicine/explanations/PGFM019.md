# Arsenic

**Acute arsenic poisoning** gives **severe abdominal pain, vomiting (blood and bile), rice-water diarrhoea** and a **garlicky odour** of the breath.

## Trigger Point

> **Rice-water diarrhoea** + **garlicky breath** + vomiting.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Phosphorus**
Can also have a garlic odour, but vomit and stools are luminous, and hepatic injury predominates.

**C. Aluminium phosphide**
Releases phosphine (fishy or garlic smell), but causes early shock and refractory hypotension, not rice-water stools.

**D. Croton seeds**
A violent purgative causing burning and watery diarrhoea, without garlic breath.

## Mind Capsule

> Garlic breath + rice-water stools = **arsenic**.
