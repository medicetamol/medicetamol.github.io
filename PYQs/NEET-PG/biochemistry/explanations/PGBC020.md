# Hereditary Non-Polyposis Colorectal Cancer

**Mismatch repair (MMR)** corrects base mismatches and small insertion/deletion loops made during DNA replication, using **MLH1, MSH2, MSH6 and PMS2**. Defects cause **Lynch syndrome (HNPCC)** with **microsatellite instability** and early colorectal and endometrial cancers.

## Trigger Point

> Defect in **mismatch repair**.

## DNA Repair Defects

| Repair pathway | Associated disease |
|---|---|
| Mismatch repair | HNPCC (Lynch syndrome) |
| Nucleotide excision repair | Xeroderma pigmentosum |
| Base excision repair | MUTYH-associated polyposis |
| Homologous recombination | BRCA1/2 breast and ovarian cancer |
| NHEJ | SCID, Ataxia-telangiectasia |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. SCID**
Defects in V(D)J recombination (Artemis, RAG) involve non-homologous end joining.
It is unrelated to mismatch repair.

**C. Bloom syndrome**
Due to a defect in **BLM helicase**, affecting homologous recombination and causing excess sister chromatid exchanges.
It is not an MMR defect.

**D. MUTYH-associated polyposis**
Involves **base excision repair** (MUTYH removes mispaired adenine opposite 8-oxoguanine).
It is a different repair pathway.

## Mind Capsule

> MMR defect = **Lynch syndrome** (microsatellite instability); NER = XP; BER = MUTYH; HR = BRCA, Bloom.
