# Prolyl Hydroxylase

**Scurvy** is due to **vitamin C deficiency**. Vitamin C is a cofactor for **prolyl and lysyl hydroxylases**, which hydroxylate collagen. Defective collagen leads to **perifollicular haemorrhages, corkscrew hairs, bleeding gums, subperiosteal haemorrhages and bone fragility**.

## Trigger Point

> **Perifollicular haemorrhages, gum bleeding, petechiae** and fractures = scurvy.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Lysyl oxidase**
A **copper-dependent** enzyme that cross-links collagen and elastin.
It is deficient in Menkes disease, not scurvy.

**C. Tyrosinase**
A copper-containing enzyme for melanin synthesis.
Its defect causes oculocutaneous albinism.

**D. Alkaline phosphatase**
A zinc-containing enzyme involved in bone mineralization.
Its defect causes hypophosphatasia.

## Mind Capsule

> Scurvy = vitamin C = **prolyl and lysyl hydroxylase**; Menkes = lysyl **oxidase** (copper).
