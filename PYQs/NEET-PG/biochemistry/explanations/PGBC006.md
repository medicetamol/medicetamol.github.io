# Glycogen Synthase (GSD Type 0)

**Glycogen synthase deficiency (GSD 0)** impairs glycogen **synthesis**, so the **liver has little or no glycogen**. The child has **fasting hypoglycaemia with ketosis**, which is relieved by feeding, and **postprandial hyperglycaemia** with high lactate. Hepatomegaly is **absent**, unlike other hepatic GSDs.

## Trigger Point

> **No glycogen in the liver** + hunger relieved by food (fasting hypoglycaemia).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Glycogen phosphorylase**
Deficiency (Hers) leads to glycogen accumulation in the liver.
Glycogen would be present, not absent.

**C. Debranching enzyme**
Deficiency (Cori) gives accumulation of abnormal glycogen with short outer branches.
Liver glycogen is present with hepatomegaly.

**D. Glucose-6-phosphatase**
Deficiency (von Gierke) leads to marked glycogen accumulation, hepatomegaly, lactic acidosis and hyperuricaemia.
Glycogen is present in excess.

## Mind Capsule

> Absent liver glycogen + fasting hypoglycaemia = **GSD 0 (glycogen synthase)**; all other hepatic GSDs have glycogen accumulation.
