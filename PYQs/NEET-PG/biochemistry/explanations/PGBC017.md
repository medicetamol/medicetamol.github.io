# Vitamin A Deficiency

**Vitamin A deficiency** causes **xerophthalmia**: night blindness, conjunctival xerosis, **Bitot's spots**, corneal xerosis and **keratomalacia (corneal softening and melting)**. Vitamin A maintains epithelial integrity and forms **rhodopsin** in the retina.

## Trigger Point

> **Dry gritty eye** with **corneal softening**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Riboflavin deficiency**
Causes angular stomatitis, cheilosis and corneal vascularization.
Corneal softening is not a feature.

**C. Viral keratitis**
Presents with pain, watering, photophobia and dendritic ulcers.
It is not a nutritional cause of corneal softening.

**D. Follicular conjunctivitis**
Lymphoid follicles in the conjunctiva due to viral or chlamydial infection.
It does not cause corneal melting.

## Mind Capsule

> Night blindness → conjunctival xerosis → Bitot's spots → corneal xerosis → **keratomalacia** (vitamin A deficiency).
