# Methionine

**Vitamin B12** is a cofactor for **methionine synthase**, which converts **homocysteine to methionine** using methyl-THF. In B12 deficiency, this step fails, so **homocysteine rises and methionine falls**, with the **methyl-folate trap** also causing megaloblastic anaemia. **Methylmalonic acid** is also raised.

## Trigger Point

> B12 deficiency with **elevated homocysteine**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cysteine**
Made from homocysteine through CBS (needs B6).
It is not affected by the B12-dependent step.

**C. Tyrosine**
Made from phenylalanine by PAH.
It is unrelated to B12.

**D. Glutamate**
Made from α-ketoglutarate and ammonia.
It is not deficient in B12 deficiency.

## Mind Capsule

> B12 deficiency = ↑ homocysteine + ↑ methylmalonic acid; folate deficiency = ↑ homocysteine only.
