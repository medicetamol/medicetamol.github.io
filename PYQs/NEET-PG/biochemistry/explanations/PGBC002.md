# Vitamin B3 (Niacin) Deficiency

**Pellagra** is due to **niacin (B3)** deficiency, the vitamin needed to make NAD⁺ and NADP⁺. It presents with the **3 Ds: Diarrhoea, Dermatitis (photosensitive, Casal's necklace) and Dementia**, and death in untreated cases (4th D).

## Trigger Point

> **Diarrhoea, dermatitis, dementia** = pellagra.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Vitamin B1 deficiency**
Causes beriberi and Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome.
Features are neuropathy, heart failure and ophthalmoplegia.

**C. Vitamin B6 deficiency**
Causes sideroblastic anaemia, neuropathy and seizures (isoniazid use).
It does not give the triad of pellagra.

**D. Vitamin B12 deficiency**
Causes megaloblastic anaemia and subacute combined degeneration.
There is no photosensitive dermatitis.

## Mind Capsule

> Pellagra = **3 Ds** (diarrhoea, dermatitis, dementia) = niacin; seen with maize diet, carcinoid, Hartnup disease and isoniazid.
