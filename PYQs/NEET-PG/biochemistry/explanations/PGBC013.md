# Biotin

**Raw egg white** contains **avidin**, which binds **biotin** tightly and stops its absorption. Biotin is a cofactor for carboxylases (pyruvate carboxylase, acetyl-CoA carboxylase, propionyl-CoA carboxylase). Deficiency causes **alopecia, scaly dermatitis, conjunctivitis and neurological symptoms**.

## Trigger Point

> Regular **raw egg** intake + hair thinning and scalp scaling.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Niacin**
Deficiency causes pellagra with photosensitive dermatitis and dementia.
There is no relation to raw eggs.

**B. Pyridoxine**
Deficiency causes seizures, neuropathy and sideroblastic anaemia.
Avidin does not affect it.

**D. Thiamine**
Deficiency causes beriberi and Wernicke's encephalopathy.
Raw eggs do not interfere with it.

## Mind Capsule

> Raw egg white = **avidin binds biotin** = alopecia and dermatitis; biotin is a coenzyme for carboxylation reactions.
