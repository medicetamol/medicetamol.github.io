# Thiamine

**Loop diuretics** and, to a lesser extent, other diuretics increase **urinary thiamine loss**. Long-term use can cause deficiency, with **dry beriberi (peripheral neuropathy, symmetrical tingling)** and **wet beriberi (heart failure)**. The question stem lists hydrochlorothiazide, but the thiamine link is best described with diuretics generally.

## Trigger Point

> Long-term diuretic + **neuropathy, heart failure** and symmetrical tingling.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Selenium**
Deficiency causes Keshan disease (cardiomyopathy).
It is not linked to diuretics or neuropathy.

**C. Vitamin B12**
Causes neuropathy and megaloblastic anaemia but not heart failure.
Diuretics do not cause it.

**D. Zinc**
Deficiency causes dermatitis, alopecia and poor wound healing.
It is not a cause of heart failure or neuropathy.

## Mind Capsule

> Neuropathy + heart failure in a diuretic user = **thiamine deficiency** (beriberi).
