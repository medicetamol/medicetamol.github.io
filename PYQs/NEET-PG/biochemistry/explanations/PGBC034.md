# Tangier Disease

**Tangier disease** is a defect of **ABCA1**, the transporter that moves cholesterol to apoA-I for HDL formation. HDL is almost absent (very low), and **cholesterol esters accumulate in macrophages**, giving **enlarged orange-yellow tonsils**, hepatosplenomegaly and neuropathy.

## Trigger Point

> **Orange tonsils** with **HDL 5 mg/dL**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Familial hypercholesterolemia**
LDL receptor defect, with high LDL and tendon xanthomas.
HDL is not selectively absent.

**C. Type I hyperlipoproteinemia**
Chylomicronaemia with high triglycerides and pancreatitis.
Triglycerides here are normal.

**D. Abetalipoproteinemia**
Absent apoB lipoproteins with acanthocytes, fat malabsorption and low cholesterol.
It does not give orange tonsils.

## Mind Capsule

> **Orange tonsils + very low HDL** = Tangier disease (ABCA1).
