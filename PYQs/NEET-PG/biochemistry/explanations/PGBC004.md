# Thiamine with Transketolase

A diet of **polished rice** removes the husk containing **thiamine (B1)**, leading to **beriberi**. Thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP) is a cofactor for **transketolase** (HMP shunt), **pyruvate dehydrogenase**, **α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase** and **branched-chain ketoacid dehydrogenase**. **RBC transketolase activity** is used to assess deficiency.

## Trigger Point

> Family consuming **only polished rice** = thiamine deficiency.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Riboflavin-glutathione reductase**
Riboflavin (as FAD) is needed for glutathione reductase, assessed in deficiency.
Polished rice is not a cause of riboflavin deficiency.

**C. Thiamine-transaminase**
Transaminases require **pyridoxal phosphate (B6)**, not thiamine.
The vitamin is correct but the enzyme is wrong.

**D. Riboflavin-transketolase**
Transketolase requires TPP (thiamine), not riboflavin.
Both the vitamin and the pairing are incorrect.

## Mind Capsule

> Polished rice = thiamine deficiency = **↓ RBC transketolase** (needs TPP).
