# Zinc Deficiency

**Zinc** is a cofactor in several enzymes (carbonic anhydrase, alkaline phosphatase, SOD, DNA/RNA polymerases) and in zinc-finger proteins. Deficiency, as in **acrodermatitis enteropathica**, causes **perioral and acral dermatitis, alopecia, diarrhoea and poor wound healing**, along with impaired taste and immunity.

## Trigger Point

> **Perioral scaly patches**, mucosal ulcers and **impaired wound healing**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Iron deficiency**
Gives microcytic anaemia, koilonychia and glossitis.
It does not cause perioral dermatitis or poor wound healing.

**C. Calcium deficiency**
Gives tetany, osteomalacia or rickets.
It has no characteristic skin lesions.

**D. Copper deficiency**
Causes anaemia, neutropenia and abnormal connective tissue (lysyl oxidase).
It is seen in Menkes disease, with kinky hair.

## Mind Capsule

> Zinc deficiency = **dermatitis (perioral, acral) + diarrhoea + alopecia** (acrodermatitis enteropathica); poor wound healing.
