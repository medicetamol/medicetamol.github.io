# Thiamine

**Wet beriberi** from **thiamine deficiency** causes high-output cardiac failure with **cardiomegaly, pedal oedema** and peripheral vasodilatation. Thiamine is needed for oxidative decarboxylation of pyruvate, so lactic acid builds up and lowers vascular resistance. Cheilosis is also reported in B-complex deficiency.

## Trigger Point

> **Pedal oedema + cardiomegaly** = wet beriberi.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Riboflavin**
Causes **cheilosis, angular stomatitis, glossitis** and corneal vascularization.
Cardiomegaly and oedema are not features.

**C. Pyridoxine**
Causes seizures, neuropathy and sideroblastic anaemia.
It does not cause high-output cardiac failure.

**D. Niacin**
Causes pellagra with diarrhoea, dermatitis and dementia.
There is no cardiomegaly.

## Mind Capsule

> Thiamine: **wet beriberi** (heart, oedema), **dry beriberi** (neuropathy), Wernicke-Korsakoff (CNS).
