# Hormone-Sensitive Lipase

A **low insulin:glucagon ratio** is seen in the **fasting state**. **Hormone-sensitive lipase** is activated (via cAMP/PKA) and mobilizes fatty acids from adipose tissue. In the fed state (high insulin), **lipoprotein lipase, glycogen synthase and PFK-1** are active.

## Trigger Point

> **Low insulin:glucagon ratio** = fasting state.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Lipoprotein lipase**
Activated by insulin in adipose tissue, clearing chylomicrons and VLDL.
It is a fed-state enzyme.

**B. Glycogen synthase**
Active in its dephosphorylated form under insulin.
Glucagon inactivates it by phosphorylation.

**C. Phosphofructokinase 1**
Stimulated by insulin through fructose-2,6-bisphosphate.
Glucagon lowers it, inhibiting glycolysis.

## Mind Capsule

> Fed (↑ insulin) = LPL, glycogen synthase, PFK-1 on; fasting (↑ glucagon) = **HSL**, glycogen phosphorylase, gluconeogenesis on.
