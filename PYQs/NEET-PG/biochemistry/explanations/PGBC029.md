# GM2 Ganglioside

**Tay-Sachs disease** is due to **hexosaminidase A** deficiency, so **GM2 ganglioside** cannot be degraded and accumulates in neurons. The clinical picture is seizures, regression, hyperacusis and a **cherry-red spot** without organomegaly.

## Trigger Point

> **Hexosaminidase A** deficiency + cherry-red spot.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. GM1 ganglioside**
Accumulates in GM1 gangliosidosis (β-galactosidase deficiency).
It is a separate disease.

**C. Sphingomyelin**
Accumulates in Niemann-Pick disease (sphingomyelinase deficiency).
It causes hepatosplenomegaly.

**D. Glucocerebroside**
Accumulates in Gaucher disease (glucocerebrosidase deficiency).
It causes organomegaly and bone disease.

## Mind Capsule

> Tay-Sachs = Hex **A** = **GM2**; Sandhoff = Hex A + B = GM2 with organomegaly.
