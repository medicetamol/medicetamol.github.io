# Alkaptonuria

**Alkaptonuria** is an autosomal recessive deficiency of **homogentisate oxidase** in the tyrosine degradation pathway. **Homogentisic acid** accumulates, and its oxidation to a pigment turns **urine black on standing**, with later **ochronosis** (pigmented cartilage) and arthritis.

## Trigger Point

> **Urine turns black on standing**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Phenylketonuria**
Phenylalanine hydroxylase deficiency, with mousy odour, intellectual disability and fair skin.
Urine does not turn black.

**C. Homocystinuria**
Cystathionine β-synthase deficiency, with ectopia lentis (downward), thrombosis and marfanoid habitus.
No black urine.

**D. Maple syrup urine disease**
Branched-chain ketoacid dehydrogenase deficiency, with sweet-smelling urine and neurological decline.
No darkening of urine.

## Mind Capsule

> Black urine on standing + ochronosis = **alkaptonuria** (homogentisate oxidase); treated with nitisinone.
