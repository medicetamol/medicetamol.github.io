# Nucleotide Excision Repair

**Xeroderma pigmentosum** is an autosomal recessive defect in **nucleotide excision repair (NER)**. **UV light** causes **thymine (pyrimidine) dimers**, which NER normally removes. Patients show **photosensitivity, hyperpigmentation, premature skin ageing and multiple skin cancers** (BCC, SCC, melanoma).

## Trigger Point

> **Skin cancer + hyperpigmentation worsening with sunlight**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Base excision repair**
Corrects single damaged bases (e.g. deaminated or oxidized).
Defects are linked to MUTYH-associated polyposis.

**C. NHEJ**
Repairs double-strand breaks; defects cause SCID and ataxia-telangiectasia.
They are not UV-related.

**D. Mismatch repair**
Corrects replication errors; its defect causes Lynch syndrome.
It does not cause photosensitivity.

## Mind Capsule

> UV damage + sunlight sensitivity = **NER defect** (XP); Cockayne syndrome also involves NER.
