# Increased Urate and Lactate

**Alcohol metabolism** raises the **NADH/NAD⁺ ratio**, which converts pyruvate to **lactate**. Lactate competes with urate for **renal tubular secretion**, reducing urate excretion. In addition, increased ATP turnover (conversion of ethanol to acetate) raises purine breakdown and urate production, which precipitates **gout**.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic alcoholic with **gouty arthritis**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Decreased NADH/NAD⁺ ratio**
Ethanol oxidation produces NADH, so the ratio **increases**.
This is the root of the metabolic changes in alcoholics.

**B. Increased urea and urate**
Urea synthesis is not increased; it may be reduced in liver disease.
Only urate is raised in this setting.

**D. Increased alkaline phosphatase**
It is raised in cholestasis or bone disease.
Alcohol raises GGT and AST > ALT, not ALP in particular.

## Mind Capsule

> Alcohol = ↑ NADH/NAD⁺ = ↑ lactate, ↓ gluconeogenesis, fatty liver, and ↑ urate (gout).
