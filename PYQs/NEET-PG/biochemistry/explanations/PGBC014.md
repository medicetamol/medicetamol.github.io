# Tay-Sachs Disease

**Tay-Sachs disease** is **hexosaminidase A** deficiency with accumulation of **GM2 ganglioside** in neurons. The child has **developmental regression, hypotonia, hyperacusis (exaggerated startle) and a cherry-red spot** on the macula, **without organomegaly**.

## Trigger Point

> Neurological regression + **cherry-red spot** + **no organomegaly**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Niemann-Pick disease**
Sphingomyelinase deficiency with a cherry-red spot, but **hepatosplenomegaly** is prominent.
Organomegaly is absent here.

**C. Hurler syndrome**
Mucopolysaccharidosis (α-L-iduronidase deficiency) with coarse facies, corneal clouding and organomegaly.
There is no cherry-red spot.

**D. Gaucher disease**
Glucocerebrosidase deficiency with hepatosplenomegaly, bone pain and crumpled-paper macrophages.
No cherry-red spot in the common type.

## Mind Capsule

> Cherry-red spot **without** organomegaly = Tay-Sachs; **with** organomegaly = Niemann-Pick.
