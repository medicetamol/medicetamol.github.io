# Homogentisate Oxidase

In **alkaptonuria**, the enzyme **homogentisate oxidase (homogentisate 1,2-dioxygenase)** of the **tyrosine** degradation pathway is deficient. **Homogentisic acid** accumulates and darkens urine on standing; children are otherwise well, and **ochronosis and arthritis** develop later in adults.

## Trigger Point

> Child with **black urine**, normal growth and development.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Homogentisate dehydrogenase**
This enzyme is not part of the tyrosine pathway.
The correct enzyme is an oxidase (dioxygenase).

**C. Tryptophan hydroxylase**
The rate-limiting enzyme in serotonin synthesis.
Its defect is not linked to black urine.

**D. Tyrosine transaminase**
Deficiency causes tyrosinaemia type II (Richner-Hanhart) with corneal and skin lesions.
It does not darken urine.

## Mind Capsule

> Black urine on standing + normal development = **alkaptonuria**; tyrosine → homogentisate → (oxidase) → maleylacetoacetate.
