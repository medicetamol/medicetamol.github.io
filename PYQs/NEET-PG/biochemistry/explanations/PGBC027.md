# Tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4)

**BH4** is the cofactor of **phenylalanine hydroxylase**, as well as tyrosine and tryptophan hydroxylases. A defect in BH4 synthesis or recycling (dihydropteridine reductase) causes **hyperphenylalaninaemia with normal PAH activity** (**malignant PKU**) and neurotransmitter deficiency (dopamine, serotonin) despite a phenylalanine-restricted diet.

## Trigger Point

> **High phenylalanine** with **normal phenylalanine hydroxylase** levels.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Tetrahydrofolate**
A one-carbon carrier, unrelated to hydroxylase reactions.
It is not the PAH cofactor.

**C. Thiamine**
A cofactor for decarboxylation (PDH, α-KGDH).
It does not act in phenylalanine hydroxylation.

**D. Pyridoxine**
A transamination and decarboxylation cofactor.
It is not needed for PAH activity.

## Mind Capsule

> Raised Phe + normal PAH = **BH4 defect** (malignant PKU); treat with BH4, L-DOPA and 5-HTP.
