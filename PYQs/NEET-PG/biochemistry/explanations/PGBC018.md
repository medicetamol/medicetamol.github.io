# Cysteine

In **homocystinuria due to cystathionine β-synthase (CBS) deficiency**, **homocysteine cannot be converted to cystathionine**, so **cysteine cannot be made** from methionine. Cysteine therefore becomes a **semi-essential (conditionally essential)** amino acid and needs to be supplied in the diet, while **methionine is restricted**. Pyridoxine (B6) is given in the responsive form.

## Trigger Point

> **CBS deficiency** = block in the transsulfuration pathway.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Methionine**
Methionine levels are **raised**, not low, so it is restricted from the diet.
It is the precursor of homocysteine.

**C. Serine**
Serine is a **substrate** that combines with homocysteine in the CBS reaction.
It is not deficient, and it is non-essential.

**D. Tryptophan**
It is an essential amino acid unrelated to this pathway.
It is needed in everyone, not specifically in CBS deficiency.

## Mind Capsule

> CBS deficiency = ↑ homocysteine and methionine, ↓ cysteine; diet = low methionine, **supplement cysteine**, B6 and betaine.
