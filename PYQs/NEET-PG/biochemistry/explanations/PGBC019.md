# Keratan Sulphate

**Keratan sulphate I** is the main glycosaminoglycan of the **cornea**, bound to proteoglycans such as lumican, keeping the collagen fibrils evenly spaced and maintaining **transparency**. It is also found in cartilage and intervertebral discs.

## Trigger Point

> **GAG for corneal transparency**.

## GAGs and Their Sites

| GAG | Main site |
|---|---|
| Keratan sulphate | Cornea, cartilage |
| Dermatan sulphate | Skin, blood vessels, heart valves |
| Heparan sulphate | Basement membrane, cell surfaces |
| Chondroitin sulphate | Cartilage, tendons |
| Hyaluronic acid | Vitreous, synovial fluid |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Dermatan sulphate**
Found in skin, tendons and heart valves.
It is not the main corneal GAG.

**C. Heparan sulphate**
Present in basement membranes and on cell surfaces (e.g. glomerular filter).
It is not the key GAG in cornea.

**D. Chondroitin sulphate**
The most abundant GAG, found in cartilage, bone and tendon.
It is not the main GAG for corneal clarity.

## Mind Capsule

> Cornea = **keratan sulphate**; vitreous and synovial fluid = hyaluronic acid.
