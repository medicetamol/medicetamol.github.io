# Thiamine (Vitamin B1) Deficiency

**Wernicke's encephalopathy** is the triad of **confusion, ataxia and ophthalmoplegia (nystagmus)** in chronic alcoholics, due to **thiamine deficiency**. It is treated with **IV thiamine before glucose** to avoid precipitating or worsening it; untreated it can progress to Korsakoff psychosis.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic alcoholic with **confusion, ataxia and painful eye movements** (ophthalmoplegia).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Vitamin A deficiency**
Causes night blindness and xerophthalmia.
It does not cause ataxia or confusion.

**C. Vitamin D deficiency**
Causes rickets and osteomalacia.
It is not associated with neurological syndromes in alcoholics.

**D. Vitamin C deficiency**
Causes scurvy with bleeding gums and poor wound healing.
There is no CNS involvement of this type.

## Mind Capsule

> Alcoholic + confusion + ataxia + ophthalmoplegia = Wernicke (thiamine); give **thiamine before glucose**.
