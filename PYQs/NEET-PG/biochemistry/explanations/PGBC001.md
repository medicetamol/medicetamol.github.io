# Factors II, VII, IX and X

**γ-Glutamyl carboxylase** requires **vitamin K** as a cofactor to convert glutamate residues to **γ-carboxyglutamate (Gla)** on the clotting factors **II, VII, IX and X** (and proteins C, S and Z). The Gla residues bind Ca²⁺, anchoring the factors to phospholipid surfaces.

## Trigger Point

> Clotting factors needing **γ-glutamyl carboxylase** = vitamin K-dependent factors.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. II, VIII, IX, X**
Includes factor VIII, which is not vitamin K-dependent.
It is deficient in haemophilia A.

**C. II, VII, IX, XI**
Includes factor XI, a contact-pathway factor that is not carboxylated.
Factor X is missing from the list.

**D. II, VIII, X, XI**
Includes factors VIII and XI, neither of which is vitamin K-dependent.
Factors VII and IX are missing.

## Mind Capsule

> Vitamin K factors = **1972** (X, IX, VII, II) + proteins C and S; warfarin blocks vitamin K epoxide reductase.
