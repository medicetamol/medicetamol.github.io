# Fibrillin-1 (FBN1)

**Marfan syndrome** is an autosomal dominant disorder of **fibrillin-1 (FBN1, chromosome 15)**, a component of extracellular microfibrils. Features are **tall stature, arachnodactyly, arm span > height, high-arched palate, ectopia lentis (upward), aortic root dilatation** (with regurgitation) and mitral valve prolapse.

## Trigger Point

> Tall, long limbs, **lens subluxation**, aortic root dilatation = Marfan.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. COL4A5**
Mutated in X-linked Alport syndrome, with nephritis, deafness and eye changes.
It is not related to the marfanoid features.

**C. COL1A1**
Mutated in osteogenesis imperfecta (brittle bones, blue sclera).
It does not cause aortic root dilation.

**D. Elastin**
Mutated in supravalvular aortic stenosis and cutis laxa; deleted in Williams syndrome.
It is not the gene in Marfan syndrome.

## Mind Capsule

> Marfan = **FBN1**, lens goes **up and out**; homocystinuria = lens goes **down and in**.
