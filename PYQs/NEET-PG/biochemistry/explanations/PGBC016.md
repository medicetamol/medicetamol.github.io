# Niacin

A **maize (corn)** diet is low in **available niacin** (bound as niacytin) and low in **tryptophan**, the precursor of niacin. This leads to **pellagra**: **diarrhoea, dermatitis and dementia**. The "dysentery" in the stem refers to the diarrhoeal component.

## Trigger Point

> Maize diet + **diarrhoea, dementia** (and dermatitis).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Thiamine**
Deficiency in polished-rice eaters, giving beriberi.
It is not linked to maize.

**C. Riboflavin**
Gives cheilosis, angular stomatitis and glossitis.
It does not cause dementia.

**D. Pyridoxine**
Gives neuropathy, seizures and sideroblastic anaemia.
It is not linked to maize.

## Mind Capsule

> Maize = pellagra (niacin, tryptophan); polished rice = beriberi (thiamine); jowar (sorghum) = pellagra (high leucine).
