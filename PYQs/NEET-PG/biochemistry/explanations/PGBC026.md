# Ketosis

During **prolonged fasting (days)**, adipose lipolysis rises, and the liver makes **ketone bodies** (acetoacetate and β-hydroxybutyrate). After about **3-5 days**, **ketones become the main fuel of the brain**, reducing glucose use and **sparing muscle protein** from gluconeogenesis.

## Trigger Point

> **5 days** without food = prolonged starvation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Gluconeogenesis**
It maintains blood glucose in starvation, but supplies only a part of the brain's energy.
The brain mainly uses ketones by this time.

**B. Glycogenolysis**
Liver glycogen is depleted within 12-24 hours.
It cannot be the source on day 5.

**D. Lipolysis**
It releases fatty acids, which the brain cannot use directly.
They are converted to ketones, which fuel the brain.

## Mind Capsule

> Brain fuel: fed = glucose; overnight = glycogenolysis; prolonged starvation = **ketones**; RBCs always need glucose.
