# Phrenicocolic Ligament

The **phrenicocolic ligament** is a peritoneal fold passing from the **splenic flexure of the colon to the diaphragm**. Its upper surface forms a shelf (**sustentaculum lienis**) on which the lower pole of the spleen rests, preventing its vertical descent.

## Trigger Point

> Structure preventing **vertical descent** of the spleen.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Hepatogastric ligament**
Part of the lesser omentum, connecting the liver to the lesser curvature of the stomach.
It has no connection with the spleen.

**C. Ligamentum teres**
The remnant of the left umbilical vein in the free margin of the falciform ligament.
It lies on the liver and anterior abdominal wall, unrelated to splenic support.

**D. Ligamentum flavum**
It is a spinal ligament between adjacent laminae of the vertebrae.
It is not an abdominal peritoneal structure.

## Mind Capsule

> Phrenicocolic ligament = **sustentaculum lienis** (shelf of the spleen).
> Splenic ligaments: gastrosplenic, splenorenal, phrenicocolic.
