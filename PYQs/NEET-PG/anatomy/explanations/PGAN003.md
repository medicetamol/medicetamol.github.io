# Glomerulus

The **renal glomerulus** is a tuft of fenestrated capillaries enclosed within **Bowman's capsule** (parietal layer of simple squamous epithelium, visceral layer of **podocytes**). The clear **Bowman's (urinary) space** separates the tuft from the capsule, and it lies in the renal cortex surrounded by cut sections of **proximal and distal convoluted tubules**.

## Trigger Point

> Rounded **capillary tuft** enclosed within a **clear capsule**, surrounded by **renal tubules**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Leydig cells of testis**
Polygonal, lipid-rich **interstitial cells** lying in clusters between seminiferous tubules. They have no capillary tuft or capsule.

**B. Pancreatic islet cells**
Pale-staining endocrine cell clusters scattered among dark **serous acini** of the exocrine pancreas. They are not enclosed in a capsule with a clear space.

**C. Hassall's corpuscles**
Concentric whorls of flattened **epithelioreticular cells** in the **thymic medulla**. They form a whorled structure and not a capillary tuft.

## Mind Capsule

> Capillary tuft + clear capsule + surrounding tubules = **glomerulus** in the renal cortex.
