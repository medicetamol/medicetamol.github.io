# Infraglenoid Tubercle

The **long head of triceps** arises from the **infraglenoid tubercle** of the scapula, while the lateral and medial heads arise from the posterior humerus. All three insert on the **olecranon process** and extend the elbow. The long head also crosses the shoulder, so it can be strained or detached with forced shoulder hyperextension.

## Trigger Point

> **Origin** of the long head of triceps.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Supraglenoid tubercle**
It is the origin of the **long head of biceps**.
It is the biceps counterpart, not the triceps.

**C. Shaft of humerus**
The lateral head arises above and the medial head below the radial groove.
The long head does not take origin from the humeral shaft.

**D. Olecranon process**
It is the **insertion** of the triceps, not the origin.
The question asks about detachment from the origin.

## Mind Capsule

> **I**nfraglenoid = **T**riceps (long head); **S**upraglenoid = **B**iceps (long head).
