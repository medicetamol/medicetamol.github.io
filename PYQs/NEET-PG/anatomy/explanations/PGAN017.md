# Facial Nerve (A) and Nerve to Mylohyoid (B)

The **posterior belly of digastric and stylohyoid** (2nd arch) are supplied by the **facial nerve**. The **anterior belly of digastric and mylohyoid** (1st arch) are supplied by the **nerve to mylohyoid**, a branch of the inferior alveolar nerve from V3.

## Trigger Point

> Nerve A runs near the angle of the jaw to the posterior belly region; nerve B runs medially near the floor of the mouth to mylohyoid.

## Pharyngeal Arch Muscles and Nerves

| Arch | Nerve | Muscles |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Mandibular (V3) | Muscles of mastication, mylohyoid, **anterior belly of digastric**, tensor tympani, tensor veli palatini |
| 2nd | Facial (VII) | Muscles of facial expression, **posterior belly of digastric**, stylohyoid, stapedius |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. A facial, B spinal accessory nerve**
The spinal accessory nerve supplies sternocleidomastoid and trapezius.
It has no supply to the floor of the mouth muscles.

**C. A mandibular, B facial**
This reverses the pairing; the posterior belly belongs to the facial nerve, not V3.
Mylohyoid is not facial-supplied.

**D. A spinal accessory, B mandibular**
The accessory nerve does not supply digastric.
Only the mandibular half of this pair (nerve to mylohyoid) is correct.

## Mind Capsule

> Digastric: **anterior belly = V3** (nerve to mylohyoid), **posterior belly = VII**.
> Develops from the 1st and 2nd arches respectively.
