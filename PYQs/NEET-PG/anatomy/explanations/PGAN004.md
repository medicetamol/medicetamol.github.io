# 6th Left Aortic Arch

The **left 6th aortic arch** gives the **proximal left pulmonary artery** and the **ductus arteriosus** (distal part). Failure of the ductus to close after birth produces a **patent ductus arteriosus (PDA)**, connecting the **aortic arch (just distal to the left subclavian) to the left pulmonary artery**.

## Trigger Point

> Vascular defect between the **aortic arch and pulmonary artery** = **PDA**.

## Derivatives of the Aortic Arches

| Arch | Derivative |
|---|---|
| **1st** | Maxillary artery |
| **2nd** | Hyoid artery and stapedial artery |
| **3rd** | Common carotid artery and proximal part of internal carotid artery |
| **4th** | **Left:** arch of aorta (between left common carotid and left subclavian)<br>**Right:** proximal part of right subclavian artery |
| **5th** | Rudimentary or absent, no derivatives |
| **6th** | **Left:** proximal left pulmonary artery and **ductus arteriosus**<br>**Right:** proximal right pulmonary artery (distal part regresses) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 4th right**
Forms the **proximal right subclavian artery**. Its defects cause an **aberrant right subclavian artery (dysphagia lusoria)**, not a communication with the pulmonary artery.

**B. 4th left**
Forms the **arch of the aorta** (between the left common carotid and left subclavian). Its defects cause an **interrupted aortic arch**, not PDA.

**C. 6th right**
Forms the **proximal right pulmonary artery**, and its distal part **regresses**. It does not form a ductus.

## Mind Capsule

> PDA = failure of closure of the ductus arteriosus (**left 6th arch**).
> 6th arch: **left** gives pulmonary artery + ductus, **right** gives pulmonary artery only.
