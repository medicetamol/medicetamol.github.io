# Ureter

**Transitional epithelium (urothelium)** has multiple layers of cells with large, dome-shaped **umbrella (superficial) cells**, allowing distension. It lines the **renal pelvis, ureters, urinary bladder and proximal urethra**.

## Key Finding

> Multilayered epithelium with dome-shaped **umbrella cells** at the surface = transitional epithelium.

## Trigger Point

> Umbrella cells at the free surface.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Gallbladder**
Lined by **simple columnar epithelium** with microvilli and no goblet cells.
It is a single layer, unlike the multilayered picture.

**C. Trachea**
Lined by **pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium** with goblet cells.
It shows cilia and no umbrella cells.

**D. Intestine**
Lined by **simple columnar epithelium** with goblet cells and microvilli.
It is also single-layered.

## Mind Capsule

> Umbrella cells = transitional epithelium = ureter, bladder, renal pelvis.
