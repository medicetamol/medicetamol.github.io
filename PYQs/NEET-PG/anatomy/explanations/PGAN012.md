# 9th Cranial Nerve

The **gag reflex** has its **afferent limb in the glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX)**, from the posterior pharyngeal wall and tonsillar area. The **efferent limb is the vagus nerve (CN X)**, which causes contraction of the pharyngeal muscles. The question asks for the nerve required for the sensory arm.

## Trigger Point

> Nerve **required** for the gag reflex = sensory (afferent) limb = **CN IX**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 10th cranial nerve**
The vagus forms the **efferent (motor) limb** of the reflex.
In this question the afferent nerve is the one expected, so CN IX is the better answer.

**C. 11th cranial nerve**
The accessory nerve supplies sternocleidomastoid and trapezius.
It has no role in the gag reflex.

**D. 12th cranial nerve**
The hypoglossal nerve supplies the intrinsic and extrinsic tongue muscles (except palatoglossus).
It does not participate in the gag reflex.

## Mind Capsule

> Gag reflex: afferent = IX, efferent = X. Palatal / uvular reflex has the same pathway.
> Carotid sinus reflex: afferent = IX, efferent = X.
