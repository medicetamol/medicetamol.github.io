# Hypoglossal Nerve

The **jugular foramen** lies between the petrous temporal and occipital bones. It transmits the **glossopharyngeal (IX), vagus (X) and accessory (XI) nerves**, along with the **inferior petrosal sinus, sigmoid sinus continuing as the internal jugular vein** and meningeal branches. The **hypoglossal nerve (XII)** exits through the separate **hypoglossal canal**.

## Trigger Point

> Which does **NOT** pass through the jugular foramen.

## Why the Others Are True

**B. Accessory nerve**
The cranial root joins the spinal root and passes through the jugular foramen.
It exits along with IX and X.

**C. Glossopharyngeal nerve**
It passes through the anterior part of the foramen with the inferior petrosal sinus.
It is a classic content of the jugular foramen.

**D. Vagus nerve**
It runs through the middle compartment of the foramen along with XI.
It descends into the carotid sheath from here.

## Mind Capsule

> Jugular foramen = **IX, X, XI** + IJV; hypoglossal canal = **XII**.
> Vernet's syndrome = IX, X, XI palsy (jugular foramen lesion).
