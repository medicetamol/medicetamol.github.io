# General Somatic Efferent

The vagus nerve carries **five** functional components: **GVA** (thoracic and abdominal viscera), **GVE** (parasympathetic to viscera), **SVE** (pharyngeal and laryngeal muscles), **GSA** (external ear and meatus, via the auricular branch) and **SVA** (taste from the epiglottis). **General somatic efferent (GSE)** fibres supply muscles derived from somites and are found only in CN III, IV, VI and XII.

## Trigger Point

> Which is **NOT** a functional component of the vagus nerve.

## Why the Others Are True

**A. General visceral afferent**
The vagus carries sensation from the heart, lungs, and gut up to the splenic flexure.
It also forms the afferent limb of many visceral reflexes.

**C. General visceral efferent**
Parasympathetic fibres supply smooth muscle and glands of the thorax and abdomen.
This is the main autonomic role of the vagus.

**D. General somatic afferent**
The **auricular branch (Arnold's nerve)** carries general sensation from the external ear and meatus.
This explains cough on ear syringing.

## Mind Capsule

> GSE nerves = **III, IV, VI, XII** (muscles from somites); vagus has GVA, GVE, SVE, GSA, SVA but no GSE.
