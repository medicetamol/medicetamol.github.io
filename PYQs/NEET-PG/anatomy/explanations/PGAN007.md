# Greater Auricular Nerve

The **greater auricular nerve (C2, C3)** is the largest ascending branch of the cervical plexus. It supplies skin over the **parotid region, angle of the mandible, and the lobule and lower auricle**, so lesions or swellings here (e.g. parotid tail) are felt as pain in this area.

## Trigger Point

> Swelling and pain **under the ear lobule**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Facial nerve**
A motor nerve to the muscles of facial expression; it has no cutaneous supply to this area.
Its involvement would cause facial weakness, not pain over the lobule.

**C. Auriculotemporal nerve**
A branch of V3 supplying the skin of the **tragus, anterior auricle and temple**.
It does not supply the skin below the lobule.

**D. Investing layer of deep cervical fascia**
A connective tissue layer that splits to enclose the parotid gland, forming its tough capsule.
It is not a nerve, so it cannot carry pain sensation as such.

## Mind Capsule

> Lower ear / lobule / angle of mandible = **C2-C3 (greater auricular)**; tragus and anterior ear = auriculotemporal (V3).
