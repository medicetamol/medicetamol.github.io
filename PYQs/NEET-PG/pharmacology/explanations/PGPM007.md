# Liraglutide

**Liraglutide** (GLP-1 receptor agonist, higher dose) is approved for **long-term weight management**, reducing appetite and slowing gastric emptying. **Sibutramine** and **fenfluramine** were withdrawn for cardiovascular risk and valvular heart disease. Other long-term options include **orlistat** and semaglutide.

## Trigger Point

> **Long-term** obesity management.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Sibutramine**
Serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor withdrawn due to cardiovascular events.
It is no longer used.

**B. Fenfluramine**
Serotonin releaser withdrawn for valvular heart disease and pulmonary hypertension.
It is no longer used for obesity.

**C. Metformin**
Used in type 2 diabetes and PCOS and may cause modest weight loss.
It is not approved for obesity.

## Mind Capsule

> Obesity drugs now: **orlistat, liraglutide, semaglutide, tirzepatide**; withdrawn: sibutramine, fenfluramine, rimonabant.
