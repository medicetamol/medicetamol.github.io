# Losartan Blocks Thromboxane A2 Receptors

**Losartan** has extra properties: it is **uricosuric** (blocks URAT1) and it **antagonizes thromboxane A2 receptors**, inhibiting platelet aggregation. Telmisartan and others do not share this action.

## Trigger Point

> **True statement** about ARBs.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Telmisartan**
Excreted mainly in bile, so dose reduction is needed in **hepatic** (biliary) impairment, not routinely in renal failure.
The statement reverses this.

**B. Irbesartan**
Mainly hepatic metabolism, but the standard teaching is that no dose adjustment is required in mild-moderate hepatic or renal impairment.
The statement is incorrect.

**C. Candesartan**
Needs caution in hepatic and renal impairment (a prodrug), and the statement that it spares renal failure is incorrect.
It is not the true statement.

## Mind Capsule

> Losartan = **uricosuric + TXA2 receptor block** (unique among ARBs); candesartan is a prodrug.
