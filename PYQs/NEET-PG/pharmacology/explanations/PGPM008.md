# Clotrimazole

**Oral candidiasis (thrush)** is a complication of **inhaled corticosteroids** (local immunosuppression), seen as white patches on the tongue and buccal mucosa. **Topical azoles (clotrimazole troches) or nystatin** treat mild cases. Rinsing the mouth or using a spacer prevents it.

## Trigger Point

> Asthmatic on **inhaled steroids** + **white patches** in the mouth.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Griseofulvin**
Treats dermatophyte infections (tinea) by disrupting microtubules.
It is not effective against *Candida*.

**C. Terbinafine**
An allylamine used for dermatophytosis and onychomycosis.
It is not used for oral thrush.

**D. Flucytosine**
Used in combination with amphotericin B for cryptococcal meningitis.
It is not first-line for thrush.

## Mind Capsule

> Thrush = **nystatin or clotrimazole** (topical), fluconazole if severe; dermatophytes = terbinafine or griseofulvin.
