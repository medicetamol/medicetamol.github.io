# Metoclopramide

**Metoclopramide** is a **D2 antagonist and 5-HT4 agonist**, which **increases lower oesophageal sphincter (LES) tone and gastric emptying** (a prokinetic). It is used in GERD with delayed emptying and in nausea and vomiting. Adverse effects include **extrapyramidal symptoms** and hyperprolactinaemia.

## Trigger Point

> GERD, drug that **contracts the LES** and **increases gastric emptying**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Pantoprazole**
A proton pump inhibitor reducing acid secretion.
It has no prokinetic action.

**C. Vonoprazan**
A potassium-competitive acid blocker, which suppresses acid.
It does not change motility.

**D. Sodium alginate**
Forms a raft over gastric contents to block reflux.
It does not affect LES tone or emptying.

## Mind Capsule

> Prokinetics: **metoclopramide, domperidone, cisapride, mosapride, prucalopride**; domperidone does not cross the BBB.
