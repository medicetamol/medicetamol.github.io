# Clarithromycin

**Clarithromycin** increases **serum digoxin** by inhibiting **P-glycoprotein** and by killing gut flora (*Eubacterium lentum*) that inactivate digoxin, leading to toxicity. Other drugs that raise digoxin include **amiodarone, verapamil, quinidine, spironolactone** and erythromycin.

## Trigger Point

> **Raised serum digoxin** after adding a concomitant drug.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Atenolol**
A β-blocker that slows the ventricular rate but does not raise digoxin levels.
It may add to bradycardia.

**C. KCl**
Potassium **reduces** digoxin toxicity by competing at the Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase site.
Hypokalaemia would increase toxicity.

**D. Triamterene**
A potassium-sparing diuretic that does not increase digoxin levels significantly.
It is not a major P-gp inhibitor.

## Mind Capsule

> Digoxin level ↑ by **macrolides, amiodarone, verapamil, quinidine**; toxicity worsened by hypokalaemia.
