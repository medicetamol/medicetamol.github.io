# Amiodarone

**Amiodarone** is a class III antiarrhythmic used in ventricular arrhythmias after MI. Its long-term toxicities include **pulmonary fibrosis**, **hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism** (fatigue, weight gain), **corneal microdeposits**, **blue-grey skin**, hepatotoxicity and photosensitivity.

## Trigger Point

> Post-MI ventricular arrhythmia drug + **pulmonary fibrosis** + fatigue and weight gain (hypothyroidism).

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Atenolol**
A β-blocker causing bradycardia, fatigue and bronchospasm.
It does not cause pulmonary fibrosis.

**C. Aspirin**
An antiplatelet causing GI bleeding and rarely bronchospasm.
It does not cause pulmonary fibrosis.

**D. Spironolactone**
A potassium-sparing diuretic causing hyperkalaemia and gynaecomastia.
It is not linked to lung fibrosis.

## Mind Capsule

> Amiodarone = **lungs, liver, thyroid, cornea, skin**; check TFTs, LFTs and baseline chest X-ray.
