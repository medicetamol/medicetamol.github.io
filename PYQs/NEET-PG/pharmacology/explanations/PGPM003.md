# Rifampicin

**Rifampicin** is a potent **CYP450 (CYP3A4) inducer**, increasing metabolism of oestrogen and progestin, so **oral contraceptives fail**. Barrier methods or a non-hormonal IUD are advised. It also reduces levels of warfarin, steroids, protease inhibitors and others.

## Trigger Point

> Antitubercular drug that reduces the effect of **OCPs**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ethambutol**
Causes optic neuritis and does not induce enzymes.
It has no effect on OCPs.

**C. Isoniazid**
An enzyme **inhibitor** (e.g. phenytoin levels rise).
It does not reduce contraceptive efficacy.

**D. Pyrazinamide**
Causes hyperuricaemia and hepatotoxicity.
It does not affect hepatic enzymes.

## Mind Capsule

> Rifampicin = **inducer** (OCP failure, red body fluids); isoniazid = inhibitor.
