# Omapatrilat

**Omapatrilat** is a **vasopeptidase inhibitor** that blocks both **ACE** and **neprilysin**. It was withdrawn because of excess **angioedema** (accumulation of bradykinin). **Sacubitril** inhibits only neprilysin, and is combined with valsartan in heart failure.

## Key Finding

> Flow diagram of the RAAS and natriuretic peptide pathways with a junction marked where two enzyme steps converge (ACE and neprilysin).

## Trigger Point

> Drug acting on **both** marked areas.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Sacubitril**
Inhibits **neprilysin** alone.
It acts at only one of the two sites.

**C. Losartan**
An AT1 receptor blocker acting at the receptor, not on the enzymes.
It does not inhibit ACE or neprilysin.

**D. Nesiritide**
Recombinant BNP, given intravenously in acute heart failure.
It is an agonist, not an enzyme inhibitor.

## Mind Capsule

> Omapatrilat = **ACE + neprilysin** (angioedema limited use); sacubitril = neprilysin only (given with valsartan).
