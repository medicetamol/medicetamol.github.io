# Inhibition of Dihydrofolate Reductase

**Methotrexate** is a folate analogue that inhibits **dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR)**, reducing tetrahydrofolate and thus **purine and thymidylate synthesis**. It is **S-phase specific**. Toxicity is reversed by **leucovorin (folinic acid) rescue**.

## Trigger Point

> **Mechanism of action** of methotrexate.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Inhibits cell replication acting on G phase**
Methotrexate acts mainly in **S phase**, not a G phase.
Vinca alkaloids act in M phase.

**C. Inhibits pyrimidine synthesis**
This is the profile of **leflunomide** (DHODH inhibition).
Methotrexate reduces purine and thymidylate synthesis through DHFR.

**D. Inhibits thymidylate synthase**
This is the mechanism of **5-fluorouracil**.
Methotrexate's effect on thymidylate is indirect.

## Mind Capsule

> Methotrexate = **DHFR inhibitor** (rescue: leucovorin); 5-FU = thymidylate synthase inhibitor; leflunomide = DHODH.
