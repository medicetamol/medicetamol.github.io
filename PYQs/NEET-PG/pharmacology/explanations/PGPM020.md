# IL-5 Receptor

**Benralizumab** is a monoclonal antibody against the **α subunit of the IL-5 receptor (IL-5Rα)** on eosinophils. It causes **antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity** and depletion of eosinophils. It is used in **severe eosinophilic asthma**.

## Trigger Point

> Receptor target of **benralizumab**.

## Biologics in Asthma

| Drug | Target |
|---|---|
| Omalizumab | IgE |
| Mepolizumab, reslizumab | IL-5 (ligand) |
| Benralizumab | IL-5 receptor α |
| Dupilumab | IL-4 receptor α (IL-4/IL-13) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. IL4**
The IL-4 receptor is the target of **dupilumab**.
Not benralizumab.

**C. IL1**
Targeted by anakinra and canakinumab.
Not used in asthma.

**D. TNF alpha**
Targeted by infliximab, adalimumab, etc.
Not used for asthma.

## Mind Capsule

> Benralizumab = **IL-5 receptor**; mepolizumab = IL-5 itself; omalizumab = IgE.
