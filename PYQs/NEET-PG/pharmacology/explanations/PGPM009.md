# Carboprost

**Carboprost (15-methyl PGF2α)** causes **bronchospasm** and is **contraindicated in asthma**. Other uterotonics are safer here. **Methylergometrine** is avoided in hypertension and pre-eclampsia, and **dinoprostone/misoprostol** do not cause bronchospasm.

## Trigger Point

> **Asthmatic** woman, drug to avoid in **postpartum haemorrhage**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Oxytocin**
First-line uterotonic with no bronchospasm; can cause water intoxication and hypotension with fast IV bolus.
It is safe in asthma.

**C. Dinoprostone**
PGE2 with bronchodilator effect, used for induction and cervical ripening.
It is not contraindicated in asthma.

**D. Methyl ergometrine**
Avoided in **hypertension, pre-eclampsia and coronary disease**, not in asthma.
It is not the drug to avoid in this stem.

## Mind Capsule

> PPH drugs: **carboprost = asthma avoid**; **methylergometrine = hypertension avoid**; oxytocin is safest.
