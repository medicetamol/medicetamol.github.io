# BPaLM

The **BPaLM regimen** (**B**edaquiline, **P**retomanid, **L**inezolid, **M**oxifloxacin) is the WHO-recommended **6-month all-oral regimen for MDR/RR-TB**. In HIV patients, ART is given as well, and monitoring for **IRIS**, drug interactions and toxicities (QT prolongation, myelosuppression, neuropathy) is needed.

## Trigger Point

> **HIV + MDR-TB**, appropriate **regimen** and **monitoring**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. INH + levofloxacin + pyrazinamide + ethambutol**
Contains INH, which is ineffective in MDR-TB.
It is not a recognized MDR regimen.

**C. INH + levofloxacin + streptomycin + ethionamide**
An older, injectable-containing regimen with INH.
It has been replaced by all-oral regimens.

**D. INH + clarithromycin + pyrazinamide + ethambutol**
Clarithromycin is not an anti-TB drug for MDR-TB, and INH is inactive in MDR.
It is an inappropriate combination.

## Mind Capsule

> MDR/RR-TB = **BPaLM for 6 months**; MDR-TB = resistance to rifampicin + isoniazid.
