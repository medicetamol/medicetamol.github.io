# Migraine

**Topiramate** is an antiepileptic effective in **migraine prophylaxis**. Adverse effects include **weight loss, paraesthesias, renal stones (carbonic anhydrase inhibition), cognitive slowing, acute angle-closure glaucoma and teratogenicity**.

## Trigger Point

> **Prophylaxis** indication for topiramate.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. LGS**
Topiramate is one of the drugs used in Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, but the classic prophylaxis indication asked is migraine.
The question asks about prophylaxis.

**C. ADHD**
Treated with methylphenidate, atomoxetine and clonidine.
Topiramate is not indicated.

**D. Dementia**
Treated with cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine.
Topiramate has no role.

## Mind Capsule

> Migraine prophylaxis: **propranolol, topiramate, valproate, amitriptyline, flunarizine**.
