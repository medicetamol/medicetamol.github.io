# Allopurinol

**Allopurinol** inhibits **xanthine oxidase**, decreasing the synthesis of uric acid (hypoxanthine → xanthine → uric acid). It is used for **prophylaxis of recurrent gout** and in tumour lysis syndrome. Do not start during an acute attack. **HLA-B*5801** predisposes to severe hypersensitivity.

## Trigger Point

> Therapy that **inhibits uric acid synthesis** in recurrent gout.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Probenecid**
A **uricosuric** that increases excretion by blocking URAT1 and OAT.
It does not inhibit synthesis.

**B. Colchicine**
Treats acute attacks by inhibiting microtubule polymerization and neutrophil migration.
It does not affect uric acid levels.

**C. Diclofenac**
An NSAID used for acute attack pain and inflammation.
It does not change uric acid synthesis.

## Mind Capsule

> Allopurinol/febuxostat = ↓ synthesis; probenecid = ↑ excretion; colchicine/NSAIDs = acute attack.
