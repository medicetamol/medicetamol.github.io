# Oxidizes Uric Acid (Uricase)

**Pegloticase** is a **pegylated recombinant uricase**. It converts **uric acid to allantoin**, which is highly soluble. It is used in refractory chronic gout. **Rasburicase** is a similar uricase used in **tumour lysis syndrome**. Both are contraindicated in **G6PD deficiency** (haemolysis and methaemoglobinaemia).

## Trigger Point

> **Mechanism of pegloticase** for raised uric acid.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Xanthine oxidase inhibition**
Mechanism of **allopurinol and febuxostat**, reducing synthesis.
Pegloticase destroys existing uric acid.

**B. URAT-1 inhibition**
Mechanism of **lesinurad and probenecid-like uricosurics**, increasing excretion.
It is not the action of pegloticase.

**D. Excretion of uric acid**
Pegloticase does not increase renal excretion of uric acid.
It converts it to a more soluble product.

## Mind Capsule

> Uricases (**rasburicase, pegloticase**) = uric acid → allantoin; avoid in G6PD deficiency.
