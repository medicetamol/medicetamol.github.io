# Rebound Hypertension

Abrupt withdrawal of **clonidine** (α2 agonist) causes a surge in sympathetic outflow, producing **rebound hypertension** with headache, tachycardia, sweating and anxiety. Therapy is tapered gradually, and the combination with **β-blockers** worsens the rebound.

## Trigger Point

> **Stopped clonidine** + headache.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Postural hypotension**
A side effect of α-blockers and during treatment with clonidine, not on stopping it.
It would not explain a headache after withdrawal.

**B. Receptor upregulation**
Contributes to the mechanism of rebound, but it is the underlying change, not the clinical event asked.
The question asks for the reason behind the headache.

**D. Receptor hypersensitivity**
Another mechanistic explanation, but the clinical effect is rebound hypertension.
The best answer is the resulting condition.

## Mind Capsule

> Abrupt stop of **clonidine / β-blockers** = rebound hypertension; taper over days.
