# IL-6 Receptor Blockade

**Tocilizumab** is a humanized monoclonal antibody against the **IL-6 receptor (IL-6R)**, blocking IL-6 signalling. It is used in **rheumatoid arthritis, giant cell arteritis, systemic JIA, cytokine release syndrome** and severe COVID-19. Infections and raised lipids are important adverse effects.

## Trigger Point

> **Mechanism of action** of tocilizumab.

## Biologics and Targets

| Drug | Target |
|---|---|
| Tocilizumab, sarilumab | IL-6 receptor |
| Anakinra, canakinumab | IL-1 |
| Infliximab, adalimumab | TNF-α |
| Secukinumab | IL-17A |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Inhibits binding of IL-1**
This is the action of **anakinra** (IL-1 receptor antagonist) and canakinumab.
Tocilizumab acts on the IL-6 axis.

**C. TNF alpha inhibition**
Done by infliximab, adalimumab, etanercept and certolizumab.
Not tocilizumab.

**D. IL-18 inhibition**
Not a standard action of any commonly used biologic like this.
It is not the target of tocilizumab.

## Mind Capsule

> Tocilizumab = **IL-6R**; anakinra = IL-1; infliximab = TNF-α.
