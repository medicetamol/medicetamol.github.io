# Start ATT, Then ART After 2 Weeks

In **HIV with TB**, **ATT is started first**, and **ART is added within 2-8 weeks**. Guidelines recommend starting ART **within about 2 weeks of ATT** when CD4 is < 50/µL, and within 8 weeks for others. This balances **survival benefit** against the risk of **IRIS** and overlapping toxicity.

## Trigger Point

> HIV patient who develops **TB**, timing of ART.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Start ART and ATT simultaneously**
Increases pill burden, overlapping toxicity and **IRIS** risk.
It is not recommended.

**C. Start ART followed by ATT**
Untreated active TB would worsen, and delaying ATT raises mortality.
TB treatment is the priority.

**D. ART alone**
Does not treat TB; ATT is essential.
It leaves active TB untreated.

## Mind Capsule

> **ATT first, ART within 2 weeks (CD4 < 50) and within 8 weeks otherwise**; exception: TB meningitis, ART delayed.
