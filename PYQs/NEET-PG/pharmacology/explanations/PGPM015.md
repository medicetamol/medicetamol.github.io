# Oprelvekin (IL-11)

**Oprelvekin (recombinant IL-11)** stimulates megakaryocyte growth and **increases platelet production**, used for **chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia**. Adverse effects include fluid retention, oedema and atrial arrhythmias. **Romiplostim and eltrombopag** are other platelet-raising agents.

## Trigger Point

> **Low platelets** after chemotherapy.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Filgrastim**
G-CSF, which increases neutrophils.
It is used for febrile neutropenia.

**C. Erythropoietin**
Raises red cells in anaemia of chronic kidney disease and chemotherapy.
It does not increase platelets.

**D. Amifostine**
A cytoprotective agent to reduce nephrotoxicity of cisplatin and xerostomia from radiation.
It does not raise platelets.

## Mind Capsule

> Platelets: **IL-11 (oprelvekin), romiplostim, eltrombopag**; neutrophils: G-CSF; RBC: EPO.
