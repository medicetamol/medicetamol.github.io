# Sodium Bicarbonate to Alkalinize Urine

In **salicylate poisoning**, **sodium bicarbonate** alkalinizes blood and urine. Salicylic acid, a weak acid, becomes ionized in alkaline urine and is trapped there (**ion trapping**), increasing its excretion. Severe cases need **haemodialysis**.

## Trigger Point

> **100 aspirin tablets** overdose.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. N-acetylcysteine**
Antidote for **paracetamol** poisoning, replenishing glutathione.
It is not used for salicylates.

**B. Pralidoxime**
Reactivates cholinesterase in **organophosphate** poisoning.
It has no role here.

**C. Glucagon**
Used for **β-blocker** overdose to treat bradycardia and hypotension.
Salicylate poisoning typically causes hypoglycaemia in CNS, treated with glucose.

## Mind Capsule

> Salicylate overdose = **alkalinize urine** (bicarbonate), glucose, dialysis for severe toxicity; paracetamol = NAC.
