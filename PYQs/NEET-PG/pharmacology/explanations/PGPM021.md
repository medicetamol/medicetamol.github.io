# Propranolol

For **migraine prophylaxis**, **propranolol** (β-blocker) is a first-line drug, and is particularly appropriate for patients in whom triptans and ergots (vasoconstrictors) are unsuitable, e.g. with **coronary artery disease risk**. Other options are topiramate, valproate and amitriptyline.

## Trigger Point

> Migraine **prophylaxis** + **family history of CAD**, previously on sumatriptan.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Topiramate**
A good prophylactic option, but propranolol is the classic first-line pick and cardioprotective in this setting.
Topiramate has cognitive and renal stone side effects.

**C. Ergotamine**
A vasoconstrictor used for **acute** attacks and **contraindicated in CAD**.
It is not for prophylaxis.

**D. Naratriptan**
A triptan used for acute abortive therapy, and contraindicated in coronary disease.
It is not for prophylaxis.

## Mind Capsule

> Triptans and ergots = **acute** migraine, avoid in CAD; prophylaxis = **propranolol, topiramate, valproate, amitriptyline**.
