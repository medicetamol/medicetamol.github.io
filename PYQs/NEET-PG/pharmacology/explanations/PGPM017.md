# 80 Hours

The concentration must fall from **4 to 1**, i.e. to one-quarter. That takes **two half-lives** (4 → 2 → 1). With a half-life of 40 hours, the time is 2 × 40 = **80 hours**.

## Working

> Start = 4, target = 1
> 4 → 2 (1 half-life) → 1 (2 half-lives)
> 2 × 40 h = **80 hours**

## Trigger Point

> Concentration **4× the therapeutic level**, half-life **40 hours**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. 40 hours**
One half-life only brings the level to 2, still twice the therapeutic level.
It is too short.

**C. 120 hours**
Three half-lives would reduce the level to 0.5, below the therapeutic value.
It is longer than needed.

**D. 140-180 hours**
Corresponds to 3.5-4.5 half-lives, far below the therapeutic value.
This is unnecessarily long.

## Mind Capsule

> Each half-life halves the concentration: 4 → 1 = **2 half-lives**; steady state or elimination takes about 4-5 half-lives.
