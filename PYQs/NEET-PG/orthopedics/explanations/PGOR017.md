# MRI Sacroiliac Joints

**Axial spondyloarthritis** can have a normal radiograph in the early (non-radiographic) phase. **MRI of the sacroiliac joints** (STIR) detects active inflammation as bone marrow oedema years before X-ray changes appear.

## Trigger Point

> Young man with **inflammatory back pain** and **anterior uveitis**, with a **normal lumbar spine X-ray**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. CT spine**
Shows structural change (erosions, sclerosis) that appears late, and adds radiation.

**C. X-ray thoracolumbar spine**
Changes appear late, and the sacroiliac joints are the earliest site.

**D. Bone scan**
Low sensitivity and specificity for early sacroiliitis.

## Mind Capsule

> Early axial SpA with a normal X-ray = MRI of the sacroiliac joints.
