# Fibrous Dysplasia

A developmental disorder (GNAS mutation) in which medullary bone is replaced by fibro-osseous tissue. The bone is expanded with a **ground-glass** texture, and the proximal femur bends into a **shepherd's crook deformity**. Polyostotic disease with **café-au-lait spots** and precocious puberty is **McCune-Albright syndrome**.

## Trigger Point

> Child with limb deformity and **hyperpigmented skin lesions**; X-ray shows an expanded bone with **ground-glass** texture.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Non-ossifying fibroma**
Eccentric metaphyseal lytic lesion with a sclerotic scalloped border, asymptomatic, with no skin lesions.

**C. Paget's disease**
Occurs in the elderly, with thickened coarse bone and ↑ ALP.

**D. Osteogenesis imperfecta**
Brittle, osteopenic bones with blue sclerae and multiple fractures, not café-au-lait spots.

## Mind Capsule

> Ground-glass bone + shepherd's crook + café-au-lait spots = Fibrous dysplasia (McCune-Albright).
