# Carpal Tunnel Syndrome and Durkan's Test

**Median nerve** compression under the flexor retinaculum: night-time numbness and tingling of the thumb, index, middle and radial half of the ring finger, relieved by shaking or hanging the arm. **Durkan's carpal compression test** is the most sensitive provocative test; Phalen's and Tinel's are also used.

## Trigger Point

> Night-time numbness and tingling of the **lateral 3 digits**, relieved by **hanging the arm down**.

## Provocative Tests

| Test | Condition |
|---|---|
| Durkan's, Phalen's, Tinel's | Carpal tunnel syndrome (median nerve) |
| Froment's | Ulnar nerve palsy (adductor pollicis) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Guyon's canal syndrome, Froment's test**
Guyon's canal is the ulnar nerve at the wrist, involving the medial digits.

**B. Carpal tunnel syndrome, Froment's test**
Correct diagnosis, but Froment's test examines the ulnar nerve.

**C. Guyon's canal syndrome, Durkan's test**
Wrong disease for the lateral 3 digits, and Durkan's tests the median nerve.

## Mind Capsule

> Nocturnal lateral 3½-digit paraesthesia = Carpal tunnel syndrome; confirm with Durkan's test.
