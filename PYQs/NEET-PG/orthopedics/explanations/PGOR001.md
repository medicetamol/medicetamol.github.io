# Ankylosing Spondylitis

An HLA-B27-associated **seronegative spondyloarthropathy** of young men: inflammatory back pain with **morning stiffness** (improves with exercise, worse with rest), sacroiliitis, **reduced chest expansion** and **anterior uveitis** (red eye). X-ray evolves from sacroiliitis to syndesmophytes and bamboo spine.

## Trigger Point

> 26-year-old male with backache, **morning stiffness**, **reduced chest expansion** and **reddening of eyes**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Rheumatoid arthritis**
Symmetric small-joint polyarthritis, commoner in women, with RF or anti-CCP positivity. It spares the sacroiliac joints.

**C. Paget's disease**
Elderly patients with bone pain, ↑ ALP and skull enlargement.

**D. Osteopetrosis**
Dense "marble" bones with fractures and marrow failure. It does not cause inflammatory back pain or uveitis.

## Mind Capsule

> Young male + inflammatory back pain + ↓ chest expansion + uveitis = Ankylosing spondylitis (HLA-B27).
