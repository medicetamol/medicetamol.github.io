# Radial Nerve

Radial nerve palsy causes **wrist drop** (loss of wrist and finger extension). A **dynamic cock-up splint** with finger-extension outriggers holds the wrist extended and assists finger extension while the nerve recovers.

## Trigger Point

> Wrist splint with straps and **finger extension supports** connected by a cord and pulley.

## Splints in Nerve Palsy

| Nerve | Deformity | Splint |
|---|---|---|
| Radial | Wrist drop | Cock-up splint (dynamic) |
| Ulnar | Claw hand | Knuckle-duster splint |
| Median | Ape thumb | Opponens splint |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Median nerve**
Needs a splint that keeps the thumb opposed.

**C. Ulnar nerve**
Claw hand is prevented by a knuckle-duster splint that blocks MCP hyperextension.

**D. Volkmann's ischemic contracture**
A flexion contracture from forearm ischaemia, managed by stretching and serial splinting, not an extension-assist splint.

## Mind Capsule

> Wrist drop (radial nerve) = cock-up splint with finger extension assist.
