# Vitamin D Deficiency (Rickets)

Vitamin D deficiency impairs mineralisation of osteoid and growth plate cartilage. The child shows **bowed legs**, wrist widening and rachitic rosary. Radiographs show **widened, frayed, cupped metaphyses**. ALP is raised.

## Trigger Point

> Radiograph with **outward bowing of the leg bones** and **widened, frayed growth ends**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Vitamin A**
Deficiency gives night blindness and xerophthalmia, not rachitic bone changes.

**C. Vitamin K**
Deficiency causes bleeding (haemorrhagic disease of the newborn).

**D. Vitamin C**
Scurvy causes subperiosteal haemorrhage and a Wimberger ring, not bowing with a frayed, cupped metaphysis.

## Mind Capsule

> Bowed legs + widened, frayed, cupped metaphyses = Rickets (vitamin D).
