# Perthes Disease

Idiopathic avascular necrosis of the capital femoral epiphysis, usually in boys aged 4-8 years (range 2-12). It presents as a **painless limp** with limited abduction and internal rotation. X-ray shows sclerosis, flattening and fragmentation of the femoral head.

## Common Paediatric Hip Disorders

| Age | Disorder |
|---|---|
| Infant | DDH |
| 4-8 years | Perthes disease |
| 10-16 years (often obese) | SCFE |

## Trigger Point

> Boy with a **painless limp** and an abnormal hip X-ray.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. DDH**
Presents in infants and toddlers, not at 11 years.

**C. TB hip**
Painful, with night cries, constitutional symptoms and muscle wasting.

**D. SCFE**
Usually an obese adolescent with pain in the hip or knee and limited internal rotation.

## Mind Capsule

> Painless limp in a child with femoral head sclerosis or flattening = Perthes disease.
