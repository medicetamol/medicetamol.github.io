# Ankylosing Spondylitis

Radiographic hallmarks are **bilateral symmetric sacroiliitis** (erosions, sclerosis, fusion) followed by **syndesmophytes**, thin vertical marginal bony bridges between vertebrae, giving the **bamboo spine**.

## Trigger Point

> Spinal radiograph with **bilateral sacroiliac changes** and **thin marginal bony bridges**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Rheumatoid arthritis**
Erosions of small peripheral joints and the cervical spine. Syndesmophytes and bamboo spine are not features.

**C. Paget's disease**
Coarse trabeculae, cortical thickening and a "picture frame" vertebra.

**D. Osteopetrosis**
Diffuse sclerosis ("rugger jersey" spine) without sacroiliac erosions or syndesmophytes.

## Mind Capsule

> Bilateral sacroiliitis + syndesmophytes = Ankylosing spondylitis (bamboo spine).
