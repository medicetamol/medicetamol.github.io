# Spondylolisthesis

Forward slip of one vertebral body over the one below. The lateral view shows a **step-off** of the anterior and posterior vertebral margins. Isthmic type (pars defect) affects L5-S1 in the young, while **degenerative** type affects L4-L5 in older women.

## Meyerding Grading

| Grade | Slip |
|---|---|
| I | 0-25% |
| II | 25-50% |
| III | 50-75% |
| IV | 75-100% |
| V | More than 100% (spondyloptosis) |

## Trigger Point

> 56-year-old woman with chronic low back pain; lateral lumbar X-ray shows **forward displacement of one vertebral body** with **malalignment of vertebral margins**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Spondylitis**
Inflammation or infection of the vertebrae (ankylosing spondylitis or TB), not a slip.

**C. Osteosarcoma**
A destructive or sclerotic bone-forming lesion with periosteal reaction.

**D. Vertebral fracture**
Loss of vertebral height or wedging, not translation of a whole vertebra.

## Mind Capsule

> Step-off of vertebral margins on a lateral view = Spondylolisthesis.
> Pars defect (spondylolysis) shows the "Scottie dog collar" on oblique view.
