# Ankylosing Spondylitis

A chronic inflammatory spondyloarthropathy beginning before 40 years: insidious low back pain with **early-morning stiffness** that improves with activity. **Enthesitis**, such as bilateral heel pain (plantar fascia or Achilles insertion), is a typical associated feature.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic low backache with **early morning stiffness** for 2 years **+** **bilateral heel pain**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. TB spine**
Localised pain with constitutional symptoms, kyphosis or gibbus and raised ESR. It lacks improvement with activity.

**C. Mechanical pain**
Worsens with activity and eases with rest, the opposite of this pattern.

**D. Disc prolapsed**
Radicular leg pain aggravated by bending or coughing. It is not bilateral heel enthesitis.

## Mind Capsule

> Young patient + inflammatory back pain + enthesitis (heel pain) = Ankylosing spondylitis.
