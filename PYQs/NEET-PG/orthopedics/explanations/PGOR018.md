# Paget's Disease of Bone

Disordered, accelerated bone remodelling in the elderly, involving the skull (**↑ hat size**), pelvis, femur, tibia and spine. Pagetic bone is vascular, so it feels **warm**. Biochemistry shows **markedly ↑ ALP** with normal calcium, phosphate and PTH. Treat with bisphosphonates.

## Biochemistry in Metabolic Bone Disease

| Disorder | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Paget's disease | Ca, PO₄, PTH normal; ALP markedly ↑ |
| Osteoporosis | All normal, including ALP |
| Osteomalacia and rickets | Ca ↓ or normal, PO₄ ↓, PTH ↑, ALP ↑ |
| Primary hyperparathyroidism | Ca ↑, PO₄ ↓, PTH ↑, ALP ↑ |

## Trigger Point

> **Increased hat size**, warm bones and **markedly ↑ ALP** with normal Ca, PO₄ and PTH.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Osteosarcoma**
A rare complication of Paget's disease, and a focal painful mass rather than diffuse disease.

**C. Multiple myeloma**
Punched-out lytic lesions, hypercalcaemia, anaemia and a normal ALP.

**D. Osteomalacia**
Low calcium or phosphate with ↑ PTH and Looser's zones.

## Mind Capsule

> Hat size ↑ + warm bones + isolated ALP ↑ = Paget's disease.
