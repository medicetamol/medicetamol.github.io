# Pseudogout (CPPD)

**Calcium pyrophosphate** crystal deposition: **rhomboid-shaped, weakly positively birefringent** crystals in synovial fluid. The knee is most often involved, and **chondrocalcinosis** appears on X-ray. Associations include hyperparathyroidism and haemochromatosis.

## Gout vs Pseudogout

| Feature | Gout | Pseudogout |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal | Monosodium urate | Calcium pyrophosphate |
| Shape | Needle | Rhomboid |
| Birefringence | Negative | Positive |

## Trigger Point

> Joint discomfort with **rhomboid-shaped, positively birefringent** crystals under polarised light.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Gout**
Needle-shaped, negatively birefringent urate crystals.

**B. Rheumatoid arthritis**
Inflammatory synovial fluid without crystals.

**D. Osteoarthritis**
Non-inflammatory fluid with low cell count and no crystals.

## Mind Capsule

> Rhomboid + positive birefringence = Pseudogout; needle + negative = Gout.

## Memory Hook

> **P for Pseudogout, P for Positive**: pseudogout crystals are positively birefringent and rhomboid.
