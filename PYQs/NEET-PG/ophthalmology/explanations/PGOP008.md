# Vitamin A (Retinoic Acid) Deficiency

**Vitamin A deficiency** causes **xerophthalmia**: drying of the conjunctiva and cornea, with night blindness. A dry, rough cornea is **corneal xerosis**.

## Trigger Point

> **Dry eyes** and **rough corneal surface**.

## WHO Classification of Xerophthalmia

| Stage | Feature |
|---|---|
| XN | Night blindness |
| X1A / X1B | Conjunctival xerosis / Bitot's spot |
| X2 | Corneal xerosis |
| X3A / X3B | Corneal ulcer or keratomalacia (under or over one-third) |
| XS / XF | Corneal scar / Xerophthalmic fundus |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Iron**
Deficiency causes anaemia, not corneal drying.

**B. Protein**
Protein-energy malnutrition is associated with vitamin A deficiency but does not itself cause xerosis.

**C. Niacin**
Deficiency causes pellagra (dermatitis, diarrhoea, dementia).

## Mind Capsule

> Night blindness → Bitot's spots → corneal xerosis → keratomalacia = **vitamin A deficiency**.
