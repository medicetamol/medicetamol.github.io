# Ethmoid Sinusitis as the Common Cause

**Orbital cellulitis** is infection **posterior to the orbital septum**. Its most common source is **ethmoid sinusitis**, spreading through the thin lamina papyracea.

## Trigger Point

> "Which is true about **orbital cellulitis**?"

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Preseptal | Orbital |
|---|---|---|
| Site | Anterior to septum | Posterior to septum |
| Proptosis | Absent | Present |
| Eye movements | Normal | Painful, restricted |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Anterior to the orbital septum**
This describes preseptal cellulitis.

**B. Topical antibiotics**
Not effective; needs IV broad-spectrum antibiotics.

**C. Normal pupil and extraocular movements**
Orbital cellulitis causes painful ophthalmoplegia, and vision may be affected.

## Mind Capsule

> Orbital cellulitis = **behind the septum**, from **ethmoid sinusitis**, painful ophthalmoplegia.
