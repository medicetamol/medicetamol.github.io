# Astigmatism

In **astigmatism**, the cornea or lens has **different powers in different meridians**, so a point of light focuses as **lines (Sturm's conoid)** and appears **streaked or radiating**.

## Trigger Point

> **Streaked, radiating** headlights at night.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Presbyopia**
Loss of accommodation causing difficulty at near.

**C. Hypermetropia**
Blur at near (and distance in higher grades), without streaking.

**D. Myopia**
Gives blurred distance vision with uniform halos.

## Mind Capsule

> Streaks and star-like distortion of light = **astigmatism**.
