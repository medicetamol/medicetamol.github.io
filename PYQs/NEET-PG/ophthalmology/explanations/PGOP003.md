# Spherical Spectacle Correction

Refractive surgery (**LASIK, femto-LASIK, ICL**) is **deferred until refraction is stable**, generally after **18 years or more**. At **15 years**, the keyed option is a **spherical (spherical-equivalent) correction**, which is easier to tolerate than a full cylindrical prescription.

## Trigger Point

> **15-year-old** with myopic astigmatism who prefers not to use full spectacles.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. ICL**
An implantable lens for stable, high refractive errors in adults.

**B. Femto-LASIK**
Needs a stable refraction and an age of 18 years or more.

**C. LASIK**
Same age and stability requirement.

## Mind Capsule

> Teenager with unstable refraction = **no refractive surgery**; wait until stable (about 18 years or more).
