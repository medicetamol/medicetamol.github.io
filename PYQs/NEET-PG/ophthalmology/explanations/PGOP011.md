# Latanoprost

**Latanoprost**, a **PGF2α analogue**, lowers intraocular pressure by increasing **uveoscleral outflow** and has **no bronchospasm**. It is the preferred drug in asthma, where **beta-blockers such as timolol are contraindicated**.

## Trigger Point

> **POAG** with **bronchial asthma**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Gemeprost**
A PGE1 analogue for cervical ripening and abortion.

**C. Carboprost**
A 15-methyl PGF2α used in postpartum haemorrhage; it **causes bronchospasm**.

**D. Alprostadil**
PGE1 used to keep the ductus open and for erectile dysfunction.

## Mind Capsule

> POAG + asthma = **prostaglandin analogue (latanoprost)**, avoid timolol.
