# Serum Ceruloplasmin

A **golden-brown ring at the corneal periphery** is the **Kayser-Fleischer ring** of **Wilson's disease**, due to **copper deposition in Descemet's membrane**. Serum ceruloplasmin (reduced) confirms the diagnosis.

## Trigger Point

> **Golden ring** on the iris/limbus of a 20-year-old.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Alpha-1 antitrypsin**
Deficiency causes liver and lung disease.

**B. Alpha-fetoprotein**
A tumour marker (hepatocellular and germ cell tumours).

**C. Serum iron**
Tested for haemochromatosis, which has no KF ring.

## Mind Capsule

> KF ring = **Wilson's disease** → low **ceruloplasmin**.
