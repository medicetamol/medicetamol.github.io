# Cysteine

In **homocystinuria (cystathionine synthase deficiency)**, homocysteine cannot be converted to cystathionine and then **cysteine**. Cysteine therefore becomes **essential** and must be supplied, while methionine is restricted.

## Trigger Point

> **Marfanoid** boy with **lens subluxation** and **cystathionine synthase deficiency**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Serine**
A non-essential amino acid; it combines with homocysteine to form cystathionine, so it is a substrate rather than a supplement.

**B. Tyrosine**
Becomes essential in phenylketonuria.

**C. Methionine**
Restricted, because it is the precursor of the accumulating homocysteine.

## Mind Capsule

> Homocystinuria: **low methionine, supplement cysteine** (plus B6, betaine); lens subluxation is downward and inward.
