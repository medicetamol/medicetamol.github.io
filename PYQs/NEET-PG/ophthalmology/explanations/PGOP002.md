# Giant Papillary Conjunctivitis

**Giant papillary conjunctivitis (GPC)** is a mechanical and immune-mediated reaction of the **upper tarsal conjunctiva** to **contact lenses, ocular prostheses or exposed sutures**. It produces large (over 1 mm) papillae.

## Trigger Point

> **Giant papillae** on the upper tarsal conjunctiva.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Trachoma**
Shows **follicles** with Arlt's line and pannus, not giant papillae.

**C. Vernal keratoconjunctivitis**
Can also show cobblestone papillae but has limbal Horner-Trantas dots and a seasonal course in young boys.

**D. OSSN**
A gelatinous or leukoplakic limbal growth, not papillae.

## Mind Capsule

> Giant papillae + contact lens or prosthesis = **GPC**; follicles = trachoma.
