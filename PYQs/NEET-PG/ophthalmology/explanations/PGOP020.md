# Pseudoexfoliation Syndrome

**Pseudoexfoliation** shows **white, dandruff-like flaky material** on the **anterior lens capsule** and pupillary margin. It is associated with **glaucoma, phacodonesis and zonular weakness**.

## Trigger Point

> **Dandruff-like deposits on the anterior lens surface** in an elderly patient.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Iris cyst**
A cystic lesion of the iris, not deposits on the lens.

**C. Persistent pupillary membrane**
Congenital strands from the iris collarette across the pupil.

**D. Pigment dispersion syndrome**
Pigment on the corneal endothelium (Krukenberg spindle) in young myopes.

## Mind Capsule

> Dandruff on the lens = **pseudoexfoliation** (risk of glaucoma, zonular weakness in surgery).
