# Exposure Keratitis

Incomplete eyelid closure (**lagophthalmos**) leaves the cornea unprotected, so it dries and develops **exposure keratitis**.

## Key Finding

> Incomplete lid closure with widened palpebral fissure and exposed ocular surface.

## Trigger Point

> **Incomplete eyelid closure** with exposed eye surface.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cataract**
A lens opacity, not related to lid closure.

**C. Difficulty in eye movement**
Would be a motility problem, not a lid problem.

**D. Glaucoma**
Raised intraocular pressure with optic nerve damage.

## Mind Capsule

> Lagophthalmos (facial palsy, proptosis) → **exposure keratitis**.
