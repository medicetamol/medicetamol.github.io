# Index Myopia

In **nuclear sclerosis**, the lens nucleus gains a **higher refractive index**, producing **index myopia**. Distance vision falls, while near vision improves without spectacles (**second sight**).

## Trigger Point

> **Poor distance vision** but **no need for near spectacles**.

## Types of Myopia

| Type | Cause |
|---|---|
| Axial | Increased axial length |
| Curvatural | Increased corneal or lens curvature |
| Index | Increased refractive index of the lens |
| Positional | Forward lens position |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Axial myopia**
Eyeball too long; does not give second sight.

**C. Curvatural myopia**
Steeper cornea (such as keratoconus) or lens.

**D. Positional myopia**
Lens displaced forward, for example after glaucoma surgery.

## Mind Capsule

> Nuclear sclerosis + second sight = **index myopia**.
