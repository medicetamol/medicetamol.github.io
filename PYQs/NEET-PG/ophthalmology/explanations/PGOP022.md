# Dermoid Cyst

A **dermoid cyst** is a **congenital choristoma** lined by epidermis with skin adnexa. It appears as a **painless, slowly growing mass** present since early life, often near the lateral canthus or brow.

## Trigger Point

> **Painless slow-growing** mass near the **lateral canthus** for **10 years**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Capillary haemangioma**
Appears in the first weeks of life, grows rapidly, then regresses.

**C. Lacrimal gland tumour**
Arises in the superotemporal orbit and pushes the eye down and in.

**D. Epidermoid cyst**
Lined only by epidermis; less common than a dermoid at this site.

## Mind Capsule

> Present since childhood + painless + slow = **dermoid cyst**.
