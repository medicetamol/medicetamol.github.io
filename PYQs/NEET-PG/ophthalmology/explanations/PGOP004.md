# MRI

A **hammer-and-chisel injury** suggests a **metallic (steel, magnetic) intraocular foreign body**. **MRI is contraindicated** because the magnet can move the fragment and damage the eye.

## Trigger Point

> **Hammer and chisel** + foreign body in the eye; "**detrimental** investigation".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. X-ray**
Safe and detects radio-opaque metallic foreign bodies.

**B. B-scan orbit**
Safe, useful when the media are opaque.

**C. CT scan**
Safe and the best test for detecting and localising an intraocular foreign body.

## Mind Capsule

> Metallic IOFB: **CT best, MRI contraindicated**.
