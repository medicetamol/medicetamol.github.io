# Limbal Dermoid

A **limbal dermoid** is a **congenital choristoma**, a firm yellowish-white nodule at the **limbus**, often inferotemporal, seen in children.

## Key Finding

> Yellowish fleshy dome-shaped nodule at the outer limbus with fine surface vessels.

## Trigger Point

> **5-year-old** with a **nodule at the limbus**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Pterygium**
A fleshy triangular growth over the cornea, usually nasal and in adults.

**B. Pinguecula**
A yellowish degenerative conjunctival nodule in adults.

**C. Dermolipoma**
A soft yellow mass in the temporal fornix region, not at the limbus.

## Mind Capsule

> Child + firm yellow nodule at the **limbus** = **limbal dermoid**.
