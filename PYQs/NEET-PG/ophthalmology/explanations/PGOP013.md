# Immature Senile Cataract

A **65-year-old** with **painless loss of vision** and a **grey-white opacity in the pupil** has **age-related (senile) cataract**. Treatment is surgical: **phacoemulsification with IOL implantation**.

## Trigger Point

> **Elderly woman** with **painless** visual loss and pupillary opacity.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Zonular cataract and lensectomy**
Zonular (lamellar) cataract is a developmental cataract in children.

**B. Rosette cataract and sugar control**
Rosette cataract follows blunt trauma, and glycaemic control alone does not clear an opacified lens.

**C. Sunflower cataract**
Occurs with an intraocular copper foreign body (chalcosis) or Wilson's disease.

## Mind Capsule

> Elderly + painless + grey pupil = **senile cataract → phaco + IOL**.
