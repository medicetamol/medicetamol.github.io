# Fuchs' Endothelial Dystrophy

**Fuchs' dystrophy** is an **endothelial dystrophy** with **guttae** on Descemet's membrane. Failure of the endothelial pump causes **corneal oedema** and finally **bullous keratopathy**.

## Trigger Point

> **Guttae** in one eye and **bullous keratopathy** in the other.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Interstitial keratitis**
Stromal inflammation with ghost vessels (syphilis).

**C. Viral corneal ulcer**
Typically a dendritic epithelial ulcer.

**D. Keratoconjunctivitis**
Inflammation of the cornea and conjunctiva, for example by adenovirus.

## Mind Capsule

> Guttae + bullous keratopathy = **Fuchs'**.
