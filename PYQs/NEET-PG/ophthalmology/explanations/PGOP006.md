# Buphthalmos (Congenital Glaucoma)

Raised intraocular pressure in an infant stretches the elastic eye wall, causing an **enlarged cornea** (over 12 mm), **watering, photophobia** and blepharospasm.

## Trigger Point

> **Watering + increased corneal size** in a one-month-old.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Hurler's syndrome**
Causes corneal clouding with a normal-sized cornea.

**C. Galactosaemia**
Causes oil-drop cataract.

**D. Cataract**
Gives a white pupil without corneal enlargement.

## Mind Capsule

> Infant with **epiphora, photophobia, large cornea** = congenital glaucoma (buphthalmos).
