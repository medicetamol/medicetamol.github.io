# Keratoconus

**Intacs** are **intrastromal corneal ring segments** placed in the corneal periphery to **flatten the cone** in **keratoconus**.

## Key Finding

> A thin curved ring segment embedded in the peripheral cornea.

## Trigger Point

> **Intacs ring** in the corneal stroma.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Cataract**
Treated by lens removal with IOL implantation.

**C. Corneal ulcer**
Treated with antimicrobials.

**D. Glaucoma**
Treated with drops, laser or filtering surgery.

## Mind Capsule

> Intacs = **keratoconus**.
