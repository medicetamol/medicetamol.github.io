# Left Optic Tract Lesion

Loss of the **right halves of both visual fields** is a **right homonymous hemianopia**, from a **left-sided retrochiasmal lesion**, here the **left optic tract**.

## Trigger Point

> Loss of vision in the **right halves of both eyes**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Optic radiation**
Lesions typically give quadrantic defects (pie in the sky or pie on the floor), and the side is not specified.

**C. Optic chiasma**
Causes bitemporal hemianopia.

**D. Right optic tract**
Would cause loss of the **left** half of both fields.

## Mind Capsule

> **Right** homonymous hemianopia = **left** tract.
