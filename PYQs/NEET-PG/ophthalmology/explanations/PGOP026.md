# FBN1 (Marfan Syndrome)

**Marfan syndrome** is caused by a **fibrillin-1 (FBN1)** mutation. A **tall boy with long limbs and bilateral ectopia lentis** (upward and outward) fits.

## Trigger Point

> **Tall boy, long limbs, bilateral ectopia lentis**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. COL5A**
Classic Ehlers-Danlos syndrome.

**B. PAX6**
Aniridia.

**C. TGFβR2**
Loeys-Dietz syndrome.

## Mind Capsule

> Marfan: **FBN1**, lens goes **up**; homocystinuria: lens goes **down**.
