# Retinoblastoma

**Retinoblastoma** is the commonest intraocular malignancy of childhood, caused by **RB1 gene** loss. It presents with **leukocoria** (white pupillary reflex), and histology shows **Flexner-Wintersteiner rosettes**, which are highly specific.

## Trigger Point

> **White pupillary reflex** in a child + **Flexner-Wintersteiner rosettes**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Rhabdomyosarcoma**
An orbital tumour presenting with proptosis; shows rhabdomyoblasts, not retinal rosettes.

**C. Medulloblastoma**
Shows Homer Wright rosettes; a cerebellar tumour.

**D. Astrocytoma**
A glial tumour without rosettes.

## Mind Capsule

> Leukocoria + **Flexner-Wintersteiner rosettes** = retinoblastoma.
