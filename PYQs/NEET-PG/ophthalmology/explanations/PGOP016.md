# Fluorescein with Cobalt Blue Filter

**Fluorescein** stains areas of **epithelial loss** in a corneal ulcer and glows **bright green under cobalt blue light**.

## Trigger Point

> "Dye and filter used to **diagnose corneal ulcers**".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Lissamine dye with green filter**
Lissamine green stains devitalised cells (dry eye), best seen in white light.

**B. Lissamine dye with cobalt blue filter**
Lissamine green is not visualised with the cobalt blue filter.

**C. Fluorescein under green filter**
Fluorescein needs blue light to fluoresce, so a green filter is incorrect.

## Mind Capsule

> **Fluorescein + cobalt blue** = corneal ulcer; lissamine green = dry eye.
