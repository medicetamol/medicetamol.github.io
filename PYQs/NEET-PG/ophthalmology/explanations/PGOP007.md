# Hard Exudates in Diabetic Retinopathy

**Hard exudates** are **yellow-white lipid deposits** in the outer plexiform layer, often arranged in rings around the macula (**diabetic maculopathy**).

## Key Finding

> Yellow-white deposits clustered around the macula.

## Trigger Point

> **Yellow-white** deposits at the **macula**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Flame-shaped haemorrhages in hypertensive retinopathy**
Red superficial haemorrhages, not yellow deposits.

**C. Soft exudates in hypertensive retinopathy**
Cotton-wool spots: fluffy white patches from nerve fibre layer infarcts.

**D. CRVO**
Shows widespread haemorrhages and venous tortuosity ("blood and thunder").

## Mind Capsule

> **Hard** exudates (yellow, lipid) = diabetic; **soft** (cotton-wool) = ischaemia.
