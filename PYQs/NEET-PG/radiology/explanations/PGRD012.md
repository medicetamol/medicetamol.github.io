# Streak Ovaries with Small Uterus

**Turner syndrome (45,X)** shows gonadal dysgenesis, so ultrasound finds **streak ovaries and a small (infantile) uterus**.

## Turner Syndrome Imaging

| System | Findings |
|---|---|
| Gonads | Streak ovaries, small uterus |
| Renal | Horseshoe or malrotated kidneys |
| Cardiac | Bicuspid aortic valve, coarctation of aorta |
| Skeletal | Short fourth metacarpal, Madelung deformity |

## Trigger Point

> Girl with **short stature and webbing of the neck**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Hepatomegaly with altered echotexture**
Not a typical Turner finding.

**B. Single kidney**
The characteristic renal anomaly is horseshoe kidney, not unilateral renal agenesis.

**C. Echo showing tricuspid stenosis**
Cardiac anomalies are left-sided: bicuspid aortic valve and coarctation.

## Mind Capsule

> Short stature + webbed neck in a girl = Turner syndrome → streak ovaries and small uterus.
