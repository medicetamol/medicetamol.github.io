# Right Middle Lobe Consolidation

A consolidated right middle lobe shows a homogeneous opacity in the right mid-lower zone that **obscures the right heart border** (silhouette sign), with a sharp upper margin along the horizontal fissure. The right hemidiaphragm stays visible.

## Silhouette Sign

| Obscured border | Lobe |
|---|---|
| Right heart border | Right middle lobe |
| Left heart border | Lingula |
| Right hemidiaphragm | Right lower lobe |
| Left hemidiaphragm | Left lower lobe |

## Trigger Point

> Fever and cough for 3-5 days with crepitations, and a **dense opacity at the lung base with a sharp margin along a lobar boundary**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Apical segment of Right lower lobe consolidation**
A posterior opacity at hilar level that does not obscure the right heart border.

**C. Posterior segment of right lower lobe consolidation**
Obscures the hemidiaphragm, not the heart border, and shows the spine sign on lateral view.

**D. Loculated pleural effusion**
A lenticular opacity meeting the chest wall at an obtuse angle, without air bronchograms.

## Mind Capsule

> Loss of the right heart border = right middle lobe; loss of the hemidiaphragm = lower lobe.
