# Snowman Heart (Supracardiac TAPVC)

In **supracardiac TAPVC**, pulmonary veins drain through a vertical vein into the left innominate vein and SVC. The dilated vertical vein and SVC form the "head" and the enlarged heart the "body", giving the **snowman or figure-of-8** shadow, seen after the first few months of life.

## Cardiac Silhouettes in CHD

| Sign | Lesion |
|---|---|
| Snowman, figure-of-8, cottage loaf | Supracardiac TAPVC |
| Boot-shaped (coeur en sabot) | Tetralogy of Fallot |
| Egg-on-side | Transposition of great arteries |
| Box-shaped | Ebstein anomaly |
| Scimitar sign | Partial anomalous pulmonary venous return |

## Trigger Point

> Chest radiograph with a **widened superior mediastinum** in a **figure-of-eight / snowman** configuration.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cottage loaf heart, Truncus arteriosus**
Cottage loaf is another name for the snowman shadow of TAPVC. Truncus shows plethoric lungs and often a right aortic arch.

**C. Egg on side, TGA**
A narrow vascular pedicle with an oval heart, not a widened superior mediastinum.

**D. Boot shaped heart, TOF**
Upturned apex with a concave pulmonary bay and oligaemic lungs.

## Mind Capsule

> Snowman or figure-of-8 heart = supracardiac TAPVC.
