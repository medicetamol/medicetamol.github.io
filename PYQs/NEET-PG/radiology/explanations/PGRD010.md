# Hysteroscopy + Laparoscopy

Combined **hysteroscopy** (uterine cavity) and **laparoscopy** (external fundal contour) is the traditional gold standard to confirm and classify **Müllerian anomalies**, such as septate versus bicornuate uterus. 3D ultrasound and MRI are now widely used non-invasive alternatives.

## Trigger Point

> Infertility patient with **USG suggestive of a uterine anomaly**; asks for the best confirmatory test.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. TVS**
A screening test, limited in distinguishing septate from bicornuate uteri.

**C. HSG**
Shows only the cavity, not the external contour.

**D. Laparoscopy**
Shows only the external contour, not the cavity.

## Mind Capsule

> Confirming a Müllerian anomaly needs both cavity and external contour = hysteroscopy + laparoscopy.
