# Psoas Major

A paired muscle arising from the transverse processes and the sides of the T12-L5 vertebral bodies and discs, lying **along the anterolateral lumbar vertebral bodies**. On CT it appears as a rounded or oval soft-tissue structure on each side of the spine, and elongated in coronal view. It joins the iliacus to form the iliopsoas.

## Trigger Point

> CT axial and coronal views with an arrow pointing to an **elongated paired muscle along the anterolateral lumbar vertebral bodies**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Gluteus Muscle**
Lies in the gluteal region around the pelvis, not alongside the lumbar vertebral bodies.

**C. latissimus dorsi**
A broad, superficial muscle of the posterior thoracolumbar region.

**D. Multifidus**
A deep paraspinal muscle posterior to the vertebrae, beside the spinous processes.

## Mind Capsule

> Paired muscle on the anterolateral lumbar vertebral bodies = psoas major.
