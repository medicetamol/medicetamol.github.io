# Putty Kidney

In end-stage **renal tuberculosis**, caseous material calcifies throughout a non-functioning kidney, giving **dense, homogeneous putty-like calcification** (autonephrectomy).

## Renal Calcification Patterns

| Pattern | Cause |
|---|---|
| Putty kidney | End-stage renal TB (autonephrectomy) |
| Medullary nephrocalcinosis | Hyperparathyroidism, distal RTA, medullary sponge kidney |
| Cortical nephrocalcinosis | Cortical necrosis, chronic glomerulonephritis |
| Staghorn calculus | Struvite stone from urease-producing infection |

## Trigger Point

> Plain radiograph of the kidney with **diffuse, homogeneous, dense calcific material** within the renal outline.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Nephrocalcinosis**
Fine, punctate calcification of the pyramids or a thin cortical rim, not a uniform dense mass.

**C. Stag horn calculus**
A branching stone that forms a cast of the pelvicalyceal system.

**D. Psoas Calcification**
Calcified TB psoas abscess lying along the psoas outline, not within the kidney.

## Mind Capsule

> Dense homogeneous calcification of the whole kidney = Putty kidney (renal TB).
