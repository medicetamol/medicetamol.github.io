# Scurvy

Vitamin C deficiency impairs collagen synthesis, causing **subperiosteal haemorrhage** and painful limbs (pseudoparalysis), with gum bleeding and perifollicular haemorrhages. Radiographic signs occur at growing metaphyses.

## Radiological Signs of Scurvy

| Sign | Description |
|---|---|
| Wimberger ring | Sclerotic ring around the epiphysis |
| Frankel's white line | Dense line at the zone of provisional calcification |
| Trümmerfeld zone | Lucent band beneath the white line |
| Pelkan spur | Metaphyseal spurs at the margins |

## Trigger Point

> 10-year-old child with **limb pain** and a **normal bone mineral density**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Rickets**
Defective mineralisation with widened, cupped, frayed physes and ↓ density. Normal BMD argues against it.

**C. Pyknodysostosis**
A sclerotic bone dysplasia with short stature and acro-osteolysis.

**D. Metaphyseal Dysplasia**
Pyle disease, with Erlenmeyer flask metaphyses and no painful limb signs.

## Mind Capsule

> Limb pain in a child + Wimberger ring + Frankel's white line = Scurvy.
