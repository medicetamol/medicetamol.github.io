# Neurons

By the **Law of Bergonie and Tribondeau**, radiosensitivity is proportional to mitotic activity and inversely related to differentiation. **Neurons** are post-mitotic and highly differentiated, so they are the most radioresistant. Marrow, spermatogonia and intestinal crypt cells are highly sensitive.

## Radiosensitivity of Tissues

| Sensitivity | Examples |
|---|---|
| High | Lymphocytes (despite not dividing), bone marrow, spermatogonia, intestinal crypt cells |
| Intermediate | Skin, vascular endothelium, lens |
| Low | Neurons, muscle, mature bone and cartilage |

## Trigger Point

> Irradiation before BMT; asks which cell is **least affected**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Spermatogonia**
Rapidly dividing and highly radiosensitive.

**B. Intestinal epithelial cells**
Crypt stem cells divide rapidly and are very radiosensitive.

**D. Bone marrow**
Highly radiosensitive, which is the reason for marrow ablation in BMT.

## Mind Capsule

> Radiosensitivity ∝ mitotic activity and undifferentiation → neurons and muscle resist.
