# Lipase

**Acute pancreatitis** (bulky oedematous pancreas with peripancreatic fluid). Serum **lipase** is more specific than amylase, rising within 4-8 hours and staying elevated for 8-14 days. Diagnosis needs two of: typical pain, lipase or amylase more than 3 times normal, and imaging findings.

## Trigger Point

> Abdominal pain, distension and organ failure; CT shows a **bulky pancreas with fluid-density lesion**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. AST**
A liver enzyme; ALT is the marker used to suggest gallstone aetiology.

**C. Creatine Kinase**
A marker of muscle injury.

**D. Blood Urea Nitrogen**
Rises with severity and is prognostic rather than diagnostic.

## Mind Capsule

> Acute pancreatitis → lipase ↑ (more specific and longer-lasting than amylase).
