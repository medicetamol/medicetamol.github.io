# CT

In a stable patient with renal trauma and haematuria, **contrast-enhanced CT with a delayed (excretory) phase** is the investigation of choice. It grades the injury, shows extravasation, parenchymal laceration and vascular injury, and assesses other organs.

## Trigger Point

> Kidney trauma with **haematuria**; asks which investigation **localises the site**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. X-ray**
Cannot show parenchymal or vascular injury.

**C. MRI**
Slow and less available in the acute setting.

**D. USG**
Detects free fluid but is operator dependent and poor at localising or grading renal injury.

## Mind Capsule

> Renal trauma with haematuria = contrast CT with delayed phase.
