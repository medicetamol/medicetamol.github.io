# Ankylosing Spondylitis

**Bilateral, symmetric sacroiliitis** with irregularity, erosions and sclerosis is the earliest and most consistent radiographic feature of ankylosing spondylitis, later progressing to joint fusion.

## Trigger Point

> Pelvic radiograph with **bilateral sacroiliac joint irregularity, erosions and sclerosis**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Healed TB spine**
Vertebral collapse with kyphosis or gibbus, and TB sacroiliitis is usually unilateral.

**C. Pagets**
Coarse trabeculae and cortical thickening, including pelvic brim thickening.

**D. Osteopetrosis**
Diffuse uniform sclerosis with bone-within-bone, without erosions.

## Mind Capsule

> Bilateral symmetric sacroiliac erosions + sclerosis = Ankylosing spondylitis.
