# IVU Is an Invasive Procedure

**Intravenous urography** involves IV injection of iodinated contrast followed by serial radiographs of the nephrogram, collecting systems, ureters and bladder. Needing cannulation and contrast makes it invasive, with risks of contrast reaction and nephropathy. CT urography has largely replaced it.

## Trigger Point

> Radiograph with **contrast outlining both kidneys, the ureters and the bladder**; asks what is true about the investigation.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Non-invasive to visualize ureteropelvic junction**
IVU needs IV contrast, so it is not non-invasive.

**C. Gold standard for bladder cancer**
Cystoscopy with biopsy is the gold standard.

**D. Done percutaneously**
Percutaneous contrast injection is antegrade pyelography. IVU uses an IV injection.

## Mind Capsule

> IVU = IV contrast; invasive. Gold standard for bladder cancer = cystoscopy.
