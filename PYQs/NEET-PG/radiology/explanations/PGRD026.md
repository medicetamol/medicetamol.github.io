# RUL Collapse

Lobar collapse shows **increased opacity with volume loss**: here the **horizontal fissure is displaced upward**. Other signs include tracheal shift to the same side, hilar elevation and compensatory hyperinflation. A central hilar mass in RUL collapse gives the Golden S sign.

## Trigger Point

> Volume loss in the right upper zone with **upward displacement of the horizontal fissure**, and ↑ opacity.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. RUL consolidation**
Air bronchograms without volume loss, and the fissure is not pulled upward.

**C. Bronchogenic carcinoma**
It can cause collapse, but the question asks for what the image shows: lobar collapse.

**D. Lung abscess**
A thick-walled cavity with an air-fluid level.

## Mind Capsule

> Opacity + fissure displaced towards it = collapse; consolidation has no volume loss.
