# Inverse Square Law

The intensity of radiation from a point source is **inversely proportional to the square of the distance** (I ∝ 1/d²). Doubling the distance cuts the intensity to one-quarter, the basis of the time, distance and shielding principles of radiation protection.

## Trigger Point

> Radiotherapy for endometrial carcinoma; asks which statement is **true**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Small blood vessels are most radioresistant**
Vascular endothelium is of intermediate sensitivity. Neurons and muscle are the most resistant.

**C. Rapidly proliferating cells are most radioresistant**
The opposite: dividing, undifferentiated cells are the most radiosensitive.

**D. Small intestinal mucosa is most radio-resistant**
Crypt epithelium divides rapidly and is highly radiosensitive.

## Mind Capsule

> Intensity ∝ 1/d²; dividing and undifferentiated cells are radiosensitive.
