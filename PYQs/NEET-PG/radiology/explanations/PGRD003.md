# Jejunum

**Valvulae conniventes** (plicae circulares) are mucosal folds most numerous in the jejunum. They **cross the entire lumen** and look like a stack of coins or coiled spring when the bowel is dilated.

## Bowel Identification on Plain X-ray

| Segment | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Jejunum | Valvulae conniventes crossing the full width, central position |
| Ileum | Smooth, featureless, distal and pelvic |
| Colon | Haustra not crossing the full width, peripheral position |

## Trigger Point

> Multiple dilated bowel loops with **valvulae conniventes extending across the lumen**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Duodenum**
A short fixed retroperitoneal C-loop, not multiple dilated loops.

**C. Ileum**
Smooth and featureless with few folds.

**D. Transverse colon**
Haustra are incomplete and do not span the entire lumen.

## Mind Capsule

> Folds crossing the full lumen = jejunum; featureless = ileum; incomplete haustra = colon.
