# Oesophagus

The oesophagus is flat in the anteroposterior direction, so a coin lodged there is seen **en face** on the AP view and **edge-on posterior to the tracheal air column** on the lateral view. A tracheal coin is edge-on on the AP view.

## Coin in Oesophagus vs Trachea

| View | Oesophagus | Trachea |
|---|---|---|
| AP | Flat face (en face) | Edge-on |
| Lateral | Edge-on, behind the air column | En face, within the air column |

## Trigger Point

> Round metallic object in the **midline** above the aortic knob, lying **posterior to the tracheal air column** on the lateral view.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Trachea**
Would appear edge-on on the AP view and within the air column on the lateral.

**C. Below diaphragm**
The object here projects over the chest, above the aortic knob.

**D. GI tract**
Beyond the oesophagus the object would project over the abdomen.

## Mind Capsule

> Coin en face on AP and behind the trachea on lateral = oesophagus.
