# Manometry

**Achalasia**: loss of inhibitory ganglion cells in the myenteric plexus leaves the lower oesophageal sphincter unable to relax and the body aperistaltic. Barium shows a dilated oesophagus tapering to a **bird's beak**. **High-resolution manometry** is the confirmatory test, showing incomplete LES relaxation and absent peristalsis.

## Trigger Point

> Barium chest X-ray with a **dilated tubular oesophagus tapering to a beak-like end** near the lower oesophageal sphincter.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. pH monitoring**
Used for reflux disease.

**C. Upper GI Endoscopy**
Excludes malignancy and pseudoachalasia but does not confirm the motility disorder.

**D. Barium swallow study**
Already shown here; it raises suspicion but is not the confirmatory test.

## Mind Capsule

> Bird's beak oesophagus = achalasia; barium suggests, manometry confirms.
