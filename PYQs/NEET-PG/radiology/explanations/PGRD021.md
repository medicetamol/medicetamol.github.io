# Putty Kidney

**Renal tuberculosis** presenting with abdominal pain and **sterile pyuria**. End-stage disease causes dense, coarse, amorphous calcification of the kidney, called **putty kidney**.

## Renal Calcification Patterns

| Pattern | Cause |
|---|---|
| Putty kidney | End-stage renal TB (autonephrectomy) |
| Medullary nephrocalcinosis | Hyperparathyroidism, distal RTA, medullary sponge kidney |
| Cortical nephrocalcinosis | Cortical necrosis, chronic glomerulonephritis |
| Staghorn calculus | Struvite stone from urease-producing infection |

## Trigger Point

> Abdominal pain with **sterile pyuria** and dense coarse calcification over one kidney on X-ray.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Nephrocalcinosis**
Fine, stippled calcification in the medulla or a thin cortical rim, from metabolic causes.

**C. Staghorn calculus**
A branching stone moulded to the pelvicalyceal system, from struvite infection.

**D. Psoas Calcification**
Calcified psoas (cold) abscess along the psoas outline, not within the kidney.

## Mind Capsule

> Sterile pyuria + dense kidney calcification = Putty kidney (renal TB).
