# Mania

Mania is a sustained (≥ **1 week**) episode of elevated, expansive or irritable mood with ↑ energy, flight of ideas, ↑ libido and disinhibition (over-familiarity). **Psychotic symptoms can occur** in mania, usually mood-congruent. Hypomania, by definition, has no psychotic features and no marked functional impairment.

## Trigger Point

> Elevated mood **+** flight of ideas **+** ↑ sexual desire **+** over-familiarity, with pseudohallucinations.

## Mania vs Hypomania

| Feature | Mania | Hypomania |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ≥ 7 days (or any, if hospitalised) | ≥ 4 days |
| Functional impairment | Marked | Not marked |
| Psychotic features | May be present | Absent |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Schizomania**
Not a standard ICD or DSM diagnosis. Elevated mood with ↑ drive and disinhibition points to mania.

**C. Hypomania**
A milder, shorter episode with no psychotic symptoms. Psychotic features rule it out.

**D. Cyclothymia**
Chronic (≥ 2 years) fluctuation of subthreshold hypomanic and depressive symptoms. It never reaches a full manic episode.

## Mind Capsule

> Elevated mood + flight of ideas + ↑ libido ± psychotic features = Mania.
> Hypomania never has psychosis.

## Memory Hook

> **DIGFAST**: Distractibility, Indiscretion, Grandiosity, Flight of ideas, Activity ↑, Sleep ↓, Talkativeness.
