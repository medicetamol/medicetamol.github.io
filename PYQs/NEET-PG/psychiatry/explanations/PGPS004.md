# Countertransference

The **therapist's** conscious and unconscious emotional reactions towards the patient. **Transference** is the reverse: the patient unconsciously redirects feelings from earlier relationships onto the therapist. Unrecognised countertransference can distort therapy.

## Trigger Point

> The **therapist** has mixed conscious and unconscious feelings **towards the patient**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Transference**
Patient's feelings towards the therapist, so the direction is opposite.

**C. Dissociation**
A disruption in the integration of consciousness, memory or identity. It is not a therapist-patient process.

**D. Preoccupation**
General mental absorption in a thought or concern. It is not a defined therapy-relationship term.

## Mind Capsule

> Transference = patient → therapist.
> Countertransference = therapist → patient.
