# Delusional Perception

A **two-stage primary delusion**: a genuine, normally perceived object (the white birds) is suddenly given a **delusional meaning** (orders from God) with no logical link. It is a Schneiderian **first-rank symptom** of schizophrenia.

## Trigger Point

> Sees **white birds** (a real percept) and attaches a bizarre meaning: **orders from God**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Visual hallucination**
A perception without any external stimulus. Here the birds are real.

**C. Thought insertion**
Belief that thoughts are placed in one's mind by an outside agent (disorder of thought possession).

**D. Passivity phenomenon**
Feeling that one's will, actions or emotions are controlled by an external force. He interprets a real percept; he does not feel controlled.

## Mind Capsule

> Real percept + delusional meaning = Delusional perception (first-rank symptom).
