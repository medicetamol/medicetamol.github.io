# Clozapine

The drug of choice for **treatment-resistant schizophrenia** (failure of ≥ 2 antipsychotics). It causes **sialorrhoea**, weight gain, hyperglycaemia and dyslipidaemia, plus the dangerous effects of agranulocytosis, seizures and myocarditis.

## Drug Facts

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Indications | Treatment-resistant schizophrenia; ↓ suicide risk in schizophrenia |
| Dangerous adverse effects | **Agranulocytosis** (regular ANC monitoring), seizures, myocarditis |
| Common adverse effects | Sialorrhoea, sedation, constipation, metabolic syndrome |

## Trigger Point

> No response to haloperidol and thioridazine **+** excessive salivation **+** ↑ blood glucose **+** hyperlipidaemia.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Ziprasidone**
Atypical antipsychotic with QTc prolongation; relatively weight-neutral. It does not cause sialorrhoea.

**B. Risperidone**
Causes hyperprolactinaemia and dose-related EPS, not hypersalivation. It is not the drug for resistant cases.

**D. Aripiprazole**
Partial D2 agonist, metabolically favourable; typical adverse effect is akathisia.

## Mind Capsule

> Resistant schizophrenia + sialorrhoea + metabolic syndrome = Clozapine (monitor ANC).
