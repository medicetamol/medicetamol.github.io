# Postpartum Blues

A common, benign, self-limiting mood disturbance with **onset on day 3–5** after delivery: tearfulness, mood lability, irritability and mild insomnia. It resolves within about 2 weeks and needs only **reassurance and support**.

## Trigger Point

> **4 days** postpartum with tearfulness, mood swings and occasional insomnia.

## Postpartum Psychiatric Disorders

| Disorder | Onset and course | Management |
|---|---|---|
| Blues | Day 3–5; resolves in ≤ 2 weeks | Reassurance |
| Depression | Weeks to months; ≥ 2 weeks of depressed mood | Antidepressant + psychotherapy |
| Psychosis | Within first 2–4 weeks; delusions, hallucinations, confusion | Emergency admission; antipsychotic ± mood stabiliser or ECT |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Postpartum depression**
Needs a persistent depressive episode (≥ 2 weeks) with pervasive low mood. Here the symptoms are mild and only 4 days old.

**C. Postpartum psychosis**
Marked by delusions, hallucinations and disorganisation. None are described.

**D. Postpartum anxiety**
Features excessive worry or panic, which are absent. The picture is tearfulness and lability.

## Mind Capsule

> Day 3–5 + tearful + labile + self-limiting = Postpartum blues; treat with reassurance only.
