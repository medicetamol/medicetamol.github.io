# Dissociative Fugue

Sudden, unplanned **travel away from home or work** with amnesia, often precipitated by severe stress. The patient may be confused about identity and is unaware of how they reached the place. (DSM-5 and ICD-11 treat fugue as a form of dissociative amnesia.)

## Trigger Point

> Found in a bizarre location, confused, with **no memory of how he got there** or of the journey.

## Dissociative Disorders

| Disorder | Hallmark |
|---|---|
| Dissociative amnesia | Inability to recall important personal information, usually traumatic |
| Dissociative fugue | Sudden unplanned travel away from home with amnesia, sometimes a new identity |
| Dissociative identity disorder | ≥ 2 distinct identity states with recurrent amnesic gaps |
| Depersonalisation-derealisation | Detachment from self or surroundings, reality testing intact |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Dissociative identity disorder**
Needs two or more distinct personality states, not a single episode of unexplained travel.

**C. Dissociative amnesia**
Memory loss without purposeful travel away from the usual surroundings.

**D. Psychotic episode**
Hallucinations or delusions with impaired reality testing, not selective amnesia for a journey.

## Mind Capsule

> Amnesia + unexplained travel away from home = Dissociative fugue.
