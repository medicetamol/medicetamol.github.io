# Wernicke's Encephalopathy

An acute neuropsychiatric emergency due to **thiamine (B1) deficiency**, classically in chronic alcoholics: **confusion, ataxia and ophthalmoplegia** (nystagmus, lateral rectus / 6th nerve palsy). Give **IV thiamine before glucose**; untreated, it can progress to Korsakoff psychosis.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic alcoholic with confusion, ataxia and **6th cranial nerve** involvement.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Korsakoff psychosis**
A chronic amnestic syndrome with confabulation and a clear sensorium; ocular signs and ataxia are not the picture.

**C. Delirium tremens**
Occurs 48–72 h after stopping alcohol, with hallucinations, tremor and autonomic hyperactivity.

**D. De Clerambault syndrome**
Erotomania, a delusional disorder in which the patient believes someone (usually of higher status) loves them.

## Mind Capsule

> Alcoholic + confusion + ataxia + ophthalmoplegia = Wernicke's; give thiamine first.

## Memory Hook

> **COAT**: Confusion, Ophthalmoplegia, Ataxia, Thiamine deficiency.
