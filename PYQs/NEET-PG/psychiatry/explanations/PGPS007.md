# Panic Disorder

Recurrent, **unexpected discrete attacks** of intense fear that peak within about 10 minutes, with autonomic symptoms (palpitations, sweating, breathlessness, chest pain) and a sense of **impending doom**. DSM-5 also requires ≥ 1 month of worry about further attacks. Treatment is SSRIs plus CBT; benzodiazepines only short term.

## Trigger Point

> 5–6 episodes in 6 months, each lasting **20–30 minutes**, with palpitations, sweating, chest pain and **feeling of impending doom**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Depression**
Pervasive low mood and anhedonia, not brief autonomic surges.

**C. Generalized anxiety disorder**
Persistent, free-floating worry on most days for ≥ 6 months. It is not episodic.

**D. Phobia**
Fear tied to a specific object or situation. These attacks are described as spontaneous.

## Mind Capsule

> Discrete episodic autonomic surge + impending doom = Panic disorder.
> Persistent worry ≥ 6 months = GAD.
