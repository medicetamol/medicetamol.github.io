# Psychiatric Evaluation

Under **Sec 84 IPC (now BNS Sec 22)**, an act done by a person who, because of **unsound mind**, could not know its nature or that it was wrong or contrary to law is not an offence (McNaughten rule). Criminal responsibility and fitness to stand trial depend on mental state, so the first step is a **psychiatric evaluation**.

## Trigger Point

> A man who killed a person and is being questioned. The question asks for the **first step** in management; the key assumes a possible mental illness.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Immediate trial of the case**
Trial can proceed only if the accused is fit to understand the proceedings; this is decided after assessment.

**C. Declared not guilty**
Guilt or insanity is decided by the court on evidence and medical opinion, not at the outset.

**D. Sentenced for murder**
Sentencing follows a trial and conviction, and needs criminal responsibility to be established first.

## Mind Capsule

> Accused with suspected unsound mind = psychiatric evaluation first (Sec 84 IPC / BNS 22).
