# Acute Stress Disorder

Trauma exposure (here, a father's violent death) followed by intrusion (flashbacks), dissociative symptoms such as **amnesia for the event**, avoidance and arousal, lasting **3 days to 1 month** (DSM-5). The same picture beyond 1 month becomes PTSD.

## Trigger Point

> Flashbacks and forgetting a father's death in a road accident **2 weeks ago**.

## Trauma-Related Disorders by Duration

| Disorder | Symptom duration |
|---|---|
| Acute stress disorder | 3 days to 1 month after trauma |
| PTSD | More than 1 month |
| Adjustment disorder | Onset within 3 months of a stressor; resolves within 6 months of it ending |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. PTSD**
Similar symptoms but present for more than 1 month. At 2 weeks the time criterion is not met.

**C. Dissociative Amnesia**
Isolated inability to recall important personal information. Here flashbacks and trauma-linked symptoms point to a stress disorder.

**D. Adjustment Disorder**
An emotional or behavioural reaction to a stressor that does not meet criteria for another disorder. It lacks the intrusion and dissociation cluster.

## Mind Capsule

> Trauma + intrusion / dissociation for 3 days to 1 month = Acute stress disorder; more than 1 month = PTSD.
