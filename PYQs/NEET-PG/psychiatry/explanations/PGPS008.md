# Squeeze Technique

A behavioural therapy for **premature ejaculation** (Masters and Johnson): the partner squeezes the coronal ridge of the glans when the man nears ejaculation, abolishing the urge. Repeated practice builds control. The **Semans' stop-start** method is the related technique.

## Trigger Point

> Very early ejaculation during intercourse, asking for **non-pharmacological** management.

## Therapies in Sexual Dysfunction

| Condition | Technique |
|---|---|
| Premature ejaculation | Squeeze (Masters-Johnson), stop-start (Semans) |
| Erectile dysfunction, low desire | Sensate focus |
| Vaginismus | Graded vaginal dilators with desensitisation |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cognitive behavioral therapy**
A general psychotherapy for anxiety, depression and OCD. It is not the specific technique for premature ejaculation.

**B. Exposure and response prevention therapy**
The behavioural therapy for OCD.

**D. Sensate focus therapy**
Graded non-genital then genital touch to reduce performance anxiety, used for erectile and desire disorders.

## Mind Capsule

> Premature ejaculation = squeeze / stop-start.
> ED and low desire = sensate focus; OCD = ERP.
