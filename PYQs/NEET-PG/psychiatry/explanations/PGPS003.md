# Derailment

**Formal thought disorder** is a disturbance in the **form (flow and linking)** of thinking, not in what is thought. In **derailment (loosening of association)**, ideas slip off onto unconnected tracks without a logical link, characteristic of schizophrenia.

## Trigger Point

> "Included in **form** of thought disorder."

## Classification of Thought Disorders

| Disorder of | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Form** (flow) | Derailment, tangentiality, circumstantiality, flight of ideas, word salad, thought block |
| **Content** | Delusions, overvalued ideas, obsessions |
| **Possession** | Thought insertion, withdrawal, broadcasting |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Obsession**
A recurrent, intrusive, ego-dystonic idea that the person recognises as their own. It is a disturbance of thought content.

**C. Thought insertion**
Belief that an outside agent places thoughts in one's mind. It is a disorder of thought possession (Schneiderian first-rank symptom).

**D. Delusion**
A fixed, false, unshakeable belief. It is a disorder of thought content.

## Mind Capsule

> Form = how thoughts flow (derailment, tangentiality, FOI); content = delusion; possession = insertion / withdrawal / broadcasting.
