# Dissociative Fugue

Stress or trauma-linked **purposeful travel away from home** with amnesia for personal identity or past. The patient has no memory of how they got to the new place. (DSM-5 and ICD-11 classify fugue under dissociative amnesia.)

## Trigger Point

> Injured in an earthquake, later found in **another town** with **no memory of how he got there or of his identity**.

## Dissociative Disorders

| Disorder | Hallmark |
|---|---|
| Dissociative amnesia | Inability to recall important personal information, usually traumatic |
| Dissociative fugue | Sudden unplanned travel away from home with amnesia, sometimes a new identity |
| Dissociative identity disorder | ≥ 2 distinct identity states with recurrent amnesic gaps |
| Depersonalisation-derealisation | Detachment from self or surroundings, reality testing intact |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Dissociative identity disorder**
Two or more distinct identity states. Here there is loss of identity with travel, not alternating personalities.

**C. Acute stress disorder**
Intrusion, avoidance and arousal lasting 3 days to 1 month. It does not include unplanned travel with loss of identity.

**D. PTSD**
Intrusion, avoidance and hyperarousal for more than 1 month, without wandering and identity loss.

## Mind Capsule

> Trauma + travel away + amnesia for identity = Dissociative fugue.
