# Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder

A **Cluster C** personality disorder marked by preoccupation with **order, perfectionism, punctuality and control**, with rigidity and excessive devotion to work. It is **ego-syntonic** (the person sees the behaviour as right) and has no true obsessions or compulsions, unlike OCD.

## Trigger Point

> Extremely particular about **time**, with a strong need for **order, control and perfectionism**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Paranoid Personality Disorder**
Pervasive distrust and suspiciousness of others (Cluster A).

**C. Narcissistic Personality Disorder**
Grandiosity, need for admiration and lack of empathy (Cluster B).

**D. Schizoid Personality Disorder**
Social detachment and restricted emotional range, preferring solitude (Cluster A).

## Mind Capsule

> Order + punctuality + perfectionism + control, ego-syntonic = OCPD.
> OCD has ego-dystonic obsessions and compulsions.

## Memory Hook

> **Weird, Wild, Worried**: Cluster A is odd or eccentric, B dramatic or erratic, C anxious or fearful (OCPD sits in C).
