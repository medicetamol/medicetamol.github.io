# Return to OT, Remove Clot, Re-Ligate

Bleeding within the first 24 hours after tonsillectomy is **reactionary haemorrhage**. The child is taken to the **operation theatre** under GA, the **clot is removed** and the bleeding point is **ligated or cauterised**.

## Trigger Point

> **Post-tonsillectomy child bleeding in the ward**.

## Post-Tonsillectomy Haemorrhage

| Type | Timing |
|---|---|
| Primary | During surgery |
| Reactionary | Within 24 hours |
| Secondary | 5-10 days, due to infection |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. OT and pressure packing**
Packing is not effective for a tonsillar fossa bleed.

**C. Cautery**
Cannot be done without clearing the clot to see the bleeding point.

**D. Conservative management**
For minor secondary bleeding with antibiotics, not active reactionary bleeding.

## Mind Capsule

> Reactionary bleed (**under 24 h**) = **OT, clot removal, ligation**.
