# Anterior Ethmoidal Artery

For **refractory anterior epistaxis from the superior anterior septum**, the target in this question is **anterior ethmoidal artery ligation**. Many texts prefer sphenopalatine ligation for refractory bleeding in general, so the key reflects the anterior septal source.

## Trigger Point

> **Anterior septal** bleeding refractory to **packing and cautery**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Sphenopalatine artery**
Usual target in posterior or refractory bleeding.

**C. Facial artery**
Supplies the vestibule through the superior labial branch; not the primary target.

**D. Internal maxillary artery**
Transantral ligation, rarely used now.

## Mind Capsule

> Refractory anterior-superior septal bleeding = **anterior ethmoidal artery**.
