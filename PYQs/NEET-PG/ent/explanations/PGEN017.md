# Walsham Forceps

**Walsham forceps** are used for **closed reduction of a displaced nasal bone fracture**; one blade is in the nose and the other outside. **Asch forceps** reduce the septum.

## Trigger Point

> Displaced **nasal bone fracture**; "instrument for closed reduction".

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Tilley's forceps**
Used for nasal dressing and packing.

**C. Luc's forceps**
Used in antral and nasal surgery to remove tissue.

**D. Bayonet forceps**
Used for nasal dressing and packing.

## Mind Capsule

> Nasal bones = **Walsham**; septum = **Asch**.
