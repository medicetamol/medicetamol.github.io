# 30-45 dB

**Rinne** is **negative** when the **air-bone (AB) gap** is larger than a fork-specific threshold. Negative at **256 and 512 Hz** but **positive at 1024 Hz** puts the gap at **30-45 dB**.

## Trigger Point

> Rinne **negative at 256 and 512 Hz**, **positive at 1024 Hz**.

## Rinne Test and AB Gap

| Rinne negative up to | AB gap |
|---|---|
| 256 Hz | 15-30 dB |
| 512 Hz | 30-45 dB |
| 1024 Hz | 45-60 dB |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. 15-30 dB**
Rinne would be negative only at 256 Hz.

**C. 45-60 dB**
Rinne would be negative even at 1024 Hz.

**D. Over 60 dB**
Rinne remains negative at all frequencies tested.

## Mind Capsule

> 256 Hz = 15-30, **512 Hz = 30-45**, 1024 Hz = 45-60 dB.
