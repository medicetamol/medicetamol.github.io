# Mitomycin C (Subglottic Stenosis)

**Mitomycin C** is an **antifibroblastic agent**. Applied topically, it **reduces scarring** and is used in **subglottic and laryngotracheal stenosis** (also in DCR and choanal atresia surgery).

## Trigger Point

> Vials of white powder; "**topical use**" of the drug.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Inlay type I myringoplasty**
A tympanic membrane repair technique using a graft, not this drug.

**C. Post-adenoidectomy bleeding**
Controlled by packing or cautery.

**D. Rhinocerebral mucormycosis**
Treated with amphotericin B and debridement.

## Mind Capsule

> Mitomycin C = **antifibrotic**, used topically in **stenosis**.
