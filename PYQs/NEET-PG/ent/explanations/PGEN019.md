# Myringoplasty

**Tubotympanic (safe) CSOM** has a **central perforation** with mucosal disease only. Once the ear is dry, **myringoplasty** repairs the perforation and is the definitive treatment.

## Trigger Point

> **Central perforation**, **conductive** loss, chronic discharge.

## Types of CSOM

| Feature | Tubotympanic | Atticoantral |
|---|---|---|
| Perforation | Central | Marginal or attic |
| Cholesteatoma | Absent | Present |
| Risk | Safe | Unsafe |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Modified radical mastoidectomy**
For atticoantral (unsafe) disease.

**C. Exploratory tympanotomy**
Done to explore the middle ear, not definitive here.

**D. Immediate broad-spectrum oral antibiotics**
Aural toilet and topical antibiotics come first, and drugs do not close the hole.

## Mind Capsule

> Safe CSOM with dry ear = **myringoplasty**.
