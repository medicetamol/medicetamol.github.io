# Endoscopic Sphenopalatine Artery Ligation

When **anterior or posterior packing and cautery fail**, the preferred next step is **endoscopic sphenopalatine artery (SPA) ligation**, because the SPA supplies most of the nasal cavity.

## Trigger Point

> **Epistaxis** where **conservative management failed**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. ICA ligation**
Not used for epistaxis; carries the risk of stroke.

**C. ECA ligation**
Older method with higher rebleeding because of collaterals.

**D. Maxillary artery ligation**
Transantral and more invasive; replaced by endoscopic SPA ligation.

## Mind Capsule

> Failed packing = **endoscopic SPA ligation** (first-choice surgical step).
