# Tracheostomy Tube (Upper Airway Examination)

The device is a **tracheostomy tube** with **three parts**: outer tube, inner tube and obturator. It is placed into the trachea and is **not an instrument for examining the upper airway**.

## Trigger Point

> Curved tube with **three components**; instrument **not used** for...

## Why the Others Are True

**A. Airway toileting**
Secretions can be suctioned through the tube.

**C. Acute nasopharyngeal obstruction**
It bypasses an upper airway obstruction.

**D. Prolonged mechanical ventilation**
A cuffed tube allows long-term ventilation.

## Mind Capsule

> Tracheostomy tube: **toileting, bypass of obstruction, ventilation**; not for examination.
