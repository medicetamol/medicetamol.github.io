# Total Laryngectomy

A large squamous cell carcinoma involving **both cords with subglottic extension and thyroid cartilage invasion** is **advanced (T4a)**. It cannot be controlled with radiation alone, so **total laryngectomy** is needed for adequate margins.

## Trigger Point

> **Both cords + subglottic extension + cartilage invasion**; not amenable to radiation alone.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Partial laryngectomy**
Only for early lesions with preserved mobility and no cartilage invasion.

**C. Emergency tracheostomy**
Secures the airway but does not remove the tumour.

**D. Submental tracheostomy**
A technique for selected maxillofacial cases, not cancer treatment.

## Mind Capsule

> Advanced laryngeal cancer with **cartilage invasion** = **total laryngectomy**.
