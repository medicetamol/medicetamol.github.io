# Left Conductive Hearing Loss

A **central tympanic membrane perforation** with an **air-bone gap and normal bone conduction** in the same ear means **conductive hearing loss** of the **left ear**.

## Trigger Point

> **Left ear air-bone gap**, **bone conduction normal**.

## Audiometry Patterns

| Type | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Normal | AC and BC within normal limits |
| Conductive | AC reduced, BC normal (air-bone gap) |
| Sensorineural | AC and BC both reduced, no gap |
| Mixed | Both reduced with a gap |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Left sensorineural**
Bone conduction would also be reduced.

**C. Right conductive**
The right ear is normal.

**D. Right sensorineural**
The right ear is normal.

## Mind Capsule

> Air-bone gap with normal BC = **conductive loss**.
