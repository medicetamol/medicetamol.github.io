# Glossopharyngeal Nerve

The **glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX)** supplies **sensation to the oropharynx, tonsil and posterior one-third of the tongue**, and motor supply to **stylopharyngeus**.

## Trigger Point

> Marked nerve in the **oropharyngeal region**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Vagus**
Supplies the pharynx motor via the pharyngeal plexus and laryngeal sensation.

**B. Facial nerve**
Taste from the anterior two-thirds of the tongue; not an oropharyngeal sensory nerve.

**D. Trigeminal nerve**
Sensation to the face and anterior tongue, and palate via its branches.

## Mind Capsule

> Oropharynx and tonsil sensation = **CN IX**.
