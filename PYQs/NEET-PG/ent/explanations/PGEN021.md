# HPV

**HPV (especially type 16)** is the most common cause of **squamous cell carcinoma of the oropharynx**, including the **base of the tongue and tonsil**.

## Trigger Point

> **SCC at the base of the tongue**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. EBV**
Linked to nasopharyngeal carcinoma.

**C. HCV**
Linked to liver disease and lymphoma.

**D. CMV**
Opportunistic infections, not a head and neck cancer cause.

## Mind Capsule

> Oropharyngeal SCC = **HPV-16**; nasopharyngeal = EBV.
