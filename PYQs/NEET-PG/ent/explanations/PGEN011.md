# Fossa of Rosenmuller

The **fossa of Rosenmuller (lateral pharyngeal recess)** lies **behind the torus tubarius** in the nasopharynx. It is the **commonest site of nasopharyngeal carcinoma**.

## Trigger Point

> **Mucosal recess** marked on nasopharyngeal endoscopy, behind the Eustachian tube opening.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Tubal tonsil**
Lymphoid tissue around the Eustachian tube opening.

**C. Opening of the Eustachian tube**
The orifice lies in front of the torus tubarius.

**D. Adenoid**
The pharyngeal tonsil on the roof and posterior wall of the nasopharynx.

## Mind Capsule

> Recess behind the torus tubarius = **fossa of Rosenmuller** (NPC site).
