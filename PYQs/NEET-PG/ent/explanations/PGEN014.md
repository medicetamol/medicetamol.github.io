# Juvenile Nasopharyngeal Angiofibroma

**JNA** is a **vascular benign tumour** of adolescent males, arising near the sphenopalatine foramen. It presents with **recurrent epistaxis and nasal obstruction**. CT shows **Holman-Miller sign** (anterior bowing of the posterior wall of the maxillary sinus).

## Trigger Point

> **Boy with recurrent epistaxis** + nasal mass + **bowing of the posterior maxillary wall**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Antrochoanal polyp**
Gives nasal obstruction without epistaxis.

**C. Rhinoscleroma**
A granulomatous disease with Mikulicz cells.

**D. Rhinosporidiosis**
A red, friable "strawberry" polyp from *Rhinosporidium seeberi*.

## Mind Capsule

> Adolescent boy + epistaxis + **Holman-Miller sign** = **JNA**.
