# Total Laryngectomy

After **total laryngectomy**, the airway is **separated from the upper aerodigestive tract**, so the patient breathes through a **permanent end tracheostoma** in the neck.

## Key Finding

> Permanent round stoma in the neck opening directly into the airway.

## Trigger Point

> **Permanent stoma** after surgery for laryngeal carcinoma.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Partial laryngectomy**
Preserves part of the larynx, so the patient breathes through the natural airway.

**B. Percutaneous tracheostomy**
A temporary opening with a tube, with the larynx still connected.

**C. Standard tracheostomy**
Also temporary; the upper airway remains connected.

## Mind Capsule

> **Permanent end-stoma** = total laryngectomy.
