# Temporal Lobe Abscess

**Otogenic intracranial abscesses** most often occur in the **temporal lobe** (from the middle ear roof) and cerebellum. CT shows a **ring lesion with central low density**.

## Trigger Point

> **Ring lesion** in the **temporal region** on CT.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Extradural abscess**
A lens-shaped collection between bone and dura, not a ring lesion in the brain.

**B. Cerebral abscess**
A general term; the lesion here is in the temporal lobe.

**D. Meningitis**
Shows meningeal enhancement, not a ring lesion.

## Mind Capsule

> Ear disease + **ring lesion** in the temporal lobe = **temporal lobe abscess**.
