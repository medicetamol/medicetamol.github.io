# Roomy Nasal Cavity

**Atrophic rhinitis (ozaena)** causes atrophy of the mucosa, turbinates and underlying bone. The nasal cavity becomes **wide and roomy**, filled with **greenish-black foul crusts**, and the patient has **merciful anosmia** (cannot smell her own foul odour).

## Trigger Point

> **Greenish-black crusts** covering turbinates and septum + anosmia.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Polyp**
Seen in allergic or chronic inflammatory conditions, not atrophic rhinitis.

**B. Hypertrophied inferior turbinate**
Occurs in hypertrophic or allergic rhinitis, the opposite of atrophy.

**D. Foreign body**
Gives unilateral foul discharge, usually in a child.

## Mind Capsule

> Crusts + foul smell + anosmia = **atrophic rhinitis** with a **roomy nasal cavity**.
