# Tympanic Branch of Glossopharyngeal Nerve

The **tonsil** is supplied by the **glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX)**. Pain is referred to the ear through its **tympanic branch (Jacobson's nerve)**, which supplies the middle ear.

## Trigger Point

> **Tonsillitis** with **ear pain**.

## Referred Otalgia

| Nerve | Source |
|---|---|
| Auriculotemporal (V3) | Teeth, TMJ |
| Glossopharyngeal (Jacobson's) | Tonsil, oropharynx |
| Vagus (Arnold's) | Larynx, hypopharynx |
| Greater auricular (C2, C3) | Neck, parotid |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Greater auricular nerve**
Supplies the lower ear and parotid area from C2-C3.

**C. Auriculotemporal nerve**
Refers pain from teeth and TMJ.

**D. Auricular branch of vagus**
Refers pain from the larynx and hypopharynx.

## Mind Capsule

> Tonsil pain to the ear = **CN IX (Jacobson's nerve)**.
