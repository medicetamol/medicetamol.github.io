# Factors VIII and IX

**Haemophilia A (factor VIII)** and **B (factor IX)** are X-linked recessive disorders of the intrinsic pathway. They cause **deep tissue bleeding: haemarthrosis, muscle haematomas and retroperitoneal haemorrhage**, with **no mucosal bleeding**. aPTT is prolonged, PT and platelets are normal.

## Trigger Point

> **Retroperitoneal haemorrhage + recurrent knee swelling** (haemarthrosis) with **no mucosal bleeding**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Factors XI and XII**
Factor XI deficiency gives mild bleeding after surgery; XII deficiency gives no bleeding.
They do not cause spontaneous haemarthrosis.

**C. Von Willebrand factor**
Gives **mucosal bleeding** (epistaxis, menorrhagia), as a platelet-type defect.
Haemarthrosis is rare.

**D. Lupus anticoagulant**
Prolongs aPTT in vitro but is associated with **thrombosis**, not bleeding.
It does not cause haemarthrosis.

## Mind Capsule

> Haemarthrosis + deep bleeds = **haemophilia (VIII/IX)**; mucosal bleeding = vWD or platelet disorder.
