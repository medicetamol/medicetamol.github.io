# Intermediate Filaments

**Mallory-Denk bodies** are eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions in hepatocytes in **alcoholic hepatitis**. They are made of **misfolded, ubiquitinated cytokeratin 8 and 18** (intermediate filaments). The patient with increasing abdominal girth (ascites) has alcoholic liver disease.

## Trigger Point

> Chronic alcoholic + **reddish (eosinophilic) inclusions** in the liver biopsy = Mallory-Denk bodies.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Actin**
A microfilament, forming the cell cortex and muscle thin filaments.
It does not form Mallory bodies.

**B. Microtubules**
Made of tubulin; they form the spindle and cilia.
They are not part of these inclusions.

**D. Myofibril**
The contractile unit of striated muscle.
It is not found in hepatocytes.

## Mind Capsule

> Mallory-Denk body = **cytokeratin (intermediate filaments)** in alcoholic hepatitis; also seen in NASH and Wilson disease.
