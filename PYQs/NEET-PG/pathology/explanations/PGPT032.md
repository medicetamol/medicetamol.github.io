# Lymphocytic Infiltration of Salivary and Lacrimal Glands

**Sjögren syndrome** is an autoimmune disease with **CD4+ T-cell-mediated lymphocytic infiltration** of lacrimal and salivary glands, destroying acini and causing **keratoconjunctivitis sicca and xerostomia**. Autoantibodies are **anti-Ro (SSA) and anti-La (SSB)**. There is an increased risk of **MALT (marginal zone) lymphoma**.

## Trigger Point

> **Dry eyes + dry mouth** + anti-Ro and anti-La.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Destruction by neutrophils**
Typical of acute bacterial inflammation.
Sjögren's is a chronic lymphocytic process.

**B. IgE-mediated hypersensitivity**
Type I reaction in allergies and asthma.
It is not the mechanism of Sjögren's.

**D. Amyloid deposition**
Amyloidosis can infiltrate glands but is not linked to anti-Ro and anti-La.
The mechanism here is autoimmune.

## Mind Capsule

> Sjögren = **anti-Ro/La** + lymphocytic gland destruction + **MALT lymphoma** risk; Schirmer test, biopsy of minor salivary gland.
