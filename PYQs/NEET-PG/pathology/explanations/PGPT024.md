# t(14;18)

**Follicular lymphoma** is composed of **centrocytes and centroblasts** with a follicular pattern. It is **CD10+ and BCL2+**, due to **t(14;18)** which places **BCL2** under the IgH promoter, blocking apoptosis.

## Trigger Point

> **Centrocytes + centroblasts + BCL2 + CD10** = follicular lymphoma.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. t(2;5)**
**NPM-ALK** in anaplastic large cell lymphoma.
It shows hallmark cells and CD30 positivity.

**B. t(11;14)**
**Cyclin D1 (BCL1)-IgH** in mantle cell lymphoma.
It is CD5+ and CD10−.

**D. t(11;14)**
This is the same translocation as option B, repeated, and refers to mantle cell lymphoma.
It does not fit follicular lymphoma.

## Mind Capsule

> t(14;18) = **follicular** (BCL2); t(11;14) = mantle cell; t(8;14) = Burkitt; t(2;5) = ALCL.
