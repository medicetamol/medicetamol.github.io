# Lymphocytic Myocarditis

**Viral (lymphocytic) myocarditis** is the commonest myocarditis (e.g. **Coxsackie B**), with fever, fatigue and **left ventricular dysfunction** in a child. Biopsy shows **interstitial lymphocytic infiltrate with myocyte injury**.

## Key Finding

> Cardiac muscle fibres with a prominent interstitial inflammatory (lymphocytic) infiltrate.

## Trigger Point

> Child + fever + LV dysfunction + **endomyocardial biopsy with inflammatory infiltrate**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Acute rheumatic fever**
Shows **Aschoff bodies** with Anitschkow cells, not a diffuse lymphocytic infiltrate.
It follows streptococcal pharyngitis.

**B. Chagas disease**
Shows *T. cruzi* amastigote nests in myocytes with inflammation; a Latin American disease.
There is no such geographic clue here.

**C. Pyogenic myocarditis**
Shows **neutrophilic infiltrates** with abscesses, in septic emboli or bacteraemia.
Neutrophils would predominate.

## Mind Capsule

> Viral myocarditis = **lymphocytes** (Coxsackie B); Aschoff body = RF; neutrophils = bacterial; giant cells = giant cell myocarditis.
