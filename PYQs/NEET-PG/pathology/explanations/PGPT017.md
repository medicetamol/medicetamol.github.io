# β2-Microglobulin

In **multiple myeloma**, a high **serum β2-microglobulin** reflects tumour burden and is the strongest poor prognostic marker. It is used in the **International Staging System (ISS)** along with serum albumin. Renal impairment also worsens outcome, but β2-microglobulin is the standard prognostic marker asked in this question.

## Trigger Point

> **Poor prognostic marker** in multiple myeloma.

## ISS Staging

| Stage | Criteria |
|---|---|
| I | β2-microglobulin < 3.5 mg/L and albumin ≥ 3.5 g/dL |
| II | Neither I nor III |
| III | β2-microglobulin ≥ 5.5 mg/L |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Serum creatinine**
Raised creatinine indicates renal damage and has prognostic value, but it is not the key marker in staging.
β2-microglobulin captures tumour mass and renal clearance.

**B. Hypercalcaemia**
A feature of myeloma (the "C" in CRAB) with clinical importance, but not a staging marker in ISS.
It is a criterion for treatment.

**D. Telomerase**
Not a standard prognostic marker for myeloma.
It is a distractor.

## Mind Capsule

> Myeloma prognosis = **β2-microglobulin** (ISS); CRAB = Calcium, Renal failure, Anaemia, Bone lesions.
