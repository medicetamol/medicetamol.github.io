# Dysplasia

**Dysplasia** is **disordered (disorganised) cellular growth** within an epithelium, with loss of normal maturation and polarity, **nuclear hyperchromasia, pleomorphism** and increased mitoses. It is **non-invasive and potentially reversible**, and is a precancerous change, as seen on a Pap smear in cervical intraepithelial neoplasia (CIN).

## Trigger Point

> Pap smear with **disorganised growth** of cells and **hyperchromatic nuclei**, with no mention of invasion.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Metaplasia**
A reversible replacement of one mature epithelium by another (e.g. columnar to squamous in the transformation zone).
The cells are mature and orderly, without nuclear atypia.

**C. Hypertrophy**
An increase in cell size without change in organisation or nuclear features.
It does not cause disorganised growth.

**D. Carcinoma**
Requires **invasion** through the basement membrane (full-thickness atypia without invasion is carcinoma in situ).
Invasion is not described, and cytology alone cannot show it.

## Mind Capsule

> Disordered growth + nuclear atypia, no invasion = **dysplasia**; invasion through basement membrane = carcinoma.
