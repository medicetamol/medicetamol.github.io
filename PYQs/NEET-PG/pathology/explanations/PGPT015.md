# Transferrin Saturation

In **iron deficiency anaemia (IDA)**, **transferrin saturation falls** (low serum iron with raised TIBC), as do **ferritin** and hepcidin. Increased are **TIBC, soluble transferrin receptor (sTfR) and erythropoietin**.

## Trigger Point

> Which is **NOT** increased in IDA.

## Iron Studies

| Parameter | IDA |
|---|---|
| Serum iron | Decreased |
| TIBC | Increased |
| Transferrin saturation | **Decreased** |
| Ferritin | Decreased |
| sTfR | Increased |

## Why the Others Are True

**A. TIBC**
Transferrin synthesis rises in iron deficiency, so total iron-binding capacity increases.
It is a classic IDA finding.

**B. Soluble transferrin receptor**
Cells upregulate transferrin receptors, which are shed into serum.
It is raised in IDA, not in anaemia of chronic disease.

**D. Erythropoietin**
Tissue hypoxia from anaemia raises erythropoietin.
It is increased as a physiological response.

## Mind Capsule

> IDA: ↓ iron, ↓ ferritin, ↓ saturation, **↑ TIBC**, ↑ sTfR; anaemia of chronic disease: ↓ iron, ↓ TIBC, ↑ ferritin.
