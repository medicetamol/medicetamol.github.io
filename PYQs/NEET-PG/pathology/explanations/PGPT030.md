# Urinary Bladder / Ureter

**Transitional epithelium (urothelium)** is a multilayered epithelium with surface **dome-shaped umbrella cells**, allowing distension. It lines the **renal pelvis, ureter, bladder and part of the urethra**.

## Key Finding

> Multilayered lining with dome-shaped surface cells over rounded/polygonal cells = urothelium.

## Trigger Point

> **Dome-shaped surface cells** over a stratified epithelium.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Oesophagus**
Lined by **non-keratinized stratified squamous epithelium**, with flat surface cells.
There are no umbrella cells.

**C. Small intestine**
Lined by **simple columnar epithelium** with villi and goblet cells.
It is single-layered.

**D. Trachea**
Lined by **pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium** with goblet cells.
It shows cilia, not dome cells.

## Mind Capsule

> Umbrella cells = **transitional epithelium**: ureter, bladder, renal pelvis.
