# Burkitt Lymphoma

**Burkitt lymphoma** is a high-grade B-cell neoplasm with **t(8;14)** placing **c-MYC** under the immunoglobulin heavy chain promoter. The **endemic (African) form** presents as a **jaw mass** and is linked to **EBV**. Histology shows medium-sized basophilic cells with lipid vacuoles and a **starry-sky pattern** from tingible-body macrophages.

## Key Finding

> Sheets of large basophilic lymphoid cells with scattered pale macrophages = **starry-sky pattern**.

## Trigger Point

> African child with **jaw mass** + **t(8;14)**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Acute myeloid leukaemia**
Myeloblasts with Auer rods and translocations like t(15;17).
It does not form a jaw mass with starry-sky pattern.

**B. Follicular lymphoma**
Associated with **t(14;18)** and BCL2, occurring in older adults with nodal follicles.
It is not rapidly growing and is not a jaw tumour.

**C. Multiple myeloma**
A plasma cell neoplasm of older adults with lytic bone lesions and M protein.
It is not seen in children.

## Mind Capsule

> **t(8;14) = c-MYC = Burkitt**; endemic form: jaw, EBV; sporadic form: abdomen (ileocecal).
