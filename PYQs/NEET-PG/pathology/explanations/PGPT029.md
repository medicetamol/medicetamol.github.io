# Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma

**Medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC)** arises from **parafollicular C cells**, secreting **calcitonin**. It stains for **chromogranin and synaptophysin** (neuroendocrine markers), and TTF-1 is positive (thyroid origin). **Amyloid** (derived from procalcitonin) is deposited in the stroma. It is linked to **RET** mutations (MEN 2A and 2B).

## Trigger Point

> Neck mass + **chromogranin, synaptophysin** positive + **amyloid deposition**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Papillary thyroid carcinoma**
Shows **Orphan Annie eye nuclei**, nuclear grooves and psammoma bodies.
It is not neuroendocrine and has no amyloid.

**B. Follicular thyroid carcinoma**
Diagnosed by capsular or vascular invasion; spreads by blood.
It does not stain for neuroendocrine markers.

**D. Anaplastic thyroid carcinoma**
Aggressive undifferentiated tumour in the elderly, with rapidly growing mass and dyspnoea.
It lacks neuroendocrine markers and is usually TTF-1 negative.

## Mind Capsule

> MTC = C cells + calcitonin + **amyloid** + RET (MEN 2); papillary = Orphan Annie nuclei.
