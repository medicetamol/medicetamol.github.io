# Hereditary Spherocytosis

**Hereditary spherocytosis** is autosomal dominant (spectrin, ankyrin, band 3 defects), producing **spherocytes** with high **MCHC**, **jaundice, splenomegaly** and **pigment gallstones**. A **positive family history** supports the diagnosis.

## Trigger Point

> Adolescent + jaundice + splenomegaly + **father similarly affected** + **high MCHC**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. IDA**
Microcytic hypochromic anaemia with **low MCHC**, with no jaundice or splenomegaly.
It is not familial haemolytic.

**C. Thalassemia major**
Presents in infancy with severe anaemia, target cells and hepatosplenomegaly; MCHC is low.
Onset in adolescence does not fit.

**D. AIHA**
Acquired haemolysis with spherocytes and a positive Coombs test.
It does not run in families.

## Mind Capsule

> Raised MCHC is typical of **spherocytes** (HS); both HS and AIHA have spherocytes, but only AIHA is Coombs positive.
