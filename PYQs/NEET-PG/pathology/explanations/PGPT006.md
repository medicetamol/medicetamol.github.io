# Membranous Nephropathy

**Membranous nephropathy** causes **nephrotic syndrome** in adults. It shows **diffuse thickening of the GBM** and **subepithelial immune deposits**. Silver stain highlights **spikes** of new basement membrane between deposits, and a **"spike and dome"** pattern. Secondary causes include **hepatitis B, SLE, malignancy and drugs**.

## Trigger Point

> Nephrotic features + **hepatitis B** + **spike and dome** pattern.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Minimal change disease**
Common in children; normal light microscopy with **foot process effacement** on EM.
There is no spike-and-dome pattern.

**C. FSGS**
Shows segmental sclerosis; associated with HIV, heroin and obesity.
No GBM spikes.

**D. PSGN-associated disease**
A nephritic syndrome with subepithelial humps, but not spike-and-dome and not nephrotic.
Hepatitis B is not the trigger.

## Mind Capsule

> Spike and dome (silver stain) = **membranous**; associations: HBV, SLE, malignancy, NSAIDs; anti-PLA2R antibodies in primary form.
