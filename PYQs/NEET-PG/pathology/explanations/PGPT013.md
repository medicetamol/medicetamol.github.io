# Blood Cancer

**Benzene** is a bone marrow toxin. Chronic exposure causes **marrow suppression (aplastic anaemia)** and is a recognised cause of **leukaemia (mainly AML) and MDS**. Weakness and easy fatigability suggest anaemia from marrow damage.

## Trigger Point

> **Benzene** exposure in a factory + fatigue.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Urinary bladder cancer**
Linked to aromatic amines (**β-naphthylamine**), aniline dyes, smoking and *S. haematobium*.
Benzene is not the main cause.

**C. Carcinoma gall bladder**
Associated with gallstones, chronic *Salmonella typhi* carriage and porcelain gallbladder.
It is not an occupational benzene cancer.

**D. Hepatocellular carcinoma**
Linked to HBV, HCV, aflatoxin, vinyl chloride and alcohol.
It is not linked to benzene.

## Mind Capsule

> Benzene = **leukaemia (AML) + aplastic anaemia**; vinyl chloride = angiosarcoma of liver; naphthylamine = bladder cancer.
