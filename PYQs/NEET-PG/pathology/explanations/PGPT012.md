# Asbestosis (Asbestos Exposure)

**Malignant mesothelioma** is caused by **asbestos exposure**, and cement factory workers can be exposed through asbestos-cement products. The risk is **not increased by smoking**, unlike the risk of bronchogenic carcinoma, which is multiplied in asbestos-exposed smokers. The option "asbestosis" represents the asbestos-related disease group.

## Trigger Point

> **Cement factory** for 20 years + **mesothelioma**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Silicosis**
Due to silica dust, in miners and sandblasters; raises TB and lung cancer risk.
It is not associated with mesothelioma.

**C. Coal workers' pneumoconiosis**
Due to coal dust, with anthracosis and progressive massive fibrosis.
There is no link to mesothelioma.

**D. Bagassosis**
A hypersensitivity pneumonitis from mouldy sugarcane bagasse (*Thermoactinomyces*).
It is not malignant.

## Mind Capsule

> Asbestos = mesothelioma, pleural plaques, bronchogenic carcinoma; **smoking does not increase mesothelioma risk**.
