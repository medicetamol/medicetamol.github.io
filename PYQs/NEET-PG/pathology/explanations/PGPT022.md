# Apoptosis and Pyroptosis

**Caspases** drive **apoptosis** (initiators 8, 9; executioner 3, 6, 7) and **pyroptosis** (inflammatory caspases 1, 4, 5, 11, which cleave gasdermin D and activate IL-1β). **Necrosis** is unregulated, and **necroptosis** is **caspase-independent**, mediated by **RIPK1, RIPK3 and MLKL**.

## Trigger Point

> Cell death types that require **caspase activation**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Necrosis and apoptosis**
Necrosis is a passive, unregulated death due to injury and does not require caspases.
Only apoptosis qualifies.

**C. Apoptosis and necroptosis**
Necroptosis proceeds through **RIPK3-MLKL**, a caspase-independent pathway.
It occurs when caspase-8 is inhibited.

**D. Apoptosis only**
Pyroptosis also requires inflammatory caspases (caspase-1 and others).
It leaves out a second caspase-dependent pathway.

## Mind Capsule

> Caspase-dependent = **apoptosis + pyroptosis**; caspase-independent = necroptosis (RIPK3-MLKL) and necrosis.
