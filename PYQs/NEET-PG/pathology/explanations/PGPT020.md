# Mismatch Repair Gene

**HNPCC (Lynch syndrome)** is autosomal dominant and results from germline mutations in **DNA mismatch repair genes** (**MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2**), leading to **microsatellite instability** and early-onset colorectal and endometrial cancers.

## Trigger Point

> **HNPCC** = defect in which repair pathway.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Nucleotide excision**
Defective in **xeroderma pigmentosum**.
It repairs UV-induced pyrimidine dimers.

**B. Base pair (base) excision**
Defective in **MUTYH-associated polyposis**.
It removes single damaged bases.

**C. Point mutation**
A type of mutation, not a repair pathway or gene.
It does not specify the defect.

## Mind Capsule

> HNPCC = **mismatch repair** (MLH1, MSH2); XP = NER; MUTYH = BER; BRCA = homologous recombination.
