# Kearns-Sayre Syndrome

The pedigree pattern matched by this question is **mitochondrial (maternal) inheritance**: an affected mother passes the trait to **all her children**, and affected fathers transmit to none. **Kearns-Sayre syndrome** is a mitochondrial DNA deletion disorder with ophthalmoplegia, retinitis pigmentosa and cardiac conduction defects.

## Trigger Point

> Pedigree showing **transmission only through females**, to all offspring.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Duchenne muscular dystrophy**
**X-linked recessive**, affecting males, with transmission through carrier mothers.
Affected fathers do not pass it to sons.

**B. Huntington's disease**
**Autosomal dominant** with anticipation and vertical transmission from either parent.
Both sexes transmit it.

**D. Marfan syndrome**
**Autosomal dominant** (FBN1), transmitted by either parent.
It is not maternal-only.

## Mind Capsule

> Mitochondrial inheritance = **maternal only, all children affected** (variable heteroplasmy); no father-to-child transmission.
