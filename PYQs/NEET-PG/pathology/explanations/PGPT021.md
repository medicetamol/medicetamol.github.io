# Follicular Lymphoma

**Follicular lymphoma** is an indolent B-cell lymphoma with **t(14;18)** causing **BCL2 overexpression**. Lymph nodes show **effaced architecture with neoplastic follicles** composed of **centrocytes (cleaved/indented nuclei)** and a few centroblasts. The cells are **CD10+, BCL2+, CD19+, CD20+** and CD5−.

## Trigger Point

> Painless nodes, **effaced architecture**, **indented nuclei**, **CD10 and BCL2 positive**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Burkitt's**
High-grade lymphoma with **c-MYC t(8;14)**, **starry-sky** pattern and CD10+, but BCL2 is negative.
It presents with rapidly growing masses.

**C. NHL**
A broad category including follicular lymphoma, not a specific diagnosis.
The immunophenotype points to a particular type.

**D. Mycosis fungoides**
A cutaneous T-cell lymphoma with **Pautrier microabscesses** and CD4+.
It does not present as a nodal CD10+ lymphoma.

## Mind Capsule

> Follicular lymphoma = **t(14;18) + BCL2 + CD10**; mantle cell = t(11;14) cyclin D1; Burkitt = t(8;14).
