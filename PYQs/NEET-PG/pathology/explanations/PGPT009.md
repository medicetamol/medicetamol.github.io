# Flow Cytometry

An elderly patient with **marked lymphocytosis and smudge cells** on peripheral smear has **chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL)**. The next step to confirm is **flow cytometry**, showing **CD5, CD19, CD23 positivity** (with weak CD20 and surface Ig). Treatment is not started in asymptomatic early-stage disease.

## Key Finding

> Many small mature lymphocytes with smudged, fragile cell remnants (smudge cells).

## Trigger Point

> Elderly + **marked lymphocytosis** + **smudge cells**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Stem cell transplantation**
Reserved for selected high-risk or relapsed patients.
It is not a diagnostic step.

**B. Bone marrow biopsy**
It is not required for diagnosis when peripheral flow cytometry is diagnostic.
It may be done later for staging or before therapy.

**D. High-dose chemotherapy**
Not indicated before confirming the diagnosis; early CLL is often only observed.
Treatment follows staging.

## Mind Capsule

> CLL = **CD5+, CD19+, CD23+** with smudge cells; confirm by flow cytometry.
