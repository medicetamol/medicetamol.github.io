# Hepatic Adenoma

**Hepatocellular (hepatic) adenoma** is a benign liver tumour in **young women on oral contraceptives** (or anabolic steroids). It can **rupture and bleed** (RUQ pain, haemoperitoneum), and carries a small risk of malignant change, so **resection** is advised.

## Trigger Point

> 35-year-old woman + **long-term OCP use** + RUQ pain + liver mass excised.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Hepatocellular carcinoma**
Occurs in cirrhosis (HBV, HCV, alcohol) and shows raised AFP.
There is no cirrhosis here.

**B. Focal nodular hyperplasia**
Shows a **central stellate scar**, usually asymptomatic and not hormone-driven.
It rarely bleeds and needs no resection.

**D. Metastasis**
Usually multiple lesions in a patient with a known primary.
No primary is mentioned.

## Mind Capsule

> OCP + liver mass + haemorrhage risk = **hepatic adenoma**; FNH has a central scar and no treatment.
