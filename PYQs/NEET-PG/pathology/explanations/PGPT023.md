# t(15;17)

**Acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL)** is defined by **t(15;17)**, which fuses **PML** with **RARA**, blocking differentiation at the promyelocyte stage. The smear shows **hypergranular promyelocytes with Auer rods (faggot cells)**, and bleeding results from **DIC**.

## Key Finding

> Large atypical cell with abundant dense purple granular cytoplasm and irregular nucleus = hypergranular promyelocyte.

## Trigger Point

> Bleeding + **hypergranular promyelocytes** on smear.

## Common AML Translocations

| Translocation | Disease |
|---|---|
| t(15;17) | APL (ATRA-responsive) |
| t(8;21) | AML M2, good prognosis |
| inv(16) | AML M4Eo, good prognosis |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. t(8;21)**
RUNX1-RUNX1T1, in AML with maturation (M2).
It has no DIC.

**C. t(14;18)**
BCL2-IGH, in **follicular lymphoma**.
It is not an AML translocation.

**D. inv(16)**
CBFB-MYH11, in AML M4 with eosinophils.
It does not give hypergranular promyelocytes.

## Mind Capsule

> **t(15;17) = PML-RARA = APL** = DIC, Auer rods, treated with ATRA and arsenic.
