# AML with t(15;17)

**Acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL, AML-M3)** shows **hypergranular promyelocytes with Auer rods (faggot cells)**, and the **t(15;17) PML-RARA** fusion. It causes **DIC**, giving bleeding with raised **PT and aPTT**. Treatment is **ATRA** (all-trans retinoic acid) with arsenic trioxide.

## Key Finding

> Abnormal large granular cells with dark nuclei and heavy cytoplasmic granules = hypergranular promyelocytes.

## Trigger Point

> Young adult + bleeding + low platelets + **raised PT and aPTT** + hypergranular cells.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. AML with t(8;21)**
RUNX1-RUNX1T1; Auer rods in myeloblasts and favourable prognosis.
It does not usually cause DIC.

**B. AML with inv(16)**
CBFB-MYH11, with abnormal eosinophils in the marrow (M4Eo).
DIC is not the hallmark.

**D. AML with t(11;22)**
Not a standard recurrent AML translocation in this classification.
It does not match hypergranular cells with DIC.

## Mind Capsule

> APL = **t(15;17) PML-RARA** = DIC + Auer rods; treat with **ATRA**.
