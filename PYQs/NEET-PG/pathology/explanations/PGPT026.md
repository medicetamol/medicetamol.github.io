# Craniopharyngioma

**Craniopharyngioma** arises from **Rathke's pouch remnants** in the suprasellar region. The **adamantinomatous type** (children) shows **wet keratin**, **calcification**, cystic "machine-oil" fluid and stellate reticulum. Presentation is with **visual disturbance (bitemporal hemianopia) and growth retardation** from pituitary compression.

## Trigger Point

> Child + suprasellar mass + **wet keratin**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Medulloblastoma**
A posterior fossa tumour with **Homer Wright rosettes** and small blue cells.
It is not suprasellar and has no keratin.

**C. Glioma**
Includes astrocytoma and optic pathway glioma; glial cells with GFAP positivity.
They do not make wet keratin.

**D. Pituitary adenoma**
Sellar tumour of monomorphic cells, common in adults, with hormonal effects.
It has no keratin or calcification.

## Mind Capsule

> Child + suprasellar + **calcification + wet keratin** = craniopharyngioma (Rathke's pouch, BRAF in papillary type, β-catenin in adamantinomatous).
