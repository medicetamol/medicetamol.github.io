# Aplastic Anaemia

**Aplastic anaemia** is marrow failure presenting as **pancytopenia** (bleeding, fever, fatigue), with a **hypocellular marrow** with fat replacement, **low reticulocyte count and absent megakaryocytes**, and **no blasts or immature cells**. There is no organomegaly or lymphadenopathy.

## Trigger Point

> Pancytopenia + **low reticulocytes**, **absent megakaryocytes** and **no immature cells**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. MDS**
Shows a **hypercellular** marrow with dysplasia and sometimes excess blasts.
Megakaryocytes are present with abnormal forms.

**C. PNH**
Haemolytic anaemia with haemoglobinuria; reticulocytes are raised, with CD55/CD59 loss.
It can progress to aplasia, but the stem shows none of the haemolysis.

**D. None**
The clinical picture is typical for aplastic anaemia.

## Mind Capsule

> Pancytopenia + hypocellular marrow + no blasts + no organomegaly = **aplastic anaemia**.
