# Capnography

A sustained **end-tidal CO₂ waveform** over several breaths is the most reliable (gold-standard) confirmation of tracheal placement. Oesophageal intubation gives no waveform or one that disappears within a few breaths. Direct visualisation of the tube passing the cords is also used.

## Trigger Point

> "**Best** method to confirm the position of the endotracheal tube."

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. X-ray chest**
Confirms tube depth (tip about 2-3 cm above the carina), but it is delayed and not the first-line confirmation.

**B. Auscultation**
A bedside adjunct that is unreliable: sounds are transmitted from the stomach or an endobronchial tube.

**D. Chest expansion**
Unreliable; chest rise can occur with gastric inflation or a misplaced tube.

## Mind Capsule

> Best confirmation of ETT = sustained capnography (EtCO₂ waveform).
