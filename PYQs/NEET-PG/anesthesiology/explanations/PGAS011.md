# Must Be Followed by a Formal Tracheostomy

**Needle cricothyroidotomy** (14-16G cannula through the cricothyroid membrane with jet insufflation) is a **temporary bridge** of about 30-45 minutes. It oxygenates but ventilates poorly, so CO₂ accumulates. A **definitive airway** (formal tracheostomy or surgical airway) must follow.

## Trigger Point

> Airway obstruction with debris in the mouth; needle passed through the front of the neck between thyroid and cricoid cartilages.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Adequate ventilation for up to 6 hours**
Effective only for about 30-45 minutes before hypercapnia limits it.

**C. Allows removal of large foreign bodies**
A narrow needle only bypasses the obstruction; it cannot remove foreign bodies.

**D. Safe for prolonged airway management**
It is a temporary measure and needs a definitive airway.

## Mind Capsule

> Needle cricothyroidotomy = 30-45 min bridge; oxygenation is adequate but CO₂ builds up → needs a definitive airway.
