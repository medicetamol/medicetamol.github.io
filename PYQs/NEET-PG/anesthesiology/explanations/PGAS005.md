# Suxamethonium

**Malignant hyperthermia** is a pharmacogenetic hypermetabolic crisis (RYR1 defect). Triggers are **volatile anaesthetics (halothane)** and **succinylcholine (suxamethonium)**, giving rigidity, hyperthermia and ↑ EtCO₂. Among the listed muscle relaxants, only suxamethonium is a trigger. Treatment is dantrolene.

## Trigger Point

> Halothane anaesthesia with **hyperthermia and muscle rigidity**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. D-tubocurarine**
A non-depolarising relaxant (histamine release), not a trigger of malignant hyperthermia.

**C. Cisatracurium**
Non-depolarising, eliminated by Hofmann degradation, and safe in susceptible patients.

**D. Rocuronium**
Non-depolarising and safe in malignant hyperthermia.

## Mind Capsule

> MH triggers = volatile agents + succinylcholine.
> Non-depolarising blockers are safe; treat with dantrolene.
