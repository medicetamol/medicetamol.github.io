# Atracurium

A benzylisoquinolinium relaxant that causes **dose-dependent histamine release** (flushing, rash, hypotension, tachycardia, sometimes bronchospasm), especially with rapid bolus injection.

## Muscle Relaxants and Histamine Release

| Group | Drugs |
|---|---|
| Histamine release | d-Tubocurarine, atracurium, mivacurium |
| Histamine-sparing | Cisatracurium, vecuronium, rocuronium, pancuronium |

## Trigger Point

> Flushing and rash over the **neck and anterior chest** soon after a muscle relaxant.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Cisatracurium**
An isomer of atracurium that is histamine-sparing and cleared by Hofmann elimination.

**C. Vecuronium**
Steroidal, cardiovascularly stable, no histamine release.

**D. Pancuronium**
Steroidal and vagolytic, causing tachycardia and hypertension rather than histamine effects.

## Mind Capsule

> Flushing + rash after a relaxant = histamine release → atracurium (cisatracurium is histamine-sparing).
