# Arachnoid Mater

At L3-L4 or L4-L5 the needle crosses the supraspinous and interspinous ligaments, ligamentum flavum, epidural space and dura, then the **arachnoid**, and enters the subarachnoid space where CSF flows. The arachnoid lies against the dura, so CSF appears right after it. The **pia** is attached to the cord and is not pierced.

## Trigger Point

> Lumbar puncture in suspected meningitis. The question asks for the **last structure encountered**.

## Layers Pierced in Lumbar Puncture

| Order | Layer |
|---|---|
| 1 | Skin and superficial fascia |
| 2 | Supraspinous ligament |
| 3 | Interspinous ligament |
| 4 | Ligamentum flavum |
| 5 | Epidural space |
| 6 | Dura mater |
| 7 | Arachnoid mater → CSF |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Ligamentum flavum**
Crossed early, just before the epidural space.

**C. Dura mater**
Pierced just before the arachnoid.

**D. Pia mater**
Closely applied to the spinal cord and not reached by the needle.

## Mind Capsule

> LP needle ends at the arachnoid → subarachnoid space (CSF); the pia is not pierced.
