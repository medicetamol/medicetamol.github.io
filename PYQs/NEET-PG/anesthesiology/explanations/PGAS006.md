# Head Tilt, Chin Lift

One hand on the forehead **tilts the head back** while the fingers of the other hand **lift the chin**, lifting the tongue away from the posterior pharynx. Contraindicated when cervical spine injury is suspected (use jaw thrust).

## Trigger Point

> Supine patient, one hand on the **forehead tilting the head backward**, the other **lifting the chin**.

## Airway Manoeuvres

| Manoeuvre | Use |
|---|---|
| Head tilt-chin lift | Opening the airway when no cervical injury is suspected |
| Jaw thrust | Suspected cervical spine injury (trauma) |
| In-line manual stabilisation | Holds the head neutral during airway interventions in trauma |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Jaw thrust**
Both hands lift the mandible at its angles with no head extension.

**C. Head extension**
Only one component. The manoeuvre named here combines extension with chin lift.

**D. In-line manual stabilization**
The head is held neutral to protect the cervical spine. There is no tilt or chin lift.

## Mind Capsule

> Forehead tilt + chin lift = head tilt-chin lift.
> Jaw thrust (both hands at mandibular angles) is for trauma.
