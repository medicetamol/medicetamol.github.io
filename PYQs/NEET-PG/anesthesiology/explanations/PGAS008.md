# Recovery Position

The lateral position for an **unresponsive patient who is breathing normally and has a pulse**. It keeps the airway open, brings the tongue forward and lets fluid drain, reducing aspiration risk. Patients without breathing or a pulse need resuscitation.

## Trigger Point

> Illustration of a person lying on their side, arm supporting the head, opposite leg bent forward, labelled the **recovery position**.

## Unconscious Patient Approach

| Pulse and breathing | Action |
|---|---|
| Both present | Recovery position |
| Pulse present, breathing absent | Rescue breathing |
| Both absent | CPR |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Pulse and breathing absent**
Cardiac arrest: CPR and defibrillation.

**B. Pulse present, breathing absent**
Respiratory arrest: airway opening and rescue breaths.

**D. Pulse absent, breathing present**
Gasping without a pulse is treated as cardiac arrest with CPR.

## Mind Capsule

> Unconscious + pulse + breathing present = recovery position.
> No pulse = CPR.
