# Cefazolin

Cardiac surgery prophylaxis targets skin flora (*Staphylococcus* and *Streptococcus*). **Cefazolin** is the first-line agent, given IV within 60 minutes before incision. Vancomycin is used for β-lactam allergy or MRSA risk. Some centres use cefuroxime (guideline-dependent).

## Drug Facts

| Drug | Dose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cefazolin | 2 g IV (3 g if ≥ 120 kg), within 60 min of incision | First-line; redose for prolonged surgery or major blood loss |
| Vancomycin | 15 mg/kg IV, started within 120 min of incision | MRSA or β-lactam allergy; infuse slowly (red man syndrome) |

## Trigger Point

> **Preoperative antibiotic prophylaxis** in **cardiac surgery**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Penicillin G**
Narrow spectrum and inactivated by staphylococcal penicillinase.

**B. Erythromycin**
A bacteriostatic macrolide with poor activity against the target flora.

**C. Azithromycin**
A macrolide with no role in cardiac surgical prophylaxis.

## Mind Capsule

> Cardiac surgery prophylaxis = cefazolin (vancomycin if MRSA or β-lactam allergy).
