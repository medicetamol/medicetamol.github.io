# Dermatitis Herpetiformis

An autoimmune subepidermal blistering disease linked to **gluten-sensitive enteropathy (coeliac disease)**. It gives intensely itchy grouped papulovesicles on the extensor surfaces (elbows, knees, buttocks). **DIF** shows **granular IgA at the tips of the dermal papillae**. Treat with **dapsone** (check G6PD) and a gluten-free diet.

## Trigger Point

> Pruritic rash on elbows and buttocks **+** coeliac disease **+** **granular IgA** at the dermal papillary tips.

## DIF Patterns

| Disease | DIF finding |
|---|---|
| Dermatitis herpetiformis | Granular IgA in dermal papillae |
| Linear IgA disease | Linear IgA at the basement membrane |
| Pemphigus | Intercellular IgG and C3 (fishnet) |
| Bullous pemphigoid | Linear IgG and C3 at the basement membrane |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Pemphigus vulgaris**
Intercellular IgG deposition with flaccid bullae.

**C. Bullous pemphigoid**
Linear IgG and C3 along the basement membrane, in the elderly with tense bullae.

**D. Psoriasis**
Scaly plaques with no immune deposits on DIF.

## Mind Capsule

> Itchy extensor vesicles + coeliac + granular IgA in papillary tips = Dermatitis herpetiformis (dapsone).
