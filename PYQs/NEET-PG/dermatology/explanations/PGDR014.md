# PCR

**Lupus vulgaris** is paucibacillary cutaneous tuberculosis: a red-brown indurated plaque with apple-jelly nodules and central scarring. AFB are scanty and cultures slow, so **PCR** (high sensitivity and specificity) is the best confirmatory test among the options. Histology shows tuberculoid granulomas.

## Trigger Point

> Indurated cheek plaque with **central atrophy**, plus **apical calcification** on chest X-ray.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Mantoux test**
Supportive but cannot confirm active cutaneous disease; it can be positive from past exposure or BCG.

**B. Slit skin smear**
Used for acid-fast bacilli in leprosy.

**D. Probe test**
A bedside clinical sign (the soft nodule yields to a probe), not a confirmatory test.

## Mind Capsule

> Lupus vulgaris = paucibacillary cutaneous TB → PCR confirms.
