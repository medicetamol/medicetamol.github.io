# Pemphigus Vulgaris

An autoimmune intra-epidermal blistering disease with IgG against **desmoglein 3** (± Dsg1), causing **suprabasal acantholysis** ("row of tombstones"). It gives flaccid bullae that rupture easily into painful erosions, with common mucosal involvement and a positive Nikolsky sign.

## Trigger Point

> **Flaccid**, easily ruptured bullae with a **suprabasal split** on biopsy.

## Level of Split in Blistering Disorders

| Level | Disorders |
|---|---|
| Suprabasal | Pemphigus vulgaris, pemphigus vegetans |
| Subcorneal / granular | Pemphigus foliaceus, SSSS |
| Subepidermal | Bullous pemphigoid, dermatitis herpetiformis |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Pemphigus vegetans**
A rare variant with vegetative plaques in flexures. It is not described here.

**C. Pemphigus foliaceous**
Superficial split in the granular layer, giving scaly, crusted erosions with no mucosal involvement.

**D. Erythema multiforme**
Target lesions with keratinocyte necrosis (often HSV or drug triggered), not acantholysis.

## Mind Capsule

> Flaccid bullae + suprabasal split = Pemphigus vulgaris; granular-layer split = foliaceus.
