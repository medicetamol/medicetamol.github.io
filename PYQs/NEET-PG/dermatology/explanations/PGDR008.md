# p-Phenylenediamine

**PPD** is the commonest hair-dye allergen. It causes **allergic contact dermatitis** (type IV hypersensitivity) in a pattern matching dye contact: neck, scalp margin, ears and eyelids. Patch testing confirms it.

## Trigger Point

> A reaction on the back of the neck, in a contact-area distribution, **after hair dye**.

## Common Contact Allergens

| Allergen | Common source |
|---|---|
| Nickel | Jewellery, buckles, watches |
| p-Phenylenediamine | Hair dye, black henna tattoos |
| Chromates | Cement, leather |
| Balsam of Peru | Fragrances, flavourings |
| Parthenium | Weed pollen (airborne contact dermatitis) |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Pollen**
Parthenium pollen causes airborne contact dermatitis of exposed skin, not a dye-contact pattern.

**B. Chromates**
Allergen in cement and leather.

**C. Balsam of Peru**
Marker for fragrance and flavouring allergy.

## Mind Capsule

> Hair dye + dermatitis in a contact distribution = PPD allergic contact dermatitis.
