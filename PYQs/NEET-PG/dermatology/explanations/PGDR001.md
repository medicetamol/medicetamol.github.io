# Amoxicillin-Clavulanate

A well-demarcated, erythematous, swollen hand is **cellulitis / erysipelas**, caused mainly by *Streptococcus pyogenes* and sometimes *S. aureus*. Amoxicillin-clavulanate covers both. MRSA risk or severe disease needs an anti-MRSA agent such as vancomycin.

## Trigger Point

> Well-demarcated erythematous swelling involving the hand and fingers.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Amikacin**
An aminoglycoside active mainly against aerobic Gram-negatives, with poor streptococcal activity and nephro- and ototoxicity.

**C. Norfloxacin**
An older fluoroquinolone used for urinary and gastrointestinal infections, with weak Gram-positive cover.

**D. Metronidazole**
Active against anaerobes and protozoa, with no useful streptococcal or staphylococcal activity.

## Mind Capsule

> Cellulitis / erysipelas = Streptococcus pyogenes (± S. aureus) → β-lactam such as amoxicillin-clavulanate.
