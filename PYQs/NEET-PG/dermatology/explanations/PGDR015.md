# Slender Dematiaceous Fungi

The mycetoma triad is **swelling, discharging sinuses and grains**. **Black grains** indicate **eumycetoma** (e.g. *Madurella mycetomatis*). KOH of the grain shows pigmented (dematiaceous) septate hyphae.

## Trigger Point

> Foot swelling **+** multiple discharging sinuses **+** **black granules**.

## Mycetoma

| Type | Organism | Grain colour |
|---|---|---|
| Eumycetoma | *Madurella mycetomatis* / *grisea* | Black |
| Eumycetoma | *Scedosporium (Pseudallescheria) boydii* | White |
| Actinomycetoma | *Actinomadura pelletieri* | Red |
| Actinomycetoma | *Nocardia*, *Actinomadura madurae*, *Streptomyces* | White to yellow |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Arthrospores**
Spore chains seen in dermatophyte infection of skin and hair, not in mycetoma grains.

**C. Yeast**
Budding cells of candidiasis or cryptococcosis, not a black-grain infection.

**D. Septate hyphae 4-5**
Generic hyaline septate hyphae (dermatophytes, moulds). A black grain implies pigmented hyphae.

## Mind Capsule

> Foot + sinuses + black grains = Eumycetoma → dematiaceous hyphae.
> Actinomycetoma = thin branching Gram-positive filaments.
