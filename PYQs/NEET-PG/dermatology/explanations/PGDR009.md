# Evaluate for Hyperandrogenism

Acne that does not respond to **isotretinoin plus antibiotics** in a woman suggests an underlying androgen excess, most often **PCOS** (also late-onset CAH or an androgen-secreting tumour). Look for hirsutism and irregular menses, and check testosterone, DHEAS and 17-OH progesterone.

## Trigger Point

> Female with acne **not resolving on oral isotretinoin and antibiotics**.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Look for dietary triggers**
High-glycaemic diet and dairy are minor modifiers, not the next investigation.

**C. Check for antibiotic resistance**
Isotretinoin acts independently of bacterial resistance, so resistance does not explain failure of both.

**D. Look for drug triggers**
Steroids, lithium, anabolic steroids and anticonvulsants can cause acneiform eruptions, but a hormonal driver is more likely with this treatment-resistant picture.

## Mind Capsule

> Acne in a woman resistant to isotretinoin and antibiotics = look for hyperandrogenism (PCOS).
