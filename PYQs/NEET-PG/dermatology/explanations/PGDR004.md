# Type 1 Lepra Reaction

A delayed (type IV) hypersensitivity reaction in borderline leprosy, often after starting MDT. Existing hypoanaesthetic patches turn **erythematous and oedematous**, with nerve pain, tenderness and risk of nerve damage. Treatment is **prednisolone** with continued MDT.

## Trigger Point

> **Erythematous, oedematous plaques over previously hypoanaesthetic patches** with pain, 2 months into MBMDT.

## Lepra Reactions

| Feature | Type 1 (reversal) | Type 2 (ENL) |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | Type IV (cell-mediated) | Type III (immune complex) |
| Seen in | Borderline leprosy | LL and BL |
| Picture | Inflamed existing patches, neuritis | New tender nodules, fever, neuritis |
| Treatment | Prednisolone | Prednisolone, thalidomide, clofazimine |

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cellulitis of face**
An acute bacterial infection with fever and no relation to pre-existing anaesthetic patches.

**C. Erythema Nodosum Leprosum**
Type 2 reaction with new crops of tender nodules and fever, mainly in LL and BL.

**D. Erysipelas**
Acute, sharply demarcated streptococcal infection with systemic toxicity.

## Mind Capsule

> Inflammation of existing patches + neuritis on MDT = Type 1 reaction.
> New tender nodules + fever = ENL.
