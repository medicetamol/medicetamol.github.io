# Phrynoderma

"Toad skin": **follicular hyperkeratosis** with rough keratotic papules over the extensor surfaces, especially the **elbows and knees**. Classically linked to **vitamin A deficiency** (also essential fatty acids and B vitamins). Night blindness supports vitamin A deficiency.

## Trigger Point

> Rough lesions over elbows and knees **+** diminished vision at night.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Keratosis pilaris**
A common, benign genetic follicular keratosis on arms and thighs, with no night blindness.

**C. Folliculitis**
Inflammation or infection of the hair follicle with pustules, not dry keratotic papules.

**D. Pyoderma**
A pus-forming bacterial skin infection.

## Mind Capsule

> Rough follicular papules on elbows and knees + night blindness = Phrynoderma (vitamin A deficiency).
