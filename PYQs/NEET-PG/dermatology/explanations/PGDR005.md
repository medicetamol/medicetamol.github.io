# Diarrhoea and Dementia (Pellagra)

**Pellagra** is niacin (B3) deficiency, giving photosensitive dermatitis on sun-exposed skin (the **Casal's necklace** around the neck), plus **diarrhoea** and **dementia**. It is seen with maize or jowar diets, Hartnup disease, carcinoid syndrome and isoniazid use.

## Trigger Point

> Symmetric dark, thickened, scaly skin over the neck and upper chest.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**B. Bitot's spots**
Seen in vitamin A deficiency (xerophthalmia).

**C. Angular stomatitis**
Typical of riboflavin (B2) deficiency (also iron deficiency).

**D. Loss of muscle coordination**
Ataxia occurs in thiamine, B12 and vitamin E deficiency, not in the pellagra triad.

## Mind Capsule

> Photosensitive necklace dermatitis + diarrhoea + dementia = Pellagra (niacin).

## Memory Hook

> **3 Ds**: Dermatitis, Diarrhoea, Dementia (a fourth D, Death, if untreated).
