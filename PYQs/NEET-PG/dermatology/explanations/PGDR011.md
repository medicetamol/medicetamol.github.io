# Malignant Melanoma

Pigmented, papillomatous (warty) naevoid lesions, brown to skin-coloured, such as a **congenital melanocytic naevus** with a verrucous surface, carry an increased risk of **malignant melanoma**. The risk is highest for large or giant congenital naevi.

## Trigger Point

> Multiple raised, papillomatous, wart-like nodules, **brown to skin-coloured**, varying in size.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Cataract**
Not an association of melanocytic naevi.

**B. Glaucoma**
Linked with the port-wine stain of Sturge-Weber syndrome, a vascular lesion.

**D. Basal cell carcinoma**
The BCC association is with naevus sebaceus and Gorlin syndrome, not pigmented naevoid lesions.

## Mind Capsule

> Pigmented papillomatous naevoid lesions → think malignant melanoma risk.
