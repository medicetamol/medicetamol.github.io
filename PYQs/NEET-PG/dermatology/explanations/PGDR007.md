# Kerion

An inflammatory form of **tinea capitis**, usually zoophilic dermatophytes. The host hypersensitivity response gives a **boggy, tender, pustular scalp mass** with hair loss and sometimes lymphadenopathy; scarring alopecia may follow. Treat with **oral griseofulvin or terbinafine**, not incision.

## Trigger Point

> Raised, boggy, tender scalp swelling with broken hairs and pustules.

## Why Not the Other Options?

**A. Folliculitis**
Superficial follicular pustules without a boggy mass or marked hair loss.

**B. Abscess**
A fluctuant pus collection needing drainage. A kerion is boggy and drained incorrectly if mistaken for an abscess.

**D. Scabies**
Intensely itchy burrows in web spaces and flexures, not a boggy scalp mass.

## Mind Capsule

> Boggy, tender, pustular scalp mass + hair loss = Kerion → oral antifungal, not I and D.
